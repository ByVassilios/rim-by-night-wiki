# Every item

Every item, weapon and piece of apparel this mod adds. Value is the base market value in silver.

## Items

| | Name | Value | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | **Bad Medicine Bag** | 350 | A pouch of tiny bones from a creature that died of disease, a Rat-spirit bound in them. Slipped to someone, it brings a month of a sickness no doctor can find. |
|  | **Egg sac** | 0 | An Ananasi's egg sac, the size of a melon. Kept between 0 and 40 C for a season it hatches; about half the time one of the spiderlings inside eats all the others and comes out an Ananasi. |
|  | **Pain-dagger** | 900 | A thin knife with a spirit of pain bound in it. Its Blade Slave pays a Gnosis to draw it, and anyone else who holds it bleeds until they let go. |
|  | **Plenipotentiary** | 250 | A ring with a stylised fox, a spirit of peace bound in it. Woken, it keeps its wearer from attack for half a minute, as long as the wearer starts nothing. |
|  | **Portable Door** | 800 | A stick of chalk with an Epiphling bound in it. A door drawn on a wall opens to whatever lies beyond, or, now and then, to somewhere else. |
|  | **Ratkin battery** | 15 | A fizzing bottle brewed out of junk by a Ratkin Engineer. Drunk, it gives a few hours of speed and strength. |
|  | **Rattle-trap** | 450 | A rattle of bone and dried seeds with a spirit of sleep bound in it. While the werebear who carries it sleeps, any foe who comes near must fight off sleep, and the bear wakes ready. |
|  | **Standard issue sunglasses** | 300 | Cheap plastic shades with a shadow spirit in them. Woken, they wrap the wearer in shadow, as the Gift. |
|  | **Tracking Web** | 600 | A ball of spider silk with a spirit of the Pattern Web bound in it. A thread laid on someone or something tugs toward them on any map or across the world, up to the wearer's Gnosis in threads. |
|  | **UPhone** | 500 | A smartphone with a Chimerling bound in it. Woken, it puts a call through to any faction from anywhere, as a comms console would. |
