# Ratkin

The wererats: Gaia's cullers, the most numerous Changing Breed and the least sane. A Ratkin is small, quick and
angry at everything human. It lives in tunnels, can't catch a disease, and calls its kind with a scream only
they hear. Ratkin fight from cover and run when it goes badly.

## How a Ratkin plays

- **Plague-proof.** No vanilla plague, infection or parasite takes hold.
- **Tunnels.** In the dark under a thick roof a Ratkin moves faster. Its Crinos digs fast.
- **Rat-holes.** Build them, and a Ratkin in Crinos or rat form can go in one and come out of another, unseen
  and untouchable on the way.
- **Keening.** At half health or downed, a Ratkin keens. The colony gets a letter and friendly Ratkin nearby
  come running.
- **Litters.** Once a day a Ratkin colonist makes up to ten tamed rats its kin. A wild rat comes when it calls.
- **The Birthing Plague.** A rite on an adult kin that might make a new Ratkin, and might kill. The odds show
  before it starts. A survivor wanders in a birthing madness for days, shorter with Ratkin colonists nearby.
- **Aspects.** Each aspect plays differently: a Blade Slave frenzies easily and starts with a dagger, a
  Twitcher's Rage jumps about, a Knife Skulker grows angry at injustice.
- **Fighting.** Crinos, with friends, then away at 40% health.

## Breeds

| Breed | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Homid** | A human kin who lived through the Birthing Plague. At the bottom of the nest. |
| **Metis** | Born of two Ratkin, deformed and sterile. The nest keeps them as guards and wardens. |
| **Rodens** | Born a rat and raised in the walls. Rodens sit at the top of the nest, as courtiers and Rat Kings. |

## Aspects

| Aspect | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Tunnel Runner** | Messengers and scouts who blend into any crowd and never stay long. |
| **Shadow Seer** | Mystics who hear the spirits and sometimes care more for the spirit world than this one. |
| **Knife Skulker** | Spies and assassins with a sense of justice. A Skulker never lets a target see the blow that kills it. |
| **Blade Slave** | The warriors, tough and thirsty for blood, each carrying a pain-dagger. |
| **Engineer** | A freak aspect: tinkerers who can make any machine run for a while out of junk. |
| **Plague Lord** | A freak aspect: healers who serve a spirit of disease, and spread what it asks. |
| **Munchmausen** | A freak aspect: liars so good that everyone believes them, themselves included. |
| **Twitcher** | A freak aspect: paranoid and forgettable, with a Rage that runs away with them. |

## Rites

| Rite | Kind | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rite of the Birthing Plague** | Mystic | 1 | A Ratkin bites a kin, human or rat, and passes on Rat's fever. Some die of it, some go mad, some come through unchanged, and a few wake as Ratkin. The odds are shown before the bite. |
| **Hazer Tag** | Minor | 1 | The Ratkin marks an unknowing patsy with its droppings. For as many days as it has Gnosis, anyone hunting the Ratkin follows the patsy's trail instead, so no hunter or nemesis can pick it out. |
| **Rite of the Pain-Dagger** | Mystic | 2 | A Blade Slave binds a spirit of pain into a knife. The dagger answers only its Blade Slave and cuts anyone else who holds it. |
| **Dedication Rite** | Mystic | 2 | A Rite of the Totem bound to one goal: the nearest enemy camp or base. The circle becomes a pack under the totem until the goal is gone, and then the pack breaks up, each member a little prouder for it. |
