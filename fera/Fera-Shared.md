# What every breed shares

Every Fera is built on Garou's rules, so most of [Being Garou](../garou/Being-Garou.md) holds for them too. This page
covers what's the same for all seven breeds and what each changes.

## Energy

- **Rage** works as Garou's for the Corax, Ratkin, Gurahl, Mokolé and Kitsune. The Ananasi and the
  Nuwisha have none.
- **Gnosis** works as Garou's, except for the Mokolé, who gain it only from sunlight.
- **Blood** is the Ananasi's pool: 10 points, refilled only by feeding. It pays for what Rage pays for in the
  others.
- **Willpower** works as Garou's.

## Forms

Each breed has its own forms on Garou's five slots: a human form, a near-man form, a war form, an in-between
form and an animal form. Not every breed has all five, and some forms open only with a Gift (the Ratkin's
Ratman and Rat Thing, the Ananasi's Araneid, the Mokolé's Reptoid). Each breed's page lists its forms and what
they add to the Attributes.

Shifting, the form bar and gear by body work as Garou's. Shifting is a Veil act unless the new form brings the
Delirium with it.

## Regeneration

The Corax, Ratkin, Gurahl, Mokolé and Nuwisha regenerate as Garou do. The Ananasi heal by spending
blood. The Kitsune heal like humans unless a Gift helps.

## Bane metals

| Breed | Burned by |
|---|---|
| Corax | Gold |
| Mokolé | Gold and silver |
| Ratkin, Gurahl | Silver |
| Kitsune | Silver hurts them like any blade, no worse |
| Ananasi, Nuwisha | Nothing |

A bane wound is aggravated and doesn't regenerate, as silver is for a Garou. Hunters who know what they're
after bring gold for a Corax.

## Frenzy

Breeds with Rage frenzy as Garou do, each in its own way. The Kitsune frenzy only as foxes. The Ananasi and the
Nuwisha never frenzy, unless a Nuwisha takes the Gift of Rage.

## Renown and rank

Each breed has its own three Renown tracks and its own rank names, and earns them by its own deeds: secrets for
the Corax, pranks for the Nuwisha, milestones for the Gurahl. Experience works as Garou's.

## Kin

Each breed has human kin, and most have animal kin too. Tamed animals of the right kind become a Fera's kin:
rats for the Ratkin, bears for the Gurahl. Ratkin and Ananasi kin never
have a First Change.

## The Joined Circle

Shifters of different breeds can form one pack with the Rite of the Joined Circle, Garou among them. It works
like Garou's Rite of the Totem. With a Nuwisha in the circle, the totem has to be a trickster.

| Rite | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rite of the Joined Circle** | Shifters of different breeds, Garou among them, sing of Gaia's need for renewal in a space decked out to please a spirit, and bind themselves to it as one pack. Their Totem dots together must reach the totem's cost. A Nuwisha in the circle means a trickster totem. |

## Merits and Backgrounds

| Merit | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Double Draught** | Can drink both of a dead body's eyes, not only one. |

| Background | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Umbral Glade** | A stretch of land where the Gauntlet is thin and Bear's children rest. Inside it a Gurahl steps sideways freely, regains Gnosis while it sleeps, and hibernates in half the time. Each dot makes it bigger: 36 cells at one dot up to 5000 at five. |
| **Mnesis** | The memories of every ancestor of its line. Each dot reaches further back, from a century to the time of the Dinosaur Kings, and lends a stronger ancestor's skill to a Mnesis quest. |
| **Wallow** | A home by the water where the Mokolé and their kin gather. On its home map it draws half again as much Gnosis from the sun, and a Mnesis trance takes half the time. |
