# Corax

The wereravens: Gaia's eyes and tattletales. A Corax is fragile, fast and curious, and can't keep a secret to
itself. It flies in two of its three forms, gold burns it, and it drinks the eyes of the dead to learn how they
died. Corax have no packs, no auspices and no real tribes; every one of them belongs to Raven and to the sun.

## How a Corax plays

- **Flight.** Crinos and Corvid can fly (a jump of 25 cells, 15 for a homid) and peck out an eye. A caravan with
  a raven in it travels faster.
- **Light bones.** A Corax dodges well but takes more from blunt blows.
- **Secrets.** A Corax learns secrets about people and factions. It can tell them to a colonist, whisper them to
  Raven, sell them to the subject's faction or its enemies, or hold one over a trader for a better price. Each
  one is Wisdom. The Secrets page on its tab lists them.
- **Raven's laws.** Holding a secret past a week costs Wisdom. Three days in a bad mood cost Honor. A season
  without flying costs Glory.
- **Shiny things.** A drafted Corax near silver, gold or jade rolls Willpower or pockets it, and carries its
  finds to its bedroom.
- **Drinking eyes.** Right-click a fresh corpse in a flying form. The left eye names the killer, the right gives
  a secret and some of the dead's best skill. One eye a body, unless the Corax has the Double Draught Merit.
- **The uPhone.** A fetish that calls any faction you know by radio.
- **Fighting.** A Corax fights from range or from the air, takes the Crinos when cornered, and flies off at
  half health.

## Breeds

| Breed | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Homid** | Born human, with thin black hair, black eyes and a ring finger longer than the middle one. Slower to take to the air than a raven-born Corax. |
| **Corvid** | Born a raven, and still a raven at heart: a fast flier, poor with tools, books and medicine. |

## Groups

| Group | What it is |
|---|---|
| **No group** | Most Corax answer to Raven alone and belong to no group. |
| **Chasers** | Gangs of young Corax who hunt the uncanny together. They break up within a year or two. |
| **Leshy** | Paranoid forest tricksters. Three in four of them were born ravens. |
| **Hermetic Order of Swift Light** | The breed's business arm: couriers and sellers of secrets. |
| **Gulls of Battle** | Seekers of old stories and new corpses. They follow the fighting. |
| **Murder's Daughters** | Women only. They pick fights to catch the Morrigan's eye, take the birdman's shape to do it, and stay in a fight longer than other Corax. |
| **Tulugaq** | The tricksters' tricksters, close to the Nuwisha. |
| **The Morrigan** | Always exactly three women, with eagles. They turn up before every great battle. |
| **The Sun-Lost** | Corax who left the sun for the deep spirit world. |
