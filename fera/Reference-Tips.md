# Learning tips

The tips the game shows the first times you meet something.

| Tip | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ananasi** | An Ananasi is a werespider. It has no Rage: it spends blood instead, which it drinks from people or animals.<br><br>Keep it above 3 blood. Badly hurt, it scatters into a swarm of spiders and reforms days later, changed. In the room of its Sylie it regains Gnosis. |
| **Corax** | A Corax is a wereraven. It trades in secrets: they show on its page in the Fera tab, and each can be sold or whispered to Raven.<br><br>It drinks the eyes of the dead to learn what they saw, it can't resist something shiny, and gold hurts it the way silver hurts a Garou. |
| **Gurahl** | A Gurahl is a werebear. It starts as an Arcas and moves through the auspices as it grows, and its Umbral Glade is the only place it steps sideways.<br><br>In winter it grows drowsy and wants to hibernate. A season away from its home fails its duty. |
| **Kitsune** | A Kitsune is a werefox. Instead of an auspice it follows a Path, chosen at the Rite of the Crossroads or by its own button.<br><br>It heals as a human does, ages slower as it rises in rank and isn't hurt by silver. It always runs from a fight it can't win. |
| **Mokolé** | A Mokolé is a weredragon. It takes Gnosis only from the sun, so meditating under a roof or by night does nothing.<br><br>Its Archid form is built from points, one for each dot of Gnosis: spend them on its Fera tab page. Its Mnesis lets it borrow an ancestor's skills in a trance. |
| **Nuwisha** | A Nuwisha is a werecoyote. It has no Rage. It wears one of Coyote's faces at a time, and each face has its Ban.<br><br>Right-click someone to play a prank on them. A good prank earns it Renown, and the same person can only be pranked once a season. |
| **Ratkin** | A Ratkin is a wererat. Its aspect, shown on its Fera tab page, sets what it's good at and what it can't help.<br><br>Tamed rats become its kin. New Ratkin come only from the Birthing Plague, a rite most mortals don't survive. Ratkin step sideways only where nobody is watching. |
| **The Changing Breeds** | One of the Changing Breeds lives in your colony now: a shapeshifter, but not a Garou.<br><br>The Fera tab shows its kind, its group and aspect, its Rage, Gnosis and Willpower, and a page of its kind's own. Each breed has its own forms, its own bane metal and its own ways. The Fera button on the bottom bar lists your Fera and the breeds you've met. |
| **The Joined Circle** | Garou's own Rite of the Totem won't take a Fera. The Rite of the Joined Circle will: it binds any shifters, Garou and Fera of every breed, into one pack under one totem.<br><br>A Nuwisha in the circle means the totem has to be a trickster. |
| **The War of Rage** | Long ago the Garou turned on the other Changing Breeds, and the fighting never quite stopped. Septs still strike Fera homes now and then, and some breeds strike back.<br><br>When your colony hears of a fight, its Garou and that breed's Fera think a little less of each other for a while. |
