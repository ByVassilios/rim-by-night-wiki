# Nuwisha

The werecoyotes: Coyote's children, who teach the proud, the foolish and the Wyrm through tricks that are often
deadly. They have no Rage and never frenzy, silver doesn't hurt them, and they regenerate like Garou. Each follows
one face of Coyote.

## How a Nuwisha plays

- **No Rage.** The Gift of Rage gives it 5 Rage for an hour. While it lasts it can frenzy and silver burns.
- **Faces of Coyote.** Pick one with the "Face of Coyote" button. The first is free; after that it can change
  once a season for experience. Each face gives something: stealth, toughness, research, trade, plants and
  more. Ptah opens the Rite of Dansing. Raven's face turns away from a Nuwisha who carries too much silver.
- **Pranks.** Right-click a person and choose "Play a prank": a tripwire, swapped food, a stolen garment, a false
  alarm, a rigged blast, a falling weight, or setting two people on each other. Most wait hours for the right
  moment. Each target once a season.
- **Renown from pranks.** The Wyrm and the proud are worth the most. Hurting an innocent costs Humor, and being
  seen by the target's side costs Cunning.
- **Sing Back the Dead.** A rank 5 rite on a fresh corpse. Coyote's odds show first. If Coyote says no, singing
  on kills the singer.
- **Sendeh's cries.** Laughter that sours moods, and a predator's cry that scares off wild animals.
- **Fighting.** At range in human form, Manabozho against a lone foe close by, coyote form to run.

## Breeds

| Breed | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Homid** | Born human. The First Change came as a revelation, not a horror. |
| **Latrani** | Born a coyote, and far more common than wolf-born Garou. |

## Aspects

A Nuwisha is born under the new moon. One who passes the Rite of Dansing becomes an Umbral Danser, with Gifts of
its own.

| Aspect | What it is |
|---|---|
| **New moon** | Coyote tricked Luna, and since then she sees every Nuwisha as a child of the new moon. |
| **Umbral Danser** | Followers of Ptah who have fasted and danced their way into the Umbra. They keep their Gifts from other Nuwisha. |

## Rites

| Rite | Kind | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rite of Dansing** | Renown | 2 | A follower of Ptah eats peyote, fasts and tells its deeds against the Wyrm. It costs three Gnosis and a psychoid leaf. Success makes it an Umbral Danser; failure bars it for a year. |
| **Sing Back the Dead** | Mystic | 5 | A song over a fresh body, and Coyote decides. If he says yes, the dead lives again and the singer loses a permanent Gnosis. If he says no, the singer may stop; singing on kills the singer and the dead stays dead. |
