# Every event

Every event the storyteller can send, what it means and what to do about it. Most can be switched off or made rarer one by one in the mod settings.

| Event | Kind | From day | Again after | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A gathering to host: Council of Autumn** | Event |  | 30 days | The Gurahl offer you the Council of Autumn to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: powwow** | Event |  | 30 days | The Gurahl offer you a powwow to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Regalia** | Event |  | 30 days | The Gurahl offer you a Regalia to host. At most once a year. |
| **Changing one** | Event |  |  | For a colony with no Fera: a stranger in the middle of a First Change stumbles to your land, feverish, with something on their trail. Take them in and they join as a Fera of their breed; the pursuers arrive a few hours later. This is the usual way a colony meets the Changing Breeds. |
| **Corax news** | Event |  | 15 days | A Corax drops in, talking fast, and leaves the colony with news it didn't know. |
| **Nuwisha visitor** | Event |  | 20 days | A lone Nuwisha wanders in, all grins, with news from the road and a prank or two in mind. A quarter as often as the other breeds' visits. |
| **Ratkin sabotage** | Threat |  | 20 days | Ratkin sneak in and sabotage the colony's machines and works. |
| **Sept demands Gaia's Breath** | Event |  | 120 days | A Garou sept learns your werebear knows Gaia's Breath and wants it taught to its Theurges. Teaching costs the Gurahl 5 Honor and breaks its oath to guard the secrets; refusing angers the sept. |
| **Swift Light courier** | Event |  | 30 days | A rare caravan of the Hermetic Order of Swift Light. It sells uPhones and news on paper, and buys your Corax's secrets while it's here. |
| **Wyrm raid with a Hatar** | Big threat |  | 30 days | A Wyrm raid with a Hatar, a werecat gone over to the Wyrm, among the raiders. |
