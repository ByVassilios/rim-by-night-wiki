# Every setting

Every setting in Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, with what it does.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fera homes in the world** | How many nests, dens, wallows and courts each breed has in a new world. Worlds already made keep theirs. |
| **War of Rage skirmishes** | How often Garou septs and Fera homes strike each other. At 0 they never do. |
| **Fera asking to join** | How often a breed that likes the colony sends one of its own to join. |
| **Fera hidden in towns** | How often an ordinary town hides an Ananasi or a breed's kin. Applies to towns settled from now on. |
| **A way in for colonies without Fera** | A stranger in the middle of their First Change may come to a colony that has no Fera yet, with whatever hunts them close behind. |
| **Gnosis only in the sun (Mokolé)** | Off, a Mokolé can seek Gnosis under a roof or by night like any shifter.  The book: a Mokolé takes Gnosis from the sun alone. |
| **Gold burns** | Off, a gold blade cuts a Corax or a Mokolé like any other.  The book: gold is the bane of the sun's children, as silver is the moon's. |
| **Abominations from Fera** | Off, a Fera the Embrace turns rises as a Garou Abomination does, with none of its breed's own fate.  The book: a Corax burns at sunrise, a Ratkin rots, a Kitsune goes up in flames, and so on. |
| **Hibernation (Gurahl)** | Off, winter doesn't make a Gurahl drowsy. It can still hibernate when told to.  The book: the werebears sleep through the winter. |
| **Fera missions** | How often Fera homes ask the colony for help. All the way left turns their missions off. |
| **Queen's orders** | How often an Ananasi's Queen sends orders. All the way left turns them off. |
| **Plague Lord's spirit quest** | How likely a plague-bearer Ratkin's spirit quest is to bring a plague with it. |
| **Birthing Pains** | How likely a Kitsune's Birthing Pains are to strike after a big Gift. |
| **Archid strength** | Scales an Ananasi's Archid form, the bigger spider shape, against the book's numbers. |
| **Codes and laws** | The Code of Ursa, the Laws of the Ananasi and Kitsune and Coyote's Rules cost Renown when broken. The books do this. |
| **A hundred Nuwisha** | No more than a hundred Nuwisha walk the world at once, and a new one comes only when another leaves. The book says so. |
| **Kitsune learn any Gift** | A Kitsune can learn a Gift from any list, at the other list's price. The book allows it. |
