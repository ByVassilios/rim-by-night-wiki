# Kitsune

The werefoxes: tricksters, sorcerers and holy warriors bound by the Laws of Heaven, and the youngest Changing
Breed. Kitsune don't regenerate, cause no Delirium and frenzy only as foxes, so they keep out of fights and win
with magic, illusion and the odd killing. Silver hurts them no more than any blade.

## How a Kitsune plays

- **Paths.** A new Kitsune has no Path. It chooses one at the Rite of the Crossroads, held by a Kitsune of rank 2
  or more. With no elder around it can choose by itself once it has the Renown. Choosing gives rank 1, the
  Path's Rage and a first Gift.
- **Long life.** Ageing halves with each rank past the first.
- **No Delirium.** Mortals who see a Kitsune in its war form know what they saw, and the Veil suffers.
- **Healing.** As a human, unless Enduring Warrior lends it regeneration for an hour.
- **Fighting.** It stays back in human form and casts. An Eji fights as a fox. It runs at 40% health.

## Breeds

| Breed | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Kojin** | Born human, and aware of being different long before the First Change. |
| **Roko** | Born a fox and often raised by wild foxes. |
| **Shinju** | Born of two Kitsune, with no deformity and no curse: a full Kitsune in every way. |

## Paths

| Aspect | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Kataribe** | The path of clay: poets, builders and loremasters. |
| **Gukutsushi** | The path of fog: dreamweavers, tricksters and healers. |
| **Doshi** | The path of lightning: sorcerers who use evil against evil, living alone in old temples. |
| **Eji** | The path of lava: holy warriors of swift, neat executions. |
| **No Path** | A young Kitsune who has not yet chosen a Path at the Rite of the Crossroads. |
