# Every mission and quest

Every mission a court, sept, cell or consilium can give, and every quest these mods offer.

| Mission | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **A threat to the protectorate** | Travel to where Wyrm-things gather near a Gurahl's protectorate and drive them off. |
| **Meeting the Great One** | Every werebear is called to one place to face a great threat. |
| **The Queen's order** | Queen Ananasa orders the colony's spiders to unweave a nest of the Wyrm. |
| **The Dragon's Dream** | Seven suns dream the same dream: a lost place of the Dragon Kings, and something waiting there. |
| **The Mandate of Heaven** | The Beast Courts name one who has lost the Mandate of Heaven. |
| **Sabotage** | A Ratkin nest wants a Pentex outpost wrecked. |
