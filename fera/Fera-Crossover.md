# Crossover

Fera works with every mod in the series. This page covers how the breeds get on with the others, and what the
Embrace does to each.

## Opinions

Each breed has its own feelings toward Garou, each Garou tribe, vampires, hunters, mages and mortals. They come
from the book and run through Core's kind opinions, so the opinion slider in Core's settings scales them too.
Anyone who feels something about the Garou feels the same about a Fera, unless they have a lean of their own.

- The Garou Nation loses a little goodwill each day while the colony keeps a Garou alongside a Ratkin, Mokolé or
  Ananasi colonist.
- The Corax, Ananasi, Gurahl and Gumagan Mokolé dislike mages who bind spirits, as Garou do.

## Hunters

Hunters who come for a colony of Fera arm for the Fera they find: gold for a Corax, often gold for a Mokolé,
plain steel for a Nuwisha or an Ananasi. With any Garou on the map they bring silver, as before.

## Kindred

A vampire can drink from a Fera, with one exception: Ananasi blood is rejected, and the vampire throws it up. An
Ananasi that drinks a vampire takes vitae, a blood point at a time, with no bond.

### The Embrace

| Breed | What happens |
|---|---|
| Corax | Rises as an Abomination, and burns to ash at the first sunrise, roof or not |
| Ratkin | Rises, then rots over fifteen days, losing pieces, and dies |
| Ananasi | Refuses the Embrace |
| Gurahl | Rises as Garou do |
| Mokolé | Rises in a frenzy that won't end, and burns at the first sunrise |
| Kitsune | Never rises. Its body burns in a burst of fire |
| Nuwisha | Never rises |

While a risen Fera lives, its breed's faction thinks worse of the colony. The Abominations setting in Fera's
settings turns all of this off; a Fera then rises as a Garou does.

## Imbued

Hunters see a Fera as a monster, as they see a Garou. Fera kills leave claw marks for a case, like Garou ones.

## Awakened

Mage Sight reads a Fera as half spirit, like a Garou. Death sight sees an Ananasi as alive.
