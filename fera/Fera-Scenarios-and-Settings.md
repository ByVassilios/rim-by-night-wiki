# Scenarios and settings

## Scenarios

Fera adds eight scenarios, one for each breed and one for a mixed pack. Each has its own player faction.

| Scenario | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Rat pack** | Three Ratkin and two rat-kin humans left the nest with a job: find somewhere quiet, dig in, and grow. The nest knows where you went. It expects to hear from you.<br><br>Note: three Ratkin of random aspects, two kin and two rats. The Ratkin nests know the colony from the start. |
| **The Watcher** | Raven sent you here to watch. Something in this corner of the world is worth knowing about, and two friends came along without asking too many questions.<br><br>Note: one Corax and two mortals. |
| **The Queen's first order** | The Queen hatched you and sent you out with two mortals who don't know what you are. She will tell you what she wants of you soon. Until then, build a web and wait.<br><br>Note: one Ananasi and two mortals. |
| **Protectorate** | You've chosen this land as your protectorate. Two human kin and a black bear came with you. Keep the land well and it will keep you.<br><br>Note: one Gurahl (Arcas) of the Forest Walkers, two kin and a black bear kin. Best in forest or tundra. |
| **New wallow** | Your stream sent you two to start a new wallow. The water here is warm and the sun is strong. Two kin came to help build it.<br><br>Note: two Mokolé of one stream with a Wallow of one, and two kin. Best somewhere wet. |
| **Sentai** | The Beast Court sent your sentai out with a Mandate. You're four of a kind, each on a different Path, and the court expects great things.<br><br>Note: four Kitsune, one of each Path. The Beast Courts know the colony from the start. |
| **Coyote's child** | You pranked this mortal once, and it went so well they stuck around. Now it's the two of you, a long way from anywhere, and you're already bored.<br><br>Note: one Nuwisha and one mortal. |
| **The Joined Circle** | One young Garou and three of the Changing Breeds, joined by the old rite. The sept wouldn't have it, so you left. Now it's just the four of you and the totem that agreed to take you.<br><br>Note: one Garou Cliath and three Fera of random breeds, already a pack. |
| **Rat-catchers' guild** | Nobody looks twice at the rat-catchers. You can go anywhere in a town at any hour with a sack nobody wants to open. The three of you have taken the work far from the old town now, and the rats came too.<br><br>Note: three Ratkin. Medieval arms and the old crafts; research is as normal. |
| **Battlefield crow** | You followed the armies for three seasons, watching and picking secrets off the dead. The war has moved on and the camp has scattered, but two camp followers who know what you are stayed with you.<br><br>Note: one Corax and two kin. Medieval arms and the old crafts; research is as normal. |

## The making menu

At the start, each Fera in the scenario opens a making menu, on the same frame as Garou's. The kind is fixed by
the scenario. You pick the group, breed, aspect and varna where the breed has them, and a Merit and a Flaw (more
with Core's **Merits at making** setting, each extra Merit with a Flaw of its own). The
beginning Gifts come by the breed's own rule.

## The Fera tab

A Fera's inspect tab has Garou's pages plus one for its kind, named after it: the Corax's secrets, the Mokolé's
Archid builder and so on.

## The Fera main tab

The Fera button on the bottom bar shows once the colony has a Fera:

- **Overview**: the colony's Fera, the breeds you've met and how they feel about you.
- **Homes**: every known home of every breed you've met.
- **A page per breed** you've met: what they are, their goodwill, your colonists of that breed and their homes.

## Alerts

- An Archid point is waiting to be spent.
- An Ananasi is low on blood.
- A drowsy Gurahl isn't in bed.

Garou's Gift-ready and Veil alerts count Fera too.

## Settings

Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, then Fera on the rail down the left, which every Rim by Night mod shares.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Fera homes in the world | How many homes each breed has in a new world |
| War of Rage skirmishes | How often septs and Fera homes strike each other. 0 turns it off |
| Fera asking to join | How often a breed that likes you sends one of its own |
| Fera hidden in towns | How often an ordinary town hides an Ananasi or a breed's kin |
| A way in for colonies without Fera | The stranger in mid-Change who comes to a colony with no Fera |
| Gnosis only in the sun | Off, a Mokolé can seek Gnosis anywhere |
| Gold burns | Off, gold cuts a Corax or a Mokolé like any blade |
| Abominations from Fera | Off, an Embraced Fera rises as a Garou does |
| Hibernation | Off, winter doesn't make a Gurahl drowsy |

The events page turns each Fera event on or off and sets how often it comes.

## In orbit

With Odyssey your colony can live in orbit. The World of Darkness stays on the ground: nobody from these mods
comes up uninvited unless "The World of Darkness reaches orbit" is on in Core's settings. Help you call still
comes, by drop pod, and leaves on Royalty's shuttle. Without Royalty it stays aboard until the ship lands. The
colony is whichever home holds most of your free colonists, so visitors go where your people are.

**Keep Fera in their breed form in vacuum.** Any form that can't wear clothes pulls the vacsuit off, and a frenzy
forces the shift. Outside the ship that kills.

No rat holes can be dug in orbit, and a Corax can't fly where there's no air. A kin animal brought aboard needs air
like any animal. Far from Gaia, caerns and stepping sideways work as they do for Garou.

## Taking Fera out of a save

Use "Remove Fera from this game" in the mod settings with the game loaded. Every Fera turns mortal, their kin
lose their mark, and the game is saved as a new save without Fera. Turn the mod off, restart and load that save.
