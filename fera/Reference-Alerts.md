# Every alert

Every alert this mod can show on the right of the screen.

| Alert | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Mokolé needs the sun** | A Mokolé gets Gnosis only from the sun, and this one is under half and hasn't been out under an open sky by day for three days. |
| **Queen's order due** | A Queen's order ends in less than a day. Failed orders cost Obedience, and enough of them bring a Myrmidon. |
| **Prank ready** | A prank the colony's Nuwisha set is due, and its target is here. It goes off at the first chance. |
| **Birthing Pains** | A fox child is due within two days. When labour ends, the Birthing Pains may take a parent, or someone close to them. The letter when the pregnancy showed has the odds. |
| **Archid points to spend** | These Mokolé have Archid points left. Spend them on the Mokolé's page in the Fera tab. |
| **Ananasi low on blood** | These Ananasi have under 3 blood. They need to feed on a person or an animal soon. |
| **Gurahl wants to hibernate** | Winter has made these Gurahl drowsy. Send them to bed or tell them to hibernate. |
