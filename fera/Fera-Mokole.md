# Mokolé

The weresaurians: Gaia's memory, last of the dragons, children of the sun. A Mokolé remembers what its line has
seen back to the age of the dinosaur kings. It has a human form, a crocodile or lizard form of its varna, and the
Archid, a dragon shaped by its own memory. Gold and silver both burn it.

## How a Mokolé plays

- **The sun.** Its auspice comes from the sun at the First Change: eclipse, night, cloud, noon, dawn or dusk. A
  seasonal stream takes it from the season instead. It gains Gnosis only by meditating in daylight under open
  sky; cloud halves it, the wallow adds half again.
- **Varna.** Each Mokolé is born to a varna that sets its Rage and its reptile form. Alligators and Gila
  monsters are slow on land, monitors quick.
- **The Archid.** Each permanent Gnosis point buys a characteristic for the war form, kept for good: armoured
  scales, wings, fire breath, poison sacs and more. Some can be taken up to three times. The Mokolé page on its
  tab has the builder.
- **Mnesis.** A trance of a few hours lends a skill for a day, at 10 plus the Background's dots.
- **The Innocents.** In the Penumbra, the stillborn may whisper, haunt its dreams or steal a memory that spoils
  the next Mnesis.
- **Stepping sideways** needs Walking Between Worlds, except for a Gumagan.
- **Fighting.** Archid, and away at a quarter health.

| Group | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Mokolé-mbembe** | The Stream of the Fighters, the largest: alligators, crocodiles and a few caimans. |
| **Gumagan** | The Stream of the Forerunners, with the deepest memories. Saltwater crocodiles and monitors. |
| **Makara** | The Stream of the Folk, diplomats to the other Fera. Gharials and muggers. |
| **Zhong Lung** | The Stream of the Philosophers, part of the Beast Courts with the Kitsune. |
