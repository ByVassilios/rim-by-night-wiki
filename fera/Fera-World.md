# The world

The Changing Breeds have been in hiding since the War of Rage. Each breed has its own faction. They're all there from the start of the game, hidden, with
homes on the world map. Like every faction in the series, each one rolls its own name.

## Homes

| Breed | Where they live |
|---|---|
| Ratkin | Nests, often under or near towns |
| Gurahl | Small homes of one werebear and a kin or two |
| Mokolé | Wallows by the water |
| Kitsune | The Beast Courts |
| Corax, Ananasi, Nuwisha | No homes of their own: they travel, or keep to themselves |

Homes grow, fall and get founded through Core's living world, and show in the Chronicle like everyone else's.
A few ordinary towns hide an Ananasi, and some hide Ratkin kin or other kin.

## First contact

A breed's faction stays hidden until the colony has one of that breed. Three to eight days later it comes out of
hiding: its goodwill resets to what it feels toward a colony with one of its own, and its nearest home writes to
you. Travelling breeds send word by a passing stranger.

Once a faction is known, its visitors, caravans and raids come like any other faction's.

## How they feel about you

Each breed starts with its own goodwill toward a colony, better if the colony has one of its kind, worse if the
colony keeps Garou. Most of them have not forgiven the War of Rage. The Ratkin dislike everyone, and the Kitsune
and Mokolé get on with each other.

## The way in

While the colony has no Fera, a stranger may turn up: someone in the middle of their First Change, running from
whatever is after them. They join at rank 0 or 1. Their pursuers arrive a few hours later: fomori, a Garou hunting
pack, or plain raiders.

## Wanderers and recruits

A breed's faction that likes you (goodwill 40 or more) may send one of its own to join, at most once a year for
each breed. A known home can also find you a Fera of a group, aspect and rank you ask for, paid in goodwill.

## The War of Rage

The old war still smoulders. Garou septs strike the breeds' homes, the Ratkin and Mokolé most often, the Kitsune
least. Ratkin nests strike back at the septs. When a fight the colony knows of happens, its
Garou and that breed's Fera feel old wounds: a worse mood for a few days and a worse opinion of each other.
