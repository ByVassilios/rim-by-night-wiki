# Ananasi

The werespiders: children of Queen Ananasa. An Ananasi has no Rage and feels little. It runs on blood, bites
with venom in every form, spins webs, and has a swarm form that crawls under doors and survives being killed.
Silver doesn't hurt it. Other shifters think it evil, and the Wyrm-serving Hatar give them reason to.

## How an Ananasi plays

- **Blood.** A pool of 10 pays for Gifts, shifts and healing. The "Spend blood" button quickens it, mends its
  worst wound (five points for an aggravated one) or purges a disease.
- **Feeding.** Blood comes back only by drinking. Right-click someone who can't resist: downed, asleep, a
  prisoner or a colony animal. It never drains a victim past a dangerous blood loss.
- **Venom.** Lilian and Pithus bites are aggravated and can leave a venom that kills if no one tends it.
- **Webs.** Pithus spins web walls and throws snares that hold a pawn until it tears free.
- **The swarm.** As a Crawlerling it takes little from bullets and arrows, a lot from fire and blasts, passes
  any door, and is the only form that can step sideways. Killed by anything but fire or a blast, it scatters
  and reforms days later at its Sylie, weak for a while and maybe with some skills lost.
- **The Sylie.** The Rite of Spinning makes one. In its room the Ananasi regains Gnosis.
- **Cold.** It breaks less often than a mortal and heals at a human's pace.
- **Fighting.** Lilian, and away at 30% health.

## Rites

| Rite | Kind | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rite of Spinning** | Mystic | 1 | The Ananasi spins its Sylie where the rite is held: a great cobweb in a quiet corner, in both worlds at once. In its room it regains Gnosis and steps sideways more easily. Each Ananasi has one; spinning another tears down the old. |
| **The Master's Needs** | Mystic | 4 | An hour spinning a cocoon around a mortal in the Ananasi's venom, downed, asleep or a prisoner. Each day inside it the mortal fights the venom's hold; each day it loses, it owes the spider a service: its senses, its body for a few seconds, or nothing more when released. Blood can't be refilled past the services owed. |
