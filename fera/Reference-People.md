# People, factions and creatures

The factions, the kinds of people and the creatures this mod adds.

## Factions

| Faction | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Battlefield crow** | A Corax who follows the armies, and the camp followers who know the secret.<br><br>The wereravens are Gaia's eyes. Where armies march the Corax go too, to watch, to carry the news and to drink the last sights from the eyes of the dead. This one has stopped at the end of a long campaign with two of their kin and means to stay a while. |
| **Couriers** | Messengers who turn up wherever there is news and leave before anyone asks how they heard it. |
| **Courtiers** | Polite, clever households bound by an old court's rules, with guests who come and go at odd hours. |
| **Coyote's own** | A Nuwisha and the mortal it pranked once.<br><br>The Nuwisha are the werecoyotes, Coyote's children and the tricksters of Gaia. They teach with jokes, and the jokes hurt. They never fight the Wyrm head on; they make it look foolish. |
| **Drifters** | Wanderers with a joke for every town and a reason to leave each one in a hurry. |
| **Hermits** | Big, slow-spoken folk who live alone deep in the woods and the cold country, and help travellers who come to them in need. |
| **Joined Circle** | A Garou and three Fera bound as one pack by the Rite of the Joined Circle.<br><br>The War of Rage set the Garou against the other Changing Breeds, and most of them never forgave it. The Rite of the Joined Circle is the old rite of truce: a pack of any shifters, bound under one totem. Few are ever made. |
| **Protectorate** | A Gurahl, a bear kin and the humans it watches over.<br><br>The Gurahl are the werebears, Gaia's healers. They nearly died out in the War of Rage, and the few left keep to the deep woods, guarding a protectorate and sleeping through the winter. Their Rage is slow to come and terrible when it does. |
| **Queen's servants** | An Ananasi and the mortals it has gathered.<br><br>The Ananasi are the werespiders, the children of Queen Ananasa. They drink blood instead of eating, scatter into a swarm of spiders when hurt, and serve the Queen above Gaia herself. They have no Rage, only hunger and patience. |
| **Rat pack** | A pack of Ratkin and the humans who keep their secrets, out on their own.<br><br>The Ratkin are the wererats, the Rat King's children, sent by Gaia to keep humanity's numbers in check. They live in the gaps: sewers, tunnels, the back rooms nobody rents. Every one of them is born of the Birthing Plague, and every one of them knows the Litany of Survival: hide, breed, and strike where it hurts. |
| **Rat-catchers' guild** | Ratkin who hide in plain sight as the town's vermin men.<br><br>The wererats have always lived where people crowd together and throw things away. A rat-catcher goes everywhere, hears everything and is never asked what's in the sack. These ones took the trade for its cover and for the company of rats, and they pass on what they learn to the nest. |
| **River folk** | Old families of the swamps and the jungle rivers, who farm gators, fish and remember a great deal. |
| **Scavengers** | Ragged crews who live under the ruins and in the old tunnels. They trade junk and explosives and trust no one. |
| **Sentai** | Four Kitsune, one of each Path, bound as a sentai.<br><br>The Kitsune are the werefoxes, the youngest of the Changing Breeds. They follow Paths instead of auspices, wield kami and sorcery, and owe their loyalty to the Beast Courts. A sentai is their pack: four foxes, four Paths, one purpose. |
| **Wallow** | Two Mokolé of one stream and their kin, at a new wallow.<br><br>The Mokolé are the weredragons, Gaia's memory. They remember everything their ancestors saw, back to the age of dinosaurs, and they take their Gnosis from the sun. Each stream is a family of one kind of reptile, and each gathers at a wallow by the water. |
| **Watchers** | A Corax and the mortals who trust it.<br><br>The Corax are the wereravens, Raven's children and Gaia's spies. They fly far, drink the eyes of the dead to see what they saw, and trade secrets the way others trade silver. They are few, and they know it. |
| **Weavers** | Quiet tradesfolk spread through many towns. They keep to themselves and pay their debts exactly. |
