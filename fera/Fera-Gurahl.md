# Gurahl

The werebears: Gaia's healers, nearly wiped out in the War of Rage and only lately awake again. A Gurahl is huge,
patient and slow to anger. When it does get angry it pours Rage into its own body. It sleeps heavy in winter, can
hibernate for a season, and its oldest Gift brings a dead shapeshifter back.

## How a Gurahl plays

- **Rage on the body.** Spend Rage for a moment of Strength or Stamina, or for an hour of bear's vigour that cuts
  the damage it takes.
- **Adamant Will.** Hard to bend its mind, while it keeps its duty.
- **Auspices in order.** Every Gurahl starts as Arcas and lives the five auspices in turn. Each step opens with a
  milestone: a kill, a lover or a peaceful season, then ranks 3 and 4. The Gifts of every auspice it has lived
  stay open.
- **Winter.** Drowsy on a winter map: it tires sooner and fights a little slower.
- **Hibernation.** Fifteen days of sleep, half inside its Glade. Sleeping it through heals everything,
  aggravated wounds too. A wound wakes it early with nothing healed.
- **Duty.** A season away from home fails its duty. It loses Willpower each day, then sinks into Bhernocht, the
  Gurahl's Harano. Coming home ends it.
- **The Umbral Glade.** A Background: a claimed stretch of land. A Gurahl steps sideways only from inside it, and
  regains Gnosis sleeping there.
- **Gaia's Breath.** Brings back a dead Garou or Fera, never kin, for a permanent Gnosis and Willpower. It gets
  harder each hour after the death, and a botch brings the body back as a fomor.
- **Fighting.** Crinos, and it stays until 20% health.

## Tribes

| Group | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Forest Walkers** | Black bears and spirit bears: the most numerous tribe, fond of powwows and respectful of elders. |
| **Ice Stalkers** | Polar bears who live in family groups, playful artists of bone and ice. |
| **Mountain Guardians** | Grizzlies: solitary, competitive, given to ritual combat. |
| **River Keepers** | Bears of every kind, givers of gifts and hosts of gatherings. |

## Auspices

| Aspect | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Arcas** | The first auspice: the First Change, the Gallivant and a head full of questions. |
| **Uzmati** | The warrior, travelling alone after the first real battle. |
| **Kojubat** | The rememberer, learning lore and starting a lifework. |
| **Kieh** | The mystic, given to reflection, rites and mentoring. |
| **Rishi** | The judge, who keeps the peace. |

## Rites

| Rite | Kind | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dreams of Winter** | Mystic | 1 | The werebear sleeps with the rite's song in its ears. When it wakes it knows what happened in the world while it slept. |
| **Rite of Rending the Gauntlet** | Mystic | 2 | In its war form the werebear claws the Gauntlet open. For an hour it may step into the spirit world from where it stands. Anyone who sees it is struck by the Delirium. |
| **Rite of Replenishment** | Mystic | 1 | Held at a sacred place, a caern, a glade or a thin spot, once a season: each success gives the werebear a point of Gnosis. |
| **River's Portent** | Mystic | 1 | The werebear catches a fish and reads it. It learns what threat comes next, as a seer's warning does. |
| **Rite of True Mating** | Accord | 2 | The werebear goes looking for a mate of its kin: one on the map, or else the nearest Gurahl home will know of one. A cub can't hold it. |
| **Rite of the Death Bear** | Death | 5 | Over a body Gaia's Breath could not wake, the werebear calls Mangi and fights it for the dead one's life. Win in a day and the dead one rises. Lose, and sometimes Mangi takes the bear instead. |
