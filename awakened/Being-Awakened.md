# Being Awakened

## The Awakening

Any adult humanlike can Awaken, modded races included, but never a vampire, ghoul, werewolf or hunter. A blood-feeder like a sanguophage can only Awaken if "Blood-feeders can become hunters and mages" is on in Core's Rules settings. The chance comes from:

- **Omens:** an eclipse, a psychic drone, toxic fallout, a flashstorm, a herd migration, an aurora, and several Anomaly events. Every colonist on the map rolls, and only the first one Awakens.
- **Near death:** getting up after nearly bleeding out.
- **Seeing magic:** an obvious spell cast in sight.
- **Extremes:** a lost lover or bonded animal, a masterwork, a big research project, a catharsis.
- **Sleeping at a Hallow.**
- **A mentor:** a mage teaches a Sleeper an hour a day for 5 to 15 days, then the Sleeper rolls.

Psychically sensitive colonists Awaken twice as often. The settings set the odds and a cap of one a season once the colony has three mages.

The colonist falls into a trance of 6 to 12 hours and walks to a Watchtower in her dreams. When she wakes, a letter names her Path. About one Awakening in ten goes wrong and makes a **Banisher**, a mage who hates magic, her own included. A true mage's daily talk can turn a colonist Banisher, slowly.

## The five Paths

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Acanthus** | Enchanters. Their souls reached Arcadia, the realm of fae and fortune, and signed the Watchtower of the Lunargent Thorn. They see the threads of luck and time, and are poorest at the plain push of Forces. |
| **Mastigos** | Warlocks. Their souls reached Pandemonium, the realm of demons and desire, and signed the Watchtower of the Iron Gauntlet. They master space and the mind, and struggle with the stubborn stuff of Matter. |
| **Moros** | Necromancers. Their souls reached Stygia, the realm of the dead and of treasure, and signed the Watchtower of the Lead Coin. They command matter and death, and are blind to the world of Spirit. |
| **Obrimos** | Theurgists. Their souls reached the Aether, the realm of angels and fire, and signed the Watchtower of the Golden Key. They wield raw Forces and the Prime stuff of magic itself, and shy from Death. |
| **Thyrsus** | Shamans. Their souls reached the Primal Wild, the realm of beasts and spirits, and signed the Watchtower of the Stone Book. They rule Life and Spirit, and find the Mind of others hard to reach. |

## Gnosis

Gnosis runs from 1 to 10. It sets the Mana cap, how much Mana she can spend at once, how many spells she can keep up, her Paradox dice, her ritual time and her Arcanum limits. It also helps her resist other kinds' mind powers. Raise it on the tab for 5 Experience.

## Mana

Mana pays for spells outside her Ruling Arcana, Mage Armor, Pattern Restoration and Paradox removal. The Mana gizmo offers:

- **Oblation** at a waking Hallow: an hour, a Mana a success.
- **Tass:** draw Mana from a stack.
- **Scouring:** lose a Physical dot for a day, or take a lethal wound, for 3 Mana.
- **Pattern Restoration:** heal a wound for 3 Mana, 2 with Life 2.

## Wisdom

Wisdom starts at 7. An Act of Hubris at or below her tier rolls Wisdom: obvious magic before outsiders, overriding a will, killing a helpless foe, Blood Sacrifice. A failure costs a dot and gives Megalomaniacal or Rampant. At 0 she goes Mad and leaves. A spell that cost Wisdom can be inured on the tab: it stops counting as Hubris but rolls two more Paradox dice.

## Growing

Beats come from work, raids, quests and Aspirations. Arcane Beats come from Obsessions, Paradox and new sights. Five of either make an Experience. Spend them on the tab for Arcana, Gnosis, rotes and Merits.

Beside the three short Aspirations, a colony mage works toward one long-term Aspiration at a time. A letter offers three: raise an Arcanum, learn a rote, tend a Hallow, scribe a grimoire, a season with no Paradox, grow wiser, uncover a Mystery, and one for her Path or Order (win a Duel Arcane for the Adamantine Arrow, rise in Status for the Silver Ladder, and so on). The Growth page and the Quests tab show how far along it is. Only what happens after it is taken counts, and it pays 2 to 4 Arcane Experience. There's no deadline. Drop one and another letter comes in five days.

An Obsession pays nothing on the way: resolving it is two Arcane Beats and a Mana, and the same one can't come back for a season. Steps count only when they mean something:

- **Master an Arcanum:** a cast at a foe, with enemies on the map, or on an ally who is down, under half health or bleeding out.
- **Study a Hallow:** a different Hallow each time. Oblations count once a day.
- **Examine an Artifact:** one she has never studied or looked at.
- **Speak with the dead:** a different dead person each time, killed by violence or by something strange.
- **Becoming wiser:** fifteen days with no Act of Hubris. One starts the count again.

Newer Obsessions: cross into Twilight, bargain with a spirit (bind it or pay its chiminage), face the Abyss (be there when an Acamoth is driven out or a Gulmoth destroyed), unmask a Seer (solve a Seer case), win a Duel Arcane, scrutinize a monster with Focused Sight, witness an Awakening (or mentor one), make magic last (an Imbued Item, or a spell let go for good), and a brush with death against a supernatural foe. Each is offered only when it can happen.

A Hubris roll and a dramatic failure each give an Arcane Beat once a day at most.
