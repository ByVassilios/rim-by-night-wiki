# The Consilium

## First contact

The Pentacle mages of the region keep to themselves. A few days after your colony's first true mage, the nearest Consilium's Herald writes, and from then on its towns show on the world map. Each Consilium has 7 to 11 named mages, led by a Hierarch.

## Orders

An apostate whose Consilium knows her can ask to join an Order from the Order page. A trial errand follows, and members get a duty about every 45 days. Each Order brings its rote Skills, its own rotes and High Speech.

| Order | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Adamantine Arrow** | Warrior-mages who hold that magic is conflict. They guard the Consilium and answer its calls to fight. |
| **Free Council** | The youngest Order, who look for magic in the made world: machines, cities, the work of Sleepers' hands. |
| **Guardians of the Veil** | The Order of secrets. They keep magic hidden from Sleepers and hunt down the mages who show it. |
| **Mysterium** | Scholars and keepers of lore. They hunt for Artifacts, grimoires and places of power, and keep them safe. |
| **Silver Ladder** | Would-be rulers of the Awakened, who mean to lead mages and, one day, the world. |
| **Seers of the Throne** | Mages who serve the Exarchs, the tyrants who rule the Supernal from the far side of the Abyss. They hold the world's Sleepers in their sleep and pay well for the work. |

## Status

Deeds add standing, 10 points to a dot of Status. Crimes and refused summons take it away. Titles follow Status, from Herald at 2 up to Councilor. When a Hierarch's seat comes free, the Consilium elects a new one.

## Sleepwalkers

A mage with the Retainer Background (see [the sworn Consilium](The-Sworn-Consilium.md#backgrounds)), or with Status 2 or more in her Order or the Consilium, has a Sleepwalker working for her
from a town near the colony: one for each dot of whichever is higher. They show on the **Contacts** page of the Awakened
tab. They need no looking after, and go when the dots do. Once a week each can be asked for one favour:

- **Goodwill:** they put in a good word, and the town's faction thinks better of the colony.
- **Seer word:** if the Seers keep a Pylon in a town near theirs, its place comes to light, with the letter to raid it,
  tell the Consilium or sell the silence.

## Services

Standing buys things from the Consilium's mages: tass, rotes, grimoires, teaching, healing and more. "Ask the Consilium..." on the Order page opens a window with a tab for each price; a greyed Ask button says what's missing. The giver depends on the service. At Status 1 or more a mage can go into debt. The Order page shows both Statuses as dots, with the standing toward the next one, her office, the Hierarch and any debt.

## The Lex Magica

A Consilium hears crimes by colony mages it knows: killing a mage, Blood Sacrifice of a person, obvious magic before Sleepers, and more. The summons offers four answers:

- **Attend the hearing.** The Provost comes for about a day. Talk your way through it in the talk window. Friendly cabals speak for you, and feuding ones against you.
- **Pay** a fine in standing.
- **Refuse,** and lose Status.
- **Duel** the Consilium's champion in a Duel Arcane.

Sentences run from cleared, through penance and lost Status, up to exile and death. An exiled mage has no Status and no Consilium.

A summons, a trial errand, a duty and a Seer case each show in the Quests tab with what's left to do and the time left.

## Missions

The nearest Consilium you know sends requests through the storyteller, like any vanilla quest:

- **Host an envoy.** One or two of its mages stay for a day and a half. Seers or Banishers come for them.
- **Lend a mage** for four days.
- **Take back a Hallow** the Seers' thralls are holding. Pays tass and silver.
- **Recover an Artifact** Banishers stole from the Mysterium. Pays the Artifact, one not yet found in your world.
- **Break a Banisher camp** near the Consilium.

Done, every colony mage that Consilium knows gains 3 standing, as for a duty; let lapse, they lose half that.

## Requests

Besides missions, the Consilium and your mages' Orders write with smaller requests. Each answers with Accept or Refuse; a refusal is noticed. Every one has its own switch and frequency in the settings.

- **Mysterium readers.** Once your grimoires are worth it (a rote's dots each, an Artifact 5), a Mysterium mage asks to read in your best library for 2 to 6 nights. Refused, your Mysterium mages lose Order standing and the others Consilium standing. A big Library also draws Seer raids.
- **Teaching.** A guest asks to learn a rote, an Arcanum dot or an Attainment from one of your mages, one lesson a night (right-click the guest).
- **Rescues.** A mage chased by Seers (the Seers follow), a mage caught in a Paradox anomaly at the map's edge (carry it to a bed), an apostate under sentence (hiding one is a crime the Consilium may find out). A rescued mage owes a service, paid as standing. A mage freed from a Paradox anomaly also leaves the Consilia owing the colony help: the **Fighters on call** row on the Overview page counts it, and **Call in** brings a few of their mages to fight.
- **Favours.** Watch a mage in a long trance, foster an apprentice for a season, send tass or a good-quality item to imbue, host two quarrelling cabals for a night, have a doctor restore a maimed mage, lend a skilled Sleepwalker for a season.
- **Order requests.** At Order Status 2, 3 and 4 each Order asks its own: tass, a catalogue and a relic hunt for the Mysterium; sparring, a guard post and a Banisher hunt for the Arrow; a witness questioned, a watcher housed and a Hallow held for the Guardians; backing, a case argued and an envoy for the Ladder; parts, a technique and a mage lent for the Free Council. They pay Order standing, more at higher Status.

A guest hurt or killed in your care costs every colony mage standing.

## Hosting gatherings

News of the Consilia reaching the colony (Sleepers Awakening, Paradoxes, duels arcane, Seer attacks, Hallows found and gone dry) is on the Chronicle page under Rim by Night, at the top of the list down the window's left side, and the colony's own mages (Awakenings, duels arcane and Paradoxes lived through) are on its Our story page. Both hold every mod's lines; click Awakened on a page's left for only the mages' lines. Click a line to find the Consilium or the mage.

The Overview page has an "Ask to host" link for the highest gathering the colony can host now (greyed with the reason when none is), and the Consilium or an Order writes when one opens. Each is hosted once a year at most. The preparation window lists what's needed; the Seers may strike on one night, and the host's skill rolls decide each night.

| Host's Status | Gathering | Nights | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consilium 2 | Cabal summit: two cabals settle a Hallow quarrel | 1 | |
| Consilium 3 | Lex Magica hearing, the host on the bench | 1 | the host's Sanctum |
| Consilium 4 | The assembly: reports, a Duel Arcane, a vote | 3 | a Hallow, free beds |
| Consilium 5 | Convocation: a war on the nearest Seer Pylon | 3 | a Hallow, free beds |
| Order 2 | Mysterium symposium, Arrow war council and duels, Guardians' secrecy review, Ladder audience, Free Council vote | 1 or 2 | a library for the symposium |

Every gathering needs meals for each guest and night. A good showing pays the host Consilium standing (Order standing at an Order's event), more for a high score. If the convocation backs the war, the Pylon is found and a raid site appears beside its town. Titles still follow Status as before.

## Cabals

Two colony mages can form a cabal from the Order page, and you name it when the first one joins. Cabalmates share their Sanctums, think better of each other, and can take the Cabal Theme Merit. The Consilium's own cabals visit, ask for help or for a share of your Hallow, send a mage who wants to join, and take sides at hearings by how much they like you.

The Consilium's cabals are also on the **Cabals** page of its window, as cards with temperament, power out of 20 and
what they think of you. **Ask them for work** gives one of their missions once every five days: an Artifact back from
Banishers (scholarly, reclusive), a rival's Hallow taken from the Seers (ambitious, militant, reckless), or a mage to
lend for quieting a careless one (ambitious, militant). Done, the cabal gains a point of power and 2 regard.

When a cabal comes within two power of the Hierarch (who stands at 10 above the cabals) and stays there for 30 days, it
calls a vote on the seat. The Consilium sits 20 to 40 days later; each side's chance is its power, a deciding mission
done for it counting two. **Back** a side from the page: 6 standing if it wins, 6 lost if it doesn't. A cabal that wins
makes its leader Hierarch.

## The Duel Arcane

Each side has Doors equal to their Willpower. Attacks open Doors and Defenses close them. The winner leaves Triumphant, and the loser loses Willpower for each Door opened.

## The tavern

A tavern counter (Furniture tab) works for a mage colony too. Its **Guests** button lets in the named
mages of the nearest Consilium you know, by Path, besides townsfolk.

A counter standing on a Hallow is a **cafe**: each party of mages leaves a point of Mana in the
Hallow, up to what it holds. Mage Sight shows a mage what any other guest is.
