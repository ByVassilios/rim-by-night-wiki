# Everything else

Every other kind of thing this mod defines that has a name and a description: Merits, Backgrounds, gatherings, places, personalities and the rest.

## Ambitions

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A quiet season** | Go a whole season without calling up a Paradox. |
| **Climb the Watchtower** | Buy a dot in one of your Path's two Ruling Arcana. |
| **Grow wiser** | Raise your Wisdom by a dot. |
| **Keep the Veil** | Go a whole season without calling up a Paradox. |
| **Learn a rote** | Learn a rote from a grimoire, a teacher or the Consilium. |
| **Raise an Arcanum** | Buy a dot in any Arcanum. |
| **Rise in Status** | Gain a dot of Status in your Order or the Consilium. |
| **Something new** | Learn a Praxis: a spell of your own making. |
| **Tend a Hallow** | Draw Mana from a Hallow with two Oblations. |
| **Two Mysteries** | Meet two Mysteries you haven't met before. |
| **Uncover a Mystery** | Meet a Mystery you haven't met before. |
| **Win a Duel Arcane** | Beat another mage in the Duel Arcane. |
| **Write it down** | Scribe one of your rotes into a grimoire. |

## Backgrounds

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Retainer** | Sleepwalker servants: mortals who can watch magic without forgetting it. Each dot keeps one in a town near the colony, who does the mage a favour once a week as a pulled string does. A Sleepwalker never counts as a witness to vulgar magic. |

## Biomes

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Pocket dimension** | A small world a mage made, with nothing in it they didn't bring. |

## Gatherings

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Adamantine Arrow war council** | Arrow mages plan their season at the colony, then test each other in duels of skill in a ring. |
| **Cabal summit** | Two cabals of the Consilium bring their quarrel over a Hallow to the colony, to settle it on neutral ground. |
| **Convocation** | Mages of several Consiliums meet at the colony, agree on a common war on a Seer Pylon, and set out from it. |
| **Free Council vote** | The Free Council votes at the colony on a new rule for the Consilium, and the colony's vote is counted. |
| **Guardians of the Veil review** | Guardians walk every room of the colony and rate how well it keeps the Mysteries from Sleepers. |
| **Lex Magica hearing** | The Consilium holds a hearing under the Lex Magica at the colony, with the host on the bench in its own Sanctum. |
| **Mysterium symposium** | Mysterium mages bring their finds to the colony's Library to compare them; the best find earns Status. |
| **Silver Ladder audience** | An elder of the Silver Ladder holds an audience at the colony, where its mage pleads a cause. |
| **The Consilium's assembly** | The Consilium meets at the colony: the officers report, a Duel Arcane is settled at the Hallow, and a vote is taken. |

## Group temperaments

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ambitious** | They want friends in the right places. They court the colony's mages, ask for rivals' Hallows, and remember who said no. |
| **Militant** | They hunt the Seers and the Abyss's servants, and they silence mages who are careless in front of Sleepers. |
| **Reckless** | They want Mana, and they want it now. A Hallow they're refused is a Hallow they'll send you to take. |
| **Reclusive** | They keep to themselves and are seldom heard from. Now and then they want an Artifact back. |
| **Scholarly** | They want books, quiet and good company to argue with. They ask you to find lost Artifacts. |

## Legacies

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Bearers of the Eternal Voice** | Mastigos persuaders who study the words that move crowds, and the ones that move them against their will. |
| **The Eleventh Question** | Moros investigators who believe every death, theft and lie leaves a trace in time for someone patient enough to read it. |
| **The Orphans of Proteus** | Thyrsus shapeshifters who hold that no body, human or beast, is the one they were born for. |
| **The Perfected Adepts** | Obrimos monks who train the body until it is fit to hold the Supernal. |
| **The Walkers in Mists** | Acanthus pathfinders who know every road through the wild and a few that aren't on any map. |

## Merits and Flaws

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Adamant Hand** | Adamantine Arrow martial arts: a melee blow struck just before a spell counts as an Order tool. |
| **Between the Ticks** | She acts at the perfect moment: once an hour, a die more on a spell. |
| **Cabal Theme** | Her cabal has a shared look, motto or ritual that she wears as part of her magical persona. While she's in the colony's cabal it counts as a persona Yantra in her spells. |
| **Destiny** | Her thread stands out in the skein of Fate. A point a day a dot, spent by itself: an even chance to turn aside a mental break, a bad wound when she is already hurt, or a failed surgery she performs. |
| **Familiar** | A spirit, ghost or Goetia is bound to her for good. It follows her, and she can draw on its Essence as Mana. Two dots for a Rank 1 being, four for Rank 2. |
| **Fast Spells** | Her aimed spells move like bullets: no one can dodge them. |
| **High Speech** | She knows the High Speech of the Supernal, and can chant it as a Yantra in her spells without belonging to an Order. |
| **Lex Magica** | The laws of the Pentacle as symbols she wields: half her Status, rounded up, is a Yantra for spells that find out or command. |
| **Mana Sensitivity** | She feels Mana before she sees it: tass near her shows itself, once a day. |
| **Occultation** | She is hard to read: a die off Focused Mage Sight on her for each dot. |
| **Potent Nimbus** | Her Nimbus hits witnesses as if she had two more Gnosis a dot, and she adds her dots to flaring it on purpose. |
| **Retainer** | Sleepwalkers who work for her: mortals who can stand to watch magic without forgetting it. Each lives in a town near the colony and does her a favour once a week. |
| **Shadow Name** | A magical persona with its own name and symbols. It is a Yantra worth a die a dot in every spell. |
| **Techné** | Free Council practice: any machine-age thing she carries is an Order tool for her Order's spells. |

## Orders

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Adamantine Arrow** | Warrior-mages who hold that magic is conflict. They guard the Consilium and answer its calls to fight. |
| **Free Council** | The youngest Order, who look for magic in the made world: machines, cities, the work of Sleepers' hands. |
| **Guardians of the Veil** | The Order of secrets. They keep magic hidden from Sleepers and hunt down the mages who show it. |
| **Mysterium** | Scholars and keepers of lore. They hunt for Artifacts, grimoires and places of power, and keep them safe. |
| **Seers of the Throne** | Mages who serve the Exarchs, the tyrants who rule the Supernal from the far side of the Abyss. They hold the world's Sleepers in their sleep and pay well for the work. |
| **Silver Ladder** | Would-be rulers of the Awakened, who mean to lead mages and, one day, the world. |

## Paths

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Acanthus** | Enchanters. Their souls reached Arcadia, the realm of fae and fortune, and signed the Watchtower of the Lunargent Thorn. They see the threads of luck and time, and are poorest at the plain push of Forces. |
| **Mastigos** | Warlocks. Their souls reached Pandemonium, the realm of demons and desire, and signed the Watchtower of the Iron Gauntlet. They master space and the mind, and struggle with the stubborn stuff of Matter. |
| **Moros** | Necromancers. Their souls reached Stygia, the realm of the dead and of treasure, and signed the Watchtower of the Lead Coin. They command matter and death, and are blind to the world of Spirit. |
| **Obrimos** | Theurgists. Their souls reached the Aether, the realm of angels and fire, and signed the Watchtower of the Golden Key. They wield raw Forces and the Prime stuff of magic itself, and shy from Death. |
| **Thyrsus** | Shamans. Their souls reached the Primal Wild, the realm of beasts and spirits, and signed the Watchtower of the Stone Book. They rule Life and Spirit, and find the Mind of others hard to reach. |

## Personalities

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Careless** | Doesn't mind who sees magic done. Its work is riskier, and it pays a third more for it. |
| **Mystery-hungry** | Wants to know everything. Asks for lore, and pays in what it knows: Arcane Experience for the mages who helped. |

## Places on the world map

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Abyssal breach** | A shrine where something tore through from the Abyss. The air there is wrong, and what came through hasn't left. |
| **Banisher camp** | A camp of Banishers, mages who hate what they are and kill the others for it. |
| **Held Hallow** | A Hallow the Seers of the Throne found first. Their thralls guard it while the Seers drink its Mana. |
| **Seer Pylon** | A house near the town where the Pylon's Seers meet, guarded by their thralls. |

|---|---|

## Scenarios

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Apostates** | Three people saw the Watchtowers in the same season and woke up afterwards with their names written on stone they had never touched. Nobody came to explain it. There is no Consilium out here that knows them, no mentor and no Order. They have each other and the little they can do. Note: three new mages at Gnosis 1, with no Hallow and no friends among the Awakened. |
| **Consilium exiles** | Two mages broke the Lex Magica and were cast out. They took what they could carry from their old Sanctum and went far enough that nobody would follow them. The Consilium remembers them, and its next Herald won't be friendly. Note: two exiled mages with no Status and a Sanctum focus. The Pentacle starts out cold toward you. |
| **Cunning folk** | People came to you for a cure, a charm or a lost cow found, and you usually helped. One night you saw what stands behind the world, and since then your charms work a little too well. The priest started asking questions, so you and your family moved on to a place with a Hallow and no neighbours.<br><br>Note: one mage and two mortal family members, with a Hallow of rating 1. Medieval arms and the old crafts; research is as normal. |
| **Hallow founders** | Two mages followed the pull of Mana for weeks and found it here: a Hallow, free and unclaimed, welling up out of the ground. They mean to build around it. The Seers of the Throne mean to take it, and they know where it is. Note: two mages and a Hallow of rating 2. The Seers come before the season is out. |
| **Hermetic scholars** | You copied grimoires by candlelight in your order's library for years. Then you and a fellow scholar took one tome you weren't meant to take and left by night, with an apprentice who wouldn't stay behind. Now you need a library of your own.<br><br>Note: two mages and one apprentice, with a tome and a Sanctum focus. Medieval arms and the old crafts; research is as normal. |
| **The lone Awakened** | One of you went to sleep ordinary and woke up able to see what the world is made of. The others still can't, and every spell cast in front of them pulls at the Abyss. Somewhere a Consilium has already noticed, and a letter is on its way. Note: one mage among sleepers. The Consilium writes within a few days. |

## Seats and offices

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Councilor** | Sits on the Hierarch's council and votes on what the Consilium does. |
| **Herald** | Carries the Consilium's word and calls mages to its hearings. |
| **Hierarch** | Rules the Consilium and leads its council. Won only by the Consilium's election or a cabal's plot, never handed out. |
| **Interfector** | Deals with the Consilium's enemies: Banishers, Seers and mages who break the Lex Magica. |
| **Provost** | Keeps the Consilium's goods, its Tass and its debts. |
| **Sentinel** | Guards the Consilium's Hallows and its meetings. |

## Tomes

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Atlas of Unfolded Rooms** | An atlas of houses whose rooms don't fit inside them. Some of the maps are folded in ways paper can't be folded. |
| **The Banefather's Liturgy** | A liturgy for a church that worshipped the spirits of what people hate. The responses are written for a congregation that never sings back. |
| **The Book of the Seventh Storm** | The diary of an Adamantine Arrow who counted the storms she called and wrote the seventh down as a set of rotes. The pages smell of ozone. |
| **The Clockmaker's Heresy** | A clockmaker's notebook full of escapements that should not work. He wrote that time is a spring, and that a spring can be wound by hand. He died old at forty. |
| **The Gambler's Concordance** | A gambler's ledger of every bet he won and lost, and why. By the end it stops being about cards. |
| **The Lantern Scrolls** | Twelve scrolls in a lead tube, written by a Mysterium lamplighter who swore that Mana could be thrown like light. Half the scrolls are scorched at the edges. |
| **The Leaden Codex** | A book with lead covers too heavy to hold open on one hand. Its author believed every metal remembers being ore and can be made to hurt like it. |
| **The Mirror Psalter** | A book of psalms with a small mirror set into the cover. Each psalm is a way to make a mind sing someone else's words. |
| **The Ossuary of Saint Absalom** | Bound in vellum the colour of old teeth. The monks who kept it lived in a charnel house and wrote down what the bones taught them. Its last owner is said to still be reading it. |
| **The Red Herbal** | A healer's herbal with every plant drawn in red ink. Read in order it teaches mending; read the other way it teaches the opposite. |
