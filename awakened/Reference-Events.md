# Every event

Every event the storyteller can send, what it means and what to do about it. Most can be switched off or made rarer one by one in the mod settings.

| Event | Kind | From day | Again after | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A gathering to host: Arrow war council** | Event |  | 30 days | The Adamantine Arrow offers you a war council to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: cabal summit** | Event |  | 30 days | A Consilium writes to offer you a cabal summit to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: convocation** | Event |  | 30 days | The Consilium offers you a convocation to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Free Council vote** | Event |  | 30 days | The Free Council offers you a vote to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Guardians review** | Event |  | 30 days | The Guardians of the Veil offer you a review to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Ladder audience** | Event |  | 30 days | The Silver Ladder offers you an audience to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Lex Magica hearing** | Event |  | 30 days | A Consilium writes to offer you a Lex Magica hearing to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Mysterium symposium** | Event |  | 30 days | The Mysterium offers you a symposium to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: the Consilium's assembly** | Event |  | 30 days | A Consilium writes to offer you its assembly to host. At most once a year. |
| **A mage asks for a lesson** | Event | 15 |  | A mage asks one of yours for a lesson: a dot more of an Arcanum or the next Attainment of a Legacy. Pays Consilium standing. |
| **A mage caught in an anomaly** | Event | 20 |  | A Consilium mage lies senseless at the edge of your land inside a Paradox anomaly of their own making. Carry them to a bed and it lets go: Consilium standing for your highest mage, and they owe a service. |
| **A mage chased by Seers** | Event | 20 |  | A mage with Seers on their heels asks to be let in. Saved, they owe your mages a service. |
| **A mage in a long trance** | Event | 20 |  | A mage going into a long trance asks you to guard their body while their mind is away. Pays Consilium standing. |
| **A maimed mage asks for a doctor** | Event | 25 |  | A maimed mage asks your doctor to make them whole. Pays Consilium standing. |
| **A Sleepwalker's skill lent** | Event | 25 |  | A mage asks to borrow one of your Sleepwalkers for their skill for a while. They walk back in at the end. |
| **Abyssal incursion** | Big threat | 30 |  | Rarely, while you have a mage, the Abyss finds a thin place on its own: a Gulmoth walks in from the map's edge, or an Acamoth slips into a colonist and nobody notices. Most Abyssal entities come from released Paradox instead. |
| **An apostate begs shelter** | Event | 30 |  | An apostate, a mage outside the Consilium, begs shelter for a while. Kept safe, it repays you. |
| **An apprentice to foster** | Event | 30 |  | The Consilium asks you to foster an apprentice and teach them. Pays Consilium standing. |
| **An Imbued Item commission** | Event | 25 |  | A mage commissions an Imbued Item from your mages. Pays Consilium standing. |
| **An order for tass** | Event | 15 |  | A mage orders tass, Mana in solid form, from your storage. Pays Consilium standing. |
| **Banisher ambush** | Big threat | 15 |  | Banishers wait by the road for a caravan with a mage in it. A mage away from home is what they hunt best. |
| **Banisher raid** | Big threat | 25 |  | A Banisher, a mage who hates magic, raids with her followers. She strips your mages' spells and goes for their souls. Only while you keep a mage. |
| **Bribed trader** | Event | 15 |  | An ordinary-looking caravan the Seers have bribed, with a cursed Imbued Item among its goods. Nothing tells it apart until it goes off, unless a mage looks with Focused Mage Sight. |
| **Cabal call** | Event |  | 10 days | A cabal of the nearest friendly Consilium calls: it visits, asks for help, wants a share of a Hallow, sends a mage who asks to join, or asks a colony mage to sit with it. |
| **Hallow drained** | Event | 20 |  | The Seers drain one of your Hallows overnight and put it to sleep, then raid a few days later to take the place. Hallow Dance can wake it again. |
| **Hunted mage** | Event |  |  | For a colony with no mage: someone who just Awakened comes running, with the Seers of the Throne or the Banishers on their trail. Take them in and they join as an apostate, and the hunters raid a day or two later. This is the usual way a colony meets the mages. |
| **Mysterium readers** | Event | 15 |  | Mysterium readers ask to study in your Library. Pays Consilium standing. |
| **Proscribed** | Big threat |  | 20 days | One of the Proscribed passes near the colony. A mage that has never crossed you walks through unless you harm it; one that has, or a Gulmoth, comes for the colony. |
| **Raiders bought** | Event | 30 |  | The Seers buy a hostile faction. For a year its raids bring a Seer along who keeps the raiders on a leash, and the first comes within days. |
| **Seer raid** | Big threat | 20 |  | A Seer of the Throne raids with her sleeper thralls, sent by the nearest Pylon's Ministry. Down the Seer and the thralls break and run. Only while you have a mage and none who serves the Seers. |
| **Seer recruiter** | Event |  | 30 days | A letter for an unhappy mage (Wisdom 5 or less, or cast out by the Consilium) from the Seers of the Throne, offering a Ministry's rotes, protection and silver to switch sides. |
| **Seers raid for grimoires** | Big threat | 30 |  | Once your Library is worth a reader's trip, a Seer of the Throne comes with her thralls for your grimoires. They make for the shelves and attack whatever holds a grimoire or an Artifact. Down the Seer and the thralls break. |
| **The Arrow asks for a guard** | Event | 20 |  | The Adamantine Arrow asks one of its mages in the colony to stand guard. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Arrow asks for a hunt** | Event | 20 |  | The Adamantine Arrow asks one of its mages in the colony to hunt Banishers. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Arrow asks for a sparring match** | Event | 20 |  | The Adamantine Arrow asks one of its mages in the colony for a sparring match. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Free Council asks for a mage lent** | Event | 20 |  | The Free Council asks to borrow one of its mages in the colony for a while. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Free Council asks for a technique shown** | Event | 20 |  | The Free Council asks one of its mages in the colony to show a technique. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Free Council asks for parts** | Event | 20 |  | The Free Council asks one of its mages in the colony for parts from storage. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Guardians ask for a Hallow held** | Event | 20 |  | The Guardians of the Veil ask one of their mages in the colony to hold a Hallow. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Guardians ask for a witness questioned** | Event | 20 |  | The Guardians of the Veil ask one of their mages in the colony to question a witness. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Guardians ask to watch from the colony** | Event | 20 |  | The Guardians of the Veil ask to keep watch from the colony. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Ladder asks for a case argued** | Event | 20 |  | The Silver Ladder asks one of its mages in the colony to argue a case. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Ladder asks for an envoy hosted** | Event | 20 |  | The Silver Ladder asks you to host an envoy. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Ladder asks for backing** | Event | 20 |  | The Silver Ladder asks one of its mages in the colony for backing. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Mysterium asks for a catalogue** | Event | 20 |  | The Mysterium asks one of its mages in the colony to catalogue a collection. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Mysterium asks for a relic hunt** | Event | 20 |  | The Mysterium asks one of its mages in the colony to hunt down a relic. Pays Order standing. |
| **The Mysterium asks for tass** | Event | 20 |  | The Mysterium asks one of its mages in the colony for tass. Pays Order standing (more at higher Order Status). |
| **Two cabals ask for neutral ground** | Event | 25 |  | Two quarrelling cabals ask to meet on neutral ground at the colony. If both leave unharmed, it pays standing. |
