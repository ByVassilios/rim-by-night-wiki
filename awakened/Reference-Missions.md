# Every mission and quest

Every mission a court, sept, cell or consilium can give, and every quest these mods offer.

| Mission | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **The Artifact in Banisher hands** | Travel to the Banisher camp that holds an Artifact a cabal wants back and break it. Your mages keep what the Banishers took. |
| **A Hallow claimed** | Travel to a Hallow the Seers have taken and scour it, so a cabal can claim it. |
| **A Paradox in the making** | Lend one of your mages to a cabal for some days, to quiet a mage who works vulgar magic in front of Sleepers. |
| **The tome hunt** | Travel to the Banisher camp that holds a tome a cabal has tracked down, break it and take the book. |
| **An envoy of the Consilium** | Host one or two of the Consilium's mages at your colony for a day and a half and keep them all alive. Seers and Banishers may attack while they stay. |
| **Called to the Consilium** | Lend one of your mages to the Consilium for 4 days. They leave when you accept and come home when the working is done. |
| **Reclaiming the Hallow** | Travel to a Hallow the Seers of the Throne hold and clear out their thralls. The Consilium shares the tass the Hallow gives. |
| **The Mysterium's lost Artifact** | Travel to the Banisher camp that robbed the Mysterium's vault and break it. Your mages keep what the Banishers took. |
| **The Banishers' camp** | Travel to a Banisher camp near the Consilium and kill or drive off every Banisher there. |
| **The Pylon's watchtower** | Travel to the house where a Pylon of the Seers of the Throne meets and break the Pylon. |
| **The tass tithe** | Bring the goods the Consilium is short of to it by caravan before the deadline. |
| **The newly Awakened** | Shelter a newly Awakened mage at your colony for a while and keep them alive. The Banishers will come for them. |
| **A student of the Path for …** | Lend one of your mages to the Consilium for some days to teach an apprentice of their Path. They come home when the days are up. |
| **The Abyssal breach** | Travel to an old shrine where something came through from the Abyss and kill everything that came through, so the breach closes. |
