# The sworn Consilium

Swear the colony to one Consilium and its mages stop being names on a roster. Each has a personality, means, a goal
of its own and an opinion of you that moves with what you do. They hand you work, owe you pledges, fight beside you
and, if they come to hate you, work against you.

## Swearing

On the Overview page of the Awakened window, use **Swear to this home**. You need:

- a colony mage with Status 1 or more at that Consilium,
- the Pentacle at peace with the colony.

One Consilium at a time. Its roster fills to 8 to 12 named mages with new apprentices. New pages appear on the
Awakened window's rail: **People**, **Council** and, while a war runs, **War**.

Leaving costs your mages a dot of Status there. Loyal mages drop to -5 regard, and the pledges the Consilium owes you
shrink a size. A Consilium that falls frees the colony with a letter.

## Pledges

Every Consilium the colony knows can owe it pledges, trivial, minor, major or life, as Kindred courts do. A Consilium's
mission pays one when it's done: trivial for an early colony, minor once raids come at a mid-game size, major for the
hardest. Once sworn, its mages owe you pledges for their own errands too.

Spend them on the **Pledges** page of the Awakened window. **Ask** lists what a Consilium does for a pledge, and a bigger
pledge covers a smaller service: tass (trivial), Sentinels at the door or a lesson worth 1 Arcane Experience to every
colony mage (minor), an Artifact (major), the Athenaeum opened, 4 Arcane Experience each (life). The services priced
in standing stay on **Ask the Consilium**. **Ledger** lists each pledge with who owes it and why; **Call in** on a row
picks what that pledge pays for. Animals bought from a Consilium are paid in pledges too. Leaving shrinks its pledges a size.

## People

The People page lists every named mage: title, cabal (Path and order for those in none) and regard, with a filter for friends, rivals
and enemies. Click a mage to see their personality, means, goal, friends and dislikes. You only see a mage's personality and means once you've **met** them: talked to them
in their town, finished a mission they gave, or looked at them with Mage Sight.

| Regard event | Change |
|---|---|
| A mission done for them | +2 |
| A mission done for their cabal or a friend | +1 |
| A step of their goal done | +3 |
| A gift from their row | +1 per 500 silver, up to +3 a season |
| Their mission refused or let lapse | -1 (Proud -3) |
| A mission done against them or a friend | -3 |
| Betraying their goal | -10, for good |

Regard drifts a point toward 0 every season.

### Personalities

| Personality | How they pay and behave |
|---|---|
| Greedy | silver x0.7, standing x1.5 |
| Cautious | silver and standing x1.25, but the biggest pledge they'll owe is a size smaller |
| Loyal | everything x1.25 while you never cross them or their friends; betrayal makes an enemy for good |
| Conniving | pays with a pledge another mage owes them |
| Cowardly | one more open mission, aimed at their rivals; pay x1.2 |
| Proud | standing x1.5, silver x0.8; refusals cost 3 regard |
| Zealous | only the fight against the Seers; those pay x1.5 |
| Generous | everything x1.3; now and then asks one back |
| Vengeful | bad blood never fades; an enemy at -5 |
| Mystery-hungry | asks for lore, and pays in Arcane Experience: one per 250 silver of the job to each colony mage there |
| Careless | riskier work, paying x1.3 |

A cabal's first mage has the personality of its temperament: Scholarly Mystery-hungry, Ambitious Proud, Reclusive
Cautious, Militant Zealous, Reckless Careless. Cabalmates are friends.

### Means and goals

Means come from the mage's title, wealth and the pledges owed to them: a poor one offers one mission at a time and
pays x0.6, a comfortable one two at x1.0, a rich one three at x1.4.

Each mage wants something, hidden until they count you a friend (regard +6) or you have a hold over them. A goal is
three steps: two missions bent to it, then the prize. Keeping the prize is a betrayal.

| Goal | What you get for it |
|---|---|
| Collector | a daimonomikon |
| Lore hunter | an Atlantean tablet or a Seer's ledger, both grimoires |
| Power seeker | silver by its means, and its vote for a year |
| Revenge | a major pledge and the enemy's goods |
| Freedom | its regard stays at +6 or more for good |
| Childe | a minor pledge now, its apprentice on your side later |
| A cure for Paradox scars | each colony mage learns, 3 Arcane Experience |
| Kingmaker | the new Hierarch starts at regard +6 |
| Homecoming | another Consilium, met |
| Buried secret | a life pledge |

## Titles

Under **Your seats** on the Overview page, a colony mage can take the title her Status there earns. A title held by
a mage makes that mage your rival. The Hierarch's seat is still won by election, as before.

| Title | Status | Duty each season |
|---|---|---|
| Herald | 2 | one hearing or message |
| Sentinel | 3 | one guard mission |
| Provost | 3 | one goods or trade errand |
| Interfector | 3 | one mission against a Banisher or Seer |
| Councilor | 4 | vote in the council |

A duty kept adds standing and a dot. Three missed in a row and the title is lost, with a dot of Status, to the next
in line. The mage's own tab shows the title and when its duty is due, and an alert warns two days ahead.

## The council

Each season the Hierarch and Councilors vote on one or two motions: bringing Awakenings before the Consilium, moving
against the Seers, hunting Banishers, the Hierarch's due. A colony Councilor votes too.

## Enemies inside

Rivals slander the colony or stymie your next mission with their own people. Enemies send hired raiders. Capture one
and question them in prisoner talk to learn who sent them, then **Accuse** that mage.

## War

A war runs against the Seers of the Throne's nearest settlement. Its score starts at 50: a season of incursions, two
of raids on their Hallow, sanctum and archive while you hold your own, then the final assault at 70, the desperate
defence at 30, or a truce.

The skirmishes come a few weeks apart. A war mission you let lapse is fought without you: the Consilium's members against the enemy's, by their strength, and members can die in it. Hover over the stages, the score and the holdings on the War page for what each one needs.

## Backgrounds

The Backgrounds page on a mage's tab buys rated Backgrounds with standing at the sworn Consilium: the new rating x 2,
never experience. Leaving the Consilium, or the Consilium falling, halves every dot. Now and then a rival strikes at
one of them, at most once a season: a letter gives six days (more with Contacts) to answer with a mission or a
favour the rival owes, or the dot is lost. The "Rival strikes on Backgrounds" setting makes strikes rarer or more common, or turns them off.

| Background | Each dot |
|---|---|
| Allies | armed mortals to call on any map with enemies on it; they never witness anything |
| Contacts | a day more to answer a strike, and a chance each season to strike back at a rival |
| Fame | more tavern guests and regulars |
| Influence | a caravan from the Consilium's faction once a season |
| Mentor | a Consilium mage of high enough rank (a member 1 to the leader 5) teaches |
| Resources | 100 x dots squared silver a season |
| Retainer | a Sleepwalker in a town near the colony who does a favour once a week, and never witnesses vulgar magic |

Retainer used to be a Merit. A mage who had it keeps the same dots, now as the Background.

## Settings

Under the Consilium heading of the Awakened settings: the sworn Consilium on or off, the war chance, enemy acts a
season and how fast regard fades. Dev mode has every step under "The Rim by Night: Spotlight".
