# What changed

What each update changed, newest first, as the grimoire shows it.

## Update of 26 November 2026

A player's ideas from the Workshop, and a few of our own. Each spell's page now lists its other buttons and its Reach effects.
### Chores and servants
- Golems and Eidolons clean the home area when idle, and take a standing order: haul, clean, mine, build, guard or follow.
- Albedo and Without a Trace can take a corpse's taint off clothes; Shaping can wash an area and cut chunks into blocks; Albedo can turn slag into steel; Celestial Fire can feed the caster Mana.
- Birth Spirit can make a servitor over a room, which reports what it did every 30 days.
- Create Life can make a homunculus for two kinds of work. Bind Spirit can raise a fire, earth, water or air elemental. Golem can stand a rock wall up as a stone giant. A Familiar can help round the house.
### Fighting
- Thunderbolt, Telekinetic Strike and Piercing Earth have new energies, and any aimed damage spell can take a shape: line, cone, burst, ring or chain. Thunderbolt chains by default.
- Telekinetic Strike can throw people back and knock them down. Ex Nihilo can raise a wall from nothing.
### Land and places
- Ex Nihilo can finish blueprints, State Change can raise rock and mountain, Transubstantiation can change the ground or make ore, Shifting the Odds can give a lucky seam.
- Pocket Dimension picks its look, can last with a name and an upkeep, and doors can lead to any lasting pocket.
### Spirits and kinds
- A spirit made to serve keeps a grudge; any mage or Garou can bargain with one for a day's service instead.
- Cleanse the Body can flush a vampire's blood out of a ghoul; Supernal Dispellation can silence a vampire's, werewolf's or hunter's powers for a while.
- Self-Repairing Machine mends a linked person's gear from far away.
### With DLCs
- Biotech: rewrite a gene, fill the blood, fill or drain a mechanoid, take a mechanoid. Royalty: open or close a psylink. Odyssey: molten ground, no breath needed, teleport to orbit.
### Fixes
- The enchanting table is 2x1, and its buttons draw it at its own shape.

## Update of 14 November 2026

### Requests and gatherings
- The Consilium and the Orders send requests: Mysterium readers come to a Library worth it, guests ask to learn a rote, an Arcanum dot or an Attainment, and some ask to be rescued.
- By the host's Consilium Status the colony can host a cabal summit at 2, a Lex Magica hearing in its Sanctum at 3, the Consilium's assembly, and more.
### Other changes
- Obsession steps pay nothing on the way; resolving one gives two Arcane Beats and a Mana.
- Scrying reaches any spot on the map, unseen too.
- Towns and sites have better rooms, with beds only where people sleep.
- A glossary of mages' words, underlined wherever an Awakened page uses them.
