# People, factions and creatures

The factions, the kinds of people and the creatures this mod adds.

## Factions

| Faction | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Agents** | Well-dressed people with good papers and blank-faced followers. Whoever employs them pays on time and never signs anything. |
| **Apostates** | Mages who Awakened on their own and have joined no Order.<br><br>Every Awakening is a journey to one of the five Watchtowers, where the mage writes their name and comes back able to rewrite the world. Most are found by a mentor and brought into one of the Pentacle Orders, which keep the knowledge of Atlantis. Apostates belong to none of them. They are free of the Orders' politics and their teaching both, and every Consilium watches them, some to recruit them, some to make sure they don't become a problem. |
| **Consilium exiles** | Mages cast out of their Consilium for breaking the Lex Magica.<br><br>A Consilium is the government of the Awakened in a region: a Hierarch, the councillors of the Orders, and a Herald who carries its word to every mage it claims. Exile is the punishment one step short of death. The exiles took what they could from their old Sanctum and went far enough that nobody followed. A Consilium remembers its exiles, though, and its Herald will come. |
| **Cunning folk** | A village wise one who is more than they look, and the family who keep their secret.<br><br>In the old villages people went to the cunning man or the wise woman for cures, charms and lost things found. Most of them knew herbs and nothing more. This one Awakened, saw the Supernal behind the world and can bend it a little. The family helps with the herbs and says nothing about the rest. |
| **Hallow founders** | Mages who found an unclaimed Hallow and mean to build their Sanctum around it.<br><br>A Hallow is a place where Mana wells up out of the world, and Mana is what lets a mage cast past their limits, heal the Pattern and make what lasts. A cabal with a Hallow has power, and everyone who wants it knows where to look: rival cabals, the Consilium that claims the land, and the Seers of the Throne, who serve the Exarchs and take Hallows to keep them from anyone else. |
| **Hermetic scholars** | Mages of the old learning who left their order with a book.<br><br>In the old orders, mages studied in towers and cloisters, copying grimoires by hand and teaching one apprentice at a time. Knowledge was hoarded, and the master's word was law. These scholars broke with their order over one tome and mean to found a library of their own around it. |
| **Lone Awakened** | A mage who has just Awakened among Sleepers.<br><br>Sleepers are everyone who never Awakened. Their disbelief is a force of its own: magic cast in front of them can tear open a hole to the Abyss, and they forget what they saw anyway. A new mage has to learn this, and the rest of the Lex Magica, before a Consilium decides they are a danger to the Mysteries. |
| **Scholars** | A quiet circle of learned households that keep to their libraries and their own rules. They trade books and curios and say little about what they study. |
| **Zealots** | Grim people who travel light and ask about strange lights and people who talk to themselves. They say they're cleaning something up. |

## Creatures and races

| | Name | Value | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | **Eidolon** | 0 | A servant of pure Mana a mage has called up out of nothing. It has no mind of its own: it hauls, guards, cleans and fights as it's told, and when the spell ends it's gone. |
|  | **Ghost** | 0 | What is left of someone who died badly: a shade bound to its body, its grave or something it owned. It can't leave the place, and the living can't see it, but it can make itself felt. |
|  | **Goetia** | 0 | A feeling grown so strong it walks on its own: rage, grief or fear given a body of Twilight. It pushes its feeling on everyone near it. |
|  | **Golem** | 0 | A thing of stone, wood or metal that a mage's spell has stood up and set to work. It has no mind of its own: it hauls, guards, cleans and fights as it's told, and when the spell ends it goes back to what it was. |
|  | **Gulmoth** | 0 | A thing from the Abyss, the nothing between the world and the Supernal. It has no body of its own, so it wears one made of every lie the world tells, and it looks wrong in ways the eye slides off. It hates everything real, and it feeds on fear and broken magic. |
|  | **Homunculus** | 0 | A small body a mage has grown out of nothing and set to work. There is nobody inside it: it hauls, cleans, builds, guards and fights as it's told, and when the spell ends it comes apart. |
