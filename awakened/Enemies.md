# Enemies

## The Seers of the Throne

They serve the Exarchs and act only while the colony has a true mage. Their Pylons hide in the region's towns. They:

- raid with sleeper thralls led by one Seer, a Disciple with her Ministry's Arcanum;
- empty a waking Hallow and put it to sleep, and raid a few days later;
- pay a hostile faction to bring a Seer along on its raids for a year;
- bribe a trader to sell a cursed Imbued Item;
- send a recruiter to a mage of low Wisdom, or an exile.

**Seer cases:** a drained Hallow, a talkative prisoner or a Seer's body is a clue. Each one rules out towns until the Pylon is found. Then you can raid it, tell the Consilium, or sell the secret back.

## Banishers

Mages who hate magic, their own included. A Banisher leads raids on the colony and ambushes caravans with a true mage. With Imbued loaded, hunters sometimes ride along and turn on their own side when she falls. A colonist Banisher may betray the colony, step by step, unless talk or Mind or Fate spells set her back.

## The Abyss

Released Paradox of 5 points or more can call something through.

- **Gulmoth** walk the map. They take half damage from mundane weapons, and they grow on Paradox, Dissonance and frightened Sleepers.
- **Acamoth** ride a colonist unseen. Bad nights first, then fights, then sabotage. Mind, Death or Fate sight finds them, and an exorcism drives them out.

## Enemy mages

Hostile mages arrive with Mage Armor and prepared spells up, fight with attack rotes, and always contain their Paradox. A Banisher counters spells better than any mage.

## The Proscribed

The Consilium's list of those it has declared enemies of every mage. Five names roam the world map, whether the colony has a mage or not. Only a colony with a mage is told of them.

- **Who is on it.** Banisher killers with one to three Sleeper followers, Seer agents with their Ministry's sign and one to three thralls, and now and then a Gulmoth loose from the Abyss at its third rank. All of them shrug off half of every wound.
- **The mark.** Each is a mark on the world map that moves every few days and shows only within 25 tiles of a home or a caravan. **Read the Proscribed** on the Awakened window's Overview opens the list.
- **Meeting one.** Send a caravan to its mark to hunt it. It's found at its camp: Banishers in their fortified camp, Seer agents in a safehouse, a Gulmoth in the ruined Sanctum it broke into. It holds the camp a few hours before coming out at you. One within 12 tiles can come to the colony: a mage that has never crossed it walks through unless harmed, and a Gulmoth, or a mage who knows the colony, attacks.
- **The reward.** The mage who kills one gains 2 Arcane Experience and 8 standing toward Status with the Consilium. The kill goes in the chronicle and can be the subject of art.
- **Setting.** **The Proscribed** turns the list off.
