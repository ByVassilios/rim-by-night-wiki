# Spellcasting

## Rotes, Praxes and improvised spells

Some spells are worked from another one in the book: Flight from Levitation, Wash Clean from Shaping, and the rest. Each says so at the top of its description. Knowing the first spell's rote, or its Praxis, teaches the ones worked from it.

- **Rotes** are spells she knows by heart. They add the mudra, her best rote Skill, to the pool.
- **Praxes** are spells she has mastered. Three successes count as exceptional.
- **Improvised** spells are anything her Arcana reach. Outside her Ruling Arcana they cost a Mana.

Spells sit on one power tile per Arcanum. Click one to cast it with its preset, or right-click it to open the builder. The Spells page of the Awakened tab shows each Arcanum's spells as icons, as the Kindred tab shows Disciplines: click one to take it off the bar or put it back. Faint icons are rotes in her Order's library she hasn't learned.

An Order's library, the Consilium's rote and grimoire, and Scribe Grimoire open one spell list with a search box, a chip for each Arcanum and dot rating, and "Can pick only".

## The builder

"Cast a spell" opens the builder. Pick a spell, then set:

- **Potency, Duration and Scale.** Each step past the free ones takes 2 dice off the pool.
- **Range:** touch, or sensory to reach anyone she can see.
- **Casting Time:** instant, or a ritual that takes longer and gets more help.
- **Reach:** Advanced factors and the spell's own Reach effects. Reach past her free Reach rolls Paradox.
- **Yantras:** High Speech, concentration, runes and a sacrament for more dice.

A line that does nothing on the picked spell (Potency on a spell that never reads it, Duration on an instant one, Scale on a spell cast on herself) is left out, and its row stays empty so the window keeps its shape.

The pool is Gnosis + Arcanum + Yantras. At -6 or worse, the spell can't be cast.

The tab's **Willpower** habit says when the mage spends Willpower on her own. **Never spend**; **To resist** (the default), a point adds two to Resolve or Composure against a hostile spell or power as it lands; **Spend freely**, which also puts a point into each casting for three more dice. Letting a spell go and Scrutiny ask for Willpower as before, whatever the habit says.

## Rituals

A ritual takes from 3 hours at Gnosis 1 down to a minute at Gnosis 9. Other mages can stand in and add their successes. Sleepers add a die.

## Choices after the cast

Some spells ask something once they land: which works a homunculus does, which animal to become, who to call. A window opens and the game pauses. Click an option to tick it and click it again to untick it, then press Done. If that pick leads to another question, it appears as the next step. Call off gives the Mana back.

## Lasting spells

A lasting spell counts against her Spell Control (her Gnosis, plus her Sanctum's rating while she's inside it). The tab lists each one with End, Let go (1 Willpower) and Let go safely (a Willpower dot).

## Paradox

Reach past free rolls Paradox dice by Gnosis. More dice come from outsiders watching, inured spells, the Abyss in her Pattern and earlier rolls this hour. The tab sets a policy: contain, release, or contain only when 5 or more dice come up.

- **Containing** rolls Wisdom. Each cancelled success is a bashing wound, and the rest becomes Backlash or Imago.
- **Releasing** spends the Reach on an Abyssal Tilt on the map, a new subject, or a warped spell.
- A Condition left to lapse adds the Abyss to her Pattern. The Scour button ends it at the price of a lethal cut.

## Disbelief

How hard the Lie presses back on your mages, from 0 to 100, shown on the Awakened tab's Overview.
A Sleeper from outside the colony who saw a vulgar spell adds 4 once they get away from the map,
twice that for a trader, and 10 at most for one caravan. The witness already added a Paradox die
to the spell they saw; the score never adds that die again. It falls by about a third of a point a
day, slower once it's high.

| Level | Score | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet | under 20 | Nothing |
| Whispers | 20 | A letter |
| Disbelief | 40 | Vulgar spells roll one more Paradox die |
| Notice | 60 | The Consilium's Sentinel asks who keeps casting where Sleepers can see |
| Hunted | 80 | Two more Paradox dice, and the Seers of the Throne raid within three days |

When the Sentinel asks (or once 12 points are laid on one mage), name the mage and half of it is
cleaned up, take the blame as a colony, or refuse. Standing with the Consilium moves 5 up, 5 down,
or 15 down (a Status dot).

## Dissonance

An obvious lasting spell seen by outsiders can be worn down by their disbelief, one factor at a time, until it ends.

## Mage Armor

With 2 dots in an Arcanum, she can put up Mage Armor for 1 Mana and an hour: armor and dodge by her dots.

## Shapes and energies

Thunderbolt, Telekinetic Strike and Piercing Earth each have more than one energy, picked on the builder as a button of its own. Thunderbolt throws a Lightning Bolt, a Fire Bolt (it can set them alight), a Frost Bolt (it chills toward hypothermia), a Radiation Bolt (no wound, but toxic buildup) or a Thunderclap (a stun, a small bruise and deafness). Telekinetic Strike is a Force Blow, an Air Blade or a Force Spike. Piercing Earth is Crushing Earth, Stone Spikes, or a Sand Blast that blinds for a few seconds. The builder's search finds a button by its name, so typing "fire" finds Thunderbolt.

Any aimed damage spell also takes a shape on the builder. A bolt hits one target, or one more for each step of Scale. A line runs from the mage through the target, 12 to 24 cells long by Scale, and a wall stops it. A cone fans out 8 to 16 cells deep. A burst hits everyone round the target, out to the Scale's radius. A ring hits everyone 4 to 10 cells round the mage, who is spared. A chain hits the target and jumps to the nearest person within 8 cells, once for each subject the Scale allows, so 16 subjects is 16 jumps. Advanced Scale makes every shape bigger still. No one inside a line, cone, burst or ring can dodge it, and only a chain's jumps can miss. Friends are hit as well, unless the mage pays a Reach to spare her side.

## Attacking with magic

On the Awakened tab's Conduct page, **Attacks with** sets what the mage fights with:

- **Weapons**: her gun or blade. Autocast still casts what its own setting allows.
- **Magic**: the hardest-hitting aimed attack rote that can reach the enemy, with its saved setup from Cast a Spell.
  Her weapon takes over only when no rote can go (out of range or sight, no Mana or Spell Control to spare).
- **Smart** (the default): a rote when it hits harder than her weapon. She keeps to the weapon while her Mana is under
  a quarter, and won't cast an Obvious rote with Sleepers watching or one that would go past her Spell Control.

Drafted, a right-click on an enemy casts the rote, as a right-click fires a gun. Undrafted, she casts it wherever she
would shoot, whatever the autocast setting.

The cast is the power button's own, so rolls, Paradox and witnesses work as they always do.

## Weapon glyphs

Six touch spells put a glyph on a fighter's weapon for the spell's Duration. The weapon glows in the Arcanum's colour,
and every swing and shot leaves a trail. Each comes with the rote of the spell it was worked from, and each costs one
Reach.

| Glyph | Worked from | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Force Wave | Kinetic Blow (Forces) | A melee blow carries on as a blade of force: the first enemy within Potency x2 cells behind the target takes it at half |
| Long Reach | Kinetic Blow (Forces, and Space 2) | Melee blows land from up to Potency cells off |
| Supernal Rounds | Ephemeral Enchantment (Prime) | +10% armour piercing a Potency on shots, and they hit beings in Twilight |
| Grave Edge | Shadow Crafting (Death) | Each wound heals 10% a Potency slower for a day |
| Doom Mark | Monkey's Paw (Fate) | Each hit lays a stack of Doom |
| Keen Edge | Alter Integrity (Matter) | +10% damage a Potency on every blow and shot |

The glyph sits on the wielder, so it follows them to the next weapon they pick up.

## Shadow fighter

Shadow Sculpting has a Reach effect, "the shadow fights" (2 Reaches): instead of a figure that draws enemies, the
caster's shadow stands up in Twilight and fights for the Duration, going for her enemies within 12 cells. It's weak,
but its blows reach the living, and only harm that touches Twilight can hurt it. It fades when the spell ends.

## Bone magic and bottled Mana

- **Bone Armour** (from Shadow Crafting): a corpse's bones close round the caster as armour for the Duration, and the
  body is left a skeleton.
- **Bone Thrall** (from Quicken Corpse): a corpse rises at once as a fast fighter that does no work, and falls apart
  when the spell ends.
- **Bottle Mana** (from Channel Mana): the caster pours up to Potency of their own Mana into a stone chunk beside them,
  and it becomes that many hallow stones.

## Cantrips

With the **Cantrips** setting on (off by default), every mage knows a cantrip for each Arcanum they have a dot in: a
small aimed spell of one wound, with a range of Gnosis x10 cells. They're obvious, so Paradox rolls as usual. With the
setting off they're hidden and can't be cast.

| Cantrip | Arcanum | The wound |
|---|---|---|
| Spark | Forces | Burn |
| Mote | Prime | Bashing, and it hits beings in Twilight |
| Grave chill | Death | Bashing, and a small chill |
| Jinx | Fate | Bashing, and a stack of Doom |
| Sting | Life | Bashing, and +5% pain for an hour |
| Pebble | Matter | Blunt |
| Twinge | Mind | Bashing to the head |
| Snag | Space | Cut |

## Doom

A hex, Grave Misfortune, Pariah and Doom Mark each lay a stack of Doom: -5 aim and dodge a stack, for a day. The
third stack goes off at once. A gun jams, or the target trips and falls, and the stacks clear.

## The land

Matter can make and change the ground for good.

- Build from Nothing builds every blueprint and frame of the colony's in its area at once, if the material is one the Potency reaches. It costs 1 Mana more for each step of Scale.
- Raise Stone puts natural rock of the map's own stone on the empty ground in its area. A +2 Reach puts overhead mountain over it too, which can cave in and draw infestations.
- Reshape the Ground turns natural ground into soil, rich soil (Life 2 as well), sand, gravel, rough stone, marsh or shallow water. Floors and bridges are left alone, and nothing built turns to water.
- Vein of Ore turns a rock wall into a vein: compacted steel at Potency 1, silver and jade at 3, gold and plasteel at 4, uranium at 5.
- Shifting the Odds can give a lucky seam: for a day, plain rock mined on the map gives ore 5% of the time a Potency. A Reach names the ore.
- With Odyssey, State Change's Molten ground (Forces 3 as well) turns the area to lava for the Duration. When it ends, the old ground comes back.

## Pocket worlds

Pocket Dimension asks how its world looks: indoors (a roof and still air) or outdoors (open sky and soil), then natural (rock walls and rough ground) or built (smooth walls and floor). With Life 2 the ground is rich soil, and an indoor pocket gets spell lights bright enough for crops. With Forces 2 you pick its temperature: cold, cool, mild or hot.

An Indefinite pocket gets a name and goes on the colony's list. A later Pocket Dimension can open one of those instead of making a new one, and a pocket's door on the map has a **Leads to** button to pick which pocket it opens on. Its Key still decides who passes. Each lasting pocket takes a Mana a week, from its maker first and then a Hallow on her map. After six days unpaid a letter warns that it closes the next day. When it closes, everything inside comes out beside its maker, or in the middle of the map if she has left.

## More tricks

- Ex Nihilo's A wall from nothing puts up a line of plain stone walls across the way from the mage to the spot, 3 cells and 2 more a step of Scale, for the Duration.
- Thunderbolt starts as a chain, jumping from the target to the next person near. Pick Bolt on the builder to hit one.
- Telekinetic Strike's "throws them back" Reach sends whoever it hits a cell a Potency and knocks them down for a few seconds.
- Familiar's "it helps round the house" Reach has the familiar clean and haul a little when it's idle.
- Self-Repairing Machine cast along a sympathetic link at a person mends a piece of their gear, wherever they are, even with a caravan.
- Golem's Stone giant (Matter 4) stands a natural rock wall up as a golem with an earth elemental's body. It mines when it has no order, and goes back to rock when the spell ends.
- A servitor sends a letter every 30 days on what it did: messes swept, enemies warned of, things mended.

## Changing kinds

No spell makes anyone a vampire, a werewolf or a hunter. Two come close the other way.

- Cleanse the Body's Flush the blood (Life 4 and Death 2, with Kindred) washes a vampire's blood out of a ghoul. The ghouling ends and the years it held back catch up at once. A blood bond stays, since it's in the mind.
- Supernal Dispellation's Silence a nature (Prime 5) stops a vampire's, werewolf's or hunter's powers for an hour a Potency: no gifts, no blood spent, no healing the way they should. The caster has to win a Clash of Wills against the subject's Resolve plus three.

## DLC spells

These show only with their DLC.

- Biotech: Transform Life's Rewrite the blood (Life 5) adds a gene the colony knows from a genepack or one of its people, or takes a xenogene away. It goes back when the spell ends, unless the Duration is Indefinite. A Reach works on germline genes instead. Knit's "fill the blood" Reach takes away blood loss and fills hemogen. Create Energy's Current gives the mechanoids in its area a fifth of their energy a Potency, and Eradicate Energy's Drain a mechanoid takes it away. Remote Control's Mechanoid makes one fight for the caster for an hour a Potency, with no mechlink. A mechanitor's mech is held unless the caster wins a Clash of Wills.
- Royalty: Psychic Genesis can open a psylink a level, or with Prime 3 close one a level. It stays, and it's an Act of Hubris.
- Odyssey: Environmental Shield at Potency 5 keeps out vacuum entirely. Body Control's "no breath needed" Reach does too. Teleportation's To orbit (Space 5) sends someone to a gravship or platform where the colony is, inside where there's air, or from orbit back home.

## Servants

A golem, an Eidolon or a quickened corpse has no mind of its own. Its Order button gives it one standing order: haul, clean, mine, build, guard a spot, or follow its maker. With no order, a golem or an Eidolon works as a trained animal and cleans the home area when it's idle. A quickened person keeps its own work and only takes Guard or Follow. Building leaves anything with a quality to the colonists.

Create Life's Homunculus (Life 5) makes a small pale body with nobody inside. It works like a golem: one standing order (haul, clean, mine, build, guard or follow), no food, sleep or mood, and it isn't one of your colonists. A faint green shimmer rises off it. With Mind 5 it's a real person instead, for two kinds of work you pick, from cleaning, hauling, growing, cutting plants, cooking, crafting, mining and construction, at 3 skill a Potency and nothing else.

Bind Spirit's An elemental (Spirit 4, and Forces 3 or Matter 3) raises one from its element near the spot: a fire, stone, water or open sky. Its Rank is the Potency, up to 3. Fire sets alight what it strikes and warms a room. Earth is slow, hard to hurt and mines. Water puts out fires and knocks down what it strikes. Air is fast, throws back what it strikes and blows gas away.

A spirit made to serve by Command Spirit or Bind Spirit keeps a grudge. When the spell ends it may turn and fight, curse the caster with a bad mood, or slip away. With Spirit 1 a mage can right-click a spirit and **bargain** with it instead: a day's service for Mana, its offering, or the bane it wants gone. A bargain leaves no grudge.

Birth Spirit can make a servitor instead of a spirit: a small made spirit in Twilight over a room, with one task. A sweeper clears filth, a warden sends a letter when an enemy walks in, a hearth keeps the room at 21°C, a gardener makes plants grow a fifth faster, a mender gives damaged things back some hit points each day, and a lamp lights the room. Only Spirit sight sees it, as a small light over its spot. Cast Indefinite (Advanced Duration, slid to the end), it lasts for good.

## Chores

Some spells take care of the dull work round the colony. Each one is a chore, so a mage whose Wisdom is Enlightened rolls Wisdom after it.

- Shaping can wash an area clean of filth, or cut its stone chunks into blocks.
- Nigredo and Albedo can take a corpse's taint off clothes, or turn a slag chunk into steel.
- Without a Trace can hide that a corpse ever wore what the subject has on.
- Celestial Fire can bring the fuel it burns (blood, Gnosis, Conviction or Mana) to the caster as Mana, up to her Gnosis.
