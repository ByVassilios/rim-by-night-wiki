# Every setting

Every setting in Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, with what it does.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chance of an Awakening** | How likely a colonist is to Awaken at a qualifying moment: coming back from near death, an eclipse or psychic drone, losing a lover or bonded animal, a masterwork, a big research breakthrough, a mental break that ends in catharsis. The mod's chance is 2%, doubled for the psychically sensitive. |
| **Mages before the Awakening slows** | Once the colony has this many mages, the next one Awakens at most once a season. A mentor's lessons aren't held back by this. |
| **Paradox dice** | Dice added to or taken from every Paradox roll, once something has put a Paradox die in it. |
| **Acts of Hubris cost Wisdom** | When off, mages never roll to lose Wisdom, and so never become Megalomaniacal, Rampant or Mad. |
| **Keep snapshots for Corridors of Time** | While a colony mage with Time 3 knows Corridors of Time, the game saves itself to a hidden folder now and then, so the spell can turn the world back. Saving can stutter a big colony for a moment. When off, Corridors of Time has nothing to go back to. |
| **Hours between snapshots** | How often the hidden snapshot is taken, in game hours. Fewer hours means more moments to go back to and more saving. |
| **Chance of a ghost** | How likely someone who dies badly (murdered, executed, burned, starved or frozen) is to leave a ghost behind. The mod's chance is 5%. |
| **Quiet spirits on home maps** | A few harmless spirits live unseen on each colony map, for Spirit magic to find. When off, the only spirits are the ones a spell calls up or Garou brings. |
| **Walking on walls** | Transform Life's wall-crawling and Alter Direction let a pawn walk over walls and rock. This changes how those pawns find their way, so turn it off if another pathing mod (Pathfinding Framework, Vehicles) has trouble with it. When off, the spells still cast but nobody climbs. |
| **The Lex Magica** | The Consilium hears of colony mages' crimes and summons them to answer. Off, nobody is ever charged. |
| **Spell camera shake** | How hard big spells shake the camera: a thunderbolt, an earthquake, Paradox. At 0% the camera stays still. |
| **Spell sky flashes** | Big spells flash the sky in their colour, and storms and curses tint it while they last. Off, the sky keeps its own light. |
| **Mage looks** | A selected mage shows the lights of their spells, their Nimbus glow and the marks of lasting spells, and Hallows show their ring and motes. Another group's mage shows them only while one of yours has Mage Sight open. Off, none of these are drawn. Casts still show. |
| **Arcane Experience gained** | How much each Arcane Beat counts. At 50%, two Arcane Beats count as one; at 200%, one counts as two. |
| **Dramatic failures for Arcane Beats** | A drafted mage's failed cast becomes a dramatic failure, with its Paradox, for an Arcane Beat. The autocast never does this. |
| **Chance of a Hallow** | How likely a newly made map is to have a Hallow on it. The mod's chance is 12.5%, about one map in eight. |
| **Long-Term Nimbus** | Mages of Gnosis 6 and up now and then cause small strange happenings near them: breakdowns, weather, wandering animals, lucky finds or bad dreams, by Path. |
| **The Proscribed** | A handful of enemies of every mage roam the world map: Banisher killers, Seer agents and Gulmoth loose from the Abyss. They roam either way, but only a colony with a mage is told of them, sees them on the map or earns anything for one. Turned off, they leave the world map; turned back on, a new list arrives in one letter. |
| **Disbelief gain** | Scales how much the colony's Disbelief rises for each Sleeper who leaves having seen vulgar magic. |
| **Disbelief fading** | Scales how fast the colony's Disbelief falls back each day as Sleepers forget. |
| **Serendipity coincidences** | Serendipity with nothing to find makes a small coincidence instead, once in five days for each goal: a wanderer who asks to join, herbal medicine just outside, a forgotten stash. Off, it finds nothing. |
| **Counterspells** | A mage with Active Sight up who isn't busy casting counters an enemy's spell cast in sight, in a Clash of Wills. Colonists only do it when drafted. |
| **A hunted mage comes** | A colony with no mage gets a stranger who has just Awakened, with the Seers of the Throne or the Banishers on their trail, some time between day 15 and day 40, and again every 30 to 60 days until one is taken in. Taken in, the stranger joins as a mage and the hunters raid within a day or two. Off, the stranger still turns up now and then as an ordinary event. |
| **Awakening gives a psylink** | A colonist who Awakens, or starts the game Awakened, gets a first psylink level if they have none, for psycast mods. Further levels come from psytrainers as usual. |
| **Cantrips** | Every mage knows a cantrip for each Arcanum they have a dot in: a small aimed spell of one wound, with a range of Gnosis x10 cells. Cantrips are obvious and roll Paradox as usual. Off, they're hidden and can't be cast. |
| **Show spell areas** | When a lasting area spell's circle and name show on the ground. "When selected" shows it while its mage is selected or the mouse is over it, and always under Mage Sight of its Arcanum. A spell let go with a dot of Willpower shows only under Mage Sight. |
| **Mana from Hallows** | How much Mana and tass a Hallow makes each day, as a share of its rating. At 100% a Hallow makes its rating in Mana a day, as the book says. |
