# Settings and FAQ

## Settings

Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, then Awakened on the rail down the left, which every Rim by
Night mod shares.

- **Awakening:** the odds of an Awakening, the mage cap before the season limit, the hunted stranger, and the Proscribed.
  With Royalty, **Awakening gives a psylink** (off by default) gives a colonist who Awakens a first psylink level, for psycast mods.
- **Paradox:** a dice offset, and Wisdom loss on or off.
- **Time:** game snapshots for the Time rewinds, and how often they're taken.
- **Beings:** ghost odds and spirits in Twilight.
- **Places:** Hallow odds, Hallow Mana and the long-term Nimbus.
- **Growth:** dramatic failures for Arcane Beats, and how much each Arcane Beat counts (25% to 200%).
- **Looks:** camera shake, sky flashes, and when a lasting spell's circle shows on the ground: always, when its mage is selected or the mouse is on it (and under Mage Sight of its Arcanum), or under Mage Sight only.
- **Consilium:** the Lex Magica on or off.
- **Event chances:** each event on or off, and how often.

## In orbit

With Odyssey your colony can live in orbit. The World of Darkness stays on the ground: nobody from these mods
comes up uninvited unless "The World of Darkness reaches orbit" is on in Core's settings. Help you call still
comes, by drop pod, and leaves on Royalty's shuttle. Without Royalty it stays aboard until the ship lands. The
colony is whichever home holds most of your free colonists, so visitors go where your people are.

No Hallow forms on an orbit home or an asteroid. A sympathetic spell between orbit and the ground costs one more
Reach. There are no Sleepers up there to witness magic, and Banishers only come up with the setting on.

## FAQ

**Is it safe on my main save?**
It's in beta, so bugs are likely. Keep a backup of any save you add it to.

**Can I add it to a running game?**
Yes. Colonists start rolling for the Awakening from then on.

**How do I take it out?**
Use "Remove Awakened from this game" in the mod settings, save, then disable the mod.

**Do I need the other Rim by Night mods?**
Only Core. Kindred, Garou and Imbued add the other kinds.

**Do I need to know the tabletop game?**
No. The grimoire explains the rules in game.
