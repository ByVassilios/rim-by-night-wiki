# Places and items

## Hallows

A Hallow is a spot where Mana wells up: a geyser, an ancient building, an old tree or a rock face. Some maps have one, and the same tile always does. Each day a waking Hallow fills up to its rating in Mana. Anything past that forms as tass beside it. Colonists who sleep there may Awaken.

A colony mage can see a Hallow's ring and label. Oblation there gives Mana. Hallow Dance wakes a sleeping Hallow or damps a waking one, and Create Truth makes a new one for 5 Mana a dot.

## Tass

Mana in matter: stone, ash, water or fruit, by the Hallow's resonance. Draw its Mana from the Mana gizmo, or eat the fruit.

## The Sanctum

Build a Sanctum focus in a room and assign up to five mages. The room's beauty sets the rating, from 1 to 5. In her own Sanctum, a mage keeps Gnosis + rating spells up, and outsiders outside the room don't see what's cast there.

## Mage Sight

The sight gizmo opens one Arcanum or more. Marks float over pawns nearby: the Awakened, the dead, spirits, the sick, the possessed. Focused Sight studies one pawn, corpse, item or place for an hour, and Scrutiny goes deeper for a Willpower. Sight slows work a little while it's open.

## The Nimbus

Every successful cast flares her Nimbus and puts her Path's Tilt on those around her. Cloak Nimbus hides it.

## Grimoires and the Library

A grimoire holds rotes. A mage can learn from one, and Scribe Grimoire writes new ones. A Library Merit adds dice.

## Tomes

Ten rare books, one per Arcanum, each a few chapters long. A tome is where the lost attack rotes, the Ossuary rule and
spreading Doom come from: no teacher, grimoire or Order hands them out.

- **Studying.** Select the tome and press Chapters... to pick the reader and a chapter. The tome must lie in a Library
  (loose or on a shelf). A chapter takes one to three days of work, faster with a better Library and a quicker
  researcher, and the reader needs its Arcanum and Occult (and sometimes Gnosis). A finished chapter teaches its
  rotes for good. Progress is kept if the reader stops, but another reader or chapter starts over.
- **The price.** Every chapter costs something, rolled when it's done: usually the tome's own price, sometimes another.
  It can be Paradox, an Act of Hubris, a bad mood (shaken, or obsessed with the book), the Abyss noticing (an Acamoth
  or a Gulmoth a few days later), a Seer Pylon learning where you are, a ghost following the reader, a spirit with a
  grudge, or a year of the reader's life.
- **Hidden chapters.** Six tomes have one, and only half their copies hold it (about one tome in three). A mage who
  knows Supernal Vision or Pierce Deception finds it at once; anyone else rolls Intelligence + Occult once a day.
- **Where they come from.** One ancient complex in five has one by a crate, one Seer or Banisher raid in ten carries
  one, the Consilium sells one at Status 4, and a cabal may ask for help taking one from a Banisher camp
  ("The tome hunt").

|---|---|---|---|
| The Lantern Scrolls | Prime | Mana Bolt, Radiance | Read the Hallows: every Hallow's Mana from anywhere |
| The Leaden Codex | Matter | Acid Arrows, Frost Shards, Shrapnel | None |
| The Ossuary of Saint Absalom | Death | Bone Shards, Grave Claws, Life Leech | The Ossuary rule: up to Gnosis bone thralls with Death 3, free of Spell Control |
| The Book of the Seventh Storm | Forces | Fireball, Telekinetic Throw, Telekinetic Choke | None |
| The Red Herbal | Life | Bleed | Life Leech heals the worst hurt of your people nearby |
| The Mirror Psalter | Mind | Psychic Feedback, Turncoat | None |
| The Atlas of Unfolded Rooms | Space | Rift | Long Reach's glyph without Space 2 |
| The Clockmaker's Heresy | Time | Flurry, Rust | None |
| The Gambler's Concordance | Fate | Doom spreads to the nearest enemy when a doomed pawn dies | Jinx with the Cantrips setting off |
| The Banefather's Liturgy | Spirit | Spirit Claws | Command Spirit |

## Imbued Items and Artifacts

The enchanting table makes Imbued Items that hold a spell. Twenty Artifacts from beyond the Abyss can turn up in the world, each with its own power and a Mana pool.
