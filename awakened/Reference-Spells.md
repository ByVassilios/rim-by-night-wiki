### Deepen Shadows

*The dark thickens until a lamp barely reaches across it.*

- **Target:** An area in sight, the Scale
- **Effect:** It goes so dark that everyone in it who isn't on the caster's side sees at half their sight. Shots into or out of it miss as they would through smoke
- **Vampires:** A vampire inside doesn't burn by day
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Darkness as cover. Enemies in it see at half range and shots through it miss as if through smoke, while your people see normally. A vampire inside doesn't burn by day, which makes it a daytime shelter for Kindred allies. The Reach blinds everyone else in it.

### Ectoplasmic Shaping

*Ghost-stuff takes the shape the mage gives it.*

- **Target:** A lump of ectoplasm in sight
- **Effect:** It becomes a simple thing made of ectoplasm: a tool, a weapon, clothes or furniture
- **Lasts:** The Duration, then the thing is gone
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Turns a lump of ectoplasm (made with the Ectoplasm spell) into a simple item: a tool, a weapon, clothes or furniture. It's gone when the spell ends, so it's for stopgaps, not stock.

### Forensic Gaze

*The body tells the mage how it died.*

- **Target:** One corpse in sight
- **Effect:** A letter: how long ago it died, and up to 1 causes of death (one per Potency). If whoever last fought them is still on the map, the letter points them out
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Corpse Mask on the body shows its false cause

**How to use it:** Read a corpse: when it died and what killed it. If the killer is still on the map, the letter points them out. A body under Corpse Mask shows the false cause.

### Grave chill

*A cantrip of Death. A breath of the grave's cold.*

- **Target:** One target in range (Gnosis x10 cells), with an attack roll
- **Effect:** The cold of the grave: one bashing wound and a small chill
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A cantrip, only with the Cantrips setting on: one wound from range, for a mage with a single dot of Death.

### Shadow Sculpting

*Shadows gather into a shape that isn't there.*

- **Target:** A spot in sight
- **Effect:** A shadow shaped like a person stands there, and enemies nearby go to see who it is
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A decoy. A shadow shaped like a person stands at a spot, and nearby enemies go to check it out. Use it to pull raiders out of cover or into a trap.

### Soul Marks

*The mage reads the scars on a soul.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** A letter with 1 facts about their soul (one per Potency), from: Awakened or Sleeper, another kind of supernatural, a ghost's or spirit's Rank, whether the soul is whole or torn, whether a mage rides them, a Paradox Condition
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Reads a soul: mage or Sleeper, what other kind of supernatural, and whether the soul is whole, torn or ridden by another mage. A cheap check on a suspicious visitor.

### Speak with the Dead

*The mage sees the dead who linger, and they see the mage.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Ghosts around the caster show, with their Rank
- **Graves:** Each body or grave within 24 cells teaches the caster 300 XP of the dead one's best skill, once per body
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Shows nearby ghosts and their Rank, and it's a training spell: each body or grave within 24 cells teaches the caster 300 XP of the dead person's best skill, once per body. Walk it past your graveyard.

### Corpse Mask

*The body lies about how it died.*

- **Target:** One corpse in sight
- **Effect:** It seems to have died of something else, picked from a list, and it looks freshly dead: the rot starts over
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Forensic Gaze sees through it

**How to use it:** Hides how someone died, and makes the corpse look fresh (the rot starts over). Cover up a killing, or keep a body fresh for a while. Forensic Gaze sees through it.

### Decay

*Wood rots, iron rusts, cloth falls to threads.*

- **Target:** One item, building or mechanoid in sight
- **Effect:** It loses 10% of its hit points (10% per Potency)
- **Mechanoid:** Its plating rusts instead: armour -10% (10% per Potency) for a day
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Its toughness, each dot takes 1 off the Potency

**How to use it:** Rots an item or a building, or rusts a mechanoid's plating so your weapons hurt it more for a day. Tough materials resist.

### Ectoplasm

*The mage draws ghost-stuff out of their own body.*

- **Target:** The caster's feet
- **Effect:** A lump of ectoplasm appears. Ectoplasmic Shaping can make things of it. Slime spreads round it and slows whoever walks through, like deep mud
- **Lasts:** The Duration, then it's gone
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Makes a lump of ectoplasm at the caster's feet, for Ectoplasmic Shaping to turn into items. The slime around it slows anyone walking through like deep mud, which works as a quick trap in a doorway.

### Ghost Shield

*The dead slide off the subject.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** A ghost's Numina and other mages' Death spells must win a Clash of Wills (a contest of the two casters' power) to affect them
- **Mood:** The dead don't trouble them: no bad mood from corpses, and deaths weigh half as much
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Protection from ghosts and enemy Death magic, and the dead stop bothering the person: no bad mood from corpses, and deaths hurt their mood half as much. Good on a doctor, a grave digger or a butcher. The Reach also halves ghosts' blows.

### Shape Ephemera

*A ghost's own stuff becomes a blade or a coat of mail.*

- **Target:** One ghost in sight
- **Effect:** Armed and armoured with its own ephemera: melee damage x1.5, and +30% sharp and blunt armour
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Its Rank, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**How to use it:** Arms and armours a ghost with its own ghost-stuff. Cast it on a ghost that fights for you. On an enemy ghost it makes things worse.

### Soul Armor

*The soul holds fast to its body.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Any spell that would tear out their soul must win a Clash of Wills (a contest of the two casters' power). Mental break threshold -5%
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Protects someone's soul from being torn out by Sever Soul, and makes them a little steadier against mental breaks.

### Soul Jar

*A bottle becomes a prison for a soul.*

- **Target:** One loose soul in sight
- **Effect:** It's held in place and can't drift home
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Holds a loose soul (one torn out by Sever Soul) in place so it can't drift home and return to its body.

### Suppress Aura

*The light of magic around the subject goes out.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Hunters and other kinds' senses take them for a Sleeper
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Hides a supernatural from hunters and from other kinds' senses: they read as a plain Sleeper. Cast it before a hunter cell visits.

### Suppress Life

*Breath and heartbeat stop, and the subject passes for dead.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** They pass for dead, and nobody attacks them until they attack. Hunters' sight passes over them
- **As a rote:** It casts itself when the mage goes down, if she has the Mana
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Playing dead, for real. Nobody attacks them until they attack, and hunters' sight passes over them. Learned as a rote, it casts itself when the mage goes down, if she has the Mana, which can save her life.

### Touch of the Grave

*The mage's hands close on what the living can't hold.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** They can strike ghosts and anyone in Twilight
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Lets a fighter hit ghosts and anyone in Twilight. Cast it on your melee fighter when a ghost attacks.

### Without a Trace

*Footprints, dirt and smears never happen.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** They leave no dirt or tracks behind and nothing a hunter can trace. Harming or killing a faction's people costs no goodwill
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Leave no evidence: no dirt, no tracks, nothing a hunter can trace, and hurting or killing a faction's people costs no goodwill. Cast it before a raid on a settlement. Cleaning a dead man's clothes is Lift the Taint, a Matter spell.

### Bone Armour

*Worked from Shadow Crafting, Death Weaving. The bones of a corpse come out and close round the caster as armour, and the body is left a dry skeleton.*

- **Target:** One corpse in sight
- **Effect:** Its bones close round the caster as armour: +12% sharp and blunt armour. The corpse is left a skeleton
- **Lasts:** The Duration, then the bones fall away
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Armour from a corpse in sight, +12% sharp and blunt a Potency, for the Duration. The corpse is left a skeleton, so it can't be raised or cast on again. Comes with Shadow Crafting's rote.

### Bone Thrall

*Worked from Quicken Corpse, Death Weaving. The dead get up to fight, fast and tireless, and fall apart when the fight is over.*

- **Target:** One corpse in sight
- **Effect:** It rises at once to fight for the colony: fast, tireless, feels no pain, does no work
- **Lasts:** The Duration, then it falls apart
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** The fighter's Quicken Corpse: the body rises fast and sure-handed, does no work, and falls apart quietly when the spell ends. Comes with Quicken Corpse's rote.

### Cold Snap

*Frost spreads over the ground in a breath.*

- **Target:** An area in sight, the Scale
- **Effect:** The ground is slick with ice: everyone in it who isn't on the caster's side moves at 60% speed
- **Frostbite:** Those on the ice may get frostbite on their fingers and toes, a 4% chance (4% per Potency) every few seconds
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Ices the ground: enemies on it move at 60% speed and may get frostbite. Your own side isn't slowed. Good across a raid's approach.

### Damage Ghost

*The mage tears at a ghost's Corpus.*

- **Target:** One ghost in sight, with an attack roll, in Twilight or not
- **Effect:** 8 blunt damage (8 per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss, and a miss lands within a few cells on whoever is there

**How to use it:** An attack that hits ghosts, whether they're manifested or hiding in Twilight. It rolls to hit.

### Devouring the Slain

*The mage feeds on a life as it leaks away.*

- **Target:** One person with an open wound, by touch
- **Effect:** The caster takes 1 Mana from them (one per Potency), and each Mana tears the wound wider
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher)

**How to use it:** Takes Mana from someone who's bleeding, and makes the wound worse with each point. Cruel and an Act of Hubris, but it works on a downed enemy when you're out of Mana.

### Ghost Gate

*A doorway the living can walk through into Twilight.*

- **Target:** A spot in sight
- **Effect:** A gate stands there. Whoever walks through takes Twilight form: no harm reaches them, and they can't harm or work. Walking through again brings them back
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A gate into Twilight. Walking through makes you safe from all harm (but unable to fight or work); walking through again brings you back. Use it as a panic room in a fight.

### Ghost Summons

*The mage calls, and the dead come.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** The nearest ghost on the map comes and stays near the caster. With none, the newest dead raise one
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Calls the nearest ghost on the map. If there are none, the most recently dead raise one. Follow it with Revenant or a familiar spell to make it useful.

### Grave Claws

*Worked from Rotting Flesh, Death Perfecting. Hands of shadow rise from the ground to tear and hold. A lost rote from the Ossuary.*

- **Target:** One target in range, with an attack roll
- **Effect:** Claws of shadow rise under them: 1 cut wounds (one per Potency), and they're held fast for 1 seconds
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Cut wounds and a hold of a second a Potency, so the next aimed spell can't be dodged. A lost rote, taught only by the Ossuary.

### Grave Edge

*Worked from Shadow Crafting, Death Weaving. Shadow worked into a weapon's edge leaves the cold of the grave in each wound.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Each hit leaves a grave cold: the wounded heal 10% slower for a day. Their weapon glows and every swing and shot leaves a trail
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Every wound the wielder deals heals slower for a day, up to half as fast at Potency 5. Comes with Shadow Crafting's rote.

### Quicken Corpse

*The dead get up and go to work.*

- **Target:** One corpse in sight
- **Effect:** It rises and joins the colony: slow, needs no food or sleep, and hauls, mines, cleans, cuts plants and builds. An animal corpse follows the caster
- **Lasts:** The Duration, then it falls apart
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Raises a corpse as a slow worker: no food, no sleep, hauls, mines, cleans, cuts plants and builds. An animal corpse follows the caster instead. It falls apart when the spell ends. It's an Act of Hubris and your colonists may not like it.

### Quicken Ghost

*The mage feeds a ghost strength.*

- **Target:** One ghost in sight
- **Effect:** It heals 2 wounds (2 per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Heals a ghost: your summoned or revenant ally after a fight.

### Rotting Flesh

*Flesh sloughs and sickens.*

- **Target:** One person in sight, with an attack roll
- **Effect:** 8 blunt damage as spreading rot (8 per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss, and a miss lands within a few cells on whoever is there
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A Death attack as spreading rot. It rolls to hit. The Reach makes the target stink, which puts people off them.

### Sever Soul

*The mage tears a sleeper's soul out of them.*

- **Target:** One Sleeper in sight, or the body of one not yet buried (the soul stays in it until then)
- **Effect:** Their soul comes out: Soulless, then Enervated after a week, then a Thrall after a month. One already Soulless goes straight to Enervated
- **The soul:** It lies at their feet and drifts home in a day unless jarred
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Resolve, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll for any mage)

**How to use it:** Tears out a Sleeper's soul. Without it they slowly fall apart: Soulless, then Enervated after a week, then a Thrall after a month. The soul lies at their feet and drifts back in a day unless you hold it with Soul Jar. It's an Act of Hubris for every mage, whatever their Wisdom. The Reaches skip straight to the later stages.

### Shadow Crafting

*Shadow hardens into a thing that can be held.*

- **Target:** A spot in sight
- **Effect:** A tool, weapon, clothes or furniture of solid shadow appears there
- **Lasts:** The Duration, then it's gone
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Makes a simple item of solid shadow: a tool, weapon, clothes or furniture. No ectoplasm needed, unlike Ectoplasmic Shaping. It's gone when the spell ends.

### Bone Shards

*Worked from Withering, Death Unraveling. A dead body made a bomb of its own bones. A lost rote from the Ossuary.*

- **Target:** One corpse in sight
- **Effect:** It bursts in a spray of bone: 1 piercing wounds (one per Potency) to every enemy within a few cells. The corpse is used up
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Turns a corpse into a bone bomb that hits every enemy round it. A lost rote, taught only by the Ossuary.

### Enervation

*Strength drains out of a limb.*

**Enervation**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Their manipulation drops to 30%: they can barely use their arms
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Stamina, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**Enervation: Legs**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Their moving drops to 30%: they can barely walk
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Stamina, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**How to use it:** Cripples without wounding. Arms: they can barely use their hands, so shooting and fighting suffer. Legs: they can barely walk, which is great for catching someone who runs. The Reach immobilizes them.

### Exorcism

*The mage unmakes a ghost's hold on the world.*

- **Target:** One ghost in sight. With Mind 4 or Spirit 4, Goetia and spirits too
- **Effect:** A manifested ghost fades back into Twilight. One already in Twilight is laid to rest
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Its Rank, each dot takes 1 off the Potency
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Gets rid of a ghost: a manifested one fades back into Twilight, one already there is laid to rest for good. With Mind 4 or Spirit 4 it works on Goetia and spirits too.

### Life Leech

*Worked from Withering, Death Unraveling. The target's life withers, and the caster drinks what's left. A lost rote from the Ossuary.*

- **Target:** One target in range, with an attack roll
- **Effect:** The flesh withers: 1 lethal wounds (one per Potency), and half of what it deals heals the caster's worst wounds. Needs Life 2 as well as Death
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Half the harm it does heals the caster. Needs Life 2. A lost rote, taught only by the Ossuary.

### Revenant

*The mage gives a ghost a body to fight with.*

- **Target:** One ghost in sight. With Mind 4 or Spirit 4, Goetia and spirits too
- **Effect:** It manifests, solid and seen, and fights for the caster
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Gives a ghost a solid body and has it fight for you. Pair it with Ghost Summons. With Mind 4 or Spirit 4 it works on Goetia and spirits too.

### Shadow Flesh

*The mage becomes a shadow on the wall.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Twilight form: no physical harm reaches them and they pass through doors, but they can't harm, work or carry. Spells still work
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Death's version of Twilit Body: no physical harm reaches them and they walk through doors, but they can't hit, work or carry. Spells still work, so a mage can cast safely in a fight.

### Withering

*Flesh dries and dies.*

- **Target:** One person in sight, with an attack roll
- **Effect:** 8 damage as withering (8 per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss, and a miss lands within a few cells on whoever is there
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Death's big attack. It rolls to hit. The Reach makes the wounds aggravated, which heal very slowly.

### Create Anchor

*A thing becomes a ghost's tie to the world.*

- **Target:** One item or building in sight
- **Effect:** It becomes a ghost's anchor, and the nearest ghost moves its haunting there. The ghost guards it, going for the caster's enemies within 5 cells
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Gives a ghost a new anchor, and the nearest ghost moves its haunting there. The ghost guards it and attacks your enemies within 5 cells: a ghostly guard for a gate or a vault.

### Create Avernian Gate

*A well opens down toward the Underworld.*

- **Target:** A spot in sight
- **Effect:** A gate to the Underworld opens. Death spells cast near it roll 2 more dice
- **Ghosts:** One climbs out each day, some of them angry
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Opens a gate to the Underworld. Death spells near it get 2 more dice, but a ghost climbs out every day, and some are angry. Strong for a Death mage, risky for the colony.

### Create Ghost

*The mage shapes a ghost from someone's echo.*

- **Target:** One person or corpse in sight
- **Effect:** A Rank 1 ghost, an echo of them, appears and serves the caster
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Makes a ghost from a person's or a corpse's echo: a Rank 1 servant (Rank 2 with the Reach).

### Deny the Reaper

*The mage turns back the clock on wounds and wear.*

- **Target:** One person or item in sight
- **Effect:** On a person, a lost part or scar from the last 1 months grows back (a month per Potency). An item is mended whole
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Regrows a lost limb or removes a scar from the last few months (a month per Potency), or mends an item completely. A strong healing spell for old injuries.

### Empty Presence

*The world forgets the subject was ever there.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Nobody attacks them until they attack, and others forget what they thought of them
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Nobody attacks the person until they attack first, and others forget what they thought of them. Use it to walk someone out of a fight, or to reset a feud.

### Sever the Awakened Soul

*Even a mage's soul can be torn loose.*

- **Target:** One mage or other supernatural in sight
- **Effect:** As Sever Soul: their soul comes out, and they go Soulless, then Enervated, then a Thrall
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Resolve, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll for any mage)

**How to use it:** Sever Soul for mages and other supernaturals. Their soul comes out and they decline the same way: Soulless, Enervated, then a Thrall. It's an Act of Hubris for every mage.

### Interconnections

*The mage sees the threads of fate that run from a person: who they are bound to, what binds them.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** A letter lists their 1 strongest ties to other people (one per Potency) and every spell on them
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Someone under Fabricate Fortune shows nothing

**How to use it:** A cheap way to learn who matters to a stranger before you deal with them, and to spot lasting spells another mage put on one of your people. Each Potency shows one more link.

### Jinx

*A cantrip of Fate. A small jinx that trips its target.*

- **Target:** One target in range (Gnosis x10 cells), with an attack roll
- **Effect:** A stumble: one bashing wound and a stack of Doom
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A cantrip, only with the Cantrips setting on: one wound from range, for a mage with a single dot of Fate.

### Oaths Fulfilled

*The mage ties a knot in someone's fate and feels it pull when the moment comes.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Pick an event: they're hurt, go down, leave the map, are captured, eat, sleep or break an oath. A letter tells you when it happens, wherever they are
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** A tripwire on one person. Use it on a prisoner you released ("leave the map" tells you when they're gone) or on an enemy leader ("go down" tells you when they fall in a fight you can't see). The letter comes wherever they are, even off the map.

### Quantum Flux

*Small chances line up in the subject's favour, and nothing quite gets in the way.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Luck bends their way in a fight. They feel 15% less pain (15% per Potency), with melee hit +2 and aim +1. A point is about one skill level
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Cast it on your best fighter just before a raid. Its primary factor is Duration, so your Fate dots go into making it last; more Potency costs dice.

### Reading the Outmost Eddies

*A small turn of luck, one way or the other, before the day is out.*

**Reading the Outmost Eddies: Good**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Some time before the spell ends, a small piece of good luck: a few coins turn up, or they get a good feeling. They also get one boon charge
- **Lasts:** Until it happens, at most the Duration
- **Resisted by:** Composure, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**Reading the Outmost Eddies: Bad**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Some time before the spell ends, a small piece of bad luck: they drop their weapon, their food goes bad or they get a bad feeling. They also get one hex charge
- **Lasts:** Until it happens, at most the Duration
- **Resisted by:** Composure, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**How to use it:** A small nudge, not a weapon. Good luck is a pleasant surprise for a colonist; bad luck can make an enemy drop their weapon at the wrong moment. Either way the person also carries one boon or hex charge (see Exceptional Luck). The "within the hour" Reach makes it happen soon instead of some time before the spell ends.

### Serendipity

*The mage names what they need, and the world shows a corner of where to find it.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Name a goal: a recruit, a cure, something hidden on the map, or the strongest enemy's weakness. A letter says where to look
- **Nothing to find:** Chance sends a small gift: a wanderer who asks to join, 1 herbal medicine, 50 silver, or word of who leads the likeliest raiders
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** The small gift comes at most once every 5 days for each goal

**How to use it:** Ask it for what you're stuck on: a doctor when nobody can treat a disease, a recruit with a skill you lack, or how to beat the enemy that keeps winning. When there's nothing out there to find, you still get a small gift. Each goal can only give that small gift once every 5 days.

### Exceptional Luck

*Fortune bends around someone, for them or against them.*

**Exceptional Luck: Boon**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** 1 boon charges (one per Potency). Each charge does one of these when it comes up: a hit on them misses, a thing they make is one quality step better, a surgery they get doesn't fail, or a mental break is skipped
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Composure, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**Exceptional Luck: Hex**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** 1 hex charges (one per Potency). Each charge does one of these when it comes up: a hit on them does double damage, or a thing they make is one quality step worse
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Composure, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**How to use it:** Boon is a spare life for a colonist: each charge turns one hit into a miss, or saves a surgery that would have gone wrong. Hex is the reverse on an enemy. Charges sit until they're used or the spell ends, so cast it before the fight, not during.

### Fabricate Fortune

*A false fate laid over the true one.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Other mages' Fate sight reads nothing on them, and spell triggers set on them don't go off
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Protection against other mages, not against bullets. Cast it on someone a rival mage is likely to spy on or curse with a trigger.

### Fools Rush In

*The subject goes in blind and somehow does fine.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Every skill they're able to use counts as at least 4, and their first talk with someone new gets +1 dice
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Turns anyone into a passable everything: a cook who can't doctor can patch a wound, a shooter can plant crops. It doesn't lower skills that are already above 4. Good on a new recruit, or on whoever has to talk to a stranger.

### Lucky Number

*The right code, the right combination, the first time.*

- **Target:** One building in sight
- **Effect:** Opens or unlocks the building, or else improves the caster's prices
- **Hackable:** Hacked at once
- **Casket or crate:** Opens, if something is inside
- **Comms console:** Call anyone you pick, with +2 goodwill for a faction (2 per Potency). It needs power
- **Anything else:** The caster trades at 2% better prices for a day
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Skips the hacking job at ancient complexes. It also opens a cryptosleep casket from across the room, so whatever is inside wakes up next to it, not next to you. The console has to have power. With nothing to open, aim it at any building before a trader arrives to get the price bonus.

### Shifting the Odds

*The mage leans on chance until what they want walks through the door.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Pick what comes within a day: a wanderer who wants to join, a trade caravan, a few farm animals, a crashed pod of goods, better prices for a day, or fighters from the friendliest faction for the next attack that day
- **Lucky seam:** Or, for a day, plain rock mined on this map gives ore 5% of the time (5% per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Order up what the colony is short of. Out of food: farm animals. Short of hands: a wanderer. Need to sell: a caravan. Expecting a raid: allies. The lucky seam is good on a map with plenty of plain rock to mine. With the "within the hour" Reach it comes the same hour.

### Warding Gesture

*A sign against ill fortune.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Another mage's Fate spell on them works only if that mage wins a Clash of Wills (a contest of the two casters' power). Melee dodge +5 besides
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Mostly a shield against enemy mages who use Fate. The dodge bonus is a small extra for anyone who has to fight hand to hand.

### Doom Mark

*Worked from Monkey's Paw, Fate Weaving. A weapon marked to bring bad luck to whoever it strikes.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Each hit lays a stack of Doom on the target: -5% aim and dodge a stack, and at three something goes wrong for them. Their weapon glows and every swing and shot leaves a trail
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Every hit lays a stack of Doom. At three, the target's gun jams or they trip, and the stacks clear. Comes with Monkey's Paw's rote.

### Grave Misfortune

*Every scratch the subject takes goes deeper than it should.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** The next 1 times they're hurt (one per Potency), each wound is 1 wounds worse
- **Lasts:** Until the hits are used, at most the Duration
- **Resisted by:** Composure, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**How to use it:** Cast it on the toughest enemy before your shooters open up. At Potency 3 the next 3 hits each count as 3 wounds. It ends early once the hits are used up.

### Monkey's Paw

*A blessing or a curse laid on a thing.*

**Monkey's Paw: Bless**

- **Target:** One item in sight
- **Effect:** It's 1 quality steps better (one per Potency), up to legendary
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**Monkey's Paw: Curse**

- **Target:** One item in sight
- **Effect:** It's 1 quality steps worse (one per Potency), down to awful
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Bless a weapon or armour before a fight, or a piece of art before you sell it to a trader. Curse an enemy's gear you can see. The item goes back to its real quality when the spell ends.

### Shared Fate

*Two fates tied in one knot.*

- **Target:** One person by touch, and the caster
- **Effect:** Every wound either of them takes, the other takes too
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Dangerous both ways: any wound on either of you lands on the other too. Cast it on an enemy you want to stop, then let a tough ally take the hits, or take the "one-way" Reach so only the caster's wounds pass across and the enemy's don't come back to you.

### Superlative Luck

*For a little while, the subject cannot do anything badly.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Their next 1 key moments (one per Potency) go as well as they can: a thing they make comes out at a master's quality, and a surgery they do can't fail
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Costs 1 Mana

**How to use it:** Save it for the moment that matters: crafting a weapon from rare materials, or surgery on someone you can't afford to lose. Each key moment uses one charge. It costs 1 Mana on top of the cast.

### Sworn Oaths

*Fate witnesses a promise. Keeping it brings luck; breaking it brings the curse that was named.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** They swear an oath. A prisoner swears never to attack the colony and is set free. A guest swears their faction keeps the peace (a vampire swears to pay the boons it owes). A colonist vows not to drink, take drugs, fight or leave
- **Kept to the end:** They get a boon
- **Broken:** A hex and a letter. A faction that breaks the peace loses goodwill with everyone
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** The best use is on prisoners you don't want to keep or kill: they swear never to attack you and walk free. A guest's oath ties their whole faction to the peace. A colonist's vow helps fight an addiction. Keep the Duration long: the boon only comes if the oath holds to the end.

### Atonement

*Every curse has a way out. The mage finds it.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Pick a curse on them of strength 1 or less: a hex, a Paradox Condition, another mage's spell or another kind's curse. Doing a task lifts it
- **Task:** Silver for a small curse, a journey of 10 tiles for a middling one, 25 tiles for a great one
- **Lasts:** Until the task is done

**How to use it:** A cure for curses you can't dispel. It doesn't lift the curse at once: it gives the person a task, and the curse lifts when the task is done. The stronger the curse, the harder the task.

### Chaos Mastery

*Unlikely things happen, all at once, to the wrong people.*

- **Target:** Up to 1 enemies (one per Potency) in an area in sight, the Scale
- **Effect:** Pick what goes wrong for them: their guns jam, a seizure, visions, a stumble, or every fire there goes out
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A crowd stopper for a raid. Jammed guns stop a firing line; putting out every fire saves a burning base. Mind the Paradox: anyone who isn't Awakened and sees it makes it riskier.

### Divine Intervention

*Fate pushes someone toward a goal, or away from it.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Goad or ban one of these: drinking, drugs, fighting or leaving home
- **Goaded:** Hexed each day they don't do it
- **Banned:** Hexed each time they do it
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Resolve, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris: a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher

**How to use it:** Steer someone's habits. Ban drugs on an addict, ban fighting on a hothead, or goad a lazy colonist to fight. Casting it on a person is an Act of Hubris for a wise mage, so expect a Wisdom roll.

### Sever Oaths

*A knot in fate cut through.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Pick a Fate working on them: a boon or hex, an oath, a trigger, Shared Fate or an Atonement. It ends if its strength is 1 or less
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** The answer to other mages' Fate magic on your people, and a way out of your own oaths. Only works if the working's strength is no more than this spell's Potency.

### Strings of Fate

*The mage pulls a string, and something happens to someone, soon.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Pick an event for them: a wanderer joins, a caravan comes, animals wander in, goods fall from the sky, a quest is offered, no raid comes for 1 days (a day per Potency), a prisoner gives in, an accident, an illness, or a lucky seam in the rock for a day
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Shifting the Odds for someone else, with harm on the list. Give a colonist a quest or a quiet stretch with no raids, or give an enemy an accident or an illness. "A prisoner gives in" is a fast way to recruit.

### Forge Destiny

*The mage writes a destiny.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** For each Potency (1), choose one: take up a new Obsession, draw her Aspirations anew, raise or lower a Merit by a dot, or gain a dot of Destiny with its Doom (enemies come within days)
- **Lasts:** A Merit dot for the Duration, the rest for good

**How to use it:** Rewrites the mage herself. Changed Obsessions and Aspirations last for good. A Merit dot only lasts as long as the spell. A dot of Destiny is powerful but brings its Doom: enemies come for her within days, so be ready.

### Miracle

*Small miracles, ready to hand.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** 1 miracles (one per Potency), each used from a button on the caster: an enemy trips, a downed friend stops bleeding, or a friend on their feet gets a boon
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Saved-up help for a fight. Cast it before a raid, then use the buttons when you need them: trip the enemy who's about to reach your shooters, or stop a downed friend from bleeding out.

### Pariah

*The whole world turns its back on someone.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Their social impact drops 10% (10% per Potency) and everyone thinks less of them. Whoever tends them gets a hex, and whoever harms them gets a boon
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris: a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher

**How to use it:** Social exile. Use it on a rival faction's speaker before talks go badly for you, or on a prisoner nobody should get attached to. Be careful with your own people: anyone who looks after them gets hexed. It's an Act of Hubris.

### Swarm of Locusts

*A plague out of an old story.*

- **Target:** An area in sight, the Scale
- **Effect:** Pick a plague: locusts eat the crops, frogs or blood rain down, an eclipse, or the wild animals there stampede off the map and trample anyone in the way. Sleepers there are shaken
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A biblical plague on an area. Locusts on an enemy's fields, a stampede to clear wild animals, an eclipse for vampire friends. It's an Act of Hubris and obvious magic, so cast it where few outsiders can see.

### Coaxing the Spirits

*The spirit of a thing wakes and does one thing that fits it.*

- **Target:** One animal, person or door in sight
- **Effect:** An animal bites or bolts, a person drops their weapon, or a door swings open or shut
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Composure for a person or animal, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A one-dot trick with several uses: make an enemy drop their gun, make a war animal turn on its handler or run, or slam a door in a raider's face from across the room.

### Exorcist's Eye

*The mage sees the spirits that share the world.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Spirits around the caster show. With Death 1, ghosts too; with Mind 1, Goetia
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Lets the mage see spirits that are hiding in Twilight. With Death 1 it shows ghosts, with Mind 1 Goetia. Cast it first when something invisible is hurting your people.

### Gremlins

*A machine's spirit sulks.*

- **Target:** One machine or mechanoid in sight
- **Effect:** It breaks down or switches itself off
- **Powered building:** It shorts out as well, starting a fire or blowing its batteries
- **Mechanoid:** It turns on its own side, berserk for 10 seconds (10 per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Breaks a machine or a mechanoid. On a building that's on a live power net it also shorts out, which can start a fire or blow batteries, so don't aim it at your own grid. A mechanoid goes berserk and attacks its own side for a while.

### Invoke Bane

**How to use it:** Keeps spirits out of an area: ones of Rank 5 or less flee and won't come in. Fomori (Wyrm-possessed people) burn while they stand in it. A good ward for a bedroom or a hospital.

- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss, and a miss lands within a few cells on whoever is there
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Spirit's big attack. It rolls to hit and hits spirits in Twilight too. Its Reaches add aggravated wounds, burn the target's fuel, or leave them open to spirits for a day.

### Shape Spirit

*A spirit becomes something else.*

- **Target:** One spirit in sight
- **Effect:** It becomes another kind of spirit, picked from a list, whole again
- **Lasts:** For good
- **Resisted by:** Its Rank, each dot takes 1 off the Potency

**How to use it:** Turns a spirit into another kind, fully healed. Make a dangerous spirit into a harmless one, or a weak one into a kind with a better Charm.

### Twilit Body

*The mage steps halfway out of the world.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Twilight form: no physical harm reaches them and they pass through doors, but they can't harm, work or carry. Spells still work
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** The person steps into Twilight: no physical harm can touch them and they walk through doors, but they can't hit, work or carry anything. They can still cast spells, so it's a safe way for a mage to cast in a fight.

### World Walker

*The mage carries someone across the Gauntlet.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They cross into the Penumbra, or back out. A spirit brought out stays in Twilight
- **Without Garou:** They cross into a small pocket of the Shadow instead, with a spirit or two in it
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** The Gauntlet, each point takes 1 off the Potency
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Sends one person across into the Penumbra, or brings them back. A spirit you pull out stays in Twilight. Without the Garou mod it leads to a small pocket of the Shadow.

### Annihilate Spirit

*The mage unmakes a spirit for good.*

- **Target:** One spirit in sight
- **Effect:** It's destroyed and never comes back
- **Lasts:** For good
- **Resisted by:** Its Rank, each dot takes 1 off the Potency
- **Watch out:** Unmaking one that meant no harm is an Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Destroys a spirit for good. Use it on a bane or a spirit that keeps coming back. Destroying one that meant no harm is an Act of Hubris.

### Birth Spirit

*A new spirit wakes out of quiet Essence.*

- **Target:** A spot in sight
- **Effect:** A Rank 1 spirit of a kind the caster picks appears, grateful and asking nothing
- **Servitor:** Or a made spirit in Twilight over the room at the spot (or the Scale's area outdoors) with one task: sweeping, keeping watch, keeping the room warm or cool, tending plants, mending things or giving light
- **Indefinite:** Cast Indefinite (Advanced Duration, slider at the end), it lasts for good. It shows under Spirit sight as a small light, and a servitor sends a letter on what it did every 30 days
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Makes a new spirit. Pick a Rank 1 spirit of any kind, or a servitor with one job over a room: sweeping, keeping watch, keeping it warm or cool, tending plants, mending things or giving light. To keep a servitor for good, cast it Indefinite (Advanced Duration, slider at the end). It sends a report every 30 days.

### Create Locus

*A place becomes a well of spirit.*

- **Target:** An area in sight, the Scale
- **Effect:** It becomes a Locus: the Gauntlet there is 1, it counts as a Resonant place for Channel Essence, and spirits in it heal
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Makes an area into a Locus: the Gauntlet is thin, spirits heal there, and it counts for Channel Essence. Make one at home and your mages can draw Mana there every day.

### Essence Fountain

*Essence wells up out of nothing.*

- **Target:** A mage or spirit, by touch
- **Effect:** 1 Mana (one per Potency) for a mage, or that much healing for a spirit, made from nothing
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Mana from nothing, anywhere. Top up a mage in a fight, or heal a spirit.

### Spirit Manse

*The mage makes a home in the Shadow.*

- **Target:** A spot in sight
- **Effect:** A small world of the caster's own opens in the Shadow, with a door at the spot that leads in
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** It needs no Garou. Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A private realm in the Shadow with a door at the spot. It works without the Garou mod. The Reaches add an Iris back to the map and a Key for it.

### Divination

*The mage looks a little way down the likeliest road.*

- **Target:** The week ahead, and one person in sight
- **Effect:** A letter with 1 answers (one per Potency): is a raid coming, is a trader coming, and will the person join, die or break
- **Afterwards:** For a week, the caster feels the next raid a few hours before it comes
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** A cheap early warning. Ask about the coming week, and the caster also senses the next raid a few hours ahead for a week. Aim it at a visitor you're thinking of recruiting to learn whether they'll join, or at a sick colonist to learn whether they'll die.

### Green Light / Red Light

*Everything happens on time for someone, or nothing does.*

**Green Light**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They move and work 10% faster (10% per Potency)
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**Red Light**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They move and work 10% slower (10% per Potency), down to half speed
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Green Light speeds up a builder, a hauler or someone running from danger. Red Light slows an enemy (down to half speed at most). Both work on anyone in sight with no roll to resist.

### Momentary Flux

*Will this turn out well? The mage feels the answer.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They show green if they'll be good for the caster, red if not: a hidden enemy, a carrier of a deadly illness, a cruel nature or a grudge
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** A quick yes or no on a stranger before you let them in. Red means trouble of some kind (a hidden enemy, a deadly illness, a cruel streak or a grudge) without saying which.

### Perfect Timing

*The mage sees the right moment coming and is ready for it.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Melee hit +2, aim +1 and social impact +5% (5% per Potency). A point is about one skill level
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** A self buff for a mage who fights or negotiates. Duration is the primary factor, so it lasts a long time for little effort.

### Postcognition

*The mage looks back down the road something has come.*

- **Target:** One person, corpse or item in sight
- **Effect:** A letter with 3 lines (3 per Potency) of what happened to it lately: fights, talks and tales
- **Case clues:** Clues and signs of monsters passing where it lies are read with 1 successes
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** The detective's spell. Read a corpse to learn who killed it, or an item to learn where it's been. Cast it where a monster walked to find case clues; each Potency is one success on the clue roll.

### Tick

*A cantrip of Time. A second stolen from the target.*

- **Target:** One target in range (Gnosis x10 cells), with an attack roll
- **Effect:** A skipped second: one bashing wound, and -10% speed for a second
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A cantrip, only with the Cantrips setting on: one wound from range, for a mage with a single dot of Time.

### Choose the Thread

*The mage sees two ways a moment could go, and picks.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **On a friend:** The next thing they make is rolled twice and the better result kept, or their next surgery can't fail
- **On anyone else:** A hex charge
- **Lasts:** Until used, at most the Duration
- **Resisted by:** Resolve if they aren't the caster's own, each dot takes 1 off the Potency

**How to use it:** On a friend, it's a safety net for one craft or one surgery. On anyone else, it lays a hex charge. It waits until it's used.

### Constant Presence

*Whatever happens to time, the subject stays as they are.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** If Corridors of Time turns the world back, they keep the skills they had. Stuns on them last half as long
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Mostly a partner for Corridors of Time: whoever has it keeps the skills they learned when the world is turned back. Halving stuns is useful in a fight against stunning weapons.

### Hung Spell

*Spells held in a moment that hasn't come yet.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Their next 1 spells (one per Potency, 1 Mana each) don't go off yet. They all go off when it ends, or when it's let go from its button
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Store spells now, fire them all at once later. Cast Hung Spell, then cast your next spells (each costs 1 Mana more); they wait instead of going off. Let them go from the button, or they go off when the spell ends. Waiting on the ground for an enemy is Spell Trap.

### Shield of Chronos

*The subject's past and future close like a book.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** Another mage's Time spell on them works only if that mage wins a Clash of Wills (a contest of the two casters' power)
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Protection against enemy mages who use Time on your people. Does nothing against mundane harm.

### Spell Trap

*Worked from Hung Spell, Time Ruling. Held spells wait on the ground until an enemy steps there.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Their next 1 spells (one per Potency, 1 Mana each) wait where the first was aimed, and go off when an enemy steps there; the others land on that enemy
- **Needs:** Fate 2 as well
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Hung Spell with its spells waiting on the spot the first one was aimed at; they go off on the first enemy who steps there. Needs Fate 2 as well.

### Tipping the Hourglass

*The subject acts first, or last.*

**Tipping the Hourglass: First**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They stop waiting: any aim or cooldown holding them ends, and they act now
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Resolve, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**Tipping the Hourglass: Last**

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They lose 1 seconds (a second per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Resolve, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist

**How to use it:** First lets a friend shoot right now instead of aiming, or skip a cooldown. Last makes an enemy lose a few seconds, often enough to get away or land the first blow.

### Veil of Moments

*Time stops for the subject's ills.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** No bleeding; illnesses and poisons freeze where they are and no new ones take hold; no mental breaks. No healing either
- **Lasts:** The Duration

**How to use it:** Stops someone getting worse while you find a doctor or a cure. They don't bleed, their illness doesn't advance and they can't break, but they don't heal either, so end it once they're being treated. The Reaches give back a Willpower or a Mana.

### Acceleration

*The subject moves through the world as a blur.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** They move 50% faster (50% per Potency), aim and attack 20% quicker (20% per Potency), and get melee dodge +5 (5 points per Potency; the Nimble trait gives 15)
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Costs 1 Mana. Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Turns a fighter into a blur: faster on foot, faster to shoot and hard to hit in melee. It's obvious, so cast it where outsiders can't see. For work, cast Tireless Work.

### Chronos' Curse

*The subject crawls through a thicker kind of time.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They move at half to a sixth their speed, aim and attack 20% slower (20% per Potency), and melee dodge -5 (5 points per Potency)
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Stamina, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist
- **Watch out:** Costs 1 Mana. Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** The strongest slow in the game. At high Potency the target barely moves. Good for pinning down a melee enemy while your shooters work. Take the "no dodging" Reach against a dodgy fighter.

### Flurry

*Worked from Acceleration, Time Perfecting. Time between blows taken away. A lost rote from the Clockmaker's Heresy.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** 1 of their melee blows (one per Potency) come with no pause between them
- **Lasts:** The Duration, or until the blows are spent

**How to use it:** A Potency of melee blows with no pause between them. A lost rote, taught only by the Clockmaker's Heresy.

### Rust

*Worked from Weight of Years, Time Perfecting. Years laid on what an enemy carries. A lost rote from the Clockmaker's Heresy.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Years pass for what they carry: their weapon and armour lose 10% of their condition (10% per Potency), and the weapon comes apart in their hands: a wound for every 2 Potency
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Wears down a target's weapon and armour without a wound. A lost rote, taught only by the Clockmaker's Heresy.

### Shifting Sands

*A few seconds of the subject's life run backward.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They go back 3 seconds (3 per Potency): back where they stood, with their cooldowns and stance as they were. Spells cast on them since are gone. Wounds stay
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Undoes the last few seconds for one person. Use it to pull a friend back from where they stepped into danger, or to strip the spells an enemy mage just put on someone. Wounds aren't undone. The Reach turns the whole world back an hour or more, to a Corridors of Time snapshot.

### Temporal Summoning

*The subject's earlier self, called up to stand in its place.*

- **Target:** One person or item in sight
- **Effect:** It becomes its earlier self. A person loses the wounds and ills of the last hour to the last week; an item stands whole
- **Lasts:** The Duration, then the present comes back, with whatever happened meanwhile
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Temporary healing. A badly hurt friend becomes their unhurt earlier self, fit to fight; when the spell ends the wounds come back, plus anything they took meanwhile. On an item, it stands whole again for the Duration. Have a doctor ready for when it ends.

### Tireless Work

*Worked from Acceleration, Time Perfecting. Hands move many times as fast at their work.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** They work many times as fast, move 50% faster (50% per Potency), aim and attack 20% quicker (20% per Potency), and get melee dodge +5 (5 points per Potency)
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Costs 1 Mana. Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Someone works many times as fast for the Duration: a builder finishes a wall in moments. A chore spell, and obvious.

### Weight of Years

*Years fall on something all at once.*

- **Target:** One item or living body in sight
- **Effect:** An item loses 10% of its hit points (10% per Potency); a body takes 1 aching wounds (one per Potency)
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** The undead are immune. Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Ages a thing or a body in an instant. Good for knocking down a wall or a door, or wearing down a living enemy. The undead don't age, so it does nothing to vampires and ghosts.

### Present as Past

*The mage sees what the enemy will do before they do it.*

- **Target:** The caster
- **Effect:** Every enemy near them shows its next target (red) and where it's headed (orange). The caster gets melee dodge +2.5 and aim +1. A point is about one skill level
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Watch out:** Costs 1 Mana

**How to use it:** A battle sense. You see who every nearby enemy will attack next and where they're going, so you can pull the target back or set an ambush. It costs 1 Mana.

### Prophecy

*The mage reads the road ahead in full.*

- **Target:** The week ahead, and one person in sight
- **Effect:** A letter with 1 detailed answers (one per Potency): the day, faction and size of a coming raid, when a trader comes, a prisoner's resistance, or what's killing someone
- **Afterwards:** For a week, the caster feels the next raid a few hours before it comes, and her next talk with the person gets +1 dice
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Divination in full detail: the day, faction and size of the next raid, so you can prepare for exactly that. It also gives dice for the next talk with the person you aimed it at.

### Rend Lifespan

*Parts of the body age in an instant while others go young.*

- **Target:** One target in range, with an attack roll
- **Effect:** 8 crushing damage (8 per Potency), as parts of the body age and others go young
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Resisted by:** Nothing, but it can miss, and a miss lands within a few cells on whoever is there
- **Watch out:** The undead are immune. Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A Time attack spell. It rolls to hit like a gun, so a miss can hit someone near the target, friend or foe. The "aggravated" Reach makes the wounds much harder to heal. It does nothing to the undead.

### Rewrite History

*The subject has always been someone else.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** Pick another past (backstory) for them, with the work it rules out
- **Vampire or werewolf:** They lose their strongest power instead (a Discipline's top dot, a Gift). Its rank Withstands the spell
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Resolve, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist
- **Watch out:** An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Changes who someone has been. Give a colonist a past that suits the work you need, or give an enemy one that rules out fighting. On a vampire or werewolf it strips their strongest power instead. It's an Act of Hubris.

### Temporal Stutter

*The subject skips forward in time and is simply gone.*

- **Target:** One person in sight
- **Effect:** They vanish from time, then come back where they stood
- **Lasts:** The Duration
- **Resisted by:** Stamina, each dot takes 1 off the Potency. The caster's own people don't resist
- **Watch out:** Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Takes one enemy out of the fight for the Duration. They come back exactly where they stood, so be ready when they do.

### Blink of an Eye

*Hours of work done in a moment.*

- **Target:** One person in sight, while they work
- **Effect:** 4 hours of their job (4 per Potency) are done at once, on a bill, a building or research
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Magic for a chore (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Enlightened). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** Finishes a job in a moment: a long build, a research project, a big crafting bill. The person has to be working on it when you cast. It's magic for a chore, which costs an Enlightened mage a Wisdom roll.

### Corridors of Time

*The mage sends someone back down the road, knowing what lies ahead.*

- **Target:** The world, through one person touched
- **Effect:** The world goes back to a snapshot from up to 1 days ago (a day per Potency), and that person keeps a letter of what they saw
- **Lasts:** Instant
- **Watch out:** Snapshots are only kept while a colony mage with Time 3 knows this spell (see the settings). An Act of Hubris (a Wisdom roll if the caster's Wisdom is Understanding or higher). Obvious: each outsider who sees it adds a Paradox die

**How to use it:** A save-scum in the story. The world goes back to a snapshot from a few days ago, and the person you touched keeps a letter of what happened, so you can avoid it this time. Snapshots are only taken while a colony mage with Time 3 knows this spell, so learn it before you need it. It's an Act of Hubris.

### Temporal Pocket

*The world stops for one person, and they get hours to themselves.*

- **Target:** One person, by touch
- **Effect:** The world stops for them for 2 hours for each step of Duration. They come out having researched or studied a skill for that long, and just as tired
- **Lasts:** Instant

**How to use it:** Time to study, for free. The person steps out of time and comes back having researched or trained a skill for hours, while no time passed for anyone else. They come out as tired as if they'd worked those hours, so let them rest.
