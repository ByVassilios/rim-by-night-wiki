# What changed

What each update changed, newest first, as the grimoire shows it.

## Update of 14 November 2026

### Courts
- Visiting got its own buttons: a caravan on a friendly town gets Enter town, and anyone in a friendly town can be talked to. Every court house has a guest crypt your vampires can sleep in while they walk the town.
- Courts ask for help against the other kinds the colony knows, such as a Garou pack hunting in the domain.
- A court can ask nine kinds of favour, about one letter every ten days, each with its own setting. Three life boons come from saving a court's vampire caught by the dawn.
- Each vampire of the thirteen clans has a rank in its clan, and the clans ask things of their own.
- At sect standing 40, 60 and 80 the colony can host the sect's gatherings.
### The blood market
- Vials, Kindred vitae and hemogen have one price that moves with hunter raids, blood hunts, the Scourge and a slipping Masquerade. Selling vials brings it down.
- A court window gets a Trade page with the prices, what your prisoners are worth, and ransoms.
### The Kindred club
- Paint a Kindred club and put a tavern counter in it: a friendly court's Kindred come after dusk, buy vials and feed on your herd, and their talk sometimes turns into a letter.
### Powers
- Taste of Death reaches the Garou's spirits; Phantom Speaker and Brother's Blood reach the whole map.
- Mage Blood can learn all four blood sorceries.
- See [[RBN_Changes_20261114|the grimoire's own changes]].
