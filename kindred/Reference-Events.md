# Every event

Every event the storyteller can send, what it means and what to do about it. Most can be switched off or made rarer one by one in the mod settings.

| Event | Kind | From day | Again after | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A blood vial order** | Event |  |  | A court's courier waits some days for blood vials of a set grade from your storage. A trivial boon for ordinary blood, minor for fine. If it leaves without them, the court thinks less of you. |
| **A chosen vessel** | Event |  |  | A court's vampire wants to drink once from one particular colonist, within the safe line, for a minor boon. Refuse and it may come anyway some night and feed if it finds them alone. |
| **A contract from Alamut** | Event |  |  | Alamut offers the clan's vampire a contract, as a mission. |
| **A duel** | Event |  |  | A court's vampire challenges one of yours to a duel at the colony, to the first one down. A win raises your standing at that court; refusing costs a little. |
| **A family errand** | Event |  |  | The Giovanni family asks the clan's vampire for a human corpse from storage. |
| **A fleshcraft client** | Event |  |  | A vampire who heard of your Tzimisce comes to be reshaped. Your fleshcrafter right-clicks it once to do the work; it pays with a minor boon or something else you'd rather have. A Sabbat client pays silver. |
| **A gathering to host: Barons' summit** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you an Anarch barons' summit to host, once that tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Blood Bath** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Sabbat Blood Bath to host, once that tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Brujah rant** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Brujah rant to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Conclave** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Conclave to host, once that tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Gangrel convocation** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Gangrel convocation to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Inner Circle's envoys** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a visit from the Inner Circle's envoys to host, once that tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Lasombra court of judgment** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Lasombra court of judgment to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Movement rant** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you an Anarch Movement rant to host, once that tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Palla Grande** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Palla Grande to host, once that tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Primogen gathering** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Primogen gathering to host, once that gathering tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Toreador salon** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Toreador salon to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: Ventrue oath-taking** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Ventrue oath-taking to host. At most once a year. |
| **A gathering to host: war party** | Event |  | 30 days | The letter that offers you a Sabbat war party to host, once that tier opens. At most once a year. |
| **A Giovanni buyer** | Event |  |  | A Giovanni courier waits some days for human corpses from your storage and pays well over their worth. |
| **A kennel order** | Event |  |  | When a colony vampire is Gangrel or knows Animalism, a court member orders a guard-trained animal from your kennel, sometimes a ghoul. It waits some days and takes the first fitting animal at home, then offers a minor boon (major for a ghoul). |
| **A lair wanted** | Event |  |  | A Tzimisce asks for a dark room to shape into a lair for a season. Anyone who looks in may see too much. It leaves a piece of living furniture behind and owes a minor boon. |
| **A Path's mentor** | Event |  |  | A follower of a Path of Enlightenment comes for a colony vampire fallen to Humanity 3 or lower and offers to teach it. The study takes a season and ends with a deed a mortal would call an atrocity. |
| **A patron's errand** | Event |  |  | A Lasombra patron sends the clan's vampire on an errand, as a mission. |
| **A request to be taught** | Event |  |  | A court vampire asks a colony vampire who has mastered a Discipline to teach it: 3, 6 or 10 lessons, one a night, for a trivial, minor or major boon. Teaching Thaumaturgy outside clan Tremere costs Camarilla standing. |
| **A revenant offered** | Event |  | 30 days | A revenant family, through a court that sponsors it, asks you to take in one of its young. A revenant makes its own vitae and knows its family's Disciplines. A Sabbat family's offer pays in Sabbat standing. |
| **A scholar asks to read** | Event |  |  | A scholar of a court asks to read in the colony's library for 2 to 5 nights. It needs a dark bed and feeds like any guest, then owes a boon sized by the library. A greedy one may try to slip a rare book out on the last night. |
| **A temple for the cult** | Event |  |  | A few Setite cultists come to worship for two days around the finest piece of art indoors, the colony's temple. |
| **A trade of secrets** | Event |  |  | A Nosferatu brood offers a trade of secrets: tell what you know of one court (it may hear and think less of you) and learn where the nearest court you didn't know about is. |
| **A vessel order** | Event |  |  | A court's courier waits some days for a living vessel of one kind from your herd. Tick Herd on a prisoner or slave who fits and it takes them, owing a minor boon. If it leaves without one, the court thinks less of you. |
| **A vizier's studies** | Event |  |  | An Assamite vizier comes to study. It is content if your lore makes a library. |
| **A voivode's guest** | Event |  |  | A Tzimisce voivode's guest comes to see your fleshcraft: living furniture, flesh armour worn, or a vampire with Vicissitude. |
| **A warren wanted** | Event |  |  | A Nosferatu asks for a dark room dug under a mountain for a season. While it stays, news from every court reaches you at once. It owes a boon when it leaves. |
| **An oath of fealty** | Event |  |  | A Ventrue elder asks the clan's vampire to swear fealty. Sworn, the colony owes the elder a trivial boon and the elder owes it a minor one for its protection. |
| **Anarch gang** | Big threat |  | 6 days | An Anarch gang raids when your standing with the Anarchs is very low or they hunt the colony. |
| **Anarch unrest** | Event |  |  | In a Camarilla colony, a vampire who resents the Prince starts stirring the others up. You answer in the letter. At most once a season. |
| **Anathema** | Big threat |  | 20 days | One of the Red List's Anathema passes near the colony with its retinue. One that has fought you before comes stalking; any other just walks through, unless you harm it or use a power on it. |
| **Anonymous benefactor** | Event |  | 30 days | The first gift from an anonymous benefactor. More follow over time. Rare, at most twice a game. |
| **Archons** | Big threat |  | 6 days | Camarilla archons raid when your standing with the Camarilla is very low, and much more often while it hunts the colony. |
| **Assamite assassin** | Threat |  |  | Someone has paid the Assamites to kill one of your vampires. One assassin comes alone by night and waits for the right moment. Likelier when a colony vampire has a named rival or a blood hunt called on them. |
| **Backing a Brujah cause** | Event |  |  | A Brujah elder asks the clan's vampire for silver for a cause, from storage. Pays in clan regard. |
| **Blood for the chantry** | Event |  |  | The chantry asks the clan's vampire for blood vials from storage. |
| **Blood hounds** | Threat |  | 6 days | The nearest Camarilla court's hounds come asking after a vampire under blood hunt that you shelter. Hand them over for a boon and standing, let them search, or turn them away. |
| **Carrying the Gangrel's news** | Event |  |  | A Gangrel elder asks the clan's vampire to carry news to a far court, as a mission. |
| **Caught by the dawn** | Event |  | 60 days | A court's vampire on foot, burned and hungry, an hour or two before sunrise. Get it indoors in the dark before dawn and it owes you a life boon; leave it and it dies in sight of the colony. |
| **Commission a masterwork** | Event |  |  | A Toreador wants art, or a Ventrue a weapon, of excellent quality or better from your storage while it waits. Excellent earns a trivial boon, masterwork a minor one, legendary a major one. |
| **Court craftsman** | Event |  |  | A court's Tzimisce or Gangrel comes by night to do a service you bought on the Boons page: fleshcraft on a colonist, or a kennel beast handed over. Never sent by the storyteller. |
| **Elder vampire** | Big threat |  |  | One elder vampire and its ghouls, worth a whole raid. Wounds that would put a younger vampire in torpor barely slow it, though fire and the sun still work. An elder that got away last time comes back first, rested and with more ghouls. |
| **Feed from your herd** | Event |  |  | A vampire who lost its herd asks to feed on the colony's vessels for a few nights, for a trivial boon (a minor one for five nights). Needs a prisoner or enough colonists. |
| **Foster a childe** | Event |  |  | A sire asks you to take its new childe for a season. Any of your vampires can give it a lesson once a night; 4, 8 or 12 lessons earn a trivial, minor or major boon. |
| **Ghoul dealer caravan** | Visitors |  | 8 days | Ghoul dealers come to trade in blood and people. Dealing with them gets talked about, and now and then hunters follow. |
| **Giovanni extortion** | Event |  |  | With a tavern or club to squeeze, a Giovanni wants silver each season to keep the dead quiet. Pay from storage, or refuse: the restless dead come within days, but the Prince you tell owes you a minor boon. |
| **Haunting** | Event |  | 20 days | Someone killed cruelly on the map comes back as a ghost and haunts where they died for ten days, frightening everyone near, your own people included, until it fades or a necromancer banishes it. Rare, and only where Kindred live. |
| **Hosting a seer** | Event |  |  | A Malkavian seer stays as a guest. On arrival it names any hunter's plant or outside vampire's thrall among your colonists. |
| **Hunter infiltrator** | Event |  |  | A stranger asks to join and the letter reads like any joiner's. It is a Society hunter who learns the colony for a few days, then goes for a vampire asleep by day with a stake. From the Suspected breach level. |
| **Hunter raid** | Big threat |  |  | A daylight raid on a colony whose Masquerade has failed: small hunter bands at Exposed, the hunting order in force at Shattered. They go straight for any vampire they've been told about and don't give up while the sun is up. Takes the place of a normal raid. |
| **Hunting a renegade** | Event |  |  | The chantry asks the clan's vampire to hunt down a Tremere renegade, as a mission. |
| **Judging a Ventrue dispute** | Event |  |  | A Ventrue elder asks the clan's vampire to judge a dispute at a far court, as a mission. |
| **Keep a torpid elder** | Event |  |  | A court asks you to keep one of its elders in a coffin through a season of torpor. Its enemies may come for it once. Woken and gone at the end, it owes a major boon. |
| **Keeping the family's ghost** | Event |  |  | A Giovanni asks you to keep a ghost the family wants guarded for a while. Pays when the guests leave content. |
| **Lend a ghoul** | Event |  |  | A court asks to borrow one of your skilled ghouls for a season. It leaves at once and walks back in when the time is up, and the court owes a minor boon. |
| **Neutral ground** | Event |  |  | Two feuding vampires of two courts ask to settle their quarrel in your best hall tonight. If both leave unharmed before dawn, each owes a trivial boon. |
| **Notes for the chantry** | Event |  |  | The chantry asks the clan's vampire for a treatise from storage. |
| **Occult trader caravan** | Visitors |  | 10 days | A trade caravan carrying the occult trader's stock: lore, relics, blood and sorcery supplies. Only to colonies with Kindred, about a quarter as often as ordinary caravans. |
| **Occultists** | Event |  |  | Mortal occultists ask your sorcerer to work a ritual for them, at a cost in blood. They pay in silver, sometimes in an artifact, and one may stay on wanting the blood. Turned away, they may talk. |
| **Raid with a vampire** | Big threat |  | 6 days | An ordinary night raid with one vampire hidden among the raiders, for a colony with no Kindred yet. It slips away before dawn; down it first and you can capture it, recruit it or ask it for the Embrace. |
| **Rogue Kindred** | Big threat |  |  | Hostile Kindred and their mortals raid by night. They carry off the downed, torpid elders included, rather than kill them. Only for colonies with Kindred; takes a raid's place. |
| **Sabbat infiltrator** | Event |  |  | A stranger asks to join, already planted by the nearest Sabbat court. The letter reads like any joiner's; what they really are shows over time. |
| **Sabbat pack** | Big threat |  | 6 days | A Sabbat war pack raids when your standing with the Sabbat is very low or it hunts the colony. It sends its shovelheads, freshly Embraced and starving, in first, then waits to see what's left. A Grimaldi colonist may answer the Sabbat's call and turn on you. |
| **Setite corruption** | Event |  |  | A mortal colonist has been meeting someone in the dark and comes back with a psychite habit, as a Setite plant. The habit shows; who fed it doesn't. |
| **Sheltering a brood** | Event |  |  | A Nosferatu brood asks to shelter at the colony for a season. The guests need dark beds; it pays when they all leave content. |
| **Someone at the door** | Event |  |  | A guest at the door at night, asking to be let in. A colony with no Kindred yet gets a fledgling or a survivor instead, and the letter doesn't say what they are; this is the usual way a colony meets its first vampire. Auspex can see what the guest hides. |
| **Sponsoring an artist** | Event |  |  | A Toreador patron places a mortal artist with you as a colonist. Alive, still here and in a good mood after a season, it pays. |
| **Staked for the sun** | Event |  | 60 days | At night, a staked vampire left at the map's edge for the sun to find. Pull the stake and shelter it, and it owes you a life boon. |
| **Surgery for a vampire** | Event |  |  | A maimed vampire asks your doctor or Tzimisce to make it whole. The healer right-clicks it once; the part grows back and it owes a minor boon. |
| **Take in a ghoul** | Event |  |  | A vampire going into torpor asks you to keep its one to three ghouls fed on vitae for a season. They work as colonists meanwhile. All alive at the end pays a minor boon, some alive a trivial one. |
| **The enemy within** | Event |  |  | An outside vampire slips in at night, feeds on a sleeping colonist and makes them its thrall. No letter. You find out the way your people would, unless someone awake sees a stranger bent over a sleeper. |
| **The Regent's inspection** | Event |  |  | A Tremere Regent inspects the colony. It is content if you have a ritual circle and a research bench to show. |
| **The Scourge** | Big threat |  | 30 days | The nearest court's Scourge comes to destroy a colony vampire of the 14th generation or higher, whatever your standing. Naming a Scourge of your own, in a domain of your own, ends these visits. |
| **The Tremere want their share** | Event |  |  | The Tremere want a share of a great library. With a Tremere in good standing, the Pyramid orders copies for a trivial boon. With none, a chantry asks to read. A colony outside the Camarilla that refuses gets a raid for the books. |
| **The wight returns** | Big threat |  |  | A wight that got away comes back at night, alone or with animals it has ghouled, and goes for the easiest blood first. At most once a season. |
| **Thin-bloods** | Big threat |  |  | A band of thin-bloods and their mortals. Only sent as the quarry of a Scourge's mission. |
| **Travellers** | Visitors |  |  | Hunters or investigators arrive dressed as ordinary visitors. Investigators ask around about what people have seen, and once they have a name they leave to pass it on. Rare while the Masquerade holds, common once it's suspected. |
| **Travellers passing** | Visitors |  |  | Hunters crossing the map on their way somewhere else, at any breach level. They want nothing from the colony, but they attack if they see a vampire. |
| **Tremere hit team** | Threat |  |  | The Tremere send a small team for a colony vampire who knows Thaumaturgy and isn't one of theirs. Comes when one is found out, and now and then after. |
| **Tremere raid for the books** | Big threat |  |  | The chantry you refused sends Tremere and their ghouls for your library. Each raider who gets away on its feet carries off a rare book, up to three. Only after a colony outside the Camarilla refuses to share its books. |
| **Under a blood hunt** | Event |  | 30 days | A vampire its own court has called a blood hunt on asks for one day's shelter. Hiding it is a crime against that court, and its hounds may follow; hide it for the day and send it on, and it owes you a life boon. |
| **Welcoming a kumpania** | Event |  |  | A Ravnos kumpania passes through to trade, as a friendly caravan. Welcoming it raises the clan's regard. |
| **Willing cattle** | Event |  |  | A few mortals who know what lives in your colony ask for its protection and offer to be fed on. Take them in and they join, devoted to the Kindred. |
