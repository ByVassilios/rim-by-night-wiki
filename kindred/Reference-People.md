# People, factions and creatures

The factions, the kinds of people and the creatures this mod adds.

## Factions

| Faction | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Abandoned childe** | A neonate alone on the rim. Their sire Embraced them, taught them almost nothing and sent them away, and no sect, prince or coterie has claimed them since.<br><br>In the Camarilla's eyes a childe released without the prince's leave is a breach of the Traditions, and the sire's crime falls on the childe. Some courts will offer a place anyway. Others will want a reason not to stake them. Until then the neonate answers to nobody, and nobody will protect them. |
| **Anarch gang** | Kindred who walked out on their prince and kept walking, with the mortal friends who came along.<br><br>The Anarchs want what the Camarilla's elders keep for themselves: domain, freedom and a say in their own unlife. They keep the Masquerade, since breaking it would end them too, but they have no time for princes. A gang settles its own disputes and is led by whoever the others will follow, usually under the title of baron. |
| **Cainite fief** | A Cainite lord's fief, held the old way, long before anyone spoke of a Camarilla.<br><br>In the Dark Medieval nights the vampires called themselves Cainites and ruled from behind castles and churches. A lord held land the way a mortal baron did, with ghoul retainers to keep the day and serfs to work the fields and feed the household. Every lord answers to an older one. Rule well and the fief grows; rule badly and someone hungrier takes it. |
| **Camarilla coterie** | A coterie of young Kindred sent by a distant prince to claim a domain for the Camarilla, with their mortal retainers.<br><br>The Camarilla holds to the Six Traditions, and above all to the Masquerade: mortals must never learn that Kindred exist. Its princes rule cities through primogen councils, sheriffs and harpies, and their elders measure every neonate by the boons they owe and are owed. Keep the Masquerade, build a domain worth having, and the prince who sent you may name you its regent. |
| **Crash survivors** | Survivors of a ship torn apart over the rim. One of them came aboard in a cryptosleep casket nobody remembers loading.<br><br>A vampire among mortals has to feed, and the people closest to hand are the ones who shared the escape pods. Whether they become a herd, ghouls, a coterie in the making or a danger to the Masquerade is up to the one who hid among them. |
| **Fallen knight** | A knight who died on campaign and rose a vampire, and the squire who would not leave.<br><br>Wars drew the Cainites the way battlefields draw crows: plenty of blood, and nobody counts the dead. Now and then one of them took a knight for the sword arm and left them behind when the army moved on. This one has no sire to answer to, only the oath they swore in life and a squire who drinks their blood. |
| **Independent clan crew** | Kindred of one of the Independent clans, out on clan business with mortals to work by day.<br><br>The Independents belong to no sect. They trade with the Camarilla and the Sabbat alike, and each clan keeps its own ways, its own secrets and its own elders, who expect to be obeyed even across the stars. The clan has a haven on this world already. What the elders want done here, they will say in their own time. |
| **Raiders** | Drifters who answer to nobody and keep late hours. |
| **Raiders** | Hard-faced agents of some distant authority, sent to settle its accounts. |
| **Raiders** | Packs who come after dark and burn what they can't carry. |
| **Raiders** | Gangs who threw off whoever used to run them and mean to stay free. |
| **Revenant on the road** | A revenant of one of the old families who have served the Kindred for centuries, out on their own.<br><br>Revenants are born, not made. Their blood carries a little of what their masters gave the family long ago, and they never need a domitor to keep it. The family sells its service to the courts, and this one has gone to find the best price. Kindred will see them as a ghoul with no master, to be used, bought or claimed. |
| **Sabbat pack** | A pack of the Sword of Caine, sent to take this world from the Camarilla, and their mortals.<br><br>The Sabbat calls the Camarilla's elders slaves of the Antediluvians and means to destroy them before Gehenna. Its packs are bound by the Vaulderie, a sharing of blood that replaces any one Kindred's hold on another with loyalty to the pack. A ductus leads them in war and a priest keeps the ritae. Mortals are herds, and the Masquerade is kept only because the war needs it. |
| **Travellers** | Strangers on the road after dark, keeping to themselves. |

## Creatures and races

| | Name | Value | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | **Dancing blade** | 0 | A weapon hanging in the air with no hand on it, cutting at whoever comes near. |
|  | **Gnome** | 0 | A spirit of the earth, a hunched thing of packed soil and stone. Slow, very hard to hurt, and it hits like a rockfall. |
|  | **Risen dead** |  | Someone who died, called back by Necromancy as a ghost or a walking corpse. |
|  | **Salamander** | 0 | A spirit of fire in the shape of a long lizard of glowing coals. Its touch sets things alight, and fire can't hurt it. |
|  | **Sylph** | 0 | A spirit of the air, a gust that holds the shape of a great bird. Fast and hard to hit, but its blows are light. |
|  | **Undine** | 0 | A spirit of water, a coiling serpent of it, solid enough to grab and drown a man. Fire can barely touch it. |
|  | **Walking furniture** | 0 | A chair, a table or a lamp scuttling about on its own legs and battering whoever it is set on. |
|  | **Walking statue** | 0 | A statue moving like a living thing, slow and heavy. Fists and clubs barely chip it. |
|  | **Walking tree** | 0 | A tree that has pulled its roots out of the ground to fight. Slow and enormously strong, it shrugs off clubs and fists, but it burns. |
