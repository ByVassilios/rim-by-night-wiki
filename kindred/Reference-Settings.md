# Every setting

Every setting in Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, with what it does.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Vampires sleep by day** | Off, a vampire keeps a mortal's hours: it sleeps at night, works by day, and is never drowsy or hard to draft in daylight. Its schedule resets to a mortal's when you change this. The sun still burns unless that is off too. |
| **The sun burns vampires** | Off, sunlight does a vampire no harm and never sends it into Rötschreck, and it walks out under the sky like anyone. |
| **Orders come before shelter** | A vampire you wake or give an order to by day (prioritized work or any other right-click order) does it while it stays under a roof, then goes back to bed. On, it keeps at the order even out in the sun. Off, it drops an order that takes it into the sun to get under a roof or into bed. Either way, work it picked up on its own is dropped for shelter, and the red fear still takes over. |
| **Sunlight damage** | How hard the sun burns a vampire caught outside by day. At 0% it does no harm at all. |
| **Blood used each night** | The blood a vampire burns to wake each evening. Lower it if feeding takes up too much of the colony's time. At 0% they only need blood for healing and powers. |
| **Blood to heal** | The blood a vampire spends to mend a wound. Lower it and a feeding goes further in a fight. |
| **Torpor length** | How long a vampire sleeps in torpor, which depends on its Humanity: a day at 10, a month at 1. A torpor sentence set by a court stays as it is. |
| **Frenzy difficulty** | Added to the difficulty of every frenzy and Rötschreck roll. Above zero the Beast gets loose more often, below zero less. |
| **Conscience difficulty** | Added to the difficulty of the Conscience roll after a sin. Above zero, vampires lose Humanity more often; below zero, less. |
| **Mercy wins Humanity back** | Once a week, a vampire on Humanity who captured a downed enemy or recruited a prisoner rolls Conscience against its Humanity plus one. On a success it wins back a dot. Not in V20, where only experience buys Humanity back. |
| **At Humanity 0** | What happens to a colony vampire whose Humanity falls to 0. Lost: it becomes a wight, turns on everyone and is never yours again (V20). Frenzy: the Beast rides it for a few hours, then lets go at Humanity 1. Rehab: it becomes a wight, but if you down it and hold it in a cell it rolls Conscience once a week, with extra dice for a colony vampire with Obeah on the map, and a success brings it back at Humanity 1. |
| **Powers camera shake** | How hard big powers shake the camera: a lightning strike, a volcanic blast, the earth opening. At 0% the camera stays still. |
| **Powers sky flashes** | Big powers flash the sky in their colour, and storms tint it while they build. Off, the sky keeps its own light. |
| **Pale skin** | Show a vampire's skin drained to an ashen grey. It looks like any living person's again while it looks alive or wears the Mask of a Thousand Faces. Cappadocians are paler still. Only the look changes; hunters spot the pallor either way. |
| **How pale** | How far a vampire's skin is drained toward ashen grey. |
| **Blood heals mortal scars** | Off, a vampire keeps the scars it had when it was Embraced, and blood heals only the ones since. On, blood heals those old scars as well. |
| **Humanity** | Off, sins never cost Humanity or a Path's rating, and every vampire counts as at Humanity 7: no dice cap by day, no inhuman looks, no beast dreams, no fall to the Beast at 0. |
| **Frenzy and Rötschreck** | Off, the Beast never takes over. Vampires and ghouls don't frenzy or flee in fear, and fire nearby doesn't drive them back. A power used on purpose can still send someone into a frenzy. |
| **Clan weaknesses** | Off, no clan or bloodline has a weakness, and ghouls take no taint from their domitor's clan. Turning it back on brings them back. |
| **Soften clan weaknesses** | Each clan's weakness still applies, in a milder form. |
| **Breaches have consequences** | Outsiders who see a vampire at work carry the story away, and the world reacts: warier visitors, then hunters, then the hunting order. Turn off to play without any of it. |
| **How much a breach counts** | How much each sighting adds to the colony's Masquerade breach score. |
| **How fast breaches fade** | How quickly the world forgets what it saw. Higher numbers bring the breach score down sooner. |
| **NPC Kindred** | How often vampires from outside the colony turn up: in the dark at night, in cryptosleep caskets, in rogue bands. 0% turns them off. |
| **Extra courts in a new world** | Every world gets one court of each sect and a haven for each Independent clan. This many more courts go to sects at random. Read when a world is made, or when the mod is added to a save. |
| **Court mission offers** | The chance each hour that a court posts a new mission, until it has three open. The default of 5% comes to about one a day; 0% stops new ones. |
| **Sleepers in ancient dangers** | The chance that one casket in a new map's ancient danger holds a Kindred instead of a soldier. Also scaled by the NPC Kindred setting. |
| **Psylinked Tremere** | With Royalty, the chance that a Tremere met outside the colony has a psylink of level 1 to 3. Its psycasts are random, from every psycast loaded, Vanilla Psycasts Expanded's paths included. At 0% none do. |
| **Wights come back** | A vampire lost to the Beast who escapes the map returns now and then as a night threat. |
| **The Red List** | A handful of Anathema the Camarilla wants destroyed roam the world map, each with a bounty. They roam either way, but only a colony in the Camarilla is told of them, sees them on the map or is paid for one. Turned off, they leave the world map; turned back on, a new list arrives in one letter. |
| **Sect politics** | Archons, Sabbat packs and Anarch gangs sent against the colony, infiltrators, thralls of outside Kindred, Anarch unrest, Assamite contracts, Setite corruption and Tremere hit teams. |
| **Kindred attacks** | Rogue bands, elder raids, and the angry dead coming back to haunt the colony. |
| **Kindred visitors** | Kindred at the door, Path mentors, the occult trader, the ghoul black market, the anonymous benefactor and revenants asking to join. |
| **A stranger at the door** | A colony with no vampire gets a pale stranger at its door by night, some time between day 15 and day 40, and again every 30 to 60 days until one is taken in. Off, the stranger only comes by chance. |
| **Scourges** | Camarilla courts seat a Scourge, who comes after colony vampires of the 14th generation and higher, and a colony with its own domain can name one. Off, nobody is sent and the office is gone from the domain page. |
| **Sire calls** | A sire out in the world calling on its childe with demands. |
| **Background world** | Courts changing by themselves while you play: coups, rival schemes, blood hunts and cities changing sect. Switched off, the courts stay as they are. |
| **Koldunic Sorcery for any clan** | Any vampire can learn the Ways of Koldunic Sorcery from a teacher, at the out-of-clan price. Off, only the Tzimisce and their bloodlines can. |
| **Sanguinus for any clan** | A Discipline that only its clan can learn (Sanguinus, the Blood Brothers' shared flesh) can be taught to anyone. Its powers that work through a circle of brothers do nothing for a vampire without one. |
| **Gargoyle rite for any clan** | Any vampire who knows rituals can learn the rite that makes a gargoyle. Off, it stays a Tremere secret. |
| **Ghouls learn guarded Disciplines** | A ghoul can learn Protean, Thaumaturgy and a Discipline only one clan has from any domitor who knows it. Off, only from a domitor of that clan. |
| **Ghouls study lore** | A ghoul reads lore books and treatises under Research work like a vampire: experience, treatises for the Disciplines it has, and rituals a ghoul can cast. Off, ghouls leave the books alone. |
| **Ghouls get a full character sheet** | The Ghoul tab gains a page list down its left side, with the Virtues on its Sheet page and an Attributes page: Virtues, Willpower and Attribute dots a ghoul buys with its experience, at a vampire's prices. They count in its rolls. Off, a ghoul keeps a mortal's Willpower and Attributes, plus whatever it has already bought. |
| **Caitiff learn their sire's Disciplines easily** | A Caitiff learns only its sire's Disciplines at the in-clan price, and one with no known sire takes the three of a random clan. Off, a Caitiff counts every Discipline as its own. |
| **New powers start off the bar** | A colony vampire's new powers stay off the command bar until you turn them on in the Kindred tab. Off, each new power gets its button at once. |
| **Any Discipline self-taught** | Any Discipline a vampire could take lessons in can be bought with experience alone, at the self-taught price. A clan's own sorcery stays closed unless its own setting opens it. Off, as in V20, only Celerity, Fortitude and Potence can be learned without a teacher. |
| **Out-of-clan experience cost** | Multiplies what each dot of an out-of-clan Discipline costs, taught or self-taught. Clan Disciplines and a Caitiff's cost the same either way. |
| **Chance to take a Discipline dot** | The chance a diablerie gives the diablerist a dot of one of the victim's Disciplines, if the victim had more of it. Not in V20, where diablerie gives only generation. |
| **Neonates can give a dot** | A neonate's blood can give a Discipline dot too. Off, only ancillae and elders have blood old enough. |
| **Diablerie opens the victim's Disciplines** | Every Discipline the victim had, and the diablerist could learn, can be bought with experience afterwards as if a teacher had started it. Not in V20. |
| **Each diablerie risks a derangement** | Every soul eaten makes the next harder to keep down: each diablerie after the first has a 10% chance of a derangement for every one before it. Not in V20. |
| **The Pyramid guards Thaumaturgy** | The colony's Tremere owe the Pyramid a yearly tithe, and a vampire of another clan seen casting Thaumaturgy by a Tremere or the Camarilla brings a Tremere hit team. Off, neither happens. |
| **A childe needs the Prince's leave** | In a sect that keeps the Traditions, a colony vampire who holds no office needs a permit to Embrace, and a childe made without one costs standing when the court meets it. Off, no permit is needed. |
| **Hunger frenzy lasts until fed** | A vampire in a hunger frenzy only comes back to itself once it has fed enough or its time runs out. Off, it may pass early, like any other frenzy. |
| **Draft vampires freely by day** | A vampire can be drafted by day with no roll and no blood spent. Off, it has to stir first: a roll with a threat on the map, or a blood point. |
| **Resonance** | The vessel's mood colours the blood a vampire drinks, giving a few bonus dice to the Disciplines that match it. Taken from the fifth edition; V20 has no such rule. Off, blood is only blood. |
| **Hemogen runs on vitae** | A vampire with Biotech's hemogen genes pays for hemogen powers from its vitae pool, and anything that gives hemogen fills the pool instead. Off, the hemogen bar is its own, filled and drained the vanilla way. |
| **Move as a cloud** | A drafted vampire on the move, or a hostile one closing in, travels as a faint cloud of its strongest Discipline, and faster. Off, vampires walk like anyone else. Needs fighting motion on. |
| **Hunters among tavern guests** | The chance that a guest at the colony's tavern is a hunter, from a strong Masquerade to a broken one. The mod's chances run from 5% to 40%. At 0% none come this way. |
| **Blood market** | Vials, Kindred vitae and hemogen share one price that moves: hunter raids, blood hunts, the Scourge and a breach push it up, and selling a lot brings it down. Off, they keep their plain market value. |
| **The lure** | A raiding vampire with Summon or Entrancement may call a lone colonist out past the walls before the raid comes in. The colonist resists like any mind power. |
