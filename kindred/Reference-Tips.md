# Learning tips

The tips the game shows the first times you meet something.

| Tip | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Clans and Disciplines** | Every vampire carries the blood of a clan.<br><br>The clan gives three in-clan Disciplines, the powers of the blood, and a weakness every member shares. The Kindred tab lists both. Disciplines are raised with experience on the Kindred tab. In-clan ones cost less, and the rest need a taste of a teacher's blood first. A selected vampire's powers sit on one button per Discipline, and most of them spend blood or Willpower.<br><br>Caitiff are Kindred with no clan. Other vampires look down on them. |
| **Diablerie** | Diablerie is drinking another vampire's soul. The Camarilla counts it among the worst crimes a vampire can commit.<br><br>The diablerist drains a helpless vampire past its blood and takes its heart. If it succeeds, its own generation may drop, by more against older blood, and it may take a dot of a Discipline. It also loses Humanity, and black veins mark its aura for years where the right powers can see them. A soul stronger than the diablerist's may not go quietly. |
| **Dice and Willpower** | The blood answers to the dice.<br><br>A roll throws one ten-sided die per dot in the traits it uses, such as Strength plus Brawl, and every die that meets the difficulty is a success. More successes mean a stronger result. A roll with no successes and at least one 1 is a botch, and something goes wrong.<br><br>Willpower buys a sure success, and it is also how a vampire holds off frenzy. Habits, on the Kindred tab, sets when it may be spent. The roll log, opened from the Kindred tab, shows recent rolls. |
| **Feeding** | The Beast is hungry, and an empty vampire falls into torpor.<br><br>Select a vampire, right-click a person or animal and pick Feed under Kindred. Mortal donors can also give blood vials with the draw blood operation at a medical bed, and a vampire drinks those like a meal. A prisoner set to "keep as vessel" is fed on whenever someone is hungry. Habits, on the Kindred tab, sets whom a vampire may feed on without being told.<br><br>Taking too much from a mortal can kill them, and feeding where outsiders can see breaks the Masquerade. |
| **Frenzy** | Hunger, fire, pain and insult can wake the Beast.<br><br>A vampire in frenzy attacks or feeds on whoever is near, and one in Rötschreck flees fire and sunlight. Fed vampires with Willpower left resist far better. Habits, on the Kindred tab, sets when a vampire spends Willpower; keep it on "to resist frenzy" unless you need it for rolls. |
| **Ghouls** | A mortal who drinks vitae becomes a ghoul.<br><br>Ghouls stop ageing, heal with the blood they carry and can learn a little of their domitor's Disciplines. They need another drink about every fifteen days or they go into withdrawal. Every drink also pulls them closer to a blood bond with the vampire who gave it. |
| **Humanity** | Every sin lets the Beast gain a little ground.<br><br>Humanity runs from 10 down to 0. When a vampire does something its morality counts as a sin, it rolls Conscience, and a failed roll costs a point of Humanity. Which acts count depends on the rating: at high Humanity even small cruelties count, while at low Humanity only killing still does.<br><br>Low Humanity means longer torpor and fewer dice by day. At 0 the Beast takes over for good. Some Kindred follow a Path of Enlightenment in its place, with its own list of sins. |
| **Hunters** | Some mortals know what walks among them, and they hunt it.<br><br>Hunters follow breaches of the Masquerade. Some arrive as ordinary visitors and ask questions before they show their stakes. A few have True Faith, which the Damned recoil from. They like to attack by day, when your vampires sleep, so mortals and ghouls must hold the line until dark. |
| **Sects and courts** | The Kindred split into sects, and every court belongs to one.<br><br>The Camarilla keeps the Masquerade under its Princes. The Sabbat, the Sword of Caine, wants the Camarilla torn down. The Anarchs refuse the elders' rule, and the Independent clans keep their own counsel. Your colony's standing with each decides which courts welcome you and which send enforcers. The Kindred button at the bottom of the screen shows your sect, your standing and the courts you know.<br><br>Courts deal in boons, favours owed and paid back. A boon owed is a debt, and it will be called in. |
| **Sunlight and daysleep** | The sun burns the Damned, and its wounds are slow to heal.<br><br>Vampires sleep through the day. Under a roof they are safe; under open sky they burn. Give them a coffin or a bed in a roofed room, and bring them in before dawn. Kindred start on a night schedule; copy it to mortal colonists in the Schedule tab to keep them awake at the same hours. |
| **The blood bond** | Drink a vampire's blood on three different nights and you will love them.<br><br>Each drink is one step of the bond, and only one a night counts. At the third step the thrall is fully bound: it adores its regnant and struggles to raise a hand against it. A bond fades only after a long time without another drink.<br><br>Mortals, ghouls and Kindred can all be bound. Feeding a ghoul binds it a little more each time. |
| **The Embrace and generation** | A vampire makes another by draining a mortal and giving back a little of its own blood.<br><br>The childe rises some hours later, of its sire's clan and one generation further from Caine. Generation decides how much blood a vampire holds, how much it can spend at once and how high its traits can go. Each step further makes the blood thinner. The Embrace also costs the sire blood.<br><br>There is no undoing it. |
| **The Kindred** | The dead walk among your colonists now.<br><br>A vampire doesn't eat or rest. It lives on vitae, the blood in its pool, and pays some of it every night. Blood also heals its wounds and powers its Disciplines.<br><br>The Kindred tab on a vampire shows its clan, generation, Disciplines, Humanity and Willpower, and is where experience is spent. |
| **The Masquerade** | The world must never learn that the Kindred exist.<br><br>When an outsider sees feeding, an obvious power or a vampire that won't die, the colony's breach score goes up. The Kindred button at the bottom of the screen shows it. A high score keeps traders away at night and brings hunters and angry courts. It falls slowly while nothing new is seen.<br><br>Your own colonists know what lives among them. Visitors, traders and caravans don't. |
| **Torpor** | Torpor is the deathlike sleep of the Damned.<br><br>A vampire in torpor lies still for days, longer the lower its Humanity. One that starved into torpor wakes only when someone gives it blood: carry a blood vial to it or give it vitae. Keep it under a roof and out of reach of enemies, since a stake or the sun can still end it for good. |
