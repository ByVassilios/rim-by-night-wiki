# Every mission and quest

Every mission a court, sept, cell or consilium can give, and every quest these mods offer.

| Mission | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Poaching *target coterie*'s herd** | Every coterie has its herd: mortals kept close, fed on gently, missed when they're gone. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants *target coterie* to go hungry, and has found where their herd is kept, a few days out at *target*.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Clear the site of everyone who holds it<br>- If you're not done in time, the mission fails |
| **A retainer stolen** | *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants one of *target coterie*'s ghouls, alive, to learn what it knows. It is laying up at *target*, not far from *taker*, with rogues to guard it.<br><br>*taker* took the task. It has to end up as your prisoner. If it dies or gets away, the mission fails. |
| **Siege of *target coterie*'s haven** | A vampire's haven is the one place it sleeps without fear. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants *target coterie* to lose that comfort. Their haven at *target* is held by their ghouls and one of their own.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Clear the site of everyone who holds it<br>- If you're not done in time, the mission fails |
| **Tithe for *patron*** | The mortals who work for *target coterie* at *target* have grown comfortable, and *patron* of *coterie* at *court* says they owe tribute. It should be collected by someone with the right kind of smile, the kind that makes a mortal reach for their wallet before they know why.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails<br><br>+ The silver collected |
| **The Keeper's tome** | The Keeper of Elysium at *target* guards a book that *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants badly: a few hundred years of somebody's secrets, bound in leather that may once have been a person. The Keeper doesn't lend.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **Relic of the lost generations** | In a crumbling diary, *patron* of *coterie* at *court* has read of something left behind in the ruin at *target* by Kindred of a generation long gone. Rogues sleep there now, and they don't know what lies beneath them.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Clear the site of everyone who holds it<br>- If you're not done in time, the mission fails |
| **The scholar's night road** | A scholar of the Blood, more at home with books than knives, has to make the night journey to *target* to see something with their own eyes. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants them to arrive in one piece.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **Blood secrets for *patron*** | Some rituals are written down once and guarded for centuries. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* has learned of one that is known only at *target*, and wants it learned there and worked for the coterie.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **Scandal in Elysium** | At court a slip of the tongue does more damage than a stake. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants the leader of *target coterie* humiliated at the next Elysium, in front of the Harpies, so that the whole court is laughing by dawn.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Come to Elysium at *settlement* with *taker* in the caravan and do the errand from the court window while the gathering lasts<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **The Harpies' whisper** | *patron* of *coterie* at *court* has a story about *target coterie*. Whether it's true hardly matters. Told in the right ear at *target*, it will reach every Harpy in the domain within the week.<br><br>*taker* took the task. Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way. It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails. |
| **Unchaining *target coterie*'s prisoner** | *target coterie* keeps someone locked away at *target*. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* has no love for the captive, but loves the trouble they'll cause once they're loose.<br><br>*taker* took the task. Clear the site of everyone who holds it. If you're not done in time, the mission fails. |
| **The switched casket** | *patron* of *coterie* at *court* knows a courier of *target coterie* is resting at *target*, not far from *taker*, with a sealed casket, and wants it swapped for a fake.<br><br>*taker* took the task. Stop the courier. Bring it down: dead, downed or in a cell. If it leaves alive, the mission fails. |
| **The unsanctioned Embrace** | *patron* of *coterie* at *court* has word of a childe Embraced without leave, and the Traditions say what comes next. It is hiding at *target*, not far from *taker*.<br><br>*taker* took the task. Bring it down: dead, downed or in a cell. If it leaves alive, the mission fails. |
| **Before the Prince's judgement** | *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants a Kindred who broke the Traditions brought in alive to answer for it. It is hiding at *target*, not far from *taker*.<br><br>*taker* took the task. It has to end up as your prisoner. If it dies or gets away, the mission fails. |
| **Mending the Masquerade at *target*** | Mortals at *target* saw too much and are talking about it: in bars, on phones, to anyone who will listen. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants them quieted before the Masquerade tears any further.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **A matter for the Primogen** | Two Kindred of *court* have been at each other's throats over a feeding ground, a slight, a ghoul, and neither will back down to an equal. *patron* of *coterie* wants it settled by someone who sits high enough that both must listen.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Come to *settlement* with *taker* in the caravan and do the errand from the court window<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **Forged testimony** | Letters, ledgers, a photograph or two: *patron* of *coterie* at *court* has had papers prepared that put *target coterie* in a very bad light. They only need to be found at *target*, by the right people.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **A stolen prestation** | A boon is only worth as much as the debtor's memory. Someone at *target* owes *target coterie* a favour, and *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants that debtor persuaded that they really owe the coterie instead.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **The skeletons of *target coterie*** | Every coterie has bones buried somewhere. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants to know where *target coterie* buried theirs, and thinks someone who sees what others miss could find out in a single night.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Come to *settlement* with *taker* in the caravan and do the errand from the court window<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **The accord of two courts** | *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants an accord with the court at *target*: feeding rights, safe passage, perhaps a marriage of interests. It needs someone both courts trust, or at least neither has reason to stake.<br><br>*taker* took the task. Take a caravan to *target* with *taker* in it and do the errand from the court window, under missions under way. It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails. |
| **Jyhad against the *rival*** | The Jyhad is fought in boardrooms and alleys alike. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants a blow struck at the *rival*, and has found one of their places a few days out: *target*.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Clear the site of everyone who holds it<br>- If you're not done in time, the mission fails |
| **Hunting the hunters** | Hunters have been asking questions near *court*, and *patron* of *coterie* wants them to stop asking. They mean to strike at *target* by night, with fire and faith and sharpened wood.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Bring their leader down: dead, downed or in a cell<br>- If the leader leaves alive, the mission fails |
| **Ashes of the blood farm** | Cages, drips and mortals kept half alive on vitae: the enemy breeds its ghouls at *target*. *patron* of *coterie* at *court* wants the place put to the torch, and everyone in it.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Clear the site of everyone who holds it<br>- If you're not done in time, the mission fails |
| **Swords for *patron*'s war** | War with the *rival* has come to *court* in earnest. *patron* of *coterie* is gathering every blade the domain can spare, and the colony's are known to be sharp.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Come to *settlement* with *taker* and at least three fighters in the caravan and do the errand from the court window<br>- The fighters come back after, some of them hurt<br>- It's one roll: fail it and the mission fails |
| **Blood tithe for *giver name*** | A ghoul in *giver*'s colours came to the gate after midnight with a sealed note. The herd at *court* has grown thin, and *giver* wants something fresher: a living mortal, brought in chains. Nobody will ask the vessel's name, and nobody will see them again.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Bring the vessel to *settlement* as a prisoner in a caravan and hand them over in the court window |
| **The courier's oath** | *giver* of *court* is sending a gift to the court at *target*: a casket of black wood, its lid sealed in wax and blood. What sleeps inside is not your concern. Whoever carries it must be discreet, and must not be curious.<br><br>*taker* has sworn the courier's oath. The casket comes by drop pod, or straight into the caravan if the colony has no home. Carry it to *target* and hand it over in the court window. Don't open it. |
| **Keeper of Elysium's peace** | Elysium is meant to be neutral ground, where even old enemies keep their fangs sheathed. At the last gathering someone forgot. *giver* of *court* wants no blood spilled under the court's roof this time, and has asked *taker* to stand at the door.<br><br>- Be at *settlement* with a caravan while Elysium is held, and stand guard from the court window |
| **Blood hunt: the Amaranth** | *giver* of *court* names a diablerist running with a rogue band. The band is headed for your lands, and the court wants the diablerist destroyed.<br><br>*taker* took the task. The band will reach *target* within a few nights. Bring the diablerist down: dead, in torpor or in a cell. If it leaves alive, the mission fails. |
| **Watching the Caitiff rogues** | Caitiff and anarch rabble have made camp at *target*, close enough to the domain to be an insult. *giver* of *court* wants to know how many there are, who leads them and what they want before deciding what to do about it.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Reach the place with a caravan and take a look<br>- Fighting is optional |
| **The Assamite's contract** | *giver* of *court* has a name and a price. The target travels with a rogue band and will pass through *target* within a few nights. This is work for an Assamite.<br><br>*taker* took the contract. Bring the target down before it leaves: dead, in torpor or in a cell. It pays in silver as well as a favour. |
| **The wight unbound** | *giver* of *court* knows about the wight your colony let escape, and wants it ended before it costs everyone the Masquerade. Word is it's coming back to *target*.<br><br>*taker* took the task. Bring it down when it comes: dead, in torpor or in a cell. If it gets away again, the mission fails. |
| **Seeking the Suspire** | After a long night of questions, someone at the court of *prince* admitted it: a vampire who has walked the road toward Golconda keeps a haven at *target*. The court laughed about it afterwards. To them Golconda is a fairy tale for Kindred who cannot bear what they are.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Take *taker* there with a caravan |
| **The rogue in chains** | The leader of a rogue band has been heard saying things about *court* that cannot go unanswered. *giver* wants a word with them in person, and a corpse makes poor conversation. The band will pass through *target* within a few nights.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Capture the leader and keep them as a prisoner<br>- If they die or get away, the mission fails |
| **The hunters' den** | Stakes, gasoline and a list of names. A cell of hunters*cell phrase* has found out more than mortals should, and they mean to strike at *target* by night. *giver* of *court* wants the cell broken before the list gets any shorter.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Bring their leader down: dead, downed or in a cell<br>- If the leader leaves alive, the mission fails |
| **Siege of the domain** | The Jyhad never sleeps for long. *giver* of *court* has word that the *rival* is sending its people through *target* within a few nights, to test the domain's borders and see who answers.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Bring their leader down: dead, in torpor or in a cell<br>- If the leader leaves alive, the mission fails |
| **The Prince's captive** | *giver* of *court* has a guest the court would rather nobody found: a vampire who knows too much, or owes too much, and is worth more undestroyed. It will arrive at *target* after dark by drop pod, staked through the heart.<br><br>*taker* agreed to keep it. Hold it as a prisoner for a week. If it escapes, dies or goes free, the mission fails. The court collects it after. |
| **A week beneath the Masquerade** | There has been talk about *target*: strange deaths, pale strangers, blood where it shouldn't be. *giver* of *court* reminds you that the Masquerade is the First Tradition for a reason, and wants a week of perfect quiet.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- If the Masquerade breach rises at all this week, the mission fails |
| **The witness must forget** | A mortal saw something they should not have, a feeding or a face that didn't heal, and ran before anyone could make them forget. *giver* of *court* wants the Masquerade mended before the story reaches the papers.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Ask around by caravan at *target* until two good leads point to where the witness is hiding<br>- Then kill them, take them, or make them forget |
| **A poacher in the domain** | Drained bodies in the alleys, and none of them taken by anyone with leave to hunt. Someone is poaching in the domain of *court*, and *giver* takes it as a personal insult.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Ask around by caravan at *target* until two good leads point to the feeder's lair<br>- Destroy the feeder, send it into torpor, or bring it back a prisoner |
| **The wayward childe** | A childe of *court* fled its sire's haven three nights ago, still hungry and still foolish. A fledgling alone in the city is a breach waiting to happen, and *giver* wants it brought home before it learns what the sun does.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Ask around by caravan at *target* until two good leads point to its hideout<br>- Talk it round or take it, then hand it over at *court* |
| **The Scourge's cull** | Thin-blooded Kindred, made without leave and barely vampires at all, have been seen near the domain. To the court they are a sign of Gehenna and an affront to the Traditions. *giver* of *court* has called on the Scourge. They will reach *target* within a few nights.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Bring their leader down: dead, in torpor or in a cell<br>- If the leader leaves alive, the mission fails |
| **Lupines at the border** | Lupines in the domain. *giver* of *court* says a pack of Garou has crossed the old border, the kind the *sept* can't or won't call back, and they will come through *target* within a few nights. Few Kindred survive a werewolf's frenzy.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Drive them out of the domain or bring their leader down<br>- The *sept* will hear of it if their leader dies |
| **Vigil at the Lupines' caern** | Few at *court* know about the pact. *giver* keeps the Wyrm's servants away from the *sept*'s caern, and in return the Garou stay off the court's streets. Now something foul is gathering against the caern at *target*, and the pact has to be honoured.<br><br>- Taken on by *taker*<br>- Send a caravan there with at least two fighters and keep it in town for five days<br>- Then the attack comes, and the best fighter among them decides it |
| **Conclave of *court*** | Some things can't be said at Elysium, where the Harpies listen and the Prince's ears are everywhere. *giver* of *court* needs a quiet place for *guest count* Kindred to meet in secret. They would come to your colony after dark and talk until an hour before dawn.<br><br>- Keep every one of them alive while they're here<br>- Someone always finds out about a conclave |
| **Sanctuary for *court*'s refugee** | A vampire under *giver name*'s protection has made powerful enemies, and they want *court*'s guest destroyed before the court can move it somewhere safer. *giver name* asks you to hide it for one night, and to ask no questions.<br><br>- It comes after dark and goes before dawn<br>- Its enemies will come looking<br>- Keep it alive |
| **A ghoul for *court*'s service** | The Kindred of *court* rule the night, but the day still needs tending: deliveries signed for, doors opened, coffins guarded while the sun is up. *giver* is short of such hands and wants to borrow …, your ghoul, for *lend days* days.<br><br>- … leaves as soon as you accept and comes home when the days are up<br>- The court feeds him or her while he or she is there |
| ***court*'s requisition** | Mortal merchants talk, and the Kindred of *court* can't afford talk. *giver* wants *want* brought to *asker*, and would rather owe your colony a favour than leave a trail of receipts.<br><br>- Send a caravan with the goods |
| **The stolen heart of *owner*** | *carrier* got away with *owner*'s heart, still wet with old blood, and took it to a hideout of the hunting order. | **The sleeper in the crypt** | From *source*, the colony has learned where an elder lies in torpor, walled away with its treasures for longer than anyone remembers.
