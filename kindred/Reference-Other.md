## Backgrounds

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Alternate identity** | A false mortal life, with papers and a past. When hunters report this vampire, each dot is a 10% chance the trail ends at the false name and their raid comes without knowing whom to look for. A masked vampire also keeps passing as mortal to visitors for an hour a dot after sunrise. |
| **Domain** | Hunting grounds the Prince grants. Each dot claims one mortal town within 40 tiles of the sworn court, picked from the row's link. Hunting there never makes the town warier. A rival poaching it is a strike to answer. |
| **Herd** | A flock of mortals who feed this vampire willingly. Each season they send blood vials worth about 4% of its blood pool for each dot, a fifth of its upkeep at 5 dots: never enough to live on. Each dot also adds a die to its hunting rolls in towns. |
| **Retainers** | Mortal ghouls kept in the sworn court's town, one for each dot. Each counts as a string there that never fades and can be pulled as any string can. Each drinks a blood point a season, paid on its own from the vampire's pool. A retainer left unfed walks away, and the dot goes with it. |
| **Status** | Standing in the eyes of the sworn court. When the colony asks the court for a service on credit, the dots stand in for court rank: 1 or 2 for a trivial boon, 3 minor, 4 major, 5 a life boon. Each dot bought also raises every court member's regard for the colony by 2. |

Sunlight deals aggravated burns every second under an open sky, and a neonate at noon has well under a minute. - Get this vampire under a roof or into a coffin now. - Caught with no roof, it digs into soft ground by itself. See [[VTM_Guide_Being_Kindred#sunlight\|Sunlight]]. |
| **Rötschreck** | Fire, sun or True Faith has this vampire running for the nearest dark, safe spot, and it attacks anything in the way. - Clear the path to shade, and put out the fire if you can. - Leave it alone until it stops. See [[VTM_Guide_Humanity_and_Frenzy#frenzy\|Frenzy]]. |
| **Staked** | A stake through the heart holds a vampire awake and helpless until someone pulls it. - To free your own, add the stake's removal as a bill on the Health tab. - A staked enemy can be kept that way for as long as you like. See [[VTM_Guide_Being_Kindred#staking\|Staking]]. |
| **Starving** | Under 15% of its pool this vampire feeds whatever the mark on its Vitae bar says, and it may frenzy at the sight of a vessel. - Keep a willing colonist or a prisoner close. Either gives up to 3 points. - A [[VTM_Guide_Feeding#blood-vials\|blood vial]] gives 1 point. - Animals are worth about half, and nothing from the 6th generation down. - Afterwards, drag the mark on the Vitae bar up. With an empty pool it can't wake and falls into torpor. See [[VTM_Guide_Feeding\|Feeding]]. |
| **Tied to the moon** | This Rafastio's Disciplines follow the moon: 2 harder at the new moon, 1 harder while it waxes or wanes and 1 easier when it's full. The Embrace is refused to them, and so is the Ferryman's Recall. See [[VTM_Guide_Embrace_Bonds_and_Ghouls\|Ghouls]]. |
| **Unsettling to mortals** | Below Humanity 5 this vampire takes social penalties with mortals. - Don't make it your negotiator or warden. - Raise Humanity with experience after a quadrum with no Humanity lost. See [[VTM_Guide_Humanity_and_Frenzy#humanity\|Humanity]]. |
| **Using a coffin** | Anyone can sleep in a coffin. Its lid keeps the sun off even under an open sky, and a full day in it is a good mood. See [[VTM_Guide_Being_Kindred#daysleep\|Daysleep]]. |
