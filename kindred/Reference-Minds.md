# Mental states and inspirations

Mental states, breaks and inspirations this mod adds.

| State | Kind | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Frenzy** | Mental state | …'s Beast has broken loose.<br><br>He or she will attack anyone in reach, friend or foe, until it passes. He or she feels no pain while it lasts. |
| **Hunger frenzy** | Mental state | … is starving, and the Beast has taken over.<br><br>He or she will bring down the nearest person or animal and drink until he or she is full, whether or not that kills them. Only blood or being knocked down will stop him or her. He or she may hold back from someone he or she cares about, but there's no telling. |
| **The Beast rides** | Mental state | … has lost the last of his or her Humanity, and the Beast has taken over. The last thing he or she said before it took him or her was a name.<br><br>For the next few hours he or she will attack anyone in reach. When it lets go, he or she will be him or herself again, barely, at Humanity 1. |
| **Rötschreck** | Mental state | … has given in to the red fear.<br><br>He or she is running from the fire or the sun and won't listen to anyone until he or she feels safe. |
| **Entranced** | Mental state | … has stopped dead in front of a work of art and can't look away. A blow will break the spell. Otherwise it passes in a minute or so. |
| **Entranced** | Mental state | Mental state: entranced by a vampire |
| **Summoned** | Mental state | Mental state: summoned by a vampire |
| **Beckoned** | Mental state | Mental state: called by a vampire |
| **Quelled** | Mental state | Mental state: quelled |
| **Slay the monster** | Mental state | … can't live with the dead any longer. Every time … smiles, he or she sees the teeth. He or she has gone to kill …. |
| **Mesmerized** | Mental state | Mental state: following an implanted order |
| **Seeking Kindred blood** | Mental state | … can't fight the craving any longer.<br><br>He or she will feed on the nearest vampire who can't stop him or her: one asleep, downed or in torpor. Friends aren't safe. |
| **The devoured soul** | Mental state | … is not … right now. The soul he or she devoured has the body, and it answers to the name …. Now and then, under it, you can hear … trying to say something.<br><br>It will feed on whoever holds still and take no orders. In a few hours it will let go. |
| **Transfixed** | Mental state | Mental state: lost in thought |
| **Walking into the dawn** | Mental state | … can't bear what he or she has become. The wolf in him or her still lifts its head at moonrise and listens for Gaia, and hears nothing. He or she is walking out to stand under the open sky and wait for the sun, the last warm thing that still knows his or her name. Arrest him or her or knock him or her down before dawn. |
| **Righteous fury** | Inspiration | … burns with righteous fury. For the next 4 days, he or she will hit harder and faster in melee. |
| **Call of the wild** | Inspiration | … hears the call of the wild. For the next 6 days, he or she will move faster and handle animals better. |
| **Strange clarity** | Inspiration | … sees connections nobody else can. For the next 6 days, he or she will research much faster. |
| **Ear to the walls** | Inspiration | … has heard every rumour going. For the next day, he or she will negotiate and trade far better. |
| **Rapture of beauty** | Inspiration | … is caught up in the beauty of the world, and it shows. For the next 6 days, he or she will be more charming and more beautiful. |
| **Hunger for knowledge** | Inspiration | … wants to learn everything at once. For the next 6 days, he or she will learn and read much faster. |
| **Born to rule** | Inspiration | … is sure of his or her right to command. For the next 6 days, he or she will negotiate better and make arrests more easily. |
| **The shadows' favour** | Inspiration | … feels the darkness on his or her side. For the next 6 days, he or she will aim faster and shoot more accurately. |
| **The flesh obeys** | Inspiration | … knows the body as a sculptor knows clay. For the next 6 days, he or she will tend wounds and operate much better. |
| **The hunter's patience** | Inspiration | … has the patience of a killer on a contract. For the next 6 days, he or she will hunt more quietly and strike faster in melee. |
| **The tempter's tongue** | Inspiration | … knows what everyone wants. For the next 6 days, he or she will be more persuasive and sell drugs for more. |
| **The family business** | Inspiration | … has a head for the family business. For the next 6 days, he or she will get better prices when trading. |
| **Wanderer's luck** | Inspiration | … has the road in his or her blood. For the next 6 days, he or she will move faster and carry more. |
