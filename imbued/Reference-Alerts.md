# Every alert

Every alert this mod can show on the right of the screen.

| Alert | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Hunter can raise a Virtue** | These hunters have 10 Conviction, enough to spend on a point of Virtue. Open their Imbued tab and press Choose a Virtue. Waiting keeps the Conviction. |
| **Hunter can learn an Edge** | These hunters have Virtue to spare and an Edge it can buy. Open their Imbued tab and press Learn an Edge. |
| **Hunter out of Conviction** | These hunters have no Conviction left. Second sight won't open for them, they can't use Edges that cost it, and to every monster they're ordinary people. It comes back after the next threat ends, and every deed of their Creed adds a point. |
| **Clue to examine** | A monster left marks here. Hunters and Bystanders examine clues as research work; each reading adds evidence to the suspect list. Clues fade in 15 days. |
| **Suspect can be named** | These people can be named: the kind is known and two sources place them there, or a hunter knows them for what they are. Being able to name someone doesn't make them guilty. Open a hunter's Imbued tab and press Cases. |
| **The dead may rise** | This map is haunted, and its dead are lying in the open. Any of them may get up at night as a shambler. Bury or burn them. |
| **Righteous fury risk** | These hunters share a map with a colonist they know is a monster, and their Creed can break into righteous fury against it. Keep their mood up, or keep them apart. |
