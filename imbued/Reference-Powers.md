# Every power

Every power in this mod in one list: the ones learned, the ones items and forms give, and the ones only NPCs use. Cooldowns are in game time.

| | Power | Cost | Reach | Cooldown | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Augur.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Augur** |  | Self | 1 h | 3 Conviction, Intelligence + Vision at 8, once a scene. The hunter carries a vision of what's coming here, for a season at most: before the next big threat strikes, a letter names it a few hours ahead. A botch gives a false vision. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Balance.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Balance** |  | 24.9 cells | 4 h | Wits + Zeal against the monster's Stamina + 2. An hour a success: it can't spend blood, Rage or Gnosis, use its powers or mend, wherever it goes. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Becalm.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Becalm** |  | Self | 15 s | 1 Conviction, Manipulation + Mercy at 7. Everyone within 5 cells a point of Mercy, the hunter too, is slow to strike and to hurry for two turns a success, and rages and frenzies end. A frenzied monster may hold on to its frenzy. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Blaze.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Blaze** |  | Self | 15 s | 2 Conviction, Strength + Mercy at 8. The brightest light near her flares, and every monster within 5 cells a success takes its lethal dice in burns: daylight outdoors 10, a sun lamp 8, a campfire 6, a lamp 5, a torch or a flare gun in the hand 3, otherwise 1. Moonlight doesn't count. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Bluster.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Bluster** |  | Self | 4 h | For four hours, once every few seconds, a shouted word turns aside a melee blow on anyone the hunter can see: her Wits + Mercy at 6 against the attacker's Dexterity + Melee. It counts as the dodge. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Brand.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Brand** |  | Touch | 15 s | Zeal at 6, by touch, on a monster. A level of lethal burning a success, and a mark every hunter can see, even through a disguise: a day at one or two successes, a week at three or four, a season at five or six, a year at seven or eight, and for good beyond. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Burden.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Burden** |  | 24.9 cells | 15 s | Stamina + Zeal against the monster's Stamina + 3. A turn a success it can barely move, for as long as she keeps it in sight. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Burn.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Burn** |  | Self | 15 s | 2 Conviction, Stamina + Zeal at 6. A turn a success, anyone she strikes by hand, or who strikes her, takes lethal dice in burns equal to her successes. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Champion.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Champion** |  | Self | 15 s | 1 Conviction, Appearance + Zeal at 6. A hostile monster who can see her turns to her and no one else, or three for a second point of Conviction, five for a third, spent as the monsters in sight need them. Each resists with Wits. Someone else hurting it frees it. Once a scene for each monster. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Cleave.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Cleave** |  | Self | 4 h | No roll. For four hours, the hunter's melee blows do two dice of extra lethal damage, or Strength + 2 with an empty hand. A real weapon holds for two turns of fighting; after that each turn takes a Zeal roll at 6, or the weapon wears. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Confront.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Confront** |  | 24.9 cells | 4 h | 1 Conviction, Manipulation + Mercy at 5, at a monster. While she keeps it in sight, every turn it means to go for her costs it a point of Willpower for each success; with too little left, it can't. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Delve.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Delve** |  | Self | 1 h | Perception + Vision at 6, once a scene. A letter lists the monsters who passed within eight cells in the last fifteen days, one a success, newest first. Every clue within eight cells is read with her successes, along with what kind of creature left it. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Demand.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Demand** |  | Self | 10 s | No roll. The hunter takes a bruise that no armor stops, and adds her Mercy to her Strength for her next blow or feat of strength. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Discern.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Discern** |  | Self | 1 h | Perception + Zeal at 6. Forty minutes a success: the hunter sees what kind of monster each one in sight is, without spending Conviction, and sees clearly in the dark or blind. It gives no protection to her mind. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Donate.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Donate** |  | Touch | 4 h | 2 Conviction, by touch, on someone of her own side. Pick an Attribute; Mercy + that Attribute at 7 moves a dot of it from her to them a success, for two turns, an hour, five hours, a day or three days by successes. She always keeps one. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Expose.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Expose** |  | Self | 1 h | 2 Conviction, Zeal at 8, once a scene. Every monster near her shows its true face to everyone who can see it: 5 cells at one success, 15 at two or three, 40 at four or five, 65 at six or seven, the whole map beyond. Mortals who see a vampire or a Garou are witnesses to an open breach. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Foresee.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Foresee** |  | Self | 1 day | Intelligence + Vision at 7, once a day. Each success is a second look: her next Edge roll takes the better of two, and her next shot or blow is likelier to land. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Hide.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Hide** |  | Self | 4 h | Wits + Mercy at 6. For four hours, monsters looking for someone to attack pass the hunter by, unless one of them beats her successes with its Perception. She shows herself by attacking, and once one monster has seen her, they all have. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Illuminate.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Illuminate** |  | Self | 1 h | Perception + Mercy at 6. Forty minutes a success: every hunter on the map sees what kind each monster in sight is, without spending Conviction. The monsters see her as a bright light and go for her. Ends Hide. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Insinuate.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Insinuate** |  | 24.9 cells | 4 h | Manipulation + Mercy at 6, at a monster, once a scene each. For four hours its blows, shots and work are a tenth worse a success. At four successes or more it runs. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_LayOnHands.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Lay on hands** |  | Touch | 10 s | No roll. Rejuvenate's touch closes another's worst wound: 1 Conviction for a bruise, 2 for anything worse. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Payback.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Payback** |  | 24.9 cells | 4 h | 5 Conviction, at a monster. Pick a human frailty (it can't mend by its own means and takes its wounds in full) or one of its powers to take away. Manipulation + Mercy against its Stamina + 4, or + 3 for a power. It lasts until the monster leaves her presence. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Pierce.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Pierce** |  | Touch | 1 h | No Conviction, Perception + Zeal at 7, by touch. A person: a letter with their last brush with a monster. A monster: who it has hurt, a week back a success. On a suspect it tells true from false: a monster's case gains her successes and its kind, and an innocent's is closed. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Pinpoint.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Pinpoint** |  | 24.9 cells | 1 h | Perception + Vision at 6, at a monster, once a scene. A letter names one of its kind's weaknesses, and from then on the colony's hunters hit that kind a quarter harder. At three successes she also finds where its kind lairs, and the lair goes on the world map. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Radiate.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Radiate** |  | Self | 15 s | Stamina + Mercy at 6. A turn a success: monsters' attacks on her miss more, a tenth a success, and on one more person within Mercy cells for each success after the first. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Ravage.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Ravage** |  | 24.9 cells | 10 s | Manipulation + Mercy at 7, at someone within Mercy cells. A monster takes a level of lethal burning a success, through any armor; a person is stunned with horror a turn a success. The hunter takes a bruise either way. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Respire.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Respire** |  | 24.9 cells | 10 s | Stamina + Mercy at 6. On someone hurt: a wound closed a success, each paid with a point of Conviction while she has more than one, or else an hour short of breath. On a monster within Mercy cells: its Stamina against her successes, a level of lethal damage for each she has over, and a point of Conviction back for every two, up to her Creed's starting level. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Restore.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Restore** |  | Touch | 1 h | 3 Conviction, Stamina + Vision at 6, one harder for each level of lethal damage (at most 10), on herself or someone she touches, once a scene. Every lethal wound becomes a bruise of the same size, and lost parts grow back. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Smite.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Smite** |  | 24.9 cells | 10 s | 1 Conviction, Dexterity + Zeal at 6, at anyone within Zeal cells. Lethal dice of her Strength plus each success after the first, through any armor. Machines within 6 cells of the strike are scrambled. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Smolder.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Smolder** |  | Self | 15 s | Manipulation + Zeal at 7. A cloud of thick black smoke round the hunter, bigger for each success. Shots into it or through it mostly miss. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Surge.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Surge** |  | Self | 4 h | 1 Conviction, no roll. For four hours her Zeal is spread a dot at a time over her Strength, Dexterity and Stamina, none above 6. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Suspend.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Suspend** |  | Self | 1 h | 2 Conviction, Charisma + Mercy at 8, once a scene. For the hour this map is sealed from every other world: nothing steps sideways into or out of the Penumbra, no spirit is called up, no shadow carries anyone across. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Trail.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Trail** |  | Touch | 1 h | Perception + Zeal at 6, by touch, on a monster. Six hours a success, her second sight marks where it is on the map, and when it leaves she's told. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Ward.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Ward** |  | Self | 4 h | Stamina + Zeal at 6. For four hours, monsters can't come within as many cells of the hunter as she has Zeal unless their Stamina beats her successes, one try each. People can shelter inside, and shots still pass. |
| <img src="img/icons/UI_HTR_Edges_Witness.png" width="32" height="32"> | **Witness** |  | Self | 1 h | Perception + Mercy at 6. Forty minutes a success: the hunter sees what kind of monster each one in sight is, without spending Conviction. Each success past the first shows one of their past meetings with a person, as evidence for the suspect list. |
