# Every event

Every event the storyteller can send, what it means and what to do about it. Most can be switched off or made rarer one by one in the mod settings.

| Event | Kind | From day | Again after | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A cell asks for silver** | Event | 15 |  | A cell asks for silver from your storage. Pays goodwill and xp. |
| **A cell asks for silver nitrate** | Event | 20 |  | A cell asks for flasks of silver nitrate from your storage. Pays goodwill and xp. |
| **A cell asks for stakes** | Event | 15 |  | A cell asks for stakes from your storage. Pays goodwill and xp. |
| **A cell asks to read the case files** | Event | 15 |  | A Hunter-Net cell asks to read your case files. Pays Hunter-Net goodwill and xp. |
| **A cell's captive to hold** | Event | 30 |  | A cell asks you to hold a captured monster for it for a while. Pays goodwill and xp. |
| **A colonist's skill lent to a cell** | Event | 25 |  | A cell asks to borrow one of your colonists for their skill for a while. They walk back in at the end; pays goodwill. |
| **A hunter asks for a lesson** | Event | 15 |  | A hunter of a cell asks one of yours for a lesson in what they know of a monster kind. Pays Hunter-Net goodwill and xp. |
| **A hunter chased by a monster** | Event | 20 |  | A hunter of a cell runs for the colony with a monster behind them and asks to be let in. Saved, the cell owes you help, called in from the Hunter-Net page. |
| **A hunter left bound** | Event | 20 |  | A cell's hunter lies tied up at the edge of your land as bait. Carry them to a bed and cut them loose for goodwill, and the cell owes you help. Whatever tied them may come back. |
| **A hunter wanted by a cover-up** | Event | 25 |  | A hunter wanted by a cover-up asks you to hide them for a while. Kept safe, the cell owes you help. |
| **A hurt hunter asks for a doctor** | Event | 20 |  | A hurt hunter of a cell asks your doctor to patch them up. Pays goodwill and xp. |
| **A meeting to host: Creed summit** | Event |  | 30 days | A cell writes to offer you a Creed summit to host. At most once a year. |
| **A meeting to host: great hunt** | Event |  | 30 days | A cell writes to offer you a great hunt to host. At most once a year. |
| **A meeting to host: regional meet** | Event |  | 30 days | A cell writes to offer you a regional meet to host, once that meeting opens. At most once a year. |
| **A new imbued to foster** | Event | 30 |  | A cell asks you to take in a newly imbued hunter and teach them. Pays goodwill and xp. |
| **Cell under attack** | Event | 10 | 20 days | A Hunter-Net cell a caravan's reach away is holding out against the walking dead and calls for help, as a quest. Taken, the cell holds three days for you to arrive. |
| **First horror returns** | Event |  |  | Once a season, the monster that made your hunters what they are comes back with more followers each time. Below half health it runs again unless you finish it. A monster let go on its word may come back the same way. |
| **Hunted stranger** | Event |  |  | For a colony with no hunters: someone who was just imbued runs onto your land at night, the monster that woke them on their trail. Take them in and they join as a hunter, and the monster comes for them a few hours later as their first horror. This is the usual way a colony meets the hunters. |
| **Hunted stranger's pursuer** | Event |  |  | The monster that followed the hunted stranger arrives, alone, after dark. It came for the one who got away and won't leave without them. Only sent by the hunted stranger's letter. |
| **Hunter-Net caravan** | Visitors |  |  | Hunter-Net's own caravan, selling the arsenal and what works on monsters. Only once your standing with the net is 40 or more. |
| **Mentor's visit** | Event | 5 | 15 days | A veteran hunter visits a colony hunter with the Mentor Background, more often for more dots. They bring either a monster kind's weakness (your hunters hit it a quarter harder) or the lair of a monster you know. |
| **Messengers' errand** | Event |  | 15 days | About once a season the Messengers speak through every screen: a spot is marked on the home map. Send any hunter there before dusk, or let it fade. |
| **Most wanted** | Big threat |  | 20 days | One of the Most Wanted passes near the colony. A monster that has never crossed you walks through unless you harm it; one that has, or the walking dead, comes for the colony. |
| **Revenant visit** | Event | 15 | 20 days | A revenant or one of the hidden walks in among ordinary visitors of a friendly faction, back for one colonist. It prowls two nights and strikes on the third. The letter reads like any visitor's. |
| **Shambler raid** | Big threat | 8 |  | The dead dig out of the ground at the map's edge at night and come for the living. More often where no vampire or Garou draws other enemies. One raid in six has a necromancer walking behind it: kill them and the dead fall. |
| **The Avengers ask for a monster put down** | Event | 20 |  | The Avengers ask one of their Creed in the colony to put a monster down. Pays more the stronger that hunter's Edges. |
| **The Defenders ask for a place guarded** | Event | 20 |  | The Defenders ask one of their Creed in the colony to guard a place. Pays more the stronger that hunter's Edges. |
| **The Innocents ask for a monster spared** | Event | 20 |  | The Innocents ask one of their Creed in the colony to spare a monster. Pays more the stronger that hunter's Edges. |
| **The Judges ask for a ruling** | Event | 20 |  | The Judges ask one of their Creed in the colony for a ruling. Pays more the stronger that hunter's Edges. |
| **The Martyrs ask for a risk taken** | Event | 20 |  | The Martyrs ask one of their Creed in the colony to take a risk for others. Pays more the stronger that hunter's Edges. |
| **The Redeemers ask for a monster talked round** | Event | 20 |  | The Redeemers ask one of their Creed in the colony to talk a monster round. Pays more the stronger that hunter's Edges. |
| **The Visionaries ask what the Messengers want** | Event | 20 |  | The Visionaries ask one of their Creed in the colony to work out what the Messengers want. Pays more the stronger that hunter's Edges. |
| **Town rumour** | Event | 10 | 15 days | Word of an attack in a nearby town: a case to take and investigate. Only while you have a hunter and no case already taken. |
| **Two cells ask for neutral ground** | Event | 25 |  | Two quarrelling cells ask to meet at the colony on neutral ground. If both leave unharmed, it pays goodwill and xp. |
