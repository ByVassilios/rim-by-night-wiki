# The sworn cell

Throw the colony in with one cell and its imbued stop being a headcount. Each has a personality, means, a goal of its
own and an opinion of you that moves with what you do. They hand you work, owe you debts, hunt beside you and, if
they come to hate you, work against you.

## Throwing in

The Hunter-Net page of the Hunters window names the nearest cell the colony knows, with **Swear to this home**. You
need:

- a colony imbued,
- the cell met and Hunter-Net on speaking terms with the colony.

One cell at a time. Its roster fills to 8 to 12 named imbued from its hunters, and the most seasoned leads. New
pages appear on the Hunters window's rail: **People**, **Council** and, while a war runs, **War**.

Leaving costs 20 goodwill with Hunter-Net. Loyal hunters drop to -5 regard, and the debts the cell owes you shrink
a size. A cell that falls frees the colony with a letter.

## Debts

Every cell the colony knows can owe it debts, trivial, minor, major or life, as Kindred courts do. A cell's mission
pays one when it's done: trivial for an early colony, minor once raids come at a mid-game size, major for the hardest.
Once you throw in with a cell, its hunters owe you debts for their own errands too.

Spend them on the **Debts** page of the Hunters window. **Ask** lists what a cell does for a debt, and a bigger debt
covers a smaller service: two plain hunter's weapons (trivial), a few of its hunters at the door or a lead that opens a
case (minor), one of its better weapons (major), a season's training, 8 experience and a point of Conviction for every
colony hunter (life). **Ledger** lists each debt with who owes it and why; **Call in** on a row picks what that debt
pays for. Trained dogs bought from a cell are paid in debts too. Leaving a sworn cell shrinks its debts a size.

## People

The People page lists every named imbued: role, cell (Creed for those in none) and regard, with a filter for friends, rivals and
enemies. Click a hunter to see their personality, means, goal, friends and dislikes. You only see a hunter's personality and means once you've **met** them: talked to them in their
town or finished a mission they gave.

| Regard event | Change |
|---|---|
| A mission done for them | +2 |
| A mission done for a friend of theirs | +1 |
| A step of their goal done | +3 |
| A gift from their row | +1 per 500 silver, up to +3 a season |
| Their mission refused or let lapse | -1 (Proud -3) |
| A mission done against them or a friend | -3 |
| Betraying their goal | -10, for good |

Regard drifts a point toward 0 every season.

### Personalities

| Personality | How they pay and behave |
|---|---|
| Greedy | silver x0.7, trust x1.5 |
| Cautious | silver and trust x1.25, but the biggest debt they'll owe is a size smaller |
| Loyal | everything x1.25 while you never cross them or their friends; betrayal makes an enemy for good |
| Conniving | pays with a debt another hunter owes them |
| Cowardly | one more open mission, aimed at their rivals; pay x1.2 |
| Proud | trust x1.5, silver x0.8; refusals cost 3 regard |
| Zealous | only the hunt; those pay x1.5 |
| Generous | everything x1.3; now and then asks one back |
| Vengeful | bad blood never fades; an enemy at -5 |
| Burned | regard grows half as fast |
| Driven | asks for fights, and pays x1.3 for them |

### Means and goals

Every imbued of a cell sits on its council, so each starts with some means. A poor hunter offers one mission at a
time and pays x0.6, a comfortable one two at x1.0, a rich one three at x1.4.

Each hunter wants something, hidden until they count you a friend (regard +6) or you have a hold over them. A goal
is three steps: two missions bent to it, then the prize. Keeping the prize is a betrayal.

| Goal | What you get for it |
|---|---|
| Collector | a hunter's weapon |
| Lore hunter | a dead cell's casebook (experience for an imbued reader) |
| Power seeker | silver by its means, and its vote for a year |
| Revenge | a major debt and the enemy's goods |
| Freedom | its regard stays at +6 or more for good |
| Childe | a minor debt now, a new hunter on your side later |
| Mercy | each colony imbued learns, 8 experience |
| Kingmaker | the new Leader starts at regard +6 |
| Homecoming | another cell, met |
| Buried secret | a life debt |

## Cells of the local hunt

The hunters round a cell work in smaller cells of their own: two to four, each around its strongest hunter, the one leading the local hunt first. Each
has a temperament that sets what it wants, what it pays in, which others it gets on with and which it feuds with. The
**Cells** page of the cell's window shows them as cards: temperament, members, power out of 20 in ten cells, and a bar
for what they think of you. While you're sworn there, that bar is the average regard of its members.

Pick one to see its leader, members, allies and feuds, and **Ask them for work**: one of their own missions, once every
five days, shown in the Quests tab to accept there. Done, the cell gains a point of power and 2 regard. A
cell that thinks -5 of you or worse gives no work.

When a rival comes within two power of the leader's cell and stays there for 30 days, it tries to take over the local hunt. The
showdown comes 20 to 40 days later: the hunters round it pick a side. Each side's chance is its power against the other's; a deciding mission
done for a side counts two. **Back** a side from the page: 6 standing if it wins, 6 lost if it doesn't, and the winners'
regard moves the same way. Plotters who win take the lead, and nobody plots again for 60 days.

| Temperament | Wants | Missions |
|---|---|---|
| Zealous | every monster dead | a monster off their hands, covering their tracks |
| Merciful | the innocent kept out of it | a shared case, covering their tracks |
| Visionary | to know why they were chosen | a shared case, a monster off their hands |
| Wary | to stay alive and unknown | covering their tracks |

## Roles

Under **Your seats** on the Hunter-Net page, once Hunter-Net's goodwill is 25 or more, a colony imbued can take a
role. A role held by a hunter makes that hunter your rival. The Leader is chosen by the cell's vote: the cell's
average regard for the colony must be +4 or more, and the colony must have done more missions for it than anyone.

| Role | Duty each season |
|---|---|
| Leader | vote and pick the next target |
| Researcher | one case or lore mission |
| Contact | chase one lead |
| Muscle | one raid |
| Medic | shelter one wounded hunter at the colony |

A duty kept adds 3 trust and a dot. Three missed in a row and the role is lost, with 10 trust, to the next in line.
The hunter's own tab shows the role and when its duty is due, and an alert warns two days ahead.

## The council

Each season the cell votes on one or two motions: vetting new hunters, going after the monsters' home, hunting every
monster or leaving the quiet ones, paying into the fund. A colony imbued with a role votes too.

## Enemies inside

Rivals slander the colony or stymie your next mission with their own people. Enemies send hired raiders. Capture one
and question them in prisoner talk to learn who sent them, then **Accuse** that hunter.

## War

With Kindred or Garou loaded, a war runs against the nearest court or sept the cell would hunt. Its score starts at
50: a season of incursions, two of raids on their havens and lairs while you hold your safehouse, armory, informant
and lab, then the final assault at 70, the desperate defence at 30, or a truce. With Imbued alone there's no war.

The skirmishes come a few weeks apart. A war mission you let lapse is fought without you: the cell's members against the enemy's, by their strength, and members can die in it. Hover over the stages, the score and the holdings on the War page for what each one needs.

## Backgrounds

The Backgrounds page on a hunter's tab buys rated Backgrounds with standing at the sworn cell: the new rating x 2,
never experience. Dots from the Imbuing or the making menu count as bought. Leaving the cell, or the cell falling,
halves every dot. Now and then a rival strikes at one of them, at most once a season: a letter gives six days (more
with Contacts) to answer with a mission or a favour the rival owes, or the dot is lost. The "Rival strikes on Backgrounds" setting makes strikes rarer or more common, or turns them off.

| Background | Each dot |
|---|---|
| Allies | a friend in a town, and armed mortals to call on any map with enemies on it; they never witness anything |
| Contacts | a person in a town, a day more to answer a strike, and a chance each season to strike back at a rival |
| Fame | more tavern guests and regulars |
| Influence | a caravan from the cell's town once a season |
| Mentor | more visits from an older hunter |
| Resources | 100 x dots squared silver a season |
| Arsenal | a crate of hunter weapons worth 150 silver a season, and 3% better prices with hunter-net |

## Settings

On the Hunting page of the Imbued settings: the sworn cell on or off, the war chance, enemy acts a season and how
fast regard fades. Dev mode has every step under "The Rim by Night: Spotlight".
