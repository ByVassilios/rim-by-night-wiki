# Every setting

Every setting in Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, with what it does.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Imbued among the wandering hunters** | About one in four of the travellers and order hunters is imbued, with two or three Edges, and sees through a vampire's mask. Off, they're all ordinary people, as in a game without Imbued. |
| **A hunted stranger comes** | A colony with no hunters gets a stranger running from a monster by night, some time between day 15 and day 40, and again every 30 to 60 days until one is taken in. Taken in, the stranger joins as a hunter, and a few hours later the monster comes after them as their first horror. Off, the stranger still turns up now and then as an ordinary event. |
| **Bait calls lone monsters** | A few nights after an animal or a corpse is bound to a bait stake, one monster comes for it: a shambler, or with Kindred or Garou loaded a young vampire (generation 10 to 13, or a shovelhead) or a fomor. With Garou, one in ten is a Black Spiral Dancer. It keeps away while anyone of the colony within 12 cells can see the stake, feeds there for a few hours, then leaves. Off, the stake only turns monster raids already on the map. |
| **Chance of the Imbuing** | How likely a colonist who sees a monster hurt someone is to wake as a hunter. The mod's chance is 2%. It is doubled for the brave, kind or tough and halved for a wimp. A near miss leaves a Bystander. |
| **Hunters before the Imbuing slows** | Once the colony has more hunters than this, the next one wakes at most once a season. |
| **Hunters hold True Faith** | Every new hunter wakes with full True Faith. Off, a hunter has only the Faith it held before. |
| **Fifth Edges from the hunter's own Creed** | A level 5 Edge can only come from the hunter's own Creed, with its primary Virtue at 10. Off, any Creed's, with the same Virtue. |
| **Elders hide from second sight** | An old vampire or a great Garou takes a Virtue roll to see for what it is. Off, second sight sees every monster. |
| **The walking dead** | Unburied corpses can rise on a haunted night. Off, the dead stay down. |
| **An exposed monster turns its town** | When a hunter names or exposes a monster in a town, its own kind there turn on the hunters, and in an ordinary town the others hiding there may too. Off, nobody takes it up. |
| **Exposure gain** | Scales how much the colony's Exposure rises for each outsider who leaves having seen a hunter's Edge at work. |
| **Exposure fading** | Scales how fast the colony's Exposure falls back each day as people forget. |
| **Case rewards** | The silver and goodwill a town pays for a solved case, against the mod's own. |
| **Edges camera shake** | How hard big Edges shake the camera: Smite landing, mostly. At 0% the camera stays still. |
| **Edges sky flashes** | Big Edges flash the sky in their colour, and storms tint it while they build. Off, the sky keeps its own light. |
| **Notes on the Hunters' Board** | How often a new note goes up on the Hunters' Board. At 100% it is one every one to three days. |
| **Most Wanted** | A handful of monsters Hunter-Net most wants dead roam the world map, one kind for each monster mod you have loaded and the walking dead's worst. They roam either way, but only a colony with an imbued is told of them, sees them on the map or is thanked for one. Turned off, they leave the world map; turned back on, a new list arrives in one letter. |
| **The hand-over** | A hunting band with a Redeemer or a Judge stops short of a colony that keeps monsters and asks for one before it attacks. |
