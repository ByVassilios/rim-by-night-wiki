# Every thought and mood

Every thought and mood this mod adds. Mood is the change to mood; "opinion" ones change what one pawn thinks of another.

| Thought | Mood | Lasts | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A case solved** | 1 |  | The hunters put one of them down. It's on the board. We can fight back. |
| **A dead one laid to rest** | 4 | 5 days | It came back for something, and we gave it a way to stop. It went down quietly. |
| **A hunter spoke against them** | -8 opinion | 15 days |  |
| **A hunter spoke for them** | 5 opinion | 15 days |  |
| **A wall of cases** | 3 |  | The board is filling up. Towns for miles around know our hunters' names. |
| **A witch hunt** | -3 | 3 days | One of the hunters accused someone of being a monster, and they weren't. Who's next? |
| **Bad news on the board** | -3 | 1 days | Another cell hurting, another name gone. The notes on the board read like a list of the dead. |
| **Called me a monster in front of everyone** | -30 opinion | 30 days |  |
| **Called me a monster** | -10 opinion | 15 days |  |
| **Cases solved** | 2 |  | There are clippings all over the board now. Every one is a monster that won't hurt anyone again. |
| **Conviction spent** | -6 |  | I can't see them any more. They could be anyone, anywhere, and I'd never know. |
| **Cut up a living person** | -25 | 60 days | They were alive and they were only a person. I can still hear them. |
| **Cut up a person's corpse** | -10 | 30 days | They weren't a monster. They were just somebody, and I took them apart on a table. |
| **Good word from the cells** | 3 | 1 days | The other cells had kind words for us on the board. We're not alone out here. |

| **Saw the dead walk** | -6 | 1 days | It was dead. It was walking. I keep telling myself I didn't see that. |
| **Talked to it like a person** | -3 | 3 days | I sat there and talked to that thing as if it could be saved. What if I'm wrong? What if I'm right? |
| **The hunters' legend** | 4 |  | There's no room left on the board. Whatever is out there in the dark should be afraid of us. |
