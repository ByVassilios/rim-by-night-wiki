# Every mission and quest

Every mission a court, sept, cell or consilium can give, and every quest these mods offer.

| Mission | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Where the dead lie down** | Travel to where the walking dead go to ground and put down every one of them. |
| **The sleeper's house** | The cell at *asker* followed one of the undead home to *site*. It sleeps there through the day behind blacked-out windows, and the people it feeds on guard the door and call it family.<br><br>- Kill or drive off everyone there |
| **The beast's den** | The cell at *asker* found a shapeshifter's den at *site*: bones in the yard, claw marks on the doorframes, and people inside who serve the same thing it does. The hunter-net posts on these always say the same thing: don't go in without silver.<br><br>- Kill or drive off everyone there<br>- Bring silver |
| **The marked hunter** | Hide a hunter from the cell at your colony for a while and keep them alive. What marked them will come looking. |
| **The stakeout** | Lend one of your hunters to the cell for some days to watch a house in its town. They come home when the days are up. |
| **The dead cell's notes** | Travel to a dead cell's safe house and put down everything there. The casebook inside is yours to keep. |
| **Newly woken** | Shelter someone newly imbued at your colony for a while and keep them alive. The dead will come for them. |
| **The grave that won't stay shut** | Travel to a grave where something the other cell couldn't finish waits, and put it down. |
| **Cleaning up after the hunt** | Lend one of your hunters to another cell for some days, to clean up after a hunt that went loud. |
| **A shared case** | Lend one of your hunters to another cell for some days, to work a case with them. |
| **The dead walk at *site*** | A cell is calling for help on hunter-net, the posts coming fast and badly spelled. The dead have come out of the ground around *site*, and the cell's hunters can't hold out alone.<br><br>- Take the call and the cell will hold for three days while your caravan gets there<br>- Put the dead down while some of the hunters still stand<br><br>+ Silver from the cell, and its gratitude |
| **The hunt for *monster*** | The trail of *monster* leads to *site*. The other cells have passed on everything they have: photographs, a floor plan, a list of the missing. The lair is a caravan's trip away, and whatever lives there is waiting.<br><br>- Clear it out<br><br>+ Conviction for every hunter there when the last of them falls<br>+ Whatever the lair holds |
| **Whispers from *town*** | *found*<br><br>Nobody in *town* is saying what did it. Take a caravan there and the hunters can look for what it left behind, ask the townsfolk what they saw, and name it once they know.<br><br>If nobody comes by *deadline*, it will kill again. |
| **One who saw too much** | Keep a witness from the cell at your colony for a while and keep them alive. The cell can't swear the witness is what they seem. |
| **Something in the crate** | Hold one of the walking dead as your prisoner until the cell comes for it. It must not die, escape or go free before then. |
| **Another pair of hands for *asker*** | Lend one of your hunters to the cell for some days to clear a nest. They come home when the job is done. |
| **Arms for the long night** | Bring the goods the cell is short of to it by caravan before the deadline. |
