# Character sheet

## Backgrounds

At the Imbuing a hunter gets the book's five dots of Background, spread at random over its nine.
Only the five that change play are kept, so most hunters have two or three dots between them.

| Background | What it does |
|---|---|
| Destiny | Once a day for each dot, a botched Edge roll is rolled again |
| Arsenal | 3% better prices a dot when this hunter trades with a [hunter cell's](Hunter-Net.md) post, and a crate of hunter weapons worth 150 silver a dot each season. A hunter who starts with it packs an extra hunter weapon a dot: a stake crossbow, a fire lance, an aspergillum, an incendiary shotgun |
| Mentor | An older hunter visits now and then with a monster's weakness or the lair of a monster the colony knows. More dots, more visits |
| Contacts | One person a dot in a town near the colony, who tells the hunter what they see. See [Contacts and allies](Hunter-Net.md#contacts-and-allies) |
| Allies | One friend a dot in a town near the colony. They count as contacts too, with the same favours |

Hunters from a save made before Contacts and Allies came in roll those two dots once, the first time the save loads.

Arsenal, Mentor, Contacts and Allies sit on the tab's Backgrounds page, beside the Backgrounds every kind shares:
Fame, Influence and Resources, and Mentor, Contacts and Allies doing more there. They rise only with standing at the
[sworn cell](The-Sworn-Cell.md#backgrounds), never with experience. Destiny shows lower on the same page, with Awareness, and is never
bought.

## Awareness

The feel for when something isn't human. A hunter gains a dot for every three monsters they've come
to know, up to 5. Each dot is a die more when second sight meets a monster too strong for it.

## Sworn foe

Once, a hunter can swear against one kind of monster, from Swear against a kind... on the Hunt page of her tab. It
can't be changed. Her blows on that kind do 10% more, counted with the colony's files under their 25% ceiling.
Tracking it by its footsteps and Pinpointing it get a die more; tracking or Pinpointing any other kind gets a die
less.

## Attributes and Willpower

A hunter has the same nine Attributes as everyone in The Rim by Night, read from their skills and
health. Experience raises them: a dot costs four times the current rating, up to 5. Each dot past an
ordinary person's gives the same small bonuses it gives Kindred and Garou (melee damage for
Strength, dodge for Dexterity, and so on).

Willpower is the ordinary kind. It pays for mundane feats, never for an Edge's cost, and a dot costs its
current rating. The **Willpower** habit on the tab says when the hunter spends it:

| Setting | What happens |
|---|---|
| Never spend | Willpower is never spent on rolls |
| To resist the mind | The default. A point goes against a monster's power over the mind (Dominate, a Gift of fear, a Mind spell) and makes it one step harder |
| Spend freely | As above, and a point on each Edge roll and each Virtue roll to sway someone, for one sure success |

## Ambitions

Each colony hunter works toward one goal at a time, taken from the hunt. A letter offers three, and
one of them suits the hunter's Creed. The tab shows the goal and how far along it is, and so does
the Quests tab. Only what happens after it is taken counts. A met ambition pays its experience the
same day, and a new letter comes the day after. There is no deadline and no failure. Drop one, or
turn all three down, and another letter comes in five days.

| Goal | Who draws it | What to do | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Close a case** | Anyone | See one of the colony's cases through to the one behind it. | 5 |
| **Destroy a monster** | Anyone | Kill two monsters with your own hands. | 5 |
| **Save someone** | Anyone | Get someone away from a monster that has hurt them, or stand over them while they're down. | 5 |
| **Redeem a monster** | Anyone | Talk a captured monster into turning from what it is. | 7 |
| **Find a lair** | Anyone | Get a lead to where a monster sleeps. | 3 |
| **Hold the line** | Defender | Go a whole season without a colonist dying. | 5 |
| **Spare a monster** | Innocent | Let a beaten monster live, or let one go on its word. | 5 |
| **Take the blow** | Martyr | Stand between a monster and someone who is down. | 5 |
| **Three monsters** | Avenger | Kill three monsters with your own hands. | 7 |
| **Unmask them** | Judge | Show two monsters for what they are, to yourself or to everyone. | 5 |
| **Mend the hurt** | Redeemer | Heal two people a monster has hurt. | 5 |
| **Learn the truth** | Visionary | Learn three things about monsters: from a body, a book, a captive or a case. | 5 |

## Experience

A hunter earns 0.3 experience a day, plus some for each kill, twice as much for a monster. The
Experience gained setting scales it. Virtues, Conviction and Edges never come from experience: those
come from deeds and cashing in Conviction.
