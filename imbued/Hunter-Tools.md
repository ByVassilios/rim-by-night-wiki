# The table, the horror and the last stand

Hunters are mortals, so most of this rewards getting ready before the fight. All of it works without
Kindred or Garou.

## The autopsy table

Build it from the Misc tab. Then lay a subject on it: a downed monster, a staked vampire, any downed
person, any prisoner, a person's corpse, or a piece of a first horror. Animals, insects and machines don't count. Right-click the subject and pick **Carry to the
autopsy table** (anyone can do this), or use vanilla's Carry while drafted. Straps stop a downed subject
crawling off and hold a prisoner down until someone carries them away. Nobody sleeps on the table.

**Hold the subject**, on the table's buttons and on by default, keeps a strapped prisoner there: wardens don't
take them back to the cell, and they bring food to the table. Turn it off and wardens take the prisoner back.

### Bills

What happens to the subject is set on the table's **Bills** tab, as on any work bench: **Examine**, **Test for
weakness**, **Dissect** or **Study a horror's piece**. A hunter or Bystander on research work does the bill. Repeat counts, pausing, the search radius and the filter work as on any
bench.

- **What's on the table first.** A prisoner, a staked monster, a corpse or a horror's piece lying on the table goes
  to the first bill that fits it. The corpse filter is only asked of corpses.
- **Fetching.** With the table empty, a bill brings the nearest corpse its filter takes. Test for weakness also
  brings the nearest item of each method ticked under **Test with** in its **Details**: silver, fire (chemfuel) or holy water. Untick a method to keep its item.
  A horror's piece is carried there by hand.
- **The filter.** A new bill takes supernatural corpses only. Untick or tick the kinds under **Corpses >
  Supernatural corpses**, and **allow mortal corpses** under humanlike corpses.
- **Test for weakness.** Each Test tries one method on the subject's kind. A miss is ruled out for that kind, colony-wide, and the next Test brings a method not yet tried. A hit on two successes gives the weakness letter, and so does ruling out all three. Either way the kind is solved and Test bills pass it over. A hit on one success only shows it reacted, so that method stays open. Only the solving Test gives the file its point. Which method hits: vampires (and ghouls) fire and holy water; Garou, Ratkin, Gurahl and Mokole silver; the walking dead fire; fomori holy water. Corax, Nuwisha, Ananasi, Kitsune and necromancers take none of the three.
- **Spent subjects.** A subject that has given all its points is passed over, and Examine looks at each subject
  once. After an Examine a corpse is free to be hauled off.

The same filters are in every stockpile, shelf and bill in the game, so a crematorium can burn only plain people and
a freezer can keep only the monsters. **Supernatural corpses** sits under Corpses, beside humanlike corpses, with a
row for each kind whose mod is loaded: vampires, ghouls, werewolves, fomori, Fera, hunters, the walking dead,
necromancers and mages, and **other supernatural corpses** for anything else.

| Procedure | Time | Roll | Gives | To the subject |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Examine | 15 minutes | Perception + Intellectual at 6 | Monster or person, the kind on 2 successes, half a point | Nothing |
| Test | 30 minutes, uses up one silver, chemfuel or holy water | Perception + Intellectual at 7 | The kind's weakness on 2 successes, 1 point | One wound of the item's damage |
| Dissect | 3 rolls, an hour each | Stamina + Intellectual at 7, added up | Up to 2 points, and proof for a case | See below |

Holy water comes in flasks: three from 20 silver and 10 steel at a smithy, once Smithing is researched, or bought from a trader who stocks the arsenal. The item must be on the map and not forbidden.

A failed Examine says nothing. A botch reads a person as a monster or a monster as a person.

| Dissection total | Corpse | Living or staked |
|---|---|---|
| Any roll botches | Ruined, no points | Wakes if it can; a stake slips |
| 0 to 2 | Ruined, 1 point | Dies, 1 point |
| 3 to 5 | Used up, 1 point | Dies, 2 points |
| 6 or more | Used up, 2 points | Lives, badly wounded and missing a part, 2 points |

### Waking up

Before each procedure, a subject may get up:

- Staked: never, unless a botch slips the stake.
- In torpor with no stake: 10% for Examine, 25% for Test, 50% for each Dissect roll.
- A downed Garou: the same, doubled while it mends.
- A dead one's corpse: 10% a procedure while a necromancer or revenant is on the map.

### Files

The colony keeps one file for each kind of monster, and one subject gives at most 2 points ever.

| Points | What it does |
|---|---|
| 1 | The file shows on the Imbued tab |
| 3 | Every colonist's blows on that kind do 10% more |
| 6 | 25% more |

Two subjects of one clan or tribe add 10% against that clan or tribe. Nothing adds up past 25%,
counting Pinpoint. A fomor has its own file. A ghoul gives half a point to the vampire file.

### The notes

The first study writes a book of hunter's notes beside the table. A hunter or Bystander can right-click it to
write a copy, which holds what was known that day. The bonuses use the most complete copy on a home map. Lose
every copy and the files go to zero, and subjects you've used stay used, so keep a copy somewhere safe.

### Cutting up a person

A person gives no points. Examining one costs nothing.

- A corpse: only the one cutting minds, at -10 mood for 30 days. A hunter doesn't mind unless their
  beliefs forbid it. Psychopaths don't mind.
- Someone alive: they die, the one cutting takes -25 mood for 60 days, the colony reacts as to an organ
  harvest, and a hunter loses 2 Conviction. Their faction never learns of it.

A dissection is also proof: a monster's case gains the successes and its kind, and an innocent's closes.
Any finding counts as a Visionary's deed. A Redeemer who dissects a living monster pays as for an execution.

## The Hunters' Board

A cork board of clippings two cells wide, built from 20 wood (Misc). Each solved case adds one, and hovering over it lists them:
when, who and where. Colonists who see it feel better the more cases it holds: +1 mood at 1 solved case, +2 at 3,
+3 at 6, +4 at 11.

The board also takes the other cells' notes: see [the Hunters' Board](Captured-Monsters-and-the-Board.md#the-hunters-board).

## Keepsakes

Two solved cases in five leave something of the culprit's, named for it: a tooth, a ring, a claw, a locket, a key or
a hat. It says who it was taken from and where, and it's nice to look at (beauty 12).

## Odd relics

Now and then a reward or a ruin turns up something odd: a yellowed knucklebone, a knotted rope,
a water-stained notebook. Right-click it and pick **Study the odd relic** to carry it to a case
board or a research bench and work out what it is. A hunter who sees it with second sight knows
it at a look.

- **The saint's knucklebone**, carried, ends a frenzy, a fear or a madness that no mood caused, once
  a day.
- **The hangman's rope** is thrown over a monster and holds it like a net. It holds two, then
  needs a week to get its grip back.
- **The dead hunter's notebook** gives one clue on an open case, then it's used up.

## The first horror

The hostile monster behind a hunter's Imbuing gets a name, and the game remembers it. All three hunters
in A fresh cell share one. A monster from inside the colony, or one that wasn't hostile, leaves no horror.

Below half health it runs for the edge of the map, though you can still kill it if you trap it or burst
it down. It comes back once a season, after dark, leading a raid with more followers each time.

A part cut off it lands as its own item, named for it, at any gore setting, and doesn't rot. Study it on the
table and the colony's blows on this monster do 10% more, up to the same 25%.

Kill it, and every hunter it made fills up to their Creed's starting Conviction and gains a point of Virtue.

## Bait

Build a **bait stake** and bind a live animal or a corpse to it: right-click the body and pick **Bind to
the bait stake**. The next group of monsters on the map goes for the bait first. It takes the body when
it gets there, and then attacks as usual. If it gets hurt on the way, it forgets the bait.

A live animal is walked over on a rope. To untie the body, select the stake and press **Release**.

Ordinary raids ignore the stake, and it doesn't bring a raid sooner.

A few nights after the binding, the stake also calls one monster of its own, at night: a shambler, or with
Kindred or Garou loaded a young vampire (generation 10 to 13, or a shovelhead) or a fomor. With Garou, one in
ten is a Black Spiral Dancer, which is far more dangerous. It waits a way off while anyone of the colony, or a turret, within
12 cells can see the stake, so hide behind a wall or further back. When nobody is watching it creeps up, feeds for
2 to 4 hours and leaves. Attacked, it fights back; at half health, or when the day comes, it runs. One body draws
one lone monster. The Imbued settings can turn this off.

## The last stand

A hunter a monster downs, with 3 or more Conviction, burns all of it by themselves, once in a fight.

| Creed | The last stand |
|---|---|
| Avenger | Gets up and fights on for half an hour |
| Defender | Monsters nearby can attack only them for a while |
| Judge | Every monster in sight is exposed and marked, and takes 25% more damage for the rest of the fight |
| Innocent | Monsters nearby stop and stare |
| Martyr | Heals everyone nearby |
| Redeemer | The nearest monster runs or stops fighting |
| Visionary | Shows that monster's lair on the world map |

If a monster kills a hunter, one Bystander who saw it has an even chance of being imbued, whatever the
limits on new hunters say.

## Trained dogs

Tame animals can learn more than guard and attack. The extra roles show on the vanilla Training tab, are taught by a
handler over several lessons, and fade the same way when nobody keeps them up. Each needs **guard** (obedience) first.

| Role | Who can learn it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Guard post | body size 0.4 or more | Holds a spot you pick with its **Guard post** button (or right-click, **Guard this spot**), and goes for any enemy within 8 cells of it |
| Watch | any | Raises the alarm when it sees an enemy, day or night, and wakes everyone asleep. Once an hour |
| Sniff out | an animal with a nose | Barks at anyone within 12 cells who hides what they are. A pawn hidden by a power shows for an hour; a guest or prisoner in disguise is known for good |
| Track | after sniff out | Follows a trail you pick with its **Track** button: a fled prisoner, quarry marked for hunting, an enemy unseen. It stays close and sniffs them out |
| Scouting | a bird that really flies | Circles a spot, shadows someone, or flies out over the land and comes back with what it saw |
| Messenger | a flier, or body size 0.3 or more | Tends one of your town contacts, or asks an allied town for a trade caravan on the comms console's terms (15 goodwill, once every four days) |
| Hunt | a predator | Once a day brings back one or two small animals from round here, about half of them still alive |
| Fight beside | after attack | Defends its master without an order, and fights better at their side for an hour |
| Spirit scout | a familiar only | Looks across to the spirit side for three hours, once a day, and comes back with what waits there |

Selected, a trained animal shows a line to its guard post or to the one it's tracking.

**The kennel** (Misc tab) takes up to six of your animals: they learn half as fast again and forget at half the rate.
Name a **kennel master** on it, and that colonist trains the kennel's animals before any other handling work.

At a case's town, a dog trained to sniff out turns up one more clue, once a case. A tracker can follow the case's
suspects too.

Members of your sworn cell sell trained dogs. **Ask for a trained animal** is on each member's row, paid
with the favours that member owes you: a trivial favour counts 1, a minor 2, a major 3 and a life debt 4. An animal
costs 1, plus 1 a role, plus 1 for every 500 silver it's worth, and comes by drop pod.

## Messengers' errands

About once a season a letter marks a spot on your own map, with a line like "Be at the old well
before dusk." The first hunter to go earns a point of Conviction and finds a survivor, a clue or an
ambush. Ignore it and it fades, with no other cost.

## Sparing

A Redeemer can right-click a downed monster and pick **Spare**. They help it up and it leaves unharmed. It isn't
hostile while it goes, it may come back, and the Redeemer earns the deed.
