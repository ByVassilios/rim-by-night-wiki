# People, factions and creatures

The factions, the kinds of people and the creatures this mod adds.

## Factions

| Faction | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Fresh cell** | Strangers who were Imbued on the same night and have stuck together since.<br><br>The Imbued are ordinary people who heard the Messengers and woke up able to see the monsters hiding among them. Nobody told them why. They have no church, no order and no leaders, only each other and the hunter-net, where cells trade what they have learned. Each hunter follows one of the creeds, and the creeds don't always agree on what to do with what they find. |
| **Hunting party** | Veteran hunters who have done this before, come to finish a job other cells found.<br><br>After a few hunts the Imbued learn what silver, fire and wood do and don't do, which creed to trust with a prisoner, and how little the people they protect want to know. Their names are on the hunter-net, and the monsters keep lists too. |
| **Lone survivor** | The only one who lived through the night the dead came.<br><br>The Messengers spoke while the others were dying, and since then the survivor has seen the walking dead for what they are. Hunters alone don't last long. Somewhere on the hunter-net there are others who have heard the same voices, and the survivor will need them. |
| **Plague doctors** | Doctors who went where the sick were dying and saw what climbed out of the plague pits.<br><br>When the dead get up, someone has to put them down and burn what's left. These hunters came to treat a sickness and found the corpses wouldn't stay buried. They carry herbs, quicklime and more spades than knives. |
| **Raiders** | People who move in silence and don't stop to talk. Nobody has traded with them and come back to say so. |
| **Settlers** | A loose network of people moving messages and goods between far-off posts. They trade what they pick up on the road and don't say who they work for. |
| **Witch-hunters** | Hunters who took up the old tools of the witch-finders.<br><br>The Imbued see the monsters for what they are, all at once and without asking to. These three answered with what their grandparents' grandparents used: cold iron, holy water, salt, a crossbow and a fire. They have no church behind them and no one to tell them they're right. |

## Kinds of people and creatures

Strength is the combat power a raid pays for one of them.

| Kind | Faction | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Cell member** | Settlers | 55 |
| **Hidden** | Raiders | 200 |
| **Hunter** | Settlers | 110 |
| **Necromancer** | Raiders | 100 |
