# The other cells

Hunter cells trade as an ordinary-looking faction whose name, color, people and post labels change every game, so its name tells you nothing.

Other cells of hunters, scattered across the world. They stay hidden until one reaches out: 3 to 8
days after the colony's first hunter, the nearest cell sends a letter naming only itself, and the
cells are allies from then on.

The other cells stay off the world map one by one until you learn of them. A letter or call for help
from a cell shows it, a raid shows where it came from, your hunters can meet its people, and a cell
you know will now and then vouch for another.

Hunters don't get a list of monsters either. A town's inspect line says who lives there only after a
hunter has seen them for what they are on that map: "Found out here so far", as a share of the town,
counting each person once.

## Cells

Four cell settlements, two of them within reach of the colony. Their people are cell members
(Bystanders with guns) and hunters with a Creed. Each cell sells the Core hunter weapons (stake
crossbow, fire lance, aspergillum, incendiary shotgun) with plain supplies, and buys anything of
value. A hunter with [Arsenal](Character-Sheet.md#backgrounds) gets better prices.

## Talking to people

In a cell's town, select a colonist and right-click anyone of the cell to **Talk to** them. The talk
window works as it does in a case: pick what to ask about, then how to put it (Kindly, Firmly,
Threaten, Bribe). Each person answers one question, then wants three days before the next (a
setting). Nobody lies, so a poor roll just gets nothing.

- **Small talk.** The weather, the neighbours, whatever is on their mind. It costs nothing, always
  gets an answer, and can go on all night.
- **What's the news?** Word of the world's cells, courts and septs.
- **Where is...** Hunters speaking to hunters can ask the way to the nearest cell you don't know
  yet. The question only shows up when there is one.
- **Have you seen...** A colonist someone took. The settlement holding them goes on the map.
- **What troubles you?** Something is feeding on a town nearby, and the cell hands you the case.

A hunter can also put **Conviction** behind any question, here or anywhere else the window opens: a
point of Conviction for Charisma + Social at 6 with one success already counted.

A threat costs the town 3 goodwill whatever the roll. A hunter talking in a court's or a sept's town
passes for an ordinary visitor. Asking kindly and bribing are safe. A threat gives it away one time
in five, a failed roll one in ten and a botch one in two. Found out, the town turns on the colony.

The same right-click questions a prisoner that isn't a known monster (**Question ...**), for where it
comes from, what it knows and what it is, and talks a witness out of what they saw.

## When a cell falls

Cells hunt the courts and septs near them, and get hunted back. A cell's hunters have names and outlive
its fall: some die, some go missing, and the rest go to the nearest cell or start a new one. While
the network is short of its cells, new ones are found about twice a year.

- **Exposure.** Each cell has a rating, shown in words once you know it. Every hunt it makes and every
  witness who gets away raises it; lying low lowers it. A more exposed cell is struck more often.
- **Sieges.** When a war is about to break a cell you know, it is under siege for a few days first, and
  a quest lets you go and help.
- **Requests.** The nearest cell you know asks for help through the storyteller: keep a witness for
  two days (about one time in three the witness is one of the hidden dead, and shows it the first
  night), hold a captured walking dead as your prisoner for five days until the cell comes for it,
  lend one of your hunters for five days (they come back with a point of Conviction), or bring
  stakes, holy water, silver nitrate or salt by caravan. Each pays silver and one of the arsenal's
  weapons, with 10 goodwill.
- **The Messengers' errands** show in the Quests tab until the spot fades at dusk.
- **Letters.** A cell you know falling gets a letter, and taking one yourself says so. The other cells
  traffic brings rumors of the rest.
- **Refugees.** Hunters who like the colony and are within reach may ask to join, as an offer in
  the Quests tab open for two days. Your hunters gain
  Conviction against whatever kind of monster did it.

Core's settings, under The world of darkness, have a switch for all of this (off: a fallen home's people just vanish), a bloodiness slider for falls with no battle, and a world pace slider.

## Standing

The colony's standing with Hunter-Net is its goodwill with the cells. Each solved
[case](Investigation-and-Lairs.md#solving-a-case) raises it by 10 to 25, more for a harder case.

| Standing | What it opens |
|---|---|
| 20 | Tips: every four days or so an open case gets a letter from a cell that knows the town. It tells what kind of monster did it, or once that's known, rules out an innocent suspect |
| 40 | A Hunter-Net trader caravan comes by now and then, selling the arsenal, medicine and chemfuel |
| 60 | **Call a cell** in the case window: two hunters come to the case's town or lair, once every ten days |
| 80 | Rumours come from 8 tiles farther, and cases pay a quarter more |

## Warnings

One time in three, a cell sends word of an afflicted raid or a revenant's visit before it comes:
what's coming, and in 6 to 12 hours.

## Calls for help

Now and then a cell 2 to 8 tiles off is attacked by the dead and asks the colony to come. The call is a
quest: take it within a day or two, and the cell's three hunters hold for three days after. Arrive in
time and keep one alive, and the cell pays 300 silver and the net thinks better of you.

## Asking for help

The other cells are an ally, so the comms console's call for military aid reaches them, and a few hunters
come to fight beside the colony.
Without a console, pin **Ask for help** on [the Hunters' Board](Captured-Monsters-and-the-Board.md#your-own-notes).

## Requests

Once the colony knows a friendly cell, the cells and the Creeds write with requests. Each answers with Accept or
Refuse; a refusal is noticed. Every one has its own switch and frequency in the settings.

- **Case files.** Once your hunter notes are worth it (autopsies and copied files), a cell asks to read them for 2 to 6
  nights. The monsters in them may come to burn them.
- **Teaching.** A guest asks to learn a skill one of your colonists has at 10 or more, what your notes know of a
  monster kind, or an Edge one of your hunters has, one lesson a night (right-click the guest).
- **Rescues.** A hunter chased by a monster (it follows after dark), a hunter wanted by a cover-up (the law may come),
  and a hunter left bound at the map's edge (carry them to a bed). Each saved life leaves the cells owing help: the
  **Fighters on call** row on the Overview page counts it, and **Call in** brings a few of the net's hunters to fight.
- **Favours.** Hold a cell's captive monster for a season (it comes drugged by drop pod; carry it to a prison bed),
  foster a new imbued, send stakes, silver or silver nitrate, host two cells at odds for a night, patch up a hurt
  hunter, lend a skilled colonist for a season.
- **Creed requests.** Each Creed asks its own: the Avengers a monster put down (a lead opens), the Defenders a family
  guarded, the Judges a ruling on a monster you hold, the Innocents one spared, the Martyrs a risk taken for another
  cell, the Redeemers a monster talked round, the Visionaries what the Messengers want (a case opens). They pay more
  by the worth of the hunter's Edges, the sum of their levels, in steps at 3, 6 and 10.

A guest hurt or killed in your care costs 10 goodwill.

## Hosting meetings

The Hunter-Net page has an "Ask to host" link for the highest meeting the colony can host now (greyed with the
reason when none is), and a cell writes when one opens. Each is hosted once a year at most.

| Goodwill | Meeting | Nights |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | Regional meet: leads shared round a table, then a hunt planned, which opens a lead | 2 |
| 60 | Creed summit: Mercy, Vision and Zeal argue what the Messengers want, then the net votes on its aim | 2 |
| 80 | Great hunt: cells muster at the colony, plan a strike on a nest (a lair opens) and march out | 3 |

Each needs free beds and meals for every guest and night; the dead may strike on one night.
The host's skill rolls decide each night, and a good showing pays goodwill and xp for the host.

## Contacts and allies

Each dot of Contacts or Allies a hunter has is one person living in a town near the colony. They show on the
**Contacts** page of the Hunters tab, under the town they live in. They need no looking after. Once a week each can be
asked for one favour:

- **Goodwill:** they put in a good word, and the town's faction thinks better of the colony.
- **Sighting:** they name something they saw that wasn't human, and a lead to a lair opens near their town.

A monster that learns one's name ends it. That happens when a hunter lets a captured monster go on its word, and to all
of a hunter's people when the hunter is taken prisoner. The dot finds someone new a season later. A contact whose town
is destroyed is gone too.

## Mentors

A hunter with the Mentor Background is visited now and then by an older hunter, more often for more
dots. The mentor brings either the weakness of a monster kind the colony hasn't pinpointed (from then
on, the colony's hunters hit that kind a quarter harder), or the lair of a monster one of the colony's
hunters knows.

## Hunters in other bands

About one in four of Core's wandering travellers and order hunters is imbued, mostly
Avengers, Defenders and Judges, with two or three Edges. They see through a vampire's mask without a
roll, and a mendicant hunter goes straight for a masked vampire. They use their Edges in a fight. A
setting turns them off.

## Exposure

How much of the cell the world has seen, from 0 to 100, shown on the Hunters tab's Overview. A
mortal from outside the colony who sees one of your hunters use an Edge (1.5) or kill a monster
(4) adds to it once they get away from the map, twice that for a trader. One caravan's story
counts for 10 at most. It falls by about a third of a point a day, slower once it's high.

| Level | Score | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet | under 20 | Nothing |
| Talk | 20 | The Hunter-Net scolds the hunter most to blame: one point of Conviction lost |
| Police | 40 | A patrol asks about a hunter seen with a weapon. Hand the weapon over, or turn them away and the nearest town thinks 15 less of you |
| Press | 60 | Every faction with towns thinks 10 less of you |
| Burned | 80 | The nearest hostile monster home learns where the cell lives and raids within three days (the walking dead, if there's none). Something picks up the cell's trail, and a new case opens |

Once 12 points are laid on one hunter, your sworn cell (or the nearest one you know) asks who did
it. Name them and half of it is cleaned up, take the blame as a colony, or refuse. Standing with
the Hunter-Net moves 5 up, 5 down or 15 down.

## Most Wanted

Hunter-Net's list of the monsters it most wants dead. Five names roam the world map, whether the
colony has an imbued or not. Only a colony with an imbued is told of them.

- **Who is on it.** One kind for each monster mod you have loaded: a vampire with Kindred, a Black
  Spiral Dancer with Garou, a Seer of the Throne with Awakened. With Imbued alone the list holds only
  the walking dead's worst: a necromancer with its shamblers, or a revenant. All of them shrug off
  half of every wound.
- **What it is.** The list never says. Only second sight tells: once one stands on a map with one of
  your imbued, its kind, its followers and its powers show in its file.
- **The mark.** Each is a mark on the world map that moves every few days and shows only within 25
  tiles of a home or a caravan. **Read Most Wanted** on the Hunters window's Overview opens the list.
- **Meeting one.** Send a caravan to its mark to hunt it. It's found where its kind lives: a
  vampire's haven, a werewolf's den, a Seer's safehouse, a necromancer's house or a grave chapel,
  and holds it a few hours before coming out at you. One within 12 tiles can come to the
  colony: a monster that has never crossed it walks through unless harmed or touched by an Edge, and
  the walking dead, or one that knows the colony, attack.
- **The reward.** The imbued who kills one gains 3 Conviction, and the sworn cell gains 10 standing
  (without one, the Hunter-Net's goodwill rises by 10). The kill goes in the chronicle and can be
  the subject of art.

## The tavern

A tavern counter (Furniture tab) works for a hunter colony too. Its **Guests** button lets in the
hunters of the nearest cell you know, besides townsfolk. A party of hunters brings word of a lair
one time in eight.

Their second sight shows them what any guest is, and they hate vampires, werewolves and the Wyrm's
things, so mixing them with Kindred or Garou guests makes for fights. Pick a bouncer or two.
