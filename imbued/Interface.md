# Menus and buttons

## The Imbued tab

Select a hunter and open **Imbued**. At the top are the Creed and primary Virtue, then Conviction and
Willpower as bars. The pages are down the left side. A gold number beside a page counts what you can do there now: a Virtue
to raise, Virtue points for an Edge, or Ambitions to pick from. Click a heading's name to fold the
section under it; the page remembers that until you quit the game.

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Sheet | The Creed, its deeds and its betrayals (hover for more); Mercy, Vision and Zeal as dots, the primary marked, with Virtue points not yet spent on an Edge ringed in gold; Willpower |
| Attributes | The nine Attributes in three columns, Physical, Social and Mental. Hover the next dot for its cost in experience (the header shows how much there is) and click it to buy |
| Edges | Every Edge the hunter has, and **Learn an Edge...** when points are waiting |
| Growth | The hunter's Ambition. Your own hunters only |
| The hunt | The seat the hunter holds at the cell the colony is sworn to, every monster this hunter knows for what it is, and the colony's open files |
| Backgrounds | The Backgrounds every kind shares, then Awareness and Destiny |
| Conduct | The habits: risking Conviction, autocast, Willpower and the fighting style. Click a habit to change it. Your own hunters only |

At the foot: **Roll log**, the colony's last rolls and how each came out, and **Cases**, which opens
the Hunters tab on its cases.

## The Hunters tab

A button on the bottom bar once the colony has a hunter. While the colony has two or more kinds from the Rim by Night mods, only the window used last keeps its button, and a list down the left side of each window names every one with its pages, so a click opens any page of any of them; the Core setting **One bottom-bar button** turns that off. Its six pages:

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Overview | The Hunter-Net's goodwill toward the colony, the nearest or sworn cell, the help cells owe you, then the hunt: how many cases are open, a captive you hold for a cell, and how long the dead stay quiet |
| Cells | Each cell the colony knows, nearest first, with who leads it. Hover a row for how far away it is and how many hunt there. **Open** opens the cell's window; a click on the rest of the row finds it on the world map |
| Cases | The [case window](Investigation-and-Lairs.md#the-case-window) |
| Hunters' Board | The notes and clippings on the colony's [Hunters' Board](Captured-Monsters-and-the-Board.md#the-hunters-board), once one is built. The board's own button opens this page |

The world's news and the colony's own story are on the Chronicle and Our story pages under Rim by Night, at the top of the list down the window's left side. Each page holds the lines of every mod you have loaded; click a mod on its left for only that mod's lines, or a name under it for one colonist or home. Hunters write who was imbued, last stands, monsters put down and dissections there, and the cells' news tells of battles won and lost, new hunters called, hunters gone missing and monsters put down.

## Edge tiles

One tile on the gizmo bar for each path the hunter has an Edge on. Click it for the path's Edges,
pick one, then pick the target. An Edge that can't be used now is greyed, with the reason.

Hover an Edge to see its roll: the Attribute and Virtue with their dice, the total and the
difficulty, or the rule for it before a target is picked ("the target's Stamina + 2"). Conviction
risked shows up only in the roll itself. The roll log puts each roll's parts in a grey line under it,
and an Edge's grimoire page has the formula in a box at the top.

## Marks

While a hunter's second sight is up, every monster in sight has a mark over it. With Discern,
Witness or Illuminate the mark names what kind it is.

## Unleash

A drafted colonist has an **Unleash** button beside Fire at will. It goes after the nearest enemy
anywhere on the map and fights as an enemy of its kind would, Edges first. Anyone with a gun backs away from melee fighters and looks for cover. A pawn scared by a power stays on the map and goes back to the fight when the panic ends. Your orders come first, and once one is done it goes back to the hunt. Undrafting ends it. While it's on, a **Pick target** button chooses the enemy it goes after. Once that enemy is down or gone it picks the nearest one again, and clicking the button clears the target early.

An Edge aimed out of range or out of sight walks the hunter closer, then fires.

## Choice lists

Most menus open a list beside the window you clicked in, or above the gizmo. A long list has filters down the left with a count beside each, and **Can pick only** hides what can't be had yet. Click a row to read about it on the right, then press **Choose**, double-click it, or press Enter. A greyed row says under it what's missing. A list with nothing to read takes the row on one click.

## Alerts

| Alert | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter can raise a Virtue | A hunter has 10 Conviction | Imbued tab, Choose a Virtue. Waiting keeps the Conviction |
| Hunter can learn an Edge | A hunter has Virtue to spare on an Edge | Imbued tab, Learn an Edge |

## Letters

| Letter | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The Imbuing | A colonist is imbued | Read it; the Messengers name the Creed |
| Warning, call for help, mentor | From [the other cells](Hunter-Net.md) | Get ready, send a caravan, or follow the lead |
