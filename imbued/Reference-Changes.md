# What changed

What each update changed, newest first, as the grimoire shows it.

## Update of 14 November 2026

### Hunter-Net
- Cells ask to read the colony's case files once they're worth it, and the monsters in them may come to burn them. Guests ask to learn a skill.
- With Hunter-Net's goodwill the colony can host meetings: a regional meet at 40, where leads are shared and a hunt is planned, and a Creed summit at 60.
### Other changes
- Towns and sites have better rooms, with beds only where people sleep.
- A glossary of hunters' words, underlined wherever an Imbued page uses them.
- See [[RBN_Changes_20261114|the grimoire's own changes]].
