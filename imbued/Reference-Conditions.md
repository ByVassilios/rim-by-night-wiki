# Every condition

Every health condition, state and effect a pawn can carry, as shown on its Health tab.

| Condition | Bad | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Attributes** |  | Strength, toughness and the rest, each dot above a living person's average worth a little. |
| **Attributes** |  | Strength, wits and the rest, raised by hard experience past what this person started with. |
| **Becalmed** | Yes | Everything feels less urgent. Striking out or hurrying takes an effort. |
| **Blustering** |  | Ready to shout a blow aside, on herself or anyone she can see. |
| **Bound** | Yes | Tied hand and foot and left out as bait. Carried to a bed, the ropes can be cut. |
| **Branded** | Yes | A hunter's burning mark, plain to every imbued eye, and no disguise hides it. |
| **Burdened** | Yes | Held to the spot by a hunter's stare. It can still fight, but it can barely move. |
| **Burning** |  | Something like lava runs under her skin. Whoever she strikes by hand, or who strikes her, is burned. |
| **Bystander** |  | Saw a monster for what it was and can't forget it. Knows the monsters are real, though nothing protects them from them, and will stand by the people who hunt them. |
| **Called out** | Yes | A hunter has called it out, and it has eyes for no one else. |
| **Carrying a vision** |  | Has seen what's coming here. When it's close, she'll know what it is. |
| **Cleaving** |  | Her melee blows do extra lethal damage. A real weapon wears under the strain. |
| **Confronted** | Yes | A hunter holds its gaze. Going for her takes more will than it may have. |
| **Demanding** |  | Paid in pain for strength: her Mercy is added to her Strength for the next blow or feat. |
| **Discerning** |  | Sees what kind of monster each one in sight is, and sees clearly in the dark or blind. |
| **Foreseeing** |  | Has seen how the next moments could go. Each second look left makes her next Edge roll, shot or blow likelier to come off. |
| **Hidden** |  | Dead, though nobody would know it. It keeps its skills and its face, and kills for one purpose. The hardest of the afflicted to find and to stop. |
| **Hiding** |  | Monsters looking for someone to attack pass this hunter by, until one of them sees through it or she attacks. |
| **Illuminating** |  | Blazing with a light only the imbued and monsters can see. Every hunter on the map knows the monsters for what they are, and the monsters go for her. |
| **Imbued** |  | Woken by something no one can name. This person sees the monsters hiding among people for what they are, and has been given the means to do something about it. |
| **Insinuated** | Yes | A hunter's question has dragged up something it lost. Its blows, shots and work are worse for four hours. |
| **Lent self** |  | Part of a hunter's self has been lent, or borrowed, for a while. |
| **Marked** | Yes | A dying Judge showed everyone what this is and where to hit it. Every blow lands harder for the rest of the fight. |
| **Necromancer** |  | A living sorcerer who calls the dead out of the ground and holds them. Wherever one walks the dead get up, and when the necromancer dies they fall. |
| **Paid back** | Yes | A hunter has cut it down to size: as frail as a person, or without one of its powers, while she's near. |
| **Radiant** |  | Wrapped in a white light that turns monsters' attacks aside, a tenth of them for each success. |
| **Rejuvenating** |  | Her body knits itself back together: bruises close in minutes and cuts in hours. |
| **Revenant** |  | Dead, and back for one person. It passes for living at a distance and mends its wounds by will alone. Once the one it came for is dead, or it is, it goes back to the grave. |
| **Shambler** |  | A corpse that got up. Slow, strong and hard to stop: it feels nothing and doesn't bleed, and only taking the head or destroying the body puts it down. |
| **Strapped down** | Yes | Held to the autopsy table by the straps. Whoever lies here isn't crawling away. |
| **Surging** |  | Her Zeal runs through her body: stronger, quicker and tougher for four hours. |
| **Trailed** |  | A hunter has touched it and can follow its trail wherever it goes. |
| **Warding** |  | Monsters can't come within as many cells of her as she has Zeal, unless their will is stronger than hers. |
| **Winded** | Yes | Gave away her own breath to heal someone. Slow and clumsy for the hour. |
| **Witnessing** |  | Sees what kind of monster each one in sight is. |
