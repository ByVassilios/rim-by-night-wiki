# Everything else

Every other kind of thing this mod defines that has a name and a description: Merits, Backgrounds, gatherings, places, personalities and the rest.

## Ambitions

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Close a case** | See one of the colony's cases through to the one behind it. |
| **Destroy a monster** | Kill two monsters with your own hands. |
| **Find a lair** | Get a lead to where a monster sleeps. |
| **Hold the line** | Go a whole season without a colonist dying. |
| **Learn the truth** | Learn three things about monsters: from a body, a book, a captive or a case. |
| **Mend the hurt** | Heal two people a monster has hurt. |
| **Redeem a monster** | Talk a captured monster into turning from what it is. |
| **Save someone** | Get someone away from a monster that has hurt them, or stand over them while they're down. |
| **Spare a monster** | Let a beaten monster live, or let one go on its word. |
| **Take the blow** | Stand between a monster and someone who is down. |
| **Three monsters** | Kill three monsters with your own hands. |
| **Unmask them** | Show two monsters for what they are, to yourself or to everyone. |

## Backgrounds

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Arsenal** | The cell's armoury. Each season it sends a crate of hunter weapons worth 150 silver for each dot. Each dot also gives 3% better prices when trading with hunter-net. A starting hunter packs one hunter weapon for each dot. |

## Creeds

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Avenger** | The monsters have to be destroyed, every one of them, and the Avenger means to do it. Of all the hunters they are the readiest to fight and the least willing to stop. |
| **Defender** | Someone has to stand between the monsters and the people they prey on. The Defender guards a home, a family or a whole colony, and fights to keep it safe. |
| **Innocent** | The Innocent sees the monsters as they are and isn't afraid of them. They ask questions where others would shoot, and trust that understanding can do more than a weapon. |
| **Judge** | Not every monster is the same, and not every one deserves the same end. The Judge weighs what a creature has done before deciding what to do about it, then acts without flinching. |
| **Martyr** | Whatever it costs, the Martyr pays it so that no one else has to. They take the blow meant for someone else and count their own pain as the price of saving others. |
| **Redeemer** | Something human may still be left inside a monster. The Redeemer looks for it, and tries to bring it back before deciding the creature is beyond saving. |
| **Visionary** | Why were they chosen, and what for? The Visionary wants to know what the monsters are, what the Imbuing means and where it all leads, and looks for the answers before anything else. |

## Edges

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Augur** | The hunter sees what's coming to this place, though the future doesn't always tell the truth. |
| **Balance** | The hunter weighs a monster and finds it wanting. For a time, whatever powers it has won't answer. |
| **Becalm** | A calm spreads out from the hunter. Fists slow, triggers wait, and rages burn out. |
| **Blaze** | The nearest light flares into something no monster can stand, and burns every one of them in reach. |
| **Bluster** | One shouted word, "No!" or "Stop!", and the blow goes wide. |
| **Brand** | The hunter's touch burns a mark into a monster that every hunter can see and no disguise hides. |
| **Burden** | Under the hunter's stare a monster grows too heavy to move. |
| **Burn** | Something like molten rock runs under the hunter's skin. Touching her burns, and so does her touch. |
| **Champion** | The hunter calls the monsters out, and they forget everyone but her. |
| **Cleave** | The hunter's weapon, or a blade of nothing in her empty hand, cuts like it was made for the monsters. |
| **Confront** | The hunter meets a monster's eyes and stands there, and hurting her becomes the hardest thing it has ever tried to do. |
| **Delve** | The hunter reads the recent past of the ground she stands on, and sees who walked there. |
| **Demand** | The hunter hurts herself to do what she couldn't, and the pain becomes strength. |
| **Discern** | The hunter sees what a monster is: the dead that don't breathe, the beast under the skin, the blood-drinker's pallor. |
| **Donate** | The hunter lends part of herself to someone else: her strength, her quickness, her wits, for as long as she can spare them. |
| **Expose** | For a moment everyone around the hunter sees the monsters as she does. |
| **Foresee** | A flash of how the next few moments could go, and the chance to choose the better one. |
| **Hide** | Monsters look straight past the hunter, as if she weren't worth the trouble, until she makes them notice her. |
| **Illuminate** | The hunter blazes with a light only the imbued and the monsters can see. Every hunter near her knows the monsters for what they are, and every monster wants the light put out. |
| **Insinuate** | One quiet question that reminds a monster of what it used to be. It falters, and sometimes it runs. |
| **Payback** | The hunter holds a monster's gaze and makes it pay: it's as frail as a person, or one of its powers is gone, for as long as she stays near. |
| **Pierce** | The hunter knows a lie when she hears one, and with a touch she sees someone's last brush with a monster, or a monster's past. |
| **Pinpoint** | The hunter looks at a monster and knows where it's weak. |
| **Radiate** | A white light wraps the hunter and those close to her, and the monsters' blows slide off it. |
| **Ravage** | The air shimmers with heat round one monster and it burns from the inside. The hunter pays for it in her own body. |
| **Rejuvenate** | The hunter's body knits itself back together far faster than it should, and her hands can close another's wounds. |
| **Respire** | The hunter breathes life into someone hurt, or draws it out of a monster. |
| **Restore** | The hunter pictures a body whole and it becomes so: deep wounds turn to bruises, and what was lost grows back. |
| **Smite** | Something that isn't quite lightning and isn't quite fire leaps from the hunter, with a crash and a stink of ammonia. |
| **Smolder** | Thick black smoke pours off the hunter, and anything shooting at her shoots blind. |
| **Surge** | The hunter's fury runs into her body: stronger, faster, harder to put down. |
| **Suspend** | The hunter shuts this place off from every other world. Whatever is here stays here and answers for itself. |
| **Trail** | One touch and the hunter can follow a monster anywhere: she sees the track it leaves, and no one else does. |
| **Ward** | A crackling edge round the hunter that monsters can't push through unless their will is stronger than hers. |
| **Witness** | The hunter sees the monsters for what they are, and glimpses the harm they've done. |

## Gatherings

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Creed summit** | Hunters of all seven Creeds argue at the colony about what the Messengers want, then vote on the net's next aim. |
| **Great hunt** | Cells from across the region gather at the colony against a known nest, and march out together from it. |
| **Regional meet** | Hunters of the nearby cells meet at the colony to share leads round a table, then plan a hunt together. |

## Group temperaments

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Merciful** | Wants the innocent kept out of it, and the monsters given a chance to be something else. Shares its cases. Pays in medicine. |
| **Visionary** | Wants to know why the Messengers chose it. Shares its cases and asks for help with what it finds. Pays in books and notes. |
| **Wary** | Wants to stay alive and unknown. Asks only for help covering its tracks. Pays in silver. |
| **Zealous** | Wants every monster dead, whatever it costs. Hands off the ones it can't reach, and asks for its tracks covered. Pays in weapons. |

## Notes from the grimoire

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A Bystander** | This colonist missed the Imbuing by a little. They have no sight and no Edges, but they know monsters exist: they panic a little less, work 10% faster at research and tending, and can examine clues. See [[HTR_Guide_Being_Imbued#bystanders\|Bystanders]]. |
| **A clue** | Examining a clue is research work, for hunters and Bystanders only. It takes ten seconds and the clue is used up. The reading goes in the case window, and you piece it together. See [[HTR_Guide_Investigation_and_Lairs#examining\|Examining]]. |
| **A piece of the horror** | A piece of a first horror can go on the [[HTR_Guide_Hunter_Tools#the-autopsy-table\|autopsy table]]. See [[HTR_Guide_Hunter_Tools#the-first-horror\|The first horror]]. |
| **A shambler** | Slow, strong and hard to stop. It feels no pain and doesn't go down to wounds, and only the head or the whole body ends it. - Fire does half again as much. - Aim for the head. - Shamblers are slow with doors. See [[HTR_Guide_The_Walking_Dead#fighting-them\|Fighting them]]. |
| **A Virtue to raise** | At 10 Conviction this hunter can cash it in for a Virtue point, which leaves them at 3. Open the Imbued tab and press Choose a Virtue. Waiting keeps the Conviction. At 7 in any Virtue, and at each point after, the hunter gains a derangement. See [[HTR_Guide_Creeds_and_Virtues#virtues\|Virtues]]. |
| **An Edge to learn** | This hunter has Virtue to spare and an Edge it can buy. Open the Imbued tab and press Learn an Edge. Unspent points keep, and a new Edge is ready at once. See [[HTR_Guide_Edges#buying-edges\|Buying Edges]]. |
| **Hunter's notes** | What the autopsy table wrote down. See [[HTR_Guide_Hunter_Tools#the-notes\|The notes]]. |
| **Out of Conviction** | At 0 this hunter is an ordinary person to every monster. No second sight wakes, so keep them out of the fight. - Conviction comes back after each threat ends, up to the Creed's level. - Deeds raise it past that. See [[HTR_Guide_Being_Imbued#conviction\|Conviction]]. |
| **Righteous fury risk** | This hunter shares a map with a colonist they know is a monster, and their Creed can break into righteous fury against it. - Keep their mood up, or keep them apart. See [[HTR_Guide_Living_with_Monsters\|Living with monsters]]. |
| **Second sight is up** | For three hours every monster this hunter sees gets a mark, and each one seen this way is known from then on. Their mind is closed, and illusions show as false. Keep them where they can see the threat. See [[HTR_Guide_Being_Imbued#second-sight\|Second sight]]. |
| **Strapped down** | The table's straps stop a downed subject crawling off, and hold a prisoner down until someone carries them away. See [[HTR_Guide_Hunter_Tools#the-autopsy-table\|The autopsy table]]. |
| **The autopsy table** | Lay a subject on it: right-click a downed monster, a staked vampire, a prisoner or a corpse and pick Carry to the autopsy table. Nobody sleeps on the table. See [[HTR_Guide_Hunter_Tools#the-autopsy-table\|The autopsy table]]. |
| **The bait stake** | Right-click a live animal or a corpse and pick Bind to the bait stake. The next group of monsters on the map goes for the bait first. Ordinary raids ignore it, and it doesn't bring a raid sooner. Select the stake and press Release to untie the body. See [[HTR_Guide_Hunter_Tools#bait\|Bait]]. |
| **This corpse may rise** | On a haunted map, unburied corpses can get up as shamblers, a fifth of them each night hour. Bury it or burn it. Anything in a grave or a sarcophagus stays down. See [[HTR_Guide_The_Walking_Dead#where-they-come-from\|Where they come from]]. |

## Recipes and operations

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Dissect** | Three rolls of an hour each, added up. Up to two points in the file and proof for a case. A corpse is used up. A living subject most likely dies, and lives through it only maimed. A subject that has given its points is passed over. |
| **Examine** | 15 minutes. Tells a monster from a person, and on two successes what kind. Half a point in the file. Nothing happens to the subject, though a monster not staked may wake. Each subject is examined once. |
| **Study a horror's piece** | 15 minutes, on a horror's piece lying on the table. On a success the colony hits that monster 10% harder, up to the ceiling, and the piece is used up. A failure leaves it to try again. |
| **Test for weakness** | 30 minutes. Pick the methods under "test with" in the bill's details: silver, fire (chemfuel) or holy water. The nearest item of a picked method not yet ruled out for the kind is used up and deals one wound of its damage. A method that does nothing special is ruled out; a hit on two successes, or ruling out all three, finds the kind's weakness and gives a point in the file. |

## Scenarios

|---|---|
| **Fresh cell** | Three strangers were on the same road the night the thing came out of the dark. Each of them heard the voices, and each of them saw it for what it was. They don't know each other, and they don't know what they've become, but they know they can't go back. Note: three new hunters with one Edge each and almost nothing else. |
| **Hunting party** | Four hunters who have done this before. The other cells passed word of a revenant's old home a few days from here, and you came to finish it. Note: four experienced hunters with two or three Edges each and hunter weapons, and a lair on the world map from the start. |
| **Lone survivor** | The others are dead. You heard the voices while it happened, and when you looked again you could see what was eating them. You ran. It didn't stop following. Note: one new hunter, alone, and the afflicted arrive within days. A hard start. |
| **Plague doctors** | You came to treat the sickness. Then the dead got up out of the pits. Two of you saw them for what they were; your assistant only saw you put them down. The plague went one way and you went the other, and the dead are following.<br><br>Note: two hunters and a mortal assistant, with herbs and quicklime. The walking dead come in a few days. Medieval arms and the old crafts; research is as normal. |
| **Witch-hunters** | You saw the thing in the miller's house for what it was, and so did two others. Nobody else believed you. Now the three of you travel with cold iron, holy water and a crossbow, and burn what needs burning.<br><br>Note: three hunters with a crossbow each in their packs. Medieval arms and the old crafts; research is as normal. |
