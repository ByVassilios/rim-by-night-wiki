# Learning tips

The tips the game shows the first times you meet something.

| Tip | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Clues and suspects** | A monster hiding among your visitors or colonists leaves marks: fang marks, claw marks, blood trails, tracks that stop dead. Each fades in 15 days.<br><br>Hunters and Bystanders examine clues as research work. Each reading adds evidence against whoever was near when it was left, most against the one who left it. With enough evidence, a hunter can name the suspect from the Suspects list on the Imbued tab. Name the wrong person and the colony pays for it. |
| **Conviction** | Conviction is the hunter's certainty that they're doing right. It pays for second sight and for the stronger Edges.<br><br>It comes back after each threat ends, up to the Creed's level, and deeds raise it past that. At 0 a hunter is an ordinary person to every monster. At 10 they can cash it all in for a Virtue point. |
| **Creeds, Virtues and deeds** | Mercy, Vision and Zeal are the hunter's Virtues, and the Creed's own Virtue always leads.<br><br>Each Creed has its deeds, such as rescuing, learning, unmasking or killing. Each deed gives a point of Conviction, once a day for each kind. Virtues rise only when a hunter at 10 Conviction cashes it in. |
| **Edges** | Edges are the powers the Messengers give. Each Creed has a path of five, and a level costs its number in points of its Virtue.<br><br>A hunter's Edges sit on tiles above their buttons, one per path. Click a tile, pick an Edge, then pick the target. A greyed Edge says why it can't be used now. |
| **Hunters among monsters** | A hunter who learns a colonist is a monster knows it for good, and how they live with it depends on their Creed.<br><br>Some talk, some watch, and an Avenger may break into righteous fury against the monster. Keep such hunters in good spirits, or apart from the monsters they know. |
| **Second sight** | When a hostile monster comes into view, a hunter spends a point of Conviction and sees clearly for three hours.<br><br>Every monster they see gets a mark that says "not human", their mind is closed to Dominate, Presence and the Delirium, and every monster seen this way is known to them for good. |
| **The afflicted** | The dead don't always stay down. Shamblers dig out at the map edge at night, and revenants arrive as ordinary visitors, back for one colonist.<br><br>While a map is haunted, its unburied dead can get up at night. Bury or burn them, and send a revenant on its way to stop it. |
| **The imbued** | One of your colonists has been imbued. The voices called the Messengers have woken them, and they now see monsters for what they are.<br><br>A hunter is still human: no fast healing, no frenzy. What they have is second sight, Conviction, Edges and each other. The Imbued tab shows their Creed, Virtues and Conviction. |
| **The other cells** | Other cells of hunters are scattered across the world, and now they know about your colony.<br><br>They trade the weapons that work, send word of the afflicted before they come, and ask for help when a cell is in trouble. Their requests show in the Quests tab. |
