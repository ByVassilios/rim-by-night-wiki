# Every alert

Every alert this mod can show on the right of the screen.

| Alert | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Witnesses on the map** | These outsiders saw something they shouldn't have. If they leave the map alive and still remember it, word gets out: the breach of the Masquerade or the Veil goes up, or a court learns who took or destroyed one of its own.  Kill them, take them prisoner, recruit them into the colony, or make them forget it. |
| **Seat duty due** | These colonists' seats in the sworn home have a duty that ends with the season in under two days. Missing it three seasons running loses the seat. See Your seats on the home's page. |
