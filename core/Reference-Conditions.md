# Every condition

Every health condition, state and effect a pawn can carry, as shown on its Health tab.

| Condition | Bad | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Bargained** |  | This spirit struck a bargain. It serves the one it made it with for a day, then goes its own way. |
| **Blood dammed** | Yes | The blood in this vampire's veins has gone still. It can't be spent, for powers or anything else, until the dam breaks. |
| **Bloodied** |  | Covered in someone else's blood. |
| **Censer smoke** | Yes | Blessed smoke in the lungs. The thing living in this body can't stand it. |
| **Cold iron's touch** | Yes | Cold iron has cut this sorcerer's hold on the dead. No corpse will rise for them for a while. |
| **Fighting beside its master** |  | Fighting at its master's side, as it was trained to. It reads their moves and strikes where they leave an opening. |
| **Fugue** | Yes | Now and then this mind simply goes away. The body wanders off, and when they come back to themselves they have no idea how they got there. |
| **Gave up** | Yes | Beaten and out of ways to run, this one has dropped its weapon and gone down on its knees. It waits to hear what happens to it. |
| **Gripped** | Yes | Held by a man catcher's collar, and dragging against it. |
| **Gripped by silver** | Yes | Held by a man catcher's collar, its silver spikes burning into the flesh. |
| **Hamstrung** | Yes | The tendons at the back of the legs are cut, so this body can only crawl. |
| **Hired ally** |  | Paid to fight and to see nothing. |
| **Hysteria** | Yes | Every fright and every slight lands twice as hard. In a vampire the Beast is never far below the surface. |
| **In Twilight** |  | The dead is on the ghostly side of the world. The living can't see it yet, and physical harm can't reach it. |
| **Manic-depression** | Yes | Long highs and long lows, and little in between. Neither has much to do with what is actually happening. |
| **Most wanted** |  | Its own kind's enemies want it dead more than almost anything else, and it has outlived everyone they sent. It shrugs off half of every wound and fights like little else in the night. |
| **Multiple personalities** | Yes | More than one person lives in this body, and they take turns. Each has its own idea of what work is worth doing. |
| **Nemesis** |  | Got away from a fight with the colony once and remembers it. Whatever hurt them most, they've learned to take, and every time they escape they come back harder. |
| **Netted** | Yes | Caught in a wire net that won't hold long. It slows the body until it's torn loose or cut away. |
| **Obsessive-compulsive** | Yes | Everything has to be clean and in its place. A dirty room is torture, and now and then they drop everything to scrub. |
| **Paranoia** | Yes | Everyone is watching, and everyone wants something. They are cold with people and sometimes shut themselves away until the feeling passes. |
| **Powers sealed** | Yes | Something has cut this creature off from its own power. It can't use any of its gifts, spend its blood or heal the way it should until the seal fades. |
| **Quicklimed** | Yes | Burned through with quicklime. Nothing can raise this body. |
| **Schizophrenia** | Yes | Voices, things at the edge of sight, and a growing sense that none of it is safe. When it gets bad they wander off, lost in what only they can see. |
| **Sees nothing** |  | Keeps what it sees to itself. |
| **Showing itself** |  | The dead lets the living see it, pale and see-through. Ordinary weapons still pass through it. |
| **Silver net** | Yes | Tangled in a net of silver wire and held down, and no Garou can change form inside it. It lasts until the net is ripped apart from inside, which takes real strength, or until someone cuts it away. |
| **Silver nitrate** | Yes | Silver salts eating into the skin. They go on burning for a few seconds. |
| **Sneaking** |  | Slipped into a hostile town by night. Its people can't see this person unless they find them: one who notices something searches the spot, and a searcher who comes close may find who is there. Attacking anyone gives this person away. |
| **Spared** | Yes | The colony let this one go after it gave up. It won't come back as a nemesis. |
| **Tie** |  | Sent by someone. |
| **Tied up** | Yes | Tied hand and foot, so this body can only lie where it's put. It lasts until the rope is worked loose, which takes a person about half a day and something stronger a few hours, or until someone cuts it. |
| **True faith** |  | A conviction so deep it has weight. Vampires who come at this person in close combat may break and run, and the mind is harder to bend. It can't survive the taste of vampire blood. |
| **Unhealing wounds** | Yes | Something in these wounds won't let a vampire's blood close them. They'll mend in a day, the slow way. |
