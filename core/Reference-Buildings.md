# Every building

Every building, piece of furniture and plant this mod adds.

## Buildings and furniture

| | Name | Value | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | **Tavern counter** |  | A counter for selling drinks, drugs and meals. Most evenings guests come by, buy from the counter and any shelf in the tavern if they like your prices, leave the silver on the counter, and stay to sit and talk. The tavern is the room the counter stands in, plus any tavern area painted next to it. The Guests button picks who may come, and guests who hate each other may come to blows. Prices are set here or from any shelf in the tavern, and the tavern can be closed for the night. Beds marked for tavern guests are rented out for the night. |
