# Everything else

Every other kind of thing this mod defines that has a name and a description: Merits, Backgrounds, gatherings, places, personalities and the rest.

## Backgrounds

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Allies** | Mortals who owe this character and will fight for them. On any map with enemies on it, "Call your allies" brings three armed mortals, one more for each dot past 2, who fight beside the colony and leave with the map. They are ready again in 30 days, 5 fewer for each dot. They see nothing they shouldn't and never count as witnesses. |
| **Contacts** | People who hear what goes on at the sworn home. Each dot gives one more day to answer a rival's strike on a Background, and makes it 20% likelier each season that a chance comes up to strike at a rival's own. |
| **Fame** | A name people know. It needs a tavern or club at the colony. Each dot brings one more party of guests a week and makes room for one more regular. |
| **Influence** | Pull with the people around the sworn home. Once a season, "Call a caravan" brings a trade caravan from the home's faction. More dots bring a richer trader with more silver to buy with. |
| **Mentor** | A member of the sworn home who teaches this character for free. The dots decide who will take them on: a plain member at 1, an officer at 2, the council at 3, the leader at 5. Choose the mentor from the row's link. Only one at a time: the one passed over turns rival. |
| **Resources** | Money from investments, property or a hidden fortune: 100, 400, 900, 1,600 or 2,500 silver each season, dropped at the colony. |

## Notes from the grimoire

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A witness** | This outsider saw something they shouldn't have. If they leave the map alive and still remember it, word gets out. - Kill them, capture them or recruit them. - Select a colonist, right-click them and pick Talk ... Out of what they saw. - Or make them forget it with a power. |

## Personalities

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cautious** | Hates being in anyone's debt. Pays well in silver and …, but only ever owes a small …. Cold to anyone from a colony it has never talked to. |
| **Conniving** | Never owes a … of its own. It pays with one another member owes it, so the colony ends up holding someone else's debt. Short with anyone who stops it for a chat. |
| **Cowardly** | Hands out work freely, one job more than its means would allow. Its missions are the dangerous kind, aimed at its enemies, and pay a fifth more. |
| **Generous** | Pays well and asks little, and its good opinion fades slowly. Once a year, after two jobs, it asks for something back. |
| **Greedy** | Pays less silver and more … for the work it hands out. |
| **Loyal** | Pays a quarter more in everything as long as the colony never works against it or its friends. Never forgives a betrayal, and takes the colony leaving the home as one. |
| **Proud** | Pays in prestige: more …, less silver. A refused mission is an insult it remembers. Short with anyone who stops it for a chat. |
| **Vengeful** | Remembers every slight. Its grudges never fade by themselves, and it turns enemy sooner than most. Short with anyone who stops it for a chat. |
| **Zealous** | Cares only for the home's cause. Every job it gives is a fight against the home's enemies, and it pays half again for them. |

## What people want

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A childe** | Wants leave to bring someone new into the home. Pays a minor favour now, and the newcomer sides with the colony. |
| **Buried secret** | Wants a witness to an old crime silenced or bought. Pays with a life favour, and the colony learns something it could use. |
| **Collector** | Wants a certain treasure taken from where it is kept. Points the colony at good finds and pays with lesser pieces of its own collection. |
| **Freedom** | Wants out of a bond it never chose. Pays with its loyalty, for good. |
| **Homecoming** | Wants back a place it lost. Pays with that place's secrets. |
| **Kingmaker** | Wants one named member in the leader's seat. The new leader starts as the colony's friend. |
| **Lore hunter** | Wants a rival's library raided. Pays with a named tome. |
| **Power seeker** | Wants its rivals' assets raided, inside the home and out. Pays with a share of the take and its vote for a year. |
| **Redemption** | Wants a way out of what it has become. Pays with teaching, and never asks for a dangerous job. |
| **Revenge** | Wants one named enemy in the home destroyed. Pays with a major favour and the enemy's goods. |
