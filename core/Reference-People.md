## Creatures and races

| | Name | Value | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | **Air elemental** | 0 | A quick, cold spirit of the open sky, a gust that learned to hold its shape. It never stays still for long. |
|  | **Animal spirit** | 0 | The spirit of a kind of beast that lives on the land beyond the Gauntlet. It teaches the Gifts of beasts, and serves the totems. |
|  | **Bane** | 0 | A spirit of the Wyrm, a thing of hate and corruption. It attacks anything alive in the Penumbra. Driving one off is a price other spirits will gladly take. |
|  | **Curiosus** | 0 | A small spirit of curiosity: spheres within spinning spheres, each one a puzzle. A Garou who keeps it near long enough to solve it comes away with Gnosis and Willpower. |
|  | **Drattosi** | 0 | A bane that feeds on other spirits, swallowing their essence whole. Left alone in the Penumbra it hunts down the spirits a Garou came to see. |
|  | **Earth elemental** | 0 | A slow, heavy spirit of the living rock, woken where the land rises in stone. It knows the patience of mountains. |
|  | **Electricity elemental** | 0 | A crackling spirit that hums in the colony's generators and wires. It is fast, bright and very hard to pin down. |
|  | **Engling** | 0 | A spirit made of pure Gnosis, called to be the prey of the Sacred Hunt. Every Garou in at its end has their Gnosis back. |
|  | **Fire elemental** | 0 | A restless spirit of flame, drawn to a steam vent or a fire burning on the land. It is quick to anger and quicker to bore. |
|  | **Ghost** | 0 | Someone who died here and came back. It walks its old routine at night and wants one thing before it can rest. |
|  | **Glass elemental** | 0 | A brittle, glittering spirit that gathers around the colony's lamps and lights. The Weaver made it, and it remembers. |
|  | **Lune** | 0 | A servant of Luna, quick and changeable as moonlight. Lunes know the rites and will teach one for the right gift. |
|  | **Nature spirit** | 0 | A small spirit of the wild: the soul of a tree, a stone, a gust of wind or a running beast. It teaches the Gifts of its kind to Garou who pay it respect. |
|  | **Pattern spider** | 0 | A spirit of the Weaver that spins webs of order over everything people build. Wary of Garou, but it will trade what it knows of machines and cities. |
|  | **Psychomachia** | 0 | A bane of hatred and murder that smells of death. Given time alone it slips through the Gauntlet and rides a beast on the other side. |
|  | **Scrag** | 0 | A slouching bane of rage and violence, all claws and teeth. Its presence stirs a werewolf's Rage until it boils over into frenzy. |
|  | **Water elemental** | 0 | A spirit of deep water, gathered where a lake or a river runs through the land. It is changeable and hard to hold. |
