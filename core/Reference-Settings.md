# Every setting

Every setting in Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, with what it does.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **The one who came back** | Now and then a colonist, prisoner or slave who died in the colony's care in the last year comes back at night, and wants one thing before it can rest. |
| **How often the dead come back** | The chance of the one who came back, as a share of the usual. It's twice as likely on a dark moon. |
| **Days before an ignored ghost turns Spectre** | How long a returning dead waits before it goes bad and turns hostile. At Never, the haunting still grows, but it never attacks. |
| **Merits at making** | How many Merits the making menu lets a new vampire, Garou or Fera take. Each Merit past the first comes with a Flaw of its own, picked or rolled. At 1, one Merit and one Flaw, as before. |
| **Grimoire** | Show the grimoire button at the bottom right: a book of everything the Rim by Night mods add and how it works. |
| **One bottom-bar button** | While the colony has two or more kinds (say vampires and mages), only the window you used last keeps its button on the bottom bar. The list down the left side of each window opens the others. |
| **Rim by Night Overview** | The Overview page under Rim by Night, at the top of the list down each window's left side: all our kinds at once, their pools, what they're doing, and who needs you. Off, Rim by Night keeps only its Chronicle and Our story pages. |
| **Missions ending soon** | A mission or an offer with this many hours left or fewer shows under "Needs you" on the Rim by Night Overview, with a red mark. |
| **Power tiles** | Put a vampire's powers on one small tile per Discipline, and a werewolf's Gifts on one tile per breed, auspice and tribe. Turn it off to give every power its own full-size button, as vanilla does. |
| **Pools over the inspect pane** | Show a selected colonist's blood, vitae, Rage, Gnosis and Willpower just above the inspect pane, where the Kindred and Garou tabs open. It hides while a tab is open. |
| **Kindred right-click submenu** | Gather feeding, staking, the Embrace and the other vampire orders under one "Kindred" entry in the right-click menu. Turn it off to list them with vanilla's orders. |
| **Power timers in the inspect pane** | List a selected vampire's running powers and the time each has left in the bottom-left inspect pane, so you don't have to open the health tab. |
| **Effects on the Kindred tab** | Keep a vampire's buffs, powers that are switched on and other harmless effects off the health tab. They are listed under Active effects on the Kindred tab's Sheet page. Wounds and anything harmful stay on the health tab. |
| **Fighting motion** | Blurred vampires dash at their foes, Potence knocks people back, and Kindred blows show on screen. Turn it off for plain vanilla melee. |
| **Power auras** | A faint glow around a vampire while a buff lasts, with a trail and a mark on its blows. |
| **Gore** | How much blood a fight with a vampire spills, and whether cut-off hands, limbs and heads fly off and lie where they land. At 0 there are no sprays and a lost part is simply gone, as in vanilla. |
| **Hunters** | Hunters and investigators who come because of Masquerade breaches, and hunter raids. |
| **Nemeses** | Hunters and our kind who get away from a fight with the colony remember it, and come back with their next raid, harder each time. |
| **Derangements** | Off, no one gains a new derangement, and the ones they have don't cause episodes or make frenzy harder to resist. |
| **The world lives on** | When a Kindred court, a Garou sept or a hunter cell falls, its people live on: some die, some go missing, the rest look for a new home that will take them, and some may come to your colony. Homes go to war on their own, settled by a quick battle of their real members. Off, a fallen home vanishes with its people, as before. |
| **The World of Darkness reaches orbit** | Off, nobody from these mods comes uninvited to a colony in orbit, and visitors wait until it lands. Help you call still comes, by drop pod. On, enemies drop in by pod too, and friendly visitors come only with Royalty, leaving on its shuttle. |
| **Bloodiness of falls** | How many die when a home falls without a battle (a town razed, a faction wiped out). Battles take their own dead. |
| **World pace** | How often homes go to war. At 100%, on a usual world, a court changes hands every two or three years, about one sept falls a year, and a hunter cell or two are wiped out. |
| **First encounter, earliest** | The earliest day a colony without vampires, Garou or hunters meets its first one: a pale stranger, a stray cub or a hunted stranger. |
| **First encounter, latest** | The latest day for that first meeting. Never earlier than the earliest day. |
| **Cover names** | Each world gives the monster factions a random name and a word for their people, so the names don't give away what they are. Off, they go by what they are: the Sabbat Packs, Pentex, the Garou Nation. A name you gave a faction yourself stays. |
| **Monsters shrug off mortal moods** | A lupus Garou ignores the vanilla moods a wolf wouldn't feel, such as kibble, rotten food or a cramped room, and a vampire far from its Humanity ignores corpses and strangers' deaths. Off, they feel them like anyone. |
| **Prisoners keep their secret** | A prisoner looks mortal on its tabs until the colony finds out what it is. Off, the tabs show it at once. |
| **Ambitions** | Off, no pawn is offered new ambitions. One already taken still counts and still pays. |
| **Days between ambition offers** | How long after three ambitions are turned down, left unanswered or dropped before the next three come. |
| **Groups plot against their leaders** | A coterie, pack, cell or cabal that grows close to the ruling group's power starts plotting to take its place. Off, none starts on its own; a group your colonist leads can still move when you say. |
| **Chance to be spotted sneaking in** | The chance each look a town's people take spots a pawn sneaking in, against the usual. At 0% nobody is ever spotted. |
| **Prejudice between kinds** | How much vampires, werewolves, hunters and mages living in one colony think less (or better) of each other for what they are, by clan, tribe, Creed and Path. Family and lovers feel half of it, and it never goes past 20 either way. At 0% they don't care. |
| **Blood-feeders can become hunters and mages** | Sanguophages and other pawns with the blood-feeder gene stay mortals with genes, never vampires. Off, they can't be imbued or Awaken. On, they can be either and keep their genes. |
| **Proximity detectors ignore the harmless** | On, a proximity detector rings only for unseen creatures hostile to the colony. Friendly spirits and ghosts, and your own people hiding with a power, leave it quiet. Off, it rings for anyone unseen, as in the base game. |
| **Beast training speed** | How fast animals learn from a keeper of our kinds or in a kennel, against the usual. |
| **Mission pay** | The silver every court, sept, cell and Consilium mission pays, against the usual. |
| **Names on each wanted list** | How many names the Red List, the Wyrm's Hunted, Most Wanted and the Proscribed each keep. A list fills back up over time; a smaller one loses no names until they are hunted down. |
| **Show wanted marks always** | Every mark on the Red List, the Wyrm's Hunted, Most Wanted and the Proscribed shows on the world map wherever it is, and can be clicked and reached. What each one is still has to be learned. |
| **Hours a wanted name holds its camp** | When a caravan runs a wanted name down, how long it and its people hold their camp before coming out to fight. |
| **Tavern guests** | How many guests come to a tavern each night, against the usual. |
| **Fights between guests** | Guests who hate each other, like a Garou and a vampire, may come to words, blows or a killing. Off, they never do. |
| **NPC cunning** | How clever enemies of our kinds are. Plain fights as before this update. Smart uses fighting styles, picks its targets, runs when desperate and may give up. Ruthless is Smart, but it goes for the weakest colonist and never gives up. Allies and colonists always fight Smart. |
| **Desperate drops hiding** | One of ours who is badly hurt, alone or losing stops hiding what it is: obvious powers, Crinos and vulgar magic in front of mortals. A drafted colonist never does. |
| **Fighting styles** | Each of ours rolls a style from its clan, tribe, auspice, Creed or Path: Brawler, Controller, Stalker, Caster or Guardian. The style decides which powers come first, how close it fights and when it pulls back. |
| **Giving up** | A beaten enemy with no way out may kneel and drop its weapon. You decide whether to let it go, take it prisoner or finish it. |
| **The nemesis learns** | A nemesis caught by a hidden foe comes back with a way to see it, goes first for the colonist who beat it, and brings one or two more of its kind. |
| **Mixed fights** | Kinds that hate each other by lore fight each other on the colony's map as well as the colony: Garou go for vampires, hunters for every monster, Seers for mages. |
| **Visitors use their powers** | Traders and visitors of our kinds sway prices, read what your people are, and scry the base before a raid. Very rarely, one feeds on a colonist alone at night. A colonist with a sense power can catch them. |
| **Colonists use powers outside fights** | Colonists with sense powers look over each visitor, the colony's trader sways prices under the Freely habit, healers mend the resting, and night watchers warn of raids an hour early. |
| **Scouts** | Before some raids a scout of our kinds comes first. Catch it to question it; let it get away unseen and the raid comes knowing where to strike. |
| **Caravan ambushes** | A hostile group of our kinds may wait on a caravan's route, at most once a month each. |
| **Grudges** | Someone with a grudge against the colony acts on their own: a small raid, a curse from afar, rumours, or hired help. |
| **Hunt planning** | Hunters coming for the colony's monsters bring the arsenal's weapons meant for them and switch to the right one, lay traps on the way in and fall back behind them, and send one with second sight ahead to find the hidden. |
| **Truces** | After two lost raids, a group of our kinds may offer a truce. |
| **Cover-ups** | A vampire or Garou deals with a witness of its own side's breach without being told: the colony's own when their box on the tab is ticked, a court's or a raider's always. It uses the surest way it has and never kills. |
| **Allow a sworn home** | Off: the swear link is gone, and a home already sworn is left quietly. |
| **War chance** | How often the world's wars pick the sworn home, against the usual chance. |
| **Enemy acts a season** | How many enemies inside the home act against the colony each season. |
| **Regard fades** | How fast members' regard drifts back toward zero each season. |
| **Rival strikes on Backgrounds** | How often a rival's act strikes one of your Backgrounds, against the usual chance. All the way left turns strikes off. |
| **Days between town questions** | How long someone in a court's, a sept's or a cell's town waits before they'll answer another question. |
