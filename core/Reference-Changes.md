# What changed

What each update changed, newest first, as the grimoire shows it.

## Grimoire update, 15 November 2026

### Ask
- Ask understands everyday words: "why is my vampire burning" finds Sunlight, and "how do I make a new vampire" finds the Embrace.
- A word only matches whole, so "rage" no longer finds "storage". Rare words count for more than ones found on every page.
- A word spelled a letter off, or without its accent, still finds its page.
- The best answer opens in a box at the top with its whole part of the page. The links above the answers keep only one mod's.
- Answers about the kinds your colony has come first.
### Why
- A new Why tab lists what just happened to your pawns and why: a frenzy, a roll that went wrong, a power that can't be used, a letter from these mods. The newest is on top, and each goes after a day.
- The grimoire's words in each row are links. The number on the tab counts the rows you haven't seen.

## Grimoire update, 14 November 2026

### The grimoire
- Every power, Gift, Edge, rite, ritual and spell page now starts with a card: what it does, its price, its reach and cooldown, and the dice the selected pawn rolls for it.
- The same card shows when you hold the mouse on a power on the gizmo bar.
- Holding the mouse on a link shows its page beside the panel, and a link inside that one opens a second.
- Game words such as Willpower, Generation or Rage are underlined the first time a page uses them, with their meaning on hover.
- Pages have a path back to their section at the top, Back and Forward, and a Pin link. Pinned pages stay on the Selected tab.
- On the Selected tab each note has the colour of its own mod.
- What changed: a page like this one for each update, under each mod's chapter.
