# Every thought and mood

Every thought and mood this mod adds. Mood is the change to mood; "opinion" ones change what one pawn thinks of another.

| Thought | Mood | Lasts | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A place I knew fell** | -3 | 8 days | I knew people there. Some of them are dead now, and nobody can tell me which. |
| **Cursed by a spirit** | -8 | 5 days | A spirit I bound cursed me as it went free. Nothing feels right since. |
| **Cursed with nightmares** | -12 | 3 days | Every time I close my eyes the same face is waiting. I wake up with someone else's hatred on me. |
| **Depressive spell** | -12 | 3 days | Nothing is worth doing. It never was. |
| **Dreamed of …** | -4 | 1 days | They were in my dream last night, standing in the dark and looking at me. They didn't say anything. They didn't have to. |
| **Dreamed of …** | 3 | 1 days | They were in my dream last night, sitting at the end of my bed like they used to. It felt so real I said their name out loud when I woke. |
| **Drove out someone I loved** | -20 opinion | 30 days |  |
| **Filthy room** | -8 |  | I can't think in here. It all has to be cleaned. All of it. |
| **Hearing voices** | -5 | 1 days | They talk all at once and none of them make sense. Some of them sound like people I knew. |
| **Manic spell** | 10 | 3 days | I can do anything tonight. Everything is wonderful and I can't sit still. |
| **My own people fell** | -8 | 8 days | They were mine, and now they are dead or scattered. I wasn't there. |
| **Named as the careless one** | -8 | 10 days | My own people gave my name when the elders asked who was seen. They had to. It still stings. |
| **Nemesis dead** | 6 | 6 days | The one who kept coming back won't come back again. |
| **Said goodbye to …** | 8 | 10 days | They're at rest now. I got to say what I never said while they were alive. It still hurts, but it's the kind of hurt that heals. |

| **The dead walk here** | -3 | 4 days | Someone who died here walks at night. Nobody talks about it at dinner. |
