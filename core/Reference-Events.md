# Every event

Every event the storyteller can send, what it means and what to do about it. Most can be switched off or made rarer one by one in the mod settings.

| Event | Kind | From day | Again after | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ambush** | Big threat |  | 5 days | A hostile group of our kinds waits on a caravan's route. A colonist with a sense in the caravan sees it first: go around and lose half a day, or face it. At most once a month for each faction. |
| **Scout** | Big threat |  | 10 days | Before a raid by a hostile faction of vampires, Garou, hunters or mages, one of them walks in unseen, looks the colony over and leaves. The raid follows within a day or so, its target already picked. Catch the scout and it kneels and talks. Takes a raid's place. |
| **The one who came back** | Event |  | 20 days | Someone who died in the colony's care in the last year comes home at night. Small signs come first, then it shows itself and asks for one thing. Help it and it rests. Ignore it and after a season it turns into a Spectre. |
