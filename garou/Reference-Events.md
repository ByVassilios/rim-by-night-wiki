# Every event

Every event the storyteller can send, what it means and what to do about it. Most can be switched off or made rarer one by one in the mod settings.

| Event | Kind | From day | Again after | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A Garou asks to be taught** | Event |  |  | A Garou of a known sept asks one of yours to teach it a Gift or rite: one lesson a night by right-click. All lessons given earns Honor, Wisdom and the sept's goodwill. |
| **A Garou chased by fomori** | Event | 15 |  | A wounded Garou of a known sept, fomori an hour or two behind, asks to be let in for a day. The fomori follow; if the Garou is alive at the end of the day, the colony gains Honor and the sept owes you aid. |
| **A Garou pinned with silver** | Event | 15 | 20 days | A Garou of a known sept has been pinned with silver and left at the map's edge for hunters. Carry it to a bed and the silver comes out: Honor, and its sept owes you aid. The hunters come looking. |
| **A maimed Garou asks for a doctor** | Event |  |  | A Garou who lost a part to silver, which no regeneration closes, asks your best doctor to make it whole. Right-click it with the doctor to operate: Honor, goodwill and the sept's aid. |
| **A moot to host: Concolation** | Event |  | 30 days | The septs offer you a Concolation, the great gathering of the Nation, to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Bone Gnawers** | Event |  | 30 days | The Bone Gnawers offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Children of Gaia** | Event |  | 30 days | The Children of Gaia offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Fianna** | Event |  | 30 days | The Fianna offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Furies** | Event |  | 30 days | The Black Furies offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Get of Fenris** | Event |  | 30 days | The Get of Fenris offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Glass Walkers** | Event |  | 30 days | The Glass Walkers offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Red Talons** | Event |  | 30 days | The Red Talons offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Shadow Lords** | Event |  | 30 days | The Shadow Lords offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Silent Striders** | Event |  | 30 days | The Silent Striders offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Silver Fangs** | Event |  | 30 days | The Silver Fangs offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Stargazers** | Event |  | 30 days | The Stargazers offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Uktena** | Event |  | 30 days | The Uktena offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Grand Moot of the Wendigo** | Event |  | 30 days | The Wendigo offer you their Grand Moot to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: Hearing** | Event |  | 30 days | A sept writes to offer you a Hearing to host. At most once a year. |
| **A moot to host: moot of the neighbours** | Event |  | 30 days | A sept writes to offer you a moot of the neighbours to host, once that moot opens. At most once a year. |
| **A pack asks to hunt your land** | Event |  |  | A sept's hunting pack, its own land hunted bare, asks to run yours for two days. It takes some of the map's wild game home, and the sept remembers. |
| **A Ronin begs shelter** | Event | 20 |  | A Ronin cast out by its sept begs a few days' shelter. It costs goodwill with the Nation now; kept safe to the end, the Ronin repays it in Honor and asks to stay. |
| **A sept asks to borrow a Kinfolk** | Event |  |  | A sept asks to borrow one of your skilled Kinfolk for a season. It walks back in at the end, and the sept owes you aid. |
| **A sept asks you to foster a cub** | Event |  |  | A sept asks a colony Garou of rank 2 or more to foster a new cub for a season and teach it the Litany: three lessons, one a night. All three given: Honor, Wisdom and goodwill. |
| **A sept asks you to keep a fetish** | Event |  |  | A sept sends you one of its fetishes by drop pod to keep for a season, then takes it back from wherever it lies. Still there: Honor, goodwill and aid. Lost, used up or carried off, and the sept is angry. |
| **A sept asks you to keep an elder** | Event |  |  | A sept asks you to keep a wounded elder for a season while it heals. Half the time the Wyrm finds out where it went. Kept to the end: Honor, goodwill and the sept's aid. |
| **A Theurge asks to visit** | Event |  |  | A Theurge of a known sept asks to commune at your caern or study one of your fetishes for a few nights. The more lore you keep, the longer it stays and the more it pays: Honor to your strongest Garou and goodwill with the Nation. |
| **A Theurge needs a fetish housing** | Event |  |  | A sept's Theurge needs a well-made thing to bind a spirit into: the best weapon or work of art of good quality or better in your storage. The maker earns Honor and the sept sends a talen back. |
| **Abomination attack** | Threat | 30 | 60 days | With Kindred loaded: an Abomination comes out of the dark to kill, with a vampire's hunger and the wolf's forms and Rage. Fire burns it like any vampire, and silver in every form. |
| **Bane-ridden beast** | Threat |  |  | A bane slips through the Gauntlet and rides the biggest wild animal on the map, or comes in its own shape if there is none. It attacks anything alive, and the ground is tainted where the ridden beast dies. |
| **Black Spiral and Sabbat raid** | Big threat | 30 |  | With Kindred loaded: Black Spiral Dancers raid with Sabbat vampires at their side, one vampire for each few hundred raid points, up to three. |
| **Black Spiral Dancer raid** | Big threat | 20 |  | Black Spiral Dancers, Garou who walked the Labyrinth and came out the Wyrm's, hunt the colony by night. |
| **Black Spiral Dancers after a fetish** | Big threat | 30 | 30 days | Black Spiral Dancers come for your strongest fetish, named in the letter. Only a fetish of level 3 or more draws them. |
| **Black Spiral Hive** | Event | 40 |  | Black Spiral Dancers dig a Hive a few days from the colony. The land around it sickens and its Dancers hunt your Garou at night. Break it for great Glory. |
| **Corrupted factory** | Event | 30 |  | Pentex opens a factory staffed by fomori a few days away. Its waste reaches your land and its fomori come for your Garou. Shut it down for Glory. |
| **First Team raid** | Big threat | 10 |  | A Pentex First Team moves in: mercenaries paid to kill werewolves, with silver blades and fomori of their own. |
| **Fomori raid** | Big threat |  |  | Fomori raid: people the Wyrm has ridden until nothing human was left. They have caught your Garou's scent, and the Delirium doesn't touch them. |
| **Garou hunters** | Big threat |  | 6 days | Once the Veil has fallen to Hunted, hunters come for your Garou by day with silver: a probing band at Hunted, the Society of Leopold in force at Exposed. Those still alive go back at nightfall. Takes the place of a normal raid. |
| **Garou pack hunt** | Big threat | 20 | 30 days | With Kindred loaded: a pack of werewolves follows the stink of your vampire to the colony, or comes for your hunters once the Nation knows they kill Garou. It gathers at the edge before attacking, and a vampire who still holds on to Humanity can go out and try to talk it down. |
| **Hunted Abomination** | Event | 30 | 60 days | With Kindred loaded: an Abomination, a Garou who rose from the Embrace, comes in hurt and asks for shelter. Garou and Kindred both hunt it, so taking it in brings a court's blood hunt and the sept's anger with it. |
| **Kin wolf joins** | Event |  |  | A wolf of one of your Garou's bloodlines comes out of the wild, tame, and treats your Garou as family. An arctic wolf in the cold. |
| **Kinfolk joins** | Event |  |  | A wanderer with the blood of one of your Garou's tribes finds the colony and joins as Kinfolk. Needs a Garou colonist. |
| **Leech sighting** | Threat | 15 | 20 days | With Kindred loaded and no vampire of your own: a vampire slips in at night, feeds on a sleeping colonist and leaves. Sense Wyrm finds it while it's here. |
| **Lost cub** | Event |  |  | Someone going through their First Change sends word to your Garou, scared and confused. Take them in from the Quests tab before the offer runs out. The only way a metis arrives outside the scenarios. |
| **Pentex front** | Event | 10 | 20 days | A trader caravan with cheap goods that is really a Pentex front. It taints the ground it stands on, and only Sense Wyrm unmasks it. |
| **Pentex subsidiary** | Event | 15 |  | Pentex opens a subsidiary a few days from a colony with Garou. Its runoff seeps into your land and its First Team comes calling until your Garou shut it down, for Glory. |
| **Sept pack** | Sent by another event |  |  | When the Wyrm raids, an allied sept sends a pack of Garou to fight beside you. Never sent by the storyteller on its own. |
| **Spirit poachers** | Threat |  | 15 days | Trappers with spirit jars come to bottle the spirits around your caern. While they hold it, the caern weakens. Kill or down them to free the spirits, or they leave in half a day with what they caught. |
| **Stray cub** | Event |  |  | For a colony with no Garou: a youth fresh from a First Change, half wild and hurt, stumbles onto your land with something following. Take them in and they are your first Garou. This is the usual way a colony meets the Garou. |
| **The Black Furies ask a guard** | Event |  |  | The Black Furies ask their Garou of the colony to stand guard. Done: tribe regard (1 to 3 by rank) and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Bone Gnawers ask for food** | Event |  |  | The Bone Gnawers ask their Garou of the colony for food from storage. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Children of Gaia ask for a peace** | Event |  |  | The Children of Gaia ask their Garou of the colony to make a peace. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Fianna ask for a tale** | Event |  |  | The Fianna ask their Garou of the colony for a tale. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Get of Fenris ask for blood** | Event |  |  | The Get of Fenris ask their Garou of the colony to spill the Wyrm's blood. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Glass Walkers ask for parts** | Event |  |  | The Glass Walkers ask their Garou of the colony for parts from storage. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Red Talons ask the wild back** | Event |  |  | The Red Talons ask their Garou of the colony to give land back to the wild. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Shadow Lords ask for backing** | Event |  |  | The Shadow Lords ask their Garou of the colony for backing. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Silent Striders ask for a runner** | Event |  |  | The Silent Striders ask their Garou of the colony to run a message. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Silver Fangs ask for a rite** | Event |  |  | The Silver Fangs ask their Garou of the colony to perform a rite. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Stargazers send a riddle** | Event |  |  | The Stargazers send their Garou of the colony a riddle to work out. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Uktena ask for a bane bound** | Event |  |  | The Uktena ask their Garou of the colony to bind a bane. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **The Uktena ask for a fetish** | Event |  |  | The Uktena hear you keep a Wyrm-tainted fetish and send for it, to lock it away. Hand it over for Honor, Wisdom and goodwill; refuse and every Uktena of the colony loses regard with the tribe. |
| **The Wendigo ask the land avenged** | Event |  |  | The Wendigo ask their Garou of the colony to avenge the land. Done: tribe regard and Renown. Refused or failed: lost regard. |
| **Travellers** | Visitors |  |  | Once there are rumours of Garou in the colony, hunters arrive as ordinary visitors carrying silver and ask questions. More often as the Veil fails. Anything a Garou does in front of them counts against the Veil. |
| **Two packs ask for neutral ground** | Event |  |  | Two feuding packs of two septs ask to meet for one night at your caern, or your best hall without one. If both leave unharmed, your strongest Garou gains Wisdom and both septs think better of you. |
| **Visiting challenger** | Event |  | 25 days | A Get of Fenris or Silver Fang of the Nation, of the same rank or one higher, comes to test your strongest Garou. Accepted, the one challenged picks a duel or a staring-down. The winner gains Glory. |
| **Wyrm's Hunted** | Big threat |  | 20 days | One of the Wyrm's Hunted passes near the colony. A Black Spiral pack that has never fought you walks through unless you harm it; one that has, or a great beast, comes for the colony. |
