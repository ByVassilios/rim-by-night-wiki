# The Veil, septs and hunters

## The Veil

The Litany forbids letting humans learn what the Garou are. The Delirium hides most of what they
see. The danger is the few who keep their wits.

### Witnesses

An outsider becomes a witness when they see a colony Garou:

- in Crinos, and have the Willpower to keep their wits (see [the Delirium](Being-Garou.md#the-delirium));
- change shape into any form;
- walk about as a Hispo, on the colony's map or in a settlement;
- work a Gift no mortal could explain (one worked in Crinos adds nothing);
- or see a body a Garou tore open with claws or teeth, until it's buried, burned or rotted away.

A message names each witness. At Willpower 9 or more they remember; below that they explain it
away. Kinfolk, fomori and the colony's own don't count.

**Dealing with them.** Kill, imprison or recruit a witness before they leave the map, or make
them forget. The "Witnesses on the map" alert lists everyone left.

**Talking them out of it.** Select any colonist, right-click the witness and pick **Talk ... out
of what they saw**. You get two tries a day:

| Way | Roll | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Explain it away | Manipulation + Social at 7 | On a success they believe it was nothing |
| Bribe | 40 silver | They always take it. Seven in ten keep quiet; the rest talk anyway, and you aren't told |
| Threaten | Strength + Social at 7 | On a success they swear to keep quiet. On a failure they run, and a witness who runs off the map counts |
| Delirium | A point of Rage | A Garou lets them see the wolf for a heartbeat, and the Delirium takes the memory. Always works |

### The score

Each witness who leaves the map adds to the Veil score: 10 for one who remembers, 3 for one who
explained it away, twice that for a trader. It fades by 1 a day.

A Garou seen by a mortal who will remember loses 5 Honor, once a day at most, and breaks the
Litany for each such witness who gets away.

| Level | Score | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet | under 20 | Nobody outside is talking about wolves |
| Rumours | 20 | Travellers tell stories. Hunters start visiting as travellers, carrying silver |
| Hunted | 50 | The stories reached hunters. Bands of them come by day |
| Exposed | 80 | The hunting order sends everything it has. The septs think 30 less of you |

A letter marks each level, and an alert stays up from Rumours on. The Garou tab shows the score.
The setting "The Veil" turns witnesses and the hunters they bring off.

### The sept asks

Once 12 points of the Veil are laid on one Garou, your sworn sept (or the nearest one you know)
sends a letter asking who did it, at most once every 15 days:

- **Name them.** The sept deals with it and cleans up half of what that Garou cost the Veil. The
  Garou is sore about it for ten days. The Nation thinks 5 better of you.
- **Take the blame as a colony.** The Nation thinks 5 less of you.
- **Refuse.** The Nation thinks 15 less of you.

## Hunters

A hunting order, the hunters of The Rim by Night: Core.

- **The raid** comes by day once the Veil is Hunted, a probing band below Exposed and the whole
  order at Exposed. The band pulls back at nightfall. It also needs Core's hunter setting.
- **Silver.** Every hunter's melee weapon is remade in silver.
- **Finishing.** A hunter goes for a downed Garou first and kills them where they lie.
- **Visits.** From Rumours on, hunters come as travellers now and then, more often as the Veil
  falls.

## The septs' faction

The septs trade as an ordinary-looking faction whose name, color, people and post labels change every game, so its name tells you nothing.

3 to 8 days after the colony's first Garou, the nearest sept sends a letter naming only itself. It's
a faction like any other, though it never raids.

The other septs stay off the world map one by one until you learn of them. A letter from a sept shows
it, your Garou can meet its people, and a sept you know will now and then vouch for another. News of a
sept you haven't met doesn't reach you, though once you know one sept you hear that a new one was founded, with no name or place.

- **Each sept** leans to a tribe and keeps a caern of a type and level, shown on the world map.
  A sept whose tribe has a grudge with yours starts out liking you less.
- **Trade.** Septs sell plain goods and a few fetishes and talens, and buy them back. Their
  caravans and visitors come as any faction's do.
- **Missions.** Once the Nation knows you, it asks for help: shut down a Pentex plant, or kill the
  banes a sept's Theurges saw coming, which cross into your map a few hours after you accept. Each
  done is worth 15 goodwill; one let lapse after 15 days costs 7. Taking in a lost cub is worth 15
  too.
- **More missions** from the nearest sept you know: host a small moot of its Garou for a day and a
  half while the Wyrm's creatures come for them, lend it one of your Garou for five days (they come
  back with 2 Honor), clear a bane nest where the Gauntlet wore through, or break a fomori camp and
  take in the Kinfolk found alive there. Each pays a fetish or a pathstone and 10 goodwill; one let
  lapse costs 5.
- **Totem tasks and vengeance** show in the Quests tab while they last: the totem's task for this
  lunar cycle, and an oath to avenge a fallen sept.
- **Talking.** In a sept's town, select a colonist and right-click anyone of the sept to **Talk
  to** them. See [Talking to people](#talking-to-people).
- **Help.** When the Wyrm raids and the Nation is your ally, half the time a pack of three sept
  Garou arrives to fight beside you. With an Opened Bridge, they come at once.
- **Moon bridges.** Gifts that open moon bridges reach twice as far to a sept or caern.

### Requests from the Nation

Once the colony knows a friendly sept, its Garou ask things of you now and then. Each request comes
as a letter in the Quests tab that you accept or refuse; each has its own switch in the settings.

- **Rescues.** A wounded Garou with fomori an hour or two behind asks to be let in for a day; a
  Ronin cast out by its sept begs five days' shelter (this costs 6 goodwill with the Nation up
  front, and kept safe to the end the Ronin joins you); a Garou pinned with silver is left at the
  map's edge for hunters, and once carried to a bed the silver comes out and the hunters come
  looking. A life saved is 3 Honor, and the sept owes you aid.
- **Fighters on call.** Each debt is one pack sent to fight beside you. The Overview page of the Garou tab
  shows how many the septs owe on its **Fighters on call** row; **Call in** sends for one.
- **Theurges.** Your caern and fetishes draw Theurges of other septs. A caern level counts 5, each
  fetish 2 a level, talens 1 each up to 5. At 6, 15 and 30 points a Theurge asks to commune at the
  caern or sit with a fetish for 2, 4 or 6 nights, for 1 to 3 Honor and goodwill.
- **The Uktena** ask for any Wyrm-tainted fetish you keep, to lock it away: 2 Honor, 2 Wisdom and
  8 goodwill. Refuse and each of your Uktena loses 2 regard with the tribe.
- **Black Spiral Dancers** raid at night for a fetish of level 3 or more, and the letter says which.
- **Teaching.** A sept Garou asks one of yours to teach a Gift or rite it knows: right-click the
  guest with the teacher, one lesson a night. All lessons given earn W20's Honor and Wisdom for
  teaching and 5 goodwill; leaving lessons ungiven costs 4.

- **Favours**, about one letter every ten days between them:
  - keep a wounded elder for a season (half the time the Wyrm finds it here): 3 Honor, 10 goodwill
    and a debt;
  - keep a sept's fetish for a season; it comes by drop pod, and the sept takes it back from a home
    map at the end (3 Honor, 10 goodwill and a debt; missing, 15 goodwill lost);
  - foster a cub: three lessons from a Garou of rank 2 or more, 2 Honor, 2 Wisdom and 10 goodwill; the cub goes home once taught, or after a season;
  - let a hunting pack run your land for two days (about four wild animals go with them);
  - hand over a weapon or work of art of good quality or better as a fetish's housing: 2 Honor,
    10 goodwill and a talen back;
  - neutral ground for two feuding packs of two septs, one night at the caern or best hall;
  - a Garou maimed by silver asks a doctor of Medicine 8 or more to operate: 2 Honor, goodwill and
    a debt;
  - lend a Kinfolk with a skill of 8 or more for a season: goodwill and a debt when they're back.

### The tribes' requests

From Fostern (rank 2) on, a Garou's own tribe asks things of it, about one request a season
between all the tribes. The Character tab shows each Garou's regard with its tribe, from -10 to 10.
A request done raises it by 1 at Fostern, 2 at Adren and 3 at Athro or higher, with Renown; refused
or failed, it falls by 1.

| Tribe | What it asks |
|---|---|
| Black Furies | guard a sacred grove on your land for 15 days; the Wyrm may come |
| Bone Gnawers | 20 meals for a poor settlement |
| Children of Gaia | host two quarrelling septs' Garou for a night and settle it |
| Fianna | tell a visiting Galliard the colony's tale (a Social roll) |
| Get of Fenris | raze a Pentex site (a mission) |
| Glass Walkers | 6 components |
| Red Talons | drive a human camp out of the wild (a mission) |
| Shadow Lords | back a Shadow Lord's rise (4 goodwill with the Nation) |
| Silent Striders | run a message: the Garou is away 6 days |
| Silver Fangs | lead any rite within the season |
| Stargazers | answer a visiting Stargazer's riddle (an Intellectual roll) |
| Uktena | clear a bane nest (a mission) |
| Wendigo | answer a wrong against the land (a mission) |

### Hosting a moot

With a caern and the Nation's goodwill, the colony can host moots. A sept writes when one opens,
and the Garou tab has an "Ask to host a moot" link for the highest one open now (greyed with the
reason when none is). Each is hosted once a year at most.

| Goodwill | Moot | Nights | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | The neighbours' moot | 1 | a caern |
| 40 | A Hearing: a famous pack's tale, then the colony judges its Renown | 2 | a caern of level 2 |
| 60 | A Grand Moot of the host's tribe, kept the tribe's own way, then a council | 2 | a caern of level 3, a sept office held |
| 80 | A Concolation: five elders speak, the great matter is voted, a war party sets out | 3 | a caern of level 4, an office, a host of rank 4; a season's notice |

The preparation window lists meals for every guest and night. The Wyrm may
strike on one night. Each tribe's Grand Moot opens with its custom: the Furies' sacred hunt, the
Gnawers' feast and old debts, the Children of Gaia's meditation or revel, the Fianna's epics, the
Fenris brawls and ordeals, the Walkers' planning meeting, the Talons' run with wolves, the Shadow
Lords' pomp under a storm, the Striders' races and tales, the Fangs' candle-lit procession, the
Stargazers' riddles, the Uktena's hidden lore and the Wendigo's old ways. The host's skill rolls
decide each night. A good showing pays goodwill and Renown; a Grand Moot or Concolation that goes
well names the host to an office at a neighbour sept: once a season it can call the sept's
fighters and sit in judgment (a Social roll), and each year the sept sends 300 silver and a talen.
The Concolation's vote can set the colony on a Pentex or bane nest mission.

A guest who dies or is hurt in your care costs 10 goodwill and the sept's chronicle remembers it.

The Nation goes on without you, and the Chronicle page under Rim by Night keeps its news. Elders are challenged, cubs go
through the First Change, and packs go after the Wyrm and don't come back. A caern can grow
stronger or lose a level to banes, and a caern that loses its last level falls: the sept is gone
and Pentex builds on the ground. Pentex also opens plants near septs, and each one costs the
nearest sept a caern level every season until someone shuts it down. You get a letter when that
happens, and shutting the plant down earns 10 goodwill with the Nation on top of the Glory. While
the Nation is short of the septs it started with, it founds a new one about once a year. With
Kindred loaded, septs fight the vampire courts near them, and with Imbued, hunter cells hunt them.
A sept no rival can reach may be struck by the Wyrm instead.

### Talking to people

Select a colonist and right-click someone to talk to them: a prisoner (**Question ...**), a witness,
or someone in a sept's, court's or hunter cell's town (**Talk to ...**). Your colonist walks over and
the talk window opens. Pick what to ask about, then how to put it. Each way shows its roll under the
button.

| Way | Roll |
|---|---|
| Kindly | Charisma + Social at 6 |
| Firmly | Manipulation + Social at 6 |
| Threaten | Strength + Social at 7. It hurts their mood |
| Bribe | 40 silver, always answers. A Garou of the sept costs double and its leader triple |
| Renown | Charisma + Social, plus a die for each rank, at 6. Only with Garou and Kinfolk |

Each roll is a point harder against anything supernatural. Nobody lies, so a poor roll just gets
nothing.

**Prisoners.** Any Garou can question a prisoner, two to four times a day. Ask where it comes from
(its home goes on the map), what it knows, or what it is (a monster you took for a mortal gives
itself away). A fomor or a Pentex agent names a Pentex site, and a Black Spiral Dancer the way to its
Hive. A threat hurts the prisoner's mood and makes it harder to recruit, but leaves no wounds.

**Townsfolk.** Each person in a town answers one question, then wants three days before the next (a
setting). You can ask:

- **Small talk.** The weather, the neighbours, whatever is on their mind. It costs nothing, always
  gets an answer, and can go on all night.
- **What's the news?** Word of the world's septs, courts and cells.
- **Where is...** Pick a place from what they could know of: a Wyrm nest (a sept's Garou and Kin,
  to a Garou), or another sept you don't know yet (Garou speaking to Garou). The question only
  shows up when there's somewhere to ask about.
- **Have you seen...** A colonist someone took. The settlement holding them goes on the map.
- **What troubles you?** The Wyrm has dug in near the sept: a Pentex site or a corrupted factory
  to clear.

A threat costs the town 3 goodwill whatever the roll. A Garou talking in a court's or a cell's town
passes for an ordinary visitor. Asking kindly and bribing are safe. A threat gives it away one time
in five, a failed roll one in ten and a botch one in two. Found out, the town turns on the colony.

### When a sept falls

A sept's elder and notables have names, the rest are a headcount, and they outlive its fall. Some
die, some go missing, and the rest go to another sept, their own tribe first, or found a new one.
Kinfolk go to the nearest sept. The same happens when the sept's settlement is razed, by you or by
another mod.

- **The Veil.** Each sept has a rating, shown in words on its inspect pane once you know it. Pentex
  sites nearby, wars and your own Garou breaching the Veil wear it down; time and rites mend it. A
  sept with a thinner Veil is attacked more often.
- **Sieges.** When a war is about to break a sept you know, it is under siege for a few days first,
  and a quest lets you go and help. Who dies on the map is dead in the world.
- **Letters.** A sept you know falling gets a letter: who did it, if anyone knows, a few of the
  dead, who is missing. Taking a sept yourself says so. Galliards at moots bring rumors of the rest.
- **Refugees.** Survivors who like the colony and are within reach may ask to join, as an offer in
  the Quests tab open for two days. With a caern,
  survivors of a nearby sept may ask to make it their sept.
- **Vengeance.** A sept lost to a war, a taken town or the Wyrm puts your Garou under an oath for 30
  days. Until it's settled they are unhappy about it, the sept's own tribe more so. The first of the
  foe one of them kills settles it, with Glory for everyone in the fight and a good mood for every
  Garou in the colony. Losing a second sept to the same foe renews the oath.

Core's settings, under The world of darkness, have a switch for all of this (off: a fallen home's people just vanish), a bloodiness slider for falls with no battle, and a world pace slider.

### Rank challenges

A caravan at a friendly sept has a **Rank challenge** button. The sept's elders set a challenge
for one of its Garou who has a full ten temporary Renown or enough for the next rank: Willpower
plus rank against 7. Winning does what the Rite of Accomplishment does. Losing costs 1 Honor, a
botch 3.

### Packs of the sept

A sept's people split into packs: two to four, each around its strongest member, the elder's own first. Each
has a temperament that sets what it wants, what it pays in, which others it gets on with and which it feuds with. The
**Packs** page of the sept's window shows them as cards: temperament, members, power out of 20 in ten cells, and a bar
for what they think of you. While you're sworn there, that bar is the average regard of its members.

Pick one to see its leader, members, allies and feuds, and **Ask them for work**: one of their own missions, once every
five days, shown in the Quests tab to accept there. Done, the pack gains a point of power and 2 regard. A
pack that thinks -5 of you or worse gives no work.

When a rival comes within two power of the elder's pack and stays there for 30 days, it challenges the elder for the sept's lead, as W20's challenge for rank. The
showdown comes 20 to 40 days later: a duel or the moot's word. Each side's chance is its power against the other's; a deciding mission
done for a side counts two. **Back** a side from the page: 6 standing if it wins, 6 lost if it doesn't, and the winners'
regard moves the same way. Plotters who win take the lead, and nobody plots again for 60 days.

| Temperament | Wants | Missions |
|---|---|---|
| Warlike | the Wyrm's servants dead | a bane for the pack, the watch in the bawn |
| Wise | the spirits heard, the lore kept | word to another sept, a bane |
| Cunning | to win without a fair fight | word to another sept, a bane |
| Traditional | the Litany kept, the caern held | the watch in the bawn, word to another sept |
| Wild | to run free and fight | a bane |

## The colony as a sept

Once the colony has a caern, it's a sept. The **Sept** tab, on the bar at the bottom, shows the
Veil and one row per office. Each office takes one colony Garou of rank 1 or more, and one Garou
holds one office. A year in office is worth Glory, Honor and Wisdom.

| Office | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Sept leader | A stronger moot for everyone, and 1 Honor for the leader at each moot |
| Master of the Rite | A die more leading a rite on a map with a caern |
| Warder | 1 Rage an hour while enemies are on the caern's map |
| Guardian | Sight and hearing up a tenth |
| Den parent | The colony's cubs and kinfolk on the map learn a quarter faster |
| Talesinger | Each Garou's deed worth 2 Renown or more is worth 1 more |
| Truthcatcher | A title only |

Without a caern the colony is a pack, not a sept, and the offices stay empty.
