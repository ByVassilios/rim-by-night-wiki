# Learning tips

The tips the game shows the first times you meet something.

| Tip | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Forms** | A Garou has five forms: Homid, Glabro, Crinos, Hispo and Lupus.<br><br>The form bar above its buttons picks one, and "In fights" sends it into Crinos when a fight starts. Crinos is the strongest and the most dangerous to be seen in: outsiders who keep their wits become witnesses to a breach of the Veil. |
| **Gnosis** | Gnosis is the Garou's bond with the spirit world. Many Gifts cost it, and so do stepping sideways and calling a spirit.<br><br>It comes back slowly through meditation, faster near a caern, and all at once from a Sacred Hunt. |
| **Harano** | Harano is the Garou's despair at a war they may be losing. Losses, failures and a pack that falls apart feed it.<br><br>Keep a Garou in Harano with its pack, hold the moot and spare it more grief. Sunk deep enough, it may walk away from the colony for good. |
| **Learning Gifts** | Experience and rank buy new Gifts.<br><br>Open the Garou tab's Gifts page and press "Learn a Gift...". A spirit can be called to teach it for Gnosis, or a higher-ranked Garou of the colony who knows it can give lessons. |
| **Rage and frenzy** | Rage is the wolf's fury. The moon gives it each night outdoors, most at the full moon, and it pays for extra actions in a fight.<br><br>When something provokes a Garou (a wound, an insult, hunger, a cage) it rolls its current Rage against the moon. Enough successes and the wolf takes over. Keep a Garou with high Rage out of trouble near the full moon, or let it spend the Rage in a fight. |
| **Renown and rank** | Garou society runs on Renown: Glory, Honor and Wisdom. Deeds give temporary points, shown over the Garou as it happens.<br><br>Ten temporary points in a track become a permanent dot through the Rite of Accomplishment, or by winning an elder's rank challenge at a sept. Enough permanent dots raise the Garou's rank, and rank opens stronger Gifts. Hover the rank on the Garou tab to see what the next one needs. |
| **Rites** | Rites mark rank, bind packs and spirits, cleanse taint and keep the caern.<br><br>Select the Garou who will lead and press "Perform a rite". The colony's Garou come to form the circle, and everyone close to the ritemaster when it ends takes part. More Garou than the rite needs make the roll easier. |
| **The Garou** | A werewolf lives in your colony now.<br><br>The Garou tab shows its tribe, breed and auspice, tonight's moon, and its Rage, Gnosis and Willpower. Its wounds close on their own, paid for in food, so keep it fed. Its Gifts sit on tiles above the buttons, one tile per list. |
| **The Penumbra** | Beside the world lies the Umbra. The Penumbra, its nearest part, is a mirror of the home map with nothing the colony built in it.<br><br>Stepping sideways costs Gnosis. Wyld glades there give it back, banes rise from the Wyrm's taint, and spirits can be met, bargained with and taught by. "Step back" returns the Garou home. |
| **The Veil** | The Litany forbids letting humans learn what the Garou are.<br><br>The Delirium makes most people forget what they saw, but a strong-willed outsider who sees a Garou in Crinos, shifting shape, using an unexplainable Gift or a body torn by claws becomes a witness. Kill, imprison or recruit a witness before they leave the map, or the Veil suffers and hunters come. |
| **The Wyrm** | The Wyrm is corruption gone mad, and its servants come for colonies with Garou: fomori, Black Spiral Dancers, Pentex's First Team.<br><br>Where they die, taint spreads on the ground and stokes a Garou's Rage. The Rite of Cleansing lifts it. Silver is the Wyrm's best weapon: its wounds don't regenerate. |
