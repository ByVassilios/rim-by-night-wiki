# Mental states and inspirations

Mental states, breaks and inspirations this mod adds.

| State | Kind | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Frenzy** | Mental state | …'s Rage has taken over.<br><br>He or she has taken the war form and will attack anyone in sight, strangers first. It lasts at least an hour, then ends once no enemy is left in sight and he or she gets hold of him or herself. |
| **Thrall of the Wyrm** | Mental state | Something worse than Rage has hold of …. Something in his or her eyes is laughing.<br><br>He or she is in the Thrall of the Wyrm and will tear into anyone, friends first as readily as foes. It will leave a mark on him or her that doesn't wash off. |
| **Fox frenzy** | Mental state | …'s Rage has broken the wrong way.<br><br>He or she has taken wolf form and is running from the fight. It lasts at least an hour, then ends once no enemy is left in sight and he or she gets hold of him or herself. |
| **Gift-bound** | Mental state | Mental state: fighting for a Garou |
