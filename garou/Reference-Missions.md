# Every mission and quest

Every mission a court, sept, cell or consilium can give, and every quest these mods offer.

| Mission | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Banes at the thin place** | Travel to a place where banes have crossed over and kill every one of them, for one of the sept's packs. |
| **The watch in the bawn** | Lend one of your Garou to a pack of the sept for some days, to stand guard in the caern's bawn. |
| **The Galliard's run** | Lend one of your Garou to carry a pack's word to another sept and bring the answer back. |
| **The stolen fetish** | Travel to the Black Spiral Dancers' hideout that holds the sept's stolen fetish and clear it. The sept's Theurges bind a fetish for you in thanks. |
| **The cub's watch** | Keep a cub from the sept at your caern until its Rite of Passage and keep it alive. Something will probably come looking. |
| **Offerings for the moon rite** | Bring the goods the sept is short of for its full-moon moot by caravan before the deadline. |
| **The Wyrm-bloated beast** | Travel to where a Wyrm-tainted animal roams the sept's land and kill it. |
| **The fallen caern** | Travel to the lodge of a dead sept and clear out the fomori who moved in. |
| **The Wyrm's holdings near *sept*** | The elders of *sept*, a sept that leans *tribe*, have found the Wyrm's hand at *target*: a Pentex subsidiary behind a friendly logo, First Team guards on the gate and fomori working the poisoned ground. They want your Garou to tear it down.<br><br>- Kill everyone there<br>- Leave it standing too long and the sept will remember it |
| **Banes upon the wind** | The Theurges of *sept*, a sept that leans *tribe*, have watched banes moving through the Umbra toward *target*, drawn by something rotten. If your Garou are ready, the banes will cross the Gauntlet within a few hours of your answer.<br><br>- Kill every one of them |
| ***sept*'s Kin in peril** | Pentex has set up at *target*, right beside *sept*, the homestead your Garou's people come from. The Kinfolk there carry the blood of the Garou, and the fomori are already watching the village. The kin can't fight them alone.<br><br>- Kill everyone at the outpost and the village is safe<br>- Leave the call unanswered, or take it and wait too long, and there won't be a village left |
| **The moot at your caern** | Host a small moot of the sept's Garou at your colony for a while and keep them all alive. The Wyrm's creatures are likely to follow them. |
| **A claw for *asker*** | Lend one of your Garou to the sept for some days to stand guard at its caern. They come home with Honor when the days are up. |
| **The wound in the Gauntlet** | Travel to a place where banes crossed through a worn Gauntlet and kill every bane there. |
| **Kinfolk in the Wyrm's grip** | Travel to the fomori camp holding the sept's Kinfolk and clear it. The Kinfolk who is still alive joins you. |
| **Purging the camp at *site*** | Travel to a camp cutting into the last wild land near the sept and clear it. |
| **Gaia's vengeance at *site*** | Travel to the camp of the people who fouled a spirit spring and clear it. |
| **Cleanse the *site*** | *description*<br><br>It stays until someone shuts it down. Send a caravan and kill or drive off everyone there.<br><br>+ Glory Renown for your Garou |
