# Every setting

Every setting in Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night, with what it does.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Renown gained** | Scales the Renown a Garou earns for deeds, and so how soon they can rise in rank. Renown lost for breaking the Litany stays as it is. |
| **Rage gained** | Scales the Rage a Garou gains from the moon, wounds, insults and the rest. Less Rage means fewer actions in a fight, fewer form shifts and fewer frenzies. At 0% a Garou only has the Rage they start the night with. |
| **Frenzy** | Off, wounds, insults and hunger never send a Garou into frenzy or the Thrall of the Wyrm. A Gift that calls up a frenzy still does, and so does the First Change. |
| **Harano** | Off, losses no longer sink a Garou into Harano. Any Harano they already have fades as usual. |
| **The Litany** | Off, breaking a tenet of the Litany costs no Honor or Wisdom and doesn't count against the Garou this season. |
| **The Delirium** | Off, no one panics or flees at the sight of a Crinos, and colonists aren't shaken by it. Outsiders remember what they saw, as with the Pierced Veil flaw, so the Veil suffers more. |
| **Challenges for alpha** | Packmates now and then challenge the alpha for the lead. Off, they never do on their own. |
| **Regeneration** | Scales how fast a Garou's wounds close on their own. Silver and other aggravated wounds still won't regenerate. |
| **Silver damage** | Scales the extra harm silver does to a Garou. At 100% it hits half again as hard as steel. |
| **Frenzy difficulty** | Added to the difficulty of every frenzy roll. Higher means the wolf takes over less often. |
| **Kinfolk who Change** | The chance that a Kinfolk with the blood for it goes through the First Change when something sets it off: from thin blood to strong. The mod's chances are 10%, 25% and 50%. |
| **The Curse** | Scales how much a Garou's Rage unsettles mortals: their mood near a Garou whose Rage beats their Willpower, and the Garou's worse luck with talk, trade and animals. At 0% the Curse is off. |
| **Kinfolk events** | Kinfolk and wolves of the tribe come to join the colony, and lost cubs turn up. |
| **A stray cub comes** | A colony with no Garou gets a stray cub at the edge of its land, some time between day 15 and day 40, and again every 30 to 60 days until one is taken in. Off, the cub only comes by chance. Needs kinfolk events on. |
| **Gear stays with the Garou** | When a Garou takes a form that can't wear or hold something, it goes with them and comes back when they change back. Off, it drops at their feet, as in the tabletop game. |
| **The Wyrm attacks** | Fomori, Black Spiral Dancers, Pentex First Teams and banes come for colonies with Garou, and Pentex opens plants nearby. |
| **The Veil** | Outsiders who keep their wits after seeing a Crinos can spread the word when they leave. Too much of that, and hunters come with silver. |
| **Veil damage** | Scales how much the Veil score rises for each outsider who leaves with what they saw. |
| **Veil healing** | Scales how fast the Veil score falls back each day as people forget. |
| **Wounds roll for frenzy once a fight** | Only the first wound of a fight, after an hour without one, makes a Garou roll for frenzy. Off, every wound does. |
| **The Thrall needs taint** | Only a Garou with Wyrm taint, one who broke the Litany this season, or one with Short Fuse can fall into the Thrall of the Wyrm when a frenzy goes badly. Off, any Garou can. |
| **Black Fury men are born Metis** | A man is only born to the Black Furies as a metis. Off, a man can be a Homid or Lupus Black Fury too. |
| **Garou to build a caern** | How many Garou the circle of the Rite of Caern Building needs, the ritemaster among them. |
| **Gnosis to build a caern** | How much Gnosis the circle pours into the Rite of Caern Building, kin giving a point each. Every Garou in it still gives up a permanent point. |
| **Totem tasks** | The chance, each lunar cycle, that a pack's totem asks a task of one of the pack. At 0% totems never ask. |
| **A misused spirit stays silent** | How long a fetish's spirit refuses to answer after it is used against its nature, in a frenzy or against a friend. |
| **A Gnosis point wakes a fetish** | A Garou who has a Gnosis point spends it to wake a fetish with no roll. Off, every use is a Gnosis roll. |
| **Gifts camera shake** | How hard big Gifts shake the camera: a storm's lightning, Gaia's Vengeance, a Great Leap landing. At 0% the camera stays still. |
| **Gifts sky flashes** | Big Gifts flash the sky in their colour, and storms tint it while they build. Off, the sky keeps its own light. |
| **Moon bridges on the world map** | Gifts that open a moon bridge or walk the Pattern Web carry the Garou to a tile in range at once. Off, those Gifts are greyed out and travel goes by caravan. |
| **Towns for each sept** | The Garou Nation keeps one sept for this many ordinary towns in the world, two of them always within a caravan's reach of the colony. Lower means more septs. Takes effect on a new world, and on new septs founded later. |
| **The challenge** | A raiding pack that isn't of the Wyrm may hold back and offer the colony's Garou a duel instead of a fight. Win, and the pack leaves; lose, and it takes silver worth the raid. |
| **The Wyrm's Hunted** | A handful of the Wyrm's servants the Garou Nation most wants dead roam the world map: Black Spiral packs and great Wyrm beasts. They roam either way, but only a colony the Nation knows is told of them, sees them on the map or earns Renown for one. Turned off, they leave the world map; turned back on, a new list arrives in one letter. |
