# Every alert

Every alert this mod can show on the right of the screen.

| Alert | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Rage running high** | These Garou have enough Rage, under tonight's moon, that the next wound or insult could set off a frenzy. Keep them away from trouble or let them rest. |
| **Gnosis spent** | These Garou have no Gnosis left, so Gifts that cost it and stepping sideways are out of reach. It comes back by meditating, by staying near a caern, and all at once from a Sacred Hunt. |
| **Harano drowning** | These Garou are sunk so deep in Harano that one of them may walk away from the colony for good. Keep them with their pack, hold the moot, and spare them more losses until it eases. |
| **Silver weapons** | Enemies here are carrying silver. Silver hits Garou harder and leaves aggravated wounds that don't regenerate. Keep your Garou at range, or take these enemies down first. |
| **Totem Ban broken** | This pack broke its totem's Ban and has lost every favor the totem gives. The Rite of Contrition makes amends. |
| **Garou seen by outsiders** | These Garou are in Crinos or Hispo while outsiders are on the map. Anyone with the will to keep their wits who sees them becomes a witness to a breach of the Veil. Shift back to Homid, or keep them out of sight until the visitors leave. |
| **Gift ready to learn** | These Garou have the rank and experience for a new Gift from their own lists. Open the Garou tab's Gifts page and press "Learn a Gift...". |
