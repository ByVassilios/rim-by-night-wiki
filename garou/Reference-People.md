# People, factions and creatures

The factions, the kinds of people and the creatures this mod adds.

## Factions

| Faction | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Garou pack** | A young pack sent by its sept to guard a wild place, with the kinfolk who came along.<br><br>Garou are the warriors of Gaia, born to fight the Wyrm and its servants. A pack is the smallest unit of the Garou Nation: a few werewolves of different tribes and auspices, bound to each other and to a totem spirit that asks a ban of them in return for its gifts. The pack's alpha leads in the hunt, but it earns its Renown with the rest of them. Keep the Litany, honour the totem, and the sept may one day ask you to guard a caern of its own. |
| **Hill village** | A village of kinfolk in the hills, where the wolf-blood still runs.<br><br>Kinfolk are the human relatives of the Garou. They don't Change, and the Delirium doesn't touch them. For generations the people of this village have married into the families the wolves watch over, and they keep the old customs without always knowing why. Somewhere among them the blood runs strong enough for one to Change. |
| **Kinfolk family** | A family with wolves in the bloodline, moved out to the edge of nowhere.<br><br>Kinfolk are the human and wolf relatives of the Garou. They don't Change, and the Delirium doesn't touch them, so they see the werewolves for what they are. The tribes count on them for children, for cover in the human world and for a home to come back to. Somewhere in this family, the blood runs strong enough for one of them to Change. |
| **Lost cub** | A Garou who went through the First Change alone, with no sept or elder to explain it.<br><br>Most cubs are found before the Change by the Garou who watch over their bloodlines, and taken to a sept to learn the Litany and pass their Rite of Passage. This one was missed. Until a sept finds them, they have no tribe that knows them, no Gifts but what instinct gives, and a Rage that does not care who is standing close. |
| **Raiders** | Hired guns on someone's payroll. Nobody who asks who pays them gets an answer. |
| **Raiders** | A hard crowd that follows a trail until it ends. |
| **Remnant of a fallen sept** | The last Garou of one tribe and their kin, who got out when their sept fell.<br><br>Every tribe of the Garou Nation keeps its own ways, its own totem and its own homeland, and its septs guard the caerns that Gaia's power wells up from. When a caern falls, its spirits die or go to the Wyrm, and the sept goes with it. These survivors carry the tribe's ways far from home and mean to build a new sept around them. |
| **Sept of the old ways** | A young pack from a sept that never took up the ways of the cities.<br><br>Septs of the old ways kept their caerns deep in the forests and high in the hills, with kinfolk villages nearby to raise the children and keep the fires lit. They fought with claw, spear and bow, and they still do. This pack was sent to find a caern of its own and guard it as the old sept guards theirs. |
| **Settlers** | Scattered settlements that keep to their own land. They trade with those they trust and tell outsiders very little. |
| **Settlers** | Ordinary people working a stretch of land nearby. They trade with their neighbors and send their young out to find work. |

## Creatures and races

| | Name | Value | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | **Cannibal spirit** |  | Great Wendigo's hunger made flesh, called by a Gift. It hunts the Garou's foes until its time runs out. |
|  | **Chimera** |  | A creature of dream, made real for a while by a Gift. It fights for the Garou who called it until it fades. |
|  | **Elemental** |  | A spirit of raw earth, fire, air or water, called up by a Gift. It fights for the Garou who called it until its time runs out. |
|  | **Great beast** |  | One of the great beasts of the first days, called back by a Gift. It fights for the Garou who called it until its time runs out. |
|  | **Great wolf** |  | A great wolf of Valhalla, called by a Gift. It fights for the Garou who called it until its time runs out. |
|  | **Hellbeast** | 0 | A beast bred in the Wyrm's own Realms: all muscle, horn and burning hide, with the Wyrm's hunger in its eyes. It hunts Gaia's children by scent across whole regions and does not stop. |
|  | **Hound of the Hunt** |  | A hound of the Wild Hunt, called by a Gift. It runs down the Garou's foes until its time runs out. |
|  | **Living tree** |  | A tree woken by a Gift and walking on its roots. It fights for the Garou who woke it until it takes root again. |
|  | **Nexus crawler** | 0 | One of the Wyrm's great destroyers: a heaving mass of chitin, mouths and grasping limbs the size of a house. It crawls out of the Umbra's dark places to unmake whatever it finds, and Garou who meet one alone seldom come back to say so. |
|  | **Shadow** |  | A shadow of the Garou who called it, given claws. One blow puts it out. |
|  | **Totem avatar** |  | An avatar of the sept's totem, crossed over from the Umbra to stand at its moot. It stays a few hours beside the ritemaster, and fights for the sept if it must. |
|  | **Wyldling** |  | A spirit of pure, mad change, loosed by a Gift. It fights for the Garou who called it until it fades. |
