# The sworn sept

Pledge the colony to one sept and its Garou stop being names in the chronicle. Each has a personality, means, a goal
of its own and an opinion of you that moves with what you do. They hand you work, owe you debts, run beside you
and, if they come to hate you, work against you.

## Pledging

The Sept window's Nation section names the nearest sept the colony knows, with **Swear to this home**. You need:

- a colony Garou,
- the sept met and the Garou Nation at peace with the colony.

One sept at a time. Its roster fills to 8 to 12 named Garou from its warriors, in packs of two and three; packmates
are friends. New pages appear on the Sept window's rail: **People**, **Council** and, while a war runs, **War**.

Leaving costs 20 goodwill with the Nation. Loyal Garou drop to -5 regard, and the debts the sept owes you
shrink a size. A sept that falls frees the colony with a letter.

## Debts

Every sept the colony knows can owe it debts, trivial, minor, major or life, as Kindred courts do. A sept's mission
pays one when it's done: trivial for an early colony, minor once raids come at a mid-game size, major for the hardest.
Once sworn, the sept's Garou owe you debts for their own errands too.

Spend them on the **Debts** page of the Garou tab. **Ask** lists what a sept does for a debt, and a bigger debt covers a
smaller service: two talens (trivial), a few of its fighters at the door or the elders teaching your Garou 3
experience each (minor), a fetish (major), one of its five greatest fetishes (life). **Ledger** lists each debt with
who owes it and why; **Call in** on a row picks what that debt pays for. Trained wolves bought from a sept are paid in
debts too. Leaving a sworn sept shrinks its debts a size.

## People

The People page lists every named Garou: office, pack (tribe for those in none) and regard, with a filter for friends, rivals and
enemies. Click a Garou to see their personality, means, goal, friends and dislikes. You only see a Garou's personality and means once you've **met** them: talked to them in their
town or finished a mission they gave.

| Regard event | Change |
|---|---|
| A mission done for them | +2 |
| A mission done for their pack or a friend | +1 |
| A step of their goal done | +3 |
| A gift from their row | +1 per 500 silver, up to +3 a season |
| Their mission refused or let lapse | -1 (Proud -3) |
| A mission done against them or a friend | -3 |
| Betraying their goal | -10, for good |

Regard drifts a point toward 0 every season.

### Personalities

| Personality | How they pay and behave |
|---|---|
| Greedy | silver x0.7, goodwill x1.5 |
| Cautious | silver and goodwill x1.25, but the biggest debt they'll owe is a size smaller |
| Loyal | everything x1.25 while you never cross them or their friends; betrayal makes an enemy for good |
| Conniving | pays with a debt another Garou owes them |
| Cowardly | one more open mission, aimed at their rivals; pay x1.2 |
| Proud | goodwill x1.5, silver x0.8; refusals cost 3 regard |
| Zealous | only the war on the Wyrm; those pay x1.5 |
| Generous | everything x1.3; now and then asks one back |
| Vengeful | bad blood never fades; an enemy at -5 |
| Rage-driven | asks only for fights, and pays in Glory: a point per 500 silver of the job to each colony Garou there |
| Harano-struck | asks for one mission fewer, all cleansing fights; regard grows half again as fast |

### Means and goals

Means come from the Garou's office, wealth and the debts owed to them: a poor one offers one mission at a time
and pays x0.6, a comfortable one two at x1.0, a rich one three at x1.4.

Each Garou wants something, hidden until they count you a friend (regard +6) or you have a hold over them. A goal
is three steps: two missions bent to it, then the prize. At the end a letter asks whether you hand the prize over or
keep it. Keeping it is a betrayal.

| Goal | What you get for it |
|---|---|
| Collector | a fetish |
| Lore hunter | a fragment of the Silver Record (teaches a rite) or a Black Spiral's confession (experience) |
| Power seeker | silver by its means, and its vote for a year |
| Revenge | a major debt and the enemy's goods |
| Freedom | its regard stays at +6 or more for good |
| Childe | a minor debt now, its cub on your side later |
| Cleansing the Wyrm | each colony Garou learns, 3 experience |
| Kingmaker | the new Sept Leader starts at regard +6 |
| Homecoming | another sept, met |
| Buried secret | a life debt |

## Offices

Under **Your seats** in the Nation section, a colony Garou can seek one of the sept's seven offices. The Garou
holding it takes the challenge: Willpower plus rank against 7, one harder for each rank the holder has over yours.
A vacant office is simply taken. The Sept Leader needs rank 5 and beats the elder. A Garou can't hold an office here
and one at the colony's own caern.

| Office | Duty each season |
|---|---|
| Sept Leader | lead one moot |
| Master of the Rite | one rite for the sept |
| Warder | defend the caern once |
| Guardian | one patrol of the bawn |
| Den parent | foster a cub when the sept asks |
| Talesinger | attend every moot |
| Truthcatcher | judge one dispute (a letter) |

A duty kept adds 3 goodwill and a dot. Three missed in a row and the office is lost, with 10 goodwill, to the next in
line, who starts at regard -3. A finished moot counts as attended. The Garou's own tab shows the office and when its
duty is due, and an alert warns two days ahead.

## The council

Each season the Sept Leader and the elders (rank 4 and up) vote on one or two motions: cubs with leave or freely, war
on the Wyrm or waiting, hunting outsiders, the share owed to the caern. A colony Sept Leader votes too, and a Garou
may offer a minor debt for your vote.

## Enemies inside

Rivals slander the colony or stymie your next mission with their own people. Enemies send Kinfolk raiders. Capture
one and question them in prisoner talk to learn who sent them, then **Accuse** that Garou. An accusation without
proof costs 10 goodwill.

## War

A war runs against the Wyrm's nearest settlement, a Hive or a Black Spiral den, started by the council or the world.
Its score starts at 50: a season of incursions, two of raids on their warrens and labs while you hold your bawn, Kin
village, guard post and fetish hoard, then the final assault at 70, the desperate defence at 30, or a truce. With
the Wyrm turned off in settings there's no war.

The skirmishes come a few weeks apart. A war mission you let lapse is fought without you: the sept's members against the enemy's, by their strength, and members can die in it. Hover over the stages, the score and the holdings on the War page for what each one needs.

## Backgrounds

The Backgrounds page on a Garou's tab buys rated Backgrounds with standing at the sworn sept: the new rating x 2,
never experience. Leaving the sept, or the sept falling, halves every dot. Now and then a rival strikes at one of
them, at most once a season: a letter gives six days (more with Contacts) to answer with a mission or a favour the
rival owes, or the dot is lost. The "Rival strikes on Backgrounds" setting makes strikes rarer or more common, or turns them off.

| Background | Each dot |
|---|---|
| Allies | armed mortals to call on any map with enemies on it; they never witness anything |
| Contacts | a day more to answer a strike, and a chance each season to strike back at a rival |
| Fame | more tavern guests and regulars |
| Influence | a caravan from the Garou Nation once a season |
| Kinfolk | kin join 25% more often; call one of the kin village's own, as many alive as the dots, while it stands |
| Mentor | a sept member of high enough rank (a member 1 to the leader 5) teaches its tribe's Gifts, paid in experience |
| Resources | 100 x dots squared silver a season |
| Rites | one rite a season of a level up to the dots, taught by the sept without a teacher on the map |
| Fetish | a fetish of up to that level lent by the sept, already attuned; the making menu's fetish is this loan |

Ancestors and Totem show on the page too, from the sheet. They come with birth and the pack and are never bought.

## Settings

Under World on the Garou settings page: the sworn sept on or off, the war chance, enemy acts a season and how fast
regard fades. Dev mode has every step under "The Rim by Night: Spotlight".
