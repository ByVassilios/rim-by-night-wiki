# Menus and buttons

Where everything is on screen, and what each tab, button and menu option does. Almost every line
in the mod's windows has a tooltip; hover over it for the details.

## The Garou tab

Select a Garou and open the **Garou** tab next to Health and Needs. The heading gives the Garou's
tribe, auspice, breed and rank, with Rage and Gnosis as bars under it.

The pages are down the left side. A gold number beside Growth means the Garou has the Renown for its next rank
or Ambitions to pick from. Click a heading's name to fold the section under it; the page remembers
that until you quit the game. Click a tribe, breed, auspice, merit, rite or totem to read its page
in the [grimoire](#the-grimoire), and the **?** at the end of a heading opens the guide on that
subject.

### Sheet

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Who they are | Tribe and its totem, breed, auspice and the moon they were born under, tonight's moon, and their Merits and Flaws |
| Rage, Gnosis and Willpower | The permanent ratings as dots, with what's there now in brackets. Hover the next dot for its cost and click it to buy (Rage and Willpower cost the current rating, Gnosis twice it). Ancestors too |

### Attributes

The nine Attributes in three columns, Physical, Social and Mental, with the current form's
changes. Hover the next dot for its cost in experience and click it to buy. See
[Character sheet](Character-Sheet.md#attributes).

### Gifts

The Gifts the Garou knows, under their rank; click one for its page. **Learning** shows any
lessons under way and the **Learn a Gift...** button. See [Gifts](Gifts.md#learning-a-gift).

### Rites

Rituals, the rites the Garou knows, lessons under way, and **Learn a Rite...**. With a caern on the
map, its level and type. See [Rites and the caern](Rites-and-the-Caern.md).

### Growth

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Ambition | Your own Garou's current goal and how far along it is |
| Rank and Renown | The rank (hover for what the next one needs), standing with the tribe, and Glory, Honor and Wisdom with temporary points in brackets |

### Pack

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Totem | The Garou's Totem dots; hover the next dot for its cost |
| Pack | The pack's totem and whether the Ban holds, the alpha, the shared Willpower, the members and kin. Buttons: **Form a Pack...**, **Join a Pack...**, **Leave the pack**, **Make amends**, **Challenge the alpha** |
| Chronicle | The pack's story |
| Seat | The seat the Garou holds at the sept the colony is sworn to |

See [Packs and kinfolk](Packs-and-Kinfolk.md).

### Backgrounds

Allies, Contacts, Kinfolk and the rest. See [The sworn sept](The-Sworn-Sept.md).

### Conduct

Your own Garou only. **Habits** holds the resting form. **In a fight** picks the fighting style,
with Shift in fights and the Willpower habit, the same switches as on the form bar; click one to
change it. **The Veil** has the cover-up switch and each witness on the map with the surest way
to deal with them.

## Buttons on a Garou

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Seek Gnosis | Meditate for Gnosis. See [Being Garou](Being-Garou.md#gnosis) |
| Perform a rite | Lead a rite. See [Rites and the caern](Rites-and-the-Caern.md#performing-a-rite) |
| Call off the rite | Shown while the Garou leads one |
| Step sideways, Step back | Cross the Gauntlet. See [The Umbra and spirits](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md#stepping-sideways) |
| Call the ancestors | With Ancestors dots, once a season. See [Character sheet](Character-Sheet.md#ancestors) |
| Fury | 1 Rage for four hours of faster blows |
| End ... | Ends a Gift that's running |

**The form bar** has a button per form, the **In fights** switch and the **Willpower** habit.
See [Forms and shifting](Forms-and-Shifting.md).

**Gift tiles** sit beside it: one tile for the breed's Gifts, one for the auspice's, one for the
tribe's, and "other Gifts". Click an icon to use it, right-click it to read the Gift in the
grimoire, and click the tile's name for the breed's, auspice's or tribe's page. Turn the Core
setting "Power tiles" off for one button per Gift.

**Dice pools.** Hover a Gift to see the roll it makes: each part with its dice (Charisma 3,
Primal-Urge 2, the Long Vigil +1), the total and the difficulty. A difficulty set by the target reads as
a rule until the Gift is aimed ("against the target's Willpower"). The roll log puts the same parts in
a grey line under each roll, and a Gift's grimoire page has the formula in a box at the top.

## Other buttons

| Where | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| A fetish (right-click) | Attune | Sit with it and wake its spirit. See [Fetishes and talens](Fetishes-and-Talens.md) |
| A spirit (right-click) | Talk to ... | Ask a favor or a lesson. See [The Umbra and spirits](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md#talking-and-chiminage) |
| The caern | Cross the bridge | Sends everyone in the circle to the sept at the other end of the Opened Bridge |
| A caravan at a hostile town | Sneak in | Walks the caravan into the town unseen, at night. See [Settings and FAQ](Settings-and-FAQ.md) |
| A caravan at a sept | Rank challenge | Asks the elders to set a challenge. See [Rank challenges](The-Veil-and-Septs.md#rank-challenges) |
| A caravan at a sept | Cross the bridge home | Takes the Opened Bridge back to the caern |
| Misc build menu | Place of power | Marks the spot for a caern |
| A drafted colonist | Unleash | On a drafted colonist, beside Fire at will. It goes after the nearest enemy anywhere on the map and fights as an enemy of its kind would: powers first, closing in unseen, pulling back to heal when badly hurt. Anyone with a gun backs away from melee fighters and looks for cover. A pawn scared by a power stays on the map and goes back to the fight when the panic ends. Your orders come first, and once one is done it goes back to the hunt. Undrafting ends it. While it's on, a **Pick target** button chooses the enemy it goes after. Once that enemy is down or gone it picks the nearest one again, and clicking the button clears the target early. A power aimed out of range or out of sight walks the pawn closer, then casts. |

## The Kinfolk tab

Select a kinfolk human or wolf. **Blood** shows the tribe, the strength of the blood, whether
they could still Change, and which moments could still wake it. **Pack** shows the pack they
stand with, and **Join a pack...**. See [Packs and kinfolk](Packs-and-Kinfolk.md#kinfolk).

## The Garou tab

On the bar at the bottom of the screen. While the colony has two or more kinds from the Rim by Night mods, only the window used last keeps its button, and a list down the left side of each window names every one with its pages, so a click opens any page of any of them; the Core setting **One bottom-bar button** turns that off. Its pages:

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Overview | The Veil's level and score and, once the colony has a caern, the sept's offices. Click an office to seat a Garou in it or leave it vacant. See [The colony as a sept](The-Veil-and-Septs.md#the-colony-as-a-sept) |
| Septs | Each sept the colony knows, nearest first, with who leads it; hover a row for how far away it is. **Open** opens the sept's window; a click on the rest of the row finds it on the world map |

The world's news and the colony's own story are on the Chronicle and Our story pages under Rim by Night, at the top of the list down the window's left side. Each page holds the lines of every mod you have loaded; click a mod on its left for only that mod's lines, or a name under it for one colonist or home. Garou write frenzies, First Changes, ranks won, rites led and Wyrm creatures killed there, and the Nation's news names its septs.

The moon calendar, opened from the moon beside the clock, lists the quadrum's fifteen nights. Each night shows its moon,
the auspice it gives, the nights for Caern Building, the moot and the season's rite, and the full
moon's extra Wyrm raids. Click a night to see its Rage at moonrise and frenzy difficulty. On the
right, each of the colony's Garou shows tonight's Rage and how many days until their own moon. See
[The moon](Being-Garou.md#the-moon).

## Letters and alerts

| Letter or alert | What it means |
|---|---|
| Rage running high | Garou who could frenzy at the next provocation under tonight's moon |
| First Change | A kinfolk has Changed |
| Pack formed, Ban broken | The totem accepted a pack, or turned from it |
| Caern opened, grown, overrun, lost | The caern's story |
| The Wyrm comes | Caern Building has started, and a raid is on its way |
| Wyrm taint, Lost to the Wyrm | A Garou is close to the end of the Thrall's road, or past it |
| The Veil: rumours, hunted, exposed | The Veil has fallen to a new level |
| The septs | The septs have noticed you |
| Pulled across | A Garou was dragged into the Penumbra |
| Witnesses on the map | Outsiders who saw something and haven't left yet |
| Gnosis spent | Garou with no Gnosis left: no Gifts that cost it, no stepping sideways |
| Harano drowning | Garou so deep in Harano that one may walk away from the colony for good |
| Silver weapons | Enemies on the map carry silver |
| Totem Ban broken | A pack lost its totem's gifts. The Rite of Contrition makes amends |

While the colony has a Garou, tonight's moon shows beside the clock at the bottom right. Click it
for the moon calendar.

## Choice lists

Most menus open a list beside the window you clicked in, or above the gizmo. A long list has filters down the left with a count beside each, and **Can pick only** hides what can't be had yet. Click a row to read about it on the right, then press **Choose**, double-click it, or press Enter. A greyed row says under it what's missing. A list with nothing to read takes the row on one click.

## The grimoire

The book button in the bottom-right corner, beside vanilla's play settings, opens the grimoire: a
panel on the right edge that leaves the game running. It covers the alerts and letters while it's
open, and its X closes it. It holds every tribe, breed, auspice, Gift, rite, totem, fetish, merit, item and creature the mod adds, made from the game's own data, with this guide in the Garou chapter. The setting "Grimoire", under The Rim by Night: Core in the mod settings, hides it.

- **Selected** shows what to do about whoever or whatever you have selected, and links to read
  more. It changes as you select something else. A vampire or Garou hiding among your prisoners
  shows nothing of what it is.
- **Ask** looks through every chapter. A question works ("how do I step sideways"): each answer shows the sentence it
  found, and clicking one opens that part of the page.
- **Browse** lists every chapter, with a "what to do" section at the top of each mod's.
- Gold, underlined words open their own page. **Back** returns to the page before.
- The info button beside a page's title opens the game's own card, with every stat.
- From the game: click a row on the Garou or Kinfolk tab, right-click a Gift's icon, or press the
  **?** beside a tab heading.
- The book opens where you left it until you restart the game.
