# Everything else

Every other kind of thing this mod defines that has a name and a description: Merits, Backgrounds, gatherings, places, personalities and the rest.

## Ambitions

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A beast of the Wyrm** | Kill one of the Wyrm's beasts: a twisted animal, a bane in beast shape, a Wyrm monster. |
| **A tale at the moot** | Host a moot, where the sept hears the colony's tales. |
| **Bind a spirit** | Perform the Rite of Binding. |
| **Cleanse the land** | Perform the Rite of Cleansing on a tainted place or thing. |
| **Hold the caern** | Be at home when enemies reach the colony's caern, and still have it once they're gone. |
| **Judge a dispute** | Settle a dispute the sept brings to you. |
| **Keep the Litany** | Go a whole season without breaking the Litany. |
| **Outwit a foe** | Take two enemies alive, beaten and made to yield. |
| **Rites for the sept** | Lead two rites, any rites at all, as their ritemaster. |
| **Slay the Wyrm's own** | Kill two creatures of the Wyrm: banes, fomori, Black Spiral Dancers or worse. |
| **Teach a Gift** | Teach a Gift you know to another Garou. |
| **Win a challenge** | Beat a Garou who comes to challenge you. |

## Backgrounds

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ancestors** | Ancestor spirits who answer the Garou's call. Set at birth and never bought: once a season the Garou can borrow an ancestor's skill for an hour. |
| **Fetish** | The sept lends a fetish of a level up to the dots, picked from the row's link and given already attuned. A starting fetish from the making menu is this loan. Losing dots below the fetish's level takes it back, and picking another returns the first. |
| **Kinfolk** | Kin who know the Garou for what it is. Each dot makes kinfolk come to join the colony 25% more often. While the kin village stands and is friendly, you can call one of its own to join, as many as the dots. Another comes only when one of them dies. |
| **Rites** | The sept's ritemasters take an interest. Each season they teach one rite for each dot, of a level up to the dots, picked from the row's link. No teacher is needed on the map, but Rituals still has to reach the rite's level. |
| **Totem** | Points the Garou brings toward its pack's totem. Set at the First Change and never bought: it is pooled when a pack forms. |

## Breeds

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Homid** | Born human and raised among people, with no idea what they were until the First Change. Homids handle tools, trade and talk well, but the spirit world comes to them slowly. |
| **Lupus** | Born a wolf, and a wolf at heart in any shape. Lupus are at home in the wild and the spirit world, and have a hard time with tools and polite talk. |
| **Metis** | The child of two Garou, which the Litany forbids. Born in the war form, always marked by some deformity, and unable to have children. Raised inside a sept, a Metis knows Garou ways better than anyone, and many Garou still look down on them. |

| **Fallen caern** | A caern that died when its sept did. Fomori live in the lodge now, and the totem's carving is cracked through. |
| **Fomori camp** | A camp of fomori, raw and crowded. They took some of the sept's kin when they came. |
| **Loggers' camp** | A camp of loggers and squatters cutting its way into the last wild land near the sept. Stumps and spoiled water spread out around it. |
| **Pentex subsidiary** | A plant run by one of Pentex's companies. It poisons the land around it, and fomori and First Team mercenaries guard it. |
| **Wyrm-bloated beast** | An animal the Wyrm got into. It's bigger than it should be, and the ground where it sleeps has gone grey. |

## Rites

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Breath of Gaia** | A deep breath of clean air at dawn, to honor Gaia. The body fights off sickness a little better for a day. |
| **Gathering for the Departed** | A howl for the dead, over their body. Everyone nearby mourns together, and the Garou's despair eases a little. |
| **Greet the Moon** | A howl to Luna, outdoors under tonight's moon. The Garou is steadier for a day. |
| **Hunting Prayer** | A quiet word to the spirits before the hunt. The Garou stalks a little quieter for a day. |
| **Moot Rite** | The gathering at the caern, once each lunar cycle. The whole colony takes heart, the ritemaster earns Honor, and each moot held makes the caern stronger. |
| **Prayer for the Prey** | Thanks given to the animals the Garou kills. Nothing of the prey goes to waste for a day. |
| **Rite of Accomplishment** | The Garou's deeds are told and honored. Every full measure of temporary Renown becomes a permanent dot, and a Garou with the Renown for the next rank takes it. |
| **Rite of Binding** | A willing spirit is bound into a talen: a one-use charm that does its task and lets the spirit go. One talen a success, up to three. |
| **Rite of Caern Building** | The greatest rite: a night-long working at a cleansed place of power, begun at dusk under a waxing moon. Three or more Garou pour in thirty Gnosis between them, kinfolk a point each, and every Garou gives up a point of Gnosis for good. The Wyrm will come while it runs. Failure burns everyone in the circle. |
| **Rite of Cleansing** | Water, smoke and a howl drive the Wyrm's taint out of a person, a patch of ground or a fetish, or ready a place of power for a caern. |
| **Rite of Contrition** | The Garou humble themselves to make amends. It brings back a totem whose Ban was broken, and clears a Litany broken this season. |
| **Rite of Ostracism** | One by one the circle names the offender, says they have no such kin, and turns away. The offender loses Renown, and their packmates shun them until the moon turns. |
| **Rite of Passage** | A cub proves themselves before the tribe and becomes a Cliath, with a Cliath's Renown and the Gifts of their auspice and tribe. |
| **Rite of Reawakening** | At the spring equinox the ritemaster leads the circle sideways into the Penumbra, where banes come at them in waves. If every bane falls, Gaia wakes: the circle's Gnosis fills, and the crops grow faster for the rest of the season. |
| **Rite of Summoning** | Performed in the Penumbra, where it comes easiest: a spirit of the chosen kind is called to the circle. |
| **Rite of the Fetish** | A willing spirit is bound into a cleansed vessel for good, making a fetish of any level up to the ritemaster's rank. |
| **Rite of the Jackdaw** | Open-mouthed sigils are carved and laid around a Garou who broke a promise of secrecy. For a day per success, they can't stop telling everyone about their own petty secrets. |
| **Rite of the Opened Bridge** | The ritemaster lays a pathstone at the heart of the caern and calls on its totem to open a road through the Umbra to the nearest friendly sept. For a year, renewed at every moot, colonists can cross from the caern to that sept and back, and the sept's Garou come at once when the Wyrm attacks. |
| **Rite of the Opened Caern** | The ritemaster wrestles with the caern's spirit to draw out its power, of whatever kind the caern holds. Losing badly burns them. |
| **Rite of the Opened Sky** | A rain dance calls down a cleansing rain over the caern. It washes out the Wyrm's taint, and for two more Gnosis a level it heals everyone under it who isn't an enemy. Vampires and the Wyrm's creatures are left in agony, but not cleansed. One Gnosis at the caern, ten anywhere else. |
| **Rite of the Totem** | Garou with no pack call a totem spirit and bind themselves to it as a pack. Their Totem dots together must reach the totem's cost. |
| **Rite of the Winter Winds** | On the longest night the circle howls, grabs burning branches and runs through the dark making every frightful noise it can, to scare the Wyrm's servants off. The next Wyrm raid this season doesn't come. |
| **Satire Rite** | A song of ridicule performed at the caern while the offender watches, remembered for generations. The offender drops a rank. If it goes badly wrong, the song turns on the ritemaster. |
| **Stone of Scorn** | A stone passes around the circle, and each Garou carves a mark of shame on it and tells a mocking tale about the offender, who has to sit and listen. The offender loses a great deal of Honor. |
| **The Great Hunt** | At midsummer Gaia names a servant of the Wyrm, and it comes. Kill it before midnight and the circle wins Glory. Let it get away and they lose Glory, and every rite is harder for a year. |
| **The Hunt** | Called against a Garou guilty of a capital crime. The offender is cast out of the colony and runs, and the hunters give no quarter. Whoever brings them down earns Honor. |
| **The Long Vigil** | At the autumn equinox the caern is hung with the year's trophies, and the circle tells their stories around a bonfire until dawn, then burns them. Until winter the circle rolls a die more on Rage and is in better spirits. |
| **Voice of the Jackal** | Ash is blown in the face of a Garou who shamed the whole sept. They lose Renown, and their voice becomes a shrill whine that hurts every dealing with others until the punishment is lifted. |

## Seats and offices

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Den parent** | Raises the sept's cubs through their first Change. Fosters a cub when the sept asks. |
| **Guardian** | Walks the bawn and turns back what doesn't belong there. |
| **Master of the Rite** | Keeps the sept's rites and leads them. Owes the sept one rite a season. |
| **Sept Leader** | Leads the sept and calls its moots. Taken only by a Garou of rank 5 who beats the elder's challenge. |
| **Talesinger** | Tells the sept's deeds at every moot, and keeps who did what straight. |
| **Truthcatcher** | Hears disputes between the sept's Garou and judges them by the Litany. |
| **Warder** | Holds the caern against whatever comes for it. |

## The Litany

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Accept an honourable surrender** | A beaten foe who yields is spared. Killing a captive is a crime, not a victory. |
| **Do not eat the flesh of humans** | People are not prey. A Garou who eats one has taken a step toward the Wyrm. |
| **Don't let your people carry you** | A Garou who can no longer do their part steps aside rather than be carried. |
| **Fight the Wyrm wherever it is** | No Garou leaves the Wyrm's servants alone once they know of them. |
| **Garou do not mate with Garou** | Two Garou together make a Metis, born deformed and barren. The Litany forbids it. |
| **In peace, the leader may be challenged** | In peace, any Garou may challenge for a higher place. This one permits rather than forbids. |
| **In war, the leader is not challenged** | When the sept fights, its leader is not questioned until the fighting is over. |
| **Keep the Veil closed** | Humans must not learn what the Garou are. The last time they did, they hunted the wolves nearly to the end. |
| **Never let a caern be violated** | A caern is Gaia's own ground. Losing one through carelessness is among the worst things a Garou can do. |
| **Respect another's territory** | A sept's land is its own. Hunting or settling there takes leave first. |
| **Respect those beneath you: all are of Gaia** | Kin, captives and animals are Gaia's too. They aren't to be harmed for no reason. |
| **Submit to those of higher station** | The lower-ranked defer to the higher, and challenge only in turn. |
| **The first share of the kill to the greatest in station** | At a feast, the highest-ranked eats first. |
