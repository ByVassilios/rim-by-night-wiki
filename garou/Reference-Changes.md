# What changed

What each update changed, newest first, as the grimoire shows it.

## Update of 14 November 2026

### Requests and favours
- The Nation asks for help: a wounded Garou with fomori behind it, a Ronin begging five days' shelter, a Garou pinned with silver at the map's edge. Saving them earns Honor.
- A sept can ask seven kinds of favour, about one letter every ten days, such as keeping a wounded elder for a season.
- Each Garou has a regard with its tribe from -10 to 10, shown on the Character tab. From Fostern on, its tribe asks things of it about once a season.
- With a caern and the Nation's goodwill the colony can host moots: the neighbours' moot at 20, a Hearing at 40, and more.
### Other changes
- The Rite of Cleansing can target a tainted fetish on the ground.
- Each form has its own comfortable temperatures.
- Dreamspeak reaches the whole map.
- Towns and sites have better rooms, with beds only where people sleep.
- See [[RBN_Changes_20261114|the grimoire's own changes]].
